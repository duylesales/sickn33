What “production-ready” actually means

Six items, in this order. The order matters more than the list — each one makes the next safer to do.

1 Secrets and environment variables — DevTools → Sources → search sk_. Anything there is public.
2 Error handling on external calls — kill your wifi mid-submit. Clear message, or a blank screen?
3 Authorization at the API, not the UI — open user A’s URL as user B. Actually blocked?
4 A CI pipeline that blocks bad deploys — push something broken on purpose. Does anything stop it?
5 Tests on the three to five flows that matter — sign-up, payment, export. Any test at all?
6 Basic observability — it breaks at 3am. Do you know before your users tell you?
