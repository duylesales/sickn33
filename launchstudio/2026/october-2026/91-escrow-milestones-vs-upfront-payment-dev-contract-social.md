💸 Daniel got a €6,200 upfront-payment demand from a cold-outreach agency — for a rebuild his **Bolt** prototype didn't even need. Before wiring a cent, he asked LaunchStudio to review the contract terms. 🧠

If your dev contract asks for full payment before any verified work, you're absorbing 100% of the delivery risk yourself — with almost no recourse if the vendor underdelivers or disappears.

❌ Full payment wired before a single verifiable deliverable exists
❌ Vague "progress checkpoints" instead of testable milestones
❌ No kill clause — you're locked in even if the vendor stops performing

✅ Escrow-style milestones tied to testable deliverables (audit, staging, production)
✅ A defined review window before each payment tranche releases
✅ A kill clause so either side can exit cleanly after any milestone

At **LaunchStudio**, we've structured every hardening engagement around fixed-scope, milestone-based contracts since 2014 through Manifera, across 160+ delivered projects. 🛡️
