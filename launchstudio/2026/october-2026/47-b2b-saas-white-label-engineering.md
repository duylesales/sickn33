As the owner of a successful digital marketing or design agency, you are sitting on a goldmine. You have deep, trusted relationships with enterprise clients. You know their pain points, their workflows, and their budgets.

## The Problem with In-House Engineering

When an agency tries to pivot into custom software development by hiring in-house developers, three things usually go wrong:

### 1. The Cost of Talent

To build secure, scalable B2B SaaS, you cannot hire junior developers. You need a Senior Backend Architect, a DevOps Specialist, and a React expert, plus someone who understands database security well enough to configure Row Level Security correctly the first time. In Europe, that team will cost you over €250,000 annually in salaries alone. If your agency doesn't have a constant, 12-month pipeline of massive software projects to keep that team fully utilized, that payroll will destroy your margins within two quarters.

### 2. The Liability Trap

Enterprise AI software requires strict security. If an in-house developer accidentally exposes an OpenAI API key or leaks a client's private database because they didn't understand Row Level Security (RLS), your agency is legally liable — not a subcontractor, not a vendor, your agency's name on the contract. This is especially dangerous given that independent audits have found 45% of AI-generated code contains exploitable vulnerabilities; a junior developer leaning on AI tools to move fast is statistically likely to ship exactly this kind of gap.

### 3. Distraction from Core Competencies

Your agency is great at UX design, strategy, and client relations. Managing Jira sprints, conducting code reviews, maintaining server infrastructure, and staying current on framework updates is a completely different business model that will distract you and your best people from what you do best — and what actually wins new business.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What exactly is White-Label Engineering?
White-label engineering is a B2B service where a specialized software firm (LaunchStudio) builds custom software on behalf of another company (your agency), completely unbranded. Your agency presents the final product to the client as your own work, and we never appear anywhere in the client-facing deliverables or contracts.

### How does the pricing and profit work?
We provide you with a fixed-price quote based on the technical requirements, for example €20,000. You add your agency's markup, design fees, and management fees, and sell it to the client for a higher figure, for example €50,000. You keep the margin, and you only pay us when the client pays you, which keeps your cash flow predictable.

### Will LaunchStudio try to steal my clients?
Absolutely not. We sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Non-Compete clauses. Our entire business model relies on making agencies successful so they bring us more projects. Stealing a client would destroy our reputation across the entire agency network we work with. We operate entirely invisibly under your brand.

### Do you handle ongoing maintenance after the app launches?
Yes. We offer white-labeled Service Level Agreements (SLAs). You charge your client a monthly retainer for "App Maintenance & Hosting," and you pay us a flat fee to actually execute the 24/7 server monitoring, dependency updates, and bug fixes, turning a one-time project into recurring revenue for your agency.

### Can your engineers join our client calls?
Yes. For complex enterprise projects, a LaunchStudio technical lead can join your client pitches or technical due diligence meetings. We use an email address from your agency's domain and introduce ourselves as your "Lead Backend Architect," giving your team instant technical credibility in front of the client's IT department.
