# Choosing Between Manifera's Amsterdam, Singapore, and Ho Chi Minh City Teams

Founders who research LaunchStudio closely enough eventually stumble on a detail that raises a question nobody quite expects: Manifera operates out of three cities — Amsterdam, Singapore, and Ho Chi Minh City — and no single page explains why, or which one actually does what. It's a fair question. If you're about to hand your AI-built prototype to an outside team, you want to know whose hands are actually on your codebase, not just which logo is on the invoice. This article explains what each of Manifera's three locations is structurally responsible for, why the split exists at all, and why — for the vast majority of founders — the answer to "which team should I choose" is that you don't choose one; you get all three, working together, by default.

## Why Manifera Is Structured Across Three Cities in the First Place

Manifera wasn't built as three competing offices bidding for the same client. It was built as one engineering organization with three deliberately different functions, each located where that function works best. Herre Roelevink founded Manifera in 2014 around a specific thesis: "Dutch management with Vietnamese mastery" — pairing European-standard client communication, project governance, and quality assurance with a large, senior engineering bench built in Vietnam, connected through a regional hub in Singapore. Eleven-plus years later, that structure is why Manifera can serve enterprise clients like Vodafone and TNO alongside solo AI-native founders using the exact same operating model, just scaled differently.

## Amsterdam: Account Management, Client Strategy, and European Time-Zone Coverage

Manifera's headquarters sits at Herengracht 420 in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and this is where client relationships live. Founders who sign with LaunchStudio typically deal directly with Amsterdam-based account management for the initial codebase review, the scoping conversation, and ongoing project communication throughout the engagement. This isn't a sales layer bolted on top of engineering — it's the team responsible for translating a founder's actual business goals into a scope document an engineering team can execute against, and for making sure that scope stays honest as the project runs.

The practical reason this matters: Amsterdam operates on CET/CEST, the same working day as the majority of LaunchStudio's client base across the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, and the wider EU. A founder in Rotterdam or Berlin isn't waiting until evening for a reply from someone who's asleep — Amsterdam is awake and working during the exact hours a European founder is checking email, reviewing a staging build, or asking a scope question. Roelevink, based in Amsterdam himself, frames the company's role this way: *"We see a shift in software needs. The challenge is no longer turning good ideas into software. It's now about the architecture and security needed to bring those products to maturity."* That framing — architecture and security as the real bottleneck — is set at the Amsterdam level and carried through everything downstream.

## Singapore: The Asia-Pacific Bridge

Manifera's Singapore office, at 100 Tras Street, plays a narrower but still important role: it's the regional hub connecting Amsterdam's European client relationships to the Ho Chi Minh City engineering center, and it's the primary point of contact for founders and enterprise clients based in the Asia-Pacific region — Singapore itself, but also founders building for markets in Southeast Asia, Australia, and beyond, who want a conversation happening in a time zone closer to their own working day.

Singapore sits roughly 5-6 hours ahead of CET and close to Ho Chi Minh City's time zone, which makes it a natural coordination point rather than a separate delivery center. For most AI-native founders — the large majority of whom are based in Europe — Singapore isn't the office you'll interact with directly day-to-day. It exists so that the handful of Asia-Pacific-based founders and Manifera's regional enterprise relationships have a properly staffed local presence, without needing to build a fourth, separate delivery pipeline to serve them.

## Ho Chi Minh City: The Primary Engineering Center

This is where the actual hardening work happens for the overwhelming majority of LaunchStudio projects. Manifera's Ho Chi Minh City office, on Pho Quang Street, is the company's primary development center — the largest concentration of senior engineers in the organization, and the team that implements the Row Level Security policies, wires the Stripe webhooks, secures the API keys, and configures the deployment pipeline that turns an AI-builder prototype into a production-ready MVP.

Vietnam's engineering talent pool is a large part of why LaunchStudio can price a Launch Ready package at €800-€1,500 or a Launch & Grow engagement at €1,500-€3,500 instead of the $30,000-$80,000 a comparable US or Western European agency typically quotes for similar scope — not because the engineering is less rigorous, but because the cost structure underneath it is fundamentally different, without sacrificing the seniority of the engineers actually doing the work. Ho Chi Minh City operates roughly 5-6 hours ahead of CET, which is a deliberate structural advantage, not a coincidence: work handed off at the end of the Amsterdam team's day is picked up by engineers who are just starting theirs, rather than sitting untouched overnight the way it would with a team in a trailing time zone.

## So Which Team Do You Actually Get?

Here's the part that surprises most founders once they understand the structure: you don't pick one city. Every LaunchStudio engagement, regardless of package tier, involves all three locations working as a single coordinated team — Amsterdam managing the relationship and scope, Ho Chi Minh City executing the engineering work, and Singapore providing regional coverage when a client's own time zone calls for it. A founder in Lisbon gets Amsterdam-hours account management and Ho Chi Minh City engineering hours that pick up right where the European day left off. A founder in Singapore or Sydney gets the same engineering team, with account coordination that can flex toward the Singapore hub when that's the more practical point of contact.

The one scenario where the "which office" question becomes genuinely relevant is enterprise clients with specific regional compliance, data-residency, or in-person meeting requirements — the kind of considerations that come up for organizations like Vodafone or TNO more than for a solo AI-native founder shipping a SaaS MVP. For that segment, Manifera's account team will actively route the engagement toward the office best equipped to handle a particular region's requirements. For the founder audience this article is written for, though, the honest answer is simpler: you're not choosing between three teams, you're getting three teams' worth of coverage for the price of one engagement.

## Why This Structure Beats a Single-City Agency

Compare this to hiring a single-city agency — a Berlin studio, a London shop, a San Francisco firm — and the tradeoff becomes clearer. A single-city team gives you one time zone, one talent pool at that city's cost structure, and one point of failure if a key person is out sick or on holiday during your launch week. Manifera's three-city structure means the account relationship, the engineering execution, and the regional coverage don't all collapse onto the same handful of people working the same eight hours. If an Amsterdam account manager is unavailable, the Ho Chi Minh City engineering team is still moving. If a specific engineer in Vietnam is on leave, the team structure — not a single freelancer — absorbs that gap.

This is also why LaunchStudio can credibly promise a 1-to-3-week hardening timeline instead of the 6-to-10-week timelines common among single-location agencies: the work genuinely does not stop when Amsterdam's day ends, because Ho Chi Minh City's day is just beginning.

- Most founders don't choose one office — every LaunchStudio engagement uses all three in a coordinated structure by default, regardless of package tier.
- Amsterdam's CET/CEST hours give European founders full working-day overlap for account management, while Ho Chi Minh City's hours sit ahead of CET, meaning engineering work continues rather than pausing overnight.
- Singapore exists primarily to serve Asia-Pacific-based founders and enterprise clients who benefit from a regional point of contact closer to their own time zone.
- The three-city structure is a large part of why LaunchStudio can offer fixed pricing well below single-city agency rates without reducing engineering seniority, and why its 1-to-3-week timelines are realistic rather than aspirational.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to choose which Manifera office works on my project?

No. Every LaunchStudio engagement draws on all three locations by default — Amsterdam for account management and scoping, Ho Chi Minh City for the engineering work itself, and Singapore for regional coordination when relevant. You don't select an office; the structure routes the work automatically.

### Why is the main engineering team based in Ho Chi Minh City rather than Amsterdam?

Ho Chi Minh City is Manifera's primary development center and largest concentration of senior engineers, operating at a cost structure that lets LaunchStudio offer fixed pricing well below typical Western European or US agency rates without reducing engineering seniority. It also sits ahead of CET, so work continues rather than pausing when the European day ends.

### What does the Amsterdam office actually do if it's not writing code?

Amsterdam handles the initial codebase review, scoping conversation, and ongoing account management throughout the engagement, operating on CET/CEST for full working-hours overlap with most LaunchStudio clients. It's the team responsible for translating a founder's business goals into an accurate scope document and keeping communication consistent as the project runs.

### When does the Singapore office actually get involved?

Primarily for founders and enterprise clients based in the Asia-Pacific region who benefit from a regional point of contact closer to their own working hours, and for coordination between the Amsterdam and Ho Chi Minh City teams. Most European founders won't interact with Singapore directly, though the office remains part of the same coordinated structure.

### Does working across three time zones slow down communication?

The opposite — it's the specific design that prevents delay. Because Amsterdam's working day and Ho Chi Minh City's working day are staggered rather than overlapping, work handed off at the end of one team's day is picked up at the start of the other's, keeping projects moving through most of a 24-hour cycle instead of stalling overnight the way a single-time-zone agency would.
