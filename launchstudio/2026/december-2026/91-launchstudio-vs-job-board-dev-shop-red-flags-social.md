If you're vetting an overseas agency off a job board, the lowest quote and the best-looking reviews are often the least reliable signals you can use.

❌ A lump-sum quote with no itemized breakdown of tasks
❌ Nobody on the team can name the senior engineer touching your database
❌ Reviews that only exist on the platform where the shop's income depends on staying five stars

✅ Insist on an itemized scope with named tasks and a named senior engineer
✅ Get a written definition of "done" you can independently verify
✅ Ask for an off-platform reference you can contact directly
