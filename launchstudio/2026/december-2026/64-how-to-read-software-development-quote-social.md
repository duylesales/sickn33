Most founders skim a quote for the total number and sign. That habit is expensive: the document tells you almost everything about how the engagement will actually go, if you know what red flags to look for.

❌ Vague scope language like "up to 40 hours" or "including but not limited to"
❌ An open-ended "additional scope billed separately" clause with no cap
❌ Zero mention of Row Level Security, webhook verification, or secret management

✅ A closed, itemized deliverables list you can use as a QA checklist
✅ A single fixed total price for a fixed, named scope — no uncapped hourly clock
✅ Security, payments, and infrastructure hardening named explicitly as line items
