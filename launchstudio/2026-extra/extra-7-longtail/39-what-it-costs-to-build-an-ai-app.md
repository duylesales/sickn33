What does it actually cost to build an AI app you can charge real money for, not just one you can click through in a demo? Most founders ask this question backwards — they price out the AI tool subscription, maybe €20 to €50 a month, and assume that's the number. It isn't. That number gets you a prototype. The real number, the one that gets you to a secure, launchable product, involves a different set of line items entirely, and almost nobody adds them up until they're already three weeks into a freelancer engagement wondering where the budget went.

Sebastian Krammer ran into exactly this in Zurich. He had an idea for TicketFlow, an event ticketing tool for small venues, a rough Lovable prototype that wasn't even fully working yet, and no real sense of what "finished" would cost. He'd heard numbers ranging from a few hundred euros to tens of thousands, from different people, none of which matched what he actually needed.

## Starting point: what "prototype only" actually means

Sebastian's starting point matters for the cost breakdown, because it wasn't a working app — it was an idea plus a partial Lovable build with a few screens roughed out and no working backend behind them. This is a meaningfully earlier stage than a founder who already has a functioning prototype that pilot users have tested, and it changes the cost math. A production build starting from "idea only" typically runs on a multiplier compared to one starting from a working prototype, since more of the core logic needs to be built rather than hardened.

## The actual cost components, one by one

Security review and fixes typically run in the €500–€1,200 range depending on how many gaps exist, covering things like authorization checks, encrypted sensitive fields, and secrets management. Payment integration, wiring up Stripe or Mollie with proper subscription or ticket-purchase handling, usually adds €400–€800. User accounts and authentication, done properly with role-based access for organizers versus ticket buyers, adds another €250–€500. Hosting, deployment, and a custom domain with SSL typically run €200–€500. Database setup, including a real persistent store replacing whatever the prototype was using, adds €350–€700. None of these line items are fixed in isolation — a project needing several of them together, like TicketFlow did, is priced as one fixed quote rather than a sum of separate invoices, but breaking it down this way shows founders where the money actually goes instead of leaving it as one opaque number.

## Why is the freelancer market so much more expensive for the same scope?

Freelancers and small toolshops typically charge €5,000–€20,000 for comparable scope — roughly 1.5 to 3 times what a specialized production studio charges for the same work — and the reason isn't that they're doing more. It's usually that they don't specialize in AI-generated code specifically, so they spend billable hours understanding a codebase they didn't write and didn't expect, time a founder ends up paying for without getting extra value from it. A traditional agency goes further in the other direction, often insisting on a full rebuild priced at €20,000 and up, because they don't trust code they didn't originate, regardless of whether it actually needs replacing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does it cost more to build an AI app starting from just an idea versus a working prototype?

Yes, generally. Starting from an idea alone means more core logic needs to be built from scratch, while a working prototype mainly needs security, payments, and infrastructure added around what already exists.

### Why do freelancers often charge more than a specialized studio for the same scope?

Freelancers typically don't specialize in AI-generated codebases, so they spend billable time understanding code they didn't write, which the founder ends up paying for without extra value in return.

### What's included in a typical fixed quote to build an AI app for production?

Commonly a security review and fixes, payment integration, proper user accounts and authentication, database setup, and production hosting with a custom domain, bundled as one fixed price.

### Is €800 to €7,500 really enough to build an AI app for production?

For most single-product SaaS, webshop, or tool-style projects, yes. Highly custom or enterprise-scale builds may exceed that range, but the majority of AI-native founder projects fall within it.

### How long does it take to build an AI app from an idea to production?

For a project starting from an idea plus a partial prototype like TicketFlow, roughly two weeks is typical, though scope and complexity shift that timeline in either direction.
