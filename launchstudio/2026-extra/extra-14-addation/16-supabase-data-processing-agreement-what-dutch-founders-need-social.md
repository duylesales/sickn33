One evening, one dropdown, and a school app's data ended up in the US. 🏫

Yusuf Kaya, a primary school teacher in Almere, built Ouderlijn with Lovable: schools send parents updates, collect permission slips and schedule parent-teacher meetings. Two schools piloted it. Then a school board with eleven schools wanted to adopt it, and its privacy officer sent a standard request:
• A verwerkersovereenkomst
• A list of sub-processors with their locations
• Confirmation that pupils' data stays in the EU

What Yusuf found when he went to check:
• The Supabase project had been created in a US region
• The Supabase organisation was registered to his personal Gmail address
• No DPA had been signed
• Permission slip scans with children's names sat in a public storage bucket

What LaunchStudio did (11 business days, €3,700):
✓ A new Supabase project in Frankfurt, under a company-owned organisation
✓ The database and files migrated
✓ The DPA signed with Yusuf
✓ Storage made private, with signed URLs
✓ Row Level Security completed per school and class
✓ Every processor documented with its location for the board
✓ A retention job removing permission slips after the school year ends

Region: [ Frankfurt ▼ ]

The school board approved Ouderlijn and rolled it out to all eleven schools, about 2,600 families. New schools now get a two-page privacy document instead of Yusuf getting a week of worry.
