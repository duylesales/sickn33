✏️ THE SLA, WITH CORRECTIONS

Availability: 99.99% ➜ 99.5% monthly target
Support: 24/7 ➜ business days, with defined response times
Fixes: within four hours ➜ modest service credits
Measured by: nothing ➜ monitoring with history

Bram Oosting built Schoonplan in Bolt: a cleaning schedule and quality inspection tool for office buildings. His first B2B prospect, a facility management company with 40 buildings in Rotterdam, asked for terms, a processing agreement and an SLA. Bram found a template online. It promised 99.99% availability, 24/7 support and four-hour fixes.

Before signing, the prospect's IT contact asked two questions: how do you measure availability, and who answers the phone at night?

The honest answers: no monitoring, a one-person company, and a backup that had never been restored.

So first, LaunchStudio built what an SLA needs underneath it (11 business days, €4,100):
✓ Monitoring with history
✓ EU hosting, with tested backups and a restore test
✓ Tenant isolation
✓ MFA
✓ Organisation export and deletion
✓ Input for a security summary

Then, with real data on what his setup actually delivered, Bram's lawyer drafted the corrected SLA above, plus a processing agreement and security summary matching the measures in place.

The facility company signed a two-year contract. Its IT contact said the realistic SLA was more convincing than the original template.

"I almost signed a promise I couldn't keep. A smaller promise backed by real measures won the deal." - Bram Oosting
