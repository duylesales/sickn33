🚨 Bram Oosting built Schoonplan in Bolt: cleaning schedules and quality inspections for office buildings. His first B2B prospect, a facility company with 40 buildings in Rotterdam, asked for terms, a processing agreement and an SLA. Bram found a template online promising 99.99% availability, 24/7 support and four-hour fixes. Then their IT contact asked how he measured availability - and who answered calls at night. 😳

He almost signed a promise he couldn't keep. A smaller promise backed by real measures won the deal. Here's where he stood: 🧠

❌ An SLA copied from a template, promising 99.99% availability
❌ No monitoring to measure any of it
❌ A one-person company promising 24/7 support
❌ A backup that had never been restored

✅ Monitoring with history, EU hosting and tested backups
✅ Tenant isolation, MFA and organisation-level export and deletion
✅ A realistic SLA from his lawyer: 99.5% monthly, business-day support with defined response times, modest service credits
✅ A processing agreement and security summary matching the measures actually in place
