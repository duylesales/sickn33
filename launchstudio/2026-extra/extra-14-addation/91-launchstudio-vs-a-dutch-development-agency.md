The Netherlands has many excellent development agencies (app bouwers, softwarebureaus) that design and build products from scratch. When a founder arrives with an AI-built prototype, though, the agency model and the founder's situation often do not match: agencies are organised around discovery, design and full builds, while the founder needs the last mile on something that already exists. Here is how a Dutch agency and LaunchStudio compare, and when each is the right call.

## Side-by-Side

| Aspect | Dutch development agency | LaunchStudio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical starting point | Idea or requirements | Working AI-built prototype |
| Approach | Discovery, design, build, launch | Keep frontend, fix what is needed, launch |
| Typical price | €20,000-€150,000+ | €800-€7,500 fixed |
| Typical timeline | 3-12 months | 1-3 weeks |
| Your AI-built frontend | Often replaced | Kept |
| Pricing model | Fixed bid or hourly, often with change requests | Fixed price after a 15-minute call |
| Ongoing relationship | Retainer common | Optional managed hosting (€49/month) or new engagements |
| Design and UX capacity | Strong | Limited; your design stays yours |

## When an Agency Is the Better Choice

- You have an idea and funding but no prototype, and no time to build one
- You need professional UX research and design
- The product is complex from day one: marketplaces with matching, heavy integrations, regulated workflows
- You want one partner for design, build and long-term development

## When LaunchStudio Is the Better Choice

- You already have a working prototype in Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, v0 or Replit
- You want to launch in weeks and validate with real customers
- Your budget is limited and needs a fixed number
- You want to keep building yourself after launch

## Why Agencies Often Propose a Rebuild

It is not bad faith. Agencies have their own stack, standards and processes, and maintaining code they did not write is risky for them. Rebuilding gives them control. For a founder, though, it means paying again for the interface and waiting months. LaunchStudio's model starts from the opposite assumption: the frontend you built is valuable, and the work is what lies underneath.

## The Middle Ground

Some founders launch with LaunchStudio, grow, and later engage an agency or a dedicated team for the next phase, with a codebase that is documented and production-ready. Manifera, the company behind LaunchStudio, also provides full development teams for that stage through its [offshore development services](https://www.manifera.com/services/offshore-software-development/).

## Questions to Ask Any Agency

1. Will you keep my existing frontend, or rebuild?
2. Is the price fixed, and what counts as a change request?
3. Who owns the code and accounts during and after the project?
4. What happens after launch, and what does it cost?
5. Have you worked with AI-generated codebases before?

## Real example

### An AI-Native Founder in Action: The Two Quotes That Told Different Stories

Sanne Brouwer, a dietitian in Groningen, built Maaltijdmaatje in Lovable: personalised meal plans and progress tracking for clients of independent dietitians. Two Dutch agencies quoted €38,000 and €52,000, both with a discovery phase and a rebuild in their preferred stack, and timelines of four to six months.

Sanne asked LaunchStudio for a quote on the same scope. After a 15-minute call, the quote was €5,400 for the Launch & Grow package: Row Level Security per dietitian and client, EU hosting, Mollie subscriptions with iDEAL, transactional email, staging, monitoring and managed hosting, keeping her Lovable frontend.

**Result:** Maaltijdmaatje launched in 14 business days with five dietitians and 180 clients. A year later, with revenue, Sanne engaged a design agency for a brand refresh of her marketing site, a project where an agency was the right fit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are agency quotes so much higher than LaunchStudio's?

Agencies typically include discovery, design and a full build, often replacing the existing frontend. LaunchStudio keeps the frontend you built and focuses on the production layer, with engineering delivered through Manifera's development centre.

### Is an agency better quality than LaunchStudio?

Not inherently; they do different work. Agencies excel at design and full builds. LaunchStudio specialises in making AI-built prototypes secure, reliable and live. Quality depends on fit as much as on skill.

### Can I use LaunchStudio first and an agency later?

Yes. Many founders launch with LaunchStudio, validate with customers and then bring in an agency or a dedicated team for the next phase, starting from a documented, production-ready codebase.

### How does Manifera's background compare with Dutch agencies?

Manifera is itself a full-cycle software company with 11+ years of experience and Dutch leadership. LaunchStudio is its specialised service for founders, which allows it to offer agency-grade engineering for last-mile work at founder prices.
