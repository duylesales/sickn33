SOUTH OF THE BORDER 🇧🇪
Checkout offers cards and iDEAL. No Bancontact. Belgian visitors without a credit card have no familiar way to pay, and abandon checkout at a high rate. The few who pay by debit card often hit failed renewals, because subscriptions charge by card only.

The awkward part: the club is Belgian.

Bert Willaert runs Hopkist, a craft beer subscription from Antwerp, built in Lovable: monthly boxes of Belgian and Dutch beers, with tasting notes and member rankings. More than half his audience was Belgian.

Opening the border (LaunchStudio, 7 business days, €2,250, plus €49/month managed hosting):
✓ Bancontact enabled in Stripe, next to iDEAL
✓ The first Bancontact or iDEAL payment sets up a SEPA Direct Debit mandate for renewals
✓ Checkout texts in Flemish Dutch and French
✓ VAT information shown clearly for business customers
✓ Failed renewals handled with a grace period and a retry link
