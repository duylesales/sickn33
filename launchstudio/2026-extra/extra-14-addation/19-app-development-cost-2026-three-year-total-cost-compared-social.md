🧮 A wedding planner's three-year arithmetic

THE AGENCY OPTION
€52,000 to build
+ €1,800 a month for hosting, maintenance and changes
× 36 months
= over €115,000, before knowing whether couples would pay

Charlotte Peeters, a wedding planner in Antwerp, did that sum and stopped. She wanted Jawoordplanner: a shared planning app for couples and their suppliers, with timelines, budgets, guest lists and supplier messages. Instead of the Brussels agency, she spent two months building it in Lovable. The interface was charming, and the flows matched how she actually plans weddings.

Underneath, though:
• Every couple's guest list and budget visible to every supplier
• Premium plan payments not connected
• The app living on a preview link

What LaunchStudio added (14 business days, €5,300, plus €49/month managed hosting):
✓ Roles for couples, planners and suppliers, with Row Level Security per wedding
✓ Stripe with Bancontact and iDEAL for Belgian and Dutch couples
✓ Transactional email for supplier messages
✓ Deployment on Charlotte's domain with managed hosting
Charlotte kept making interface changes in Lovable afterwards.

THE OPTION SHE TOOK, YEAR ONE
€5,300 launch
+ hosting
+ two later feature jobs
= about €9,500 in total, under a tenth of the agency's three-year projection

Year one also brought 85 weddings planned in the app.

"The agency's build price scared me. Their monthly retainer scared me more once I multiplied it by thirty-six." - Charlotte Peeters
