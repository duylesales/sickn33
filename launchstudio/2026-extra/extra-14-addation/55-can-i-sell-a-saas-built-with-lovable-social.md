🤝 THE NEGOTIATION: selling a Lovable-built SaaS

THE COMPANY
Feestdraaiboek, built by Nicole Verbruggen in Lovable: a planning tool for corporate event agencies, with timelines, supplier lists and client approvals. Two years, 85 agency customers. A larger event software company made an offer.

THE BUYER'S FINDINGS
• The Supabase project and Stripe account registered to Nicole's personal email
• A service role key in the git history
• RLS on the suppliers table letting any agency read every other agency's supplier contacts
• No processor agreements on file
• Nobody besides Nicole had ever deployed the app

THE BUYER'S PROPOSAL
A 30% price reduction, or a six-month escrow.

NICOLE'S COUNTER
"Give me eight weeks."

THE EIGHT WEEKS (LaunchStudio, 18 business days, €6,900)
✓ Every account consolidated under the company
✓ Keys rotated, history cleaned
✓ RLS fixed across all tables
✓ Processor agreements signed and documented
✓ Deployment from GitHub, with staging
✓ A restore test
✓ An operating document
✓ An independent audit summary added to the data room

THE BUYER'S FOLLOW-UP REVIEW
Every item closed.

THE FINAL TERMS
The original price. A standard two-month transition period.

"The buyer didn't care that AI wrote the code. They cared that I couldn't prove anyone had checked it." - Nicole Verbruggen
