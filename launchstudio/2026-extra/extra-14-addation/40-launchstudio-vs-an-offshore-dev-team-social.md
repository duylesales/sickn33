Tessa Brouwer spent ten years in print production, then built Drukklaar in Cursor: a SaaS for small print shops that checks uploaded files for print readiness, generates quotes and tracks orders through production. Encouraged by an advisor, she hired a three-person offshore team through a marketplace before launching.

The team built lots of features. Drukklaar still wasn't live. Priorities shifted every week, because Tessa had no customer feedback to steer by.

So she paused the team and asked LaunchStudio to launch what already existed. The new numbers:
