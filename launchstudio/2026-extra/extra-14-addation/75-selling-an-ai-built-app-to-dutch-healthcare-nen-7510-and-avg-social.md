Before the fix, nobody could produce a single line of this. 🏠

Wendy Koster, a former district nurse, built Zorgdagboek in Lovable: home care workers record visit notes, medication observations and family messages on their phones. A home care organisation in Overijssel with 300 staff wanted a pilot. Its information security officer sent 40 questions referencing NEN 7510 and NEN 7513.

Wendy could answer few of them:
• Every care worker could read every client's notes
• No record of who viewed what
• The database ran outside the EU
• No second factor at login
• Notes sent to an AI API for summaries, with no agreement

What LaunchStudio built (17 business days, €6,800):
✓ Access limited to clients assigned to each worker's team
✓ An access log table recording every view and change of client records, with user, time and action
✓ A review screen for the organisation's privacy officer
✓ Migration to an EU region
✓ MFA for all staff
✓ Client identifiers removed before AI summarisation, under a processing agreement with training disabled
✓ A security measures document and an incident procedure

The security officer approved a six-month pilot with 60 care workers, with certification plans to be revisited after the pilot.

"The care workers loved the app. The security officer needed to know who had read which note, and I couldn't tell her." - Wendy Koster
