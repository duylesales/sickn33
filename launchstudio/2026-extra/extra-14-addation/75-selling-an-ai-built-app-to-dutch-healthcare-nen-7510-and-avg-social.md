🚨 Wendy Koster, a former district nurse, built Zorgdagboek in Lovable: home care workers record visit notes, medication observations and family messages on their phones. A home care organisation with 300 staff in Overijssel wanted a pilot. Then their information security officer sent 40 questions referencing NEN 7510 and NEN 7513. 😳

The care workers loved the app. The security officer needed to know who had read which note - and Wendy couldn't tell her. Here's what the questionnaire exposed: 🧠

❌ Every care worker could read every client's notes
❌ No record of who viewed or changed what
❌ The database running outside the EU, and login with no second factor
❌ Notes sent to an AI API for summaries without any agreement

✅ Access restricted to clients assigned to each worker's team
✅ An access log recording every view and change of client records, plus a review screen for the privacy officer
✅ Migration to an EU region and MFA for all staff
✅ Client identifiers removed before AI summarisation under a processing agreement with training disabled, plus security documentation and an incident procedure
