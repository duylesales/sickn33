The launch invoice is the number everyone compares, and it is the least useful one. An app that costs €40,000 to build and €2,000 a month to keep alive costs more over three years than its build price suggests, and the gap between routes changes once you count maintenance, hosting, changes and your own time. This article compares app development cost in 2026 over a three-year horizon.

## The Costs After Launch

**Hosting and services.** Database, hosting, email, monitoring, domain. Early-stage apps typically spend €25-€150 per month; agency-managed hosting is often priced higher as a service contract.

**Maintenance.** Dependencies need updates, providers change APIs, browsers evolve. A common rule of thumb is 15-20% of the original build cost per year for professionally built software. For AI-built apps, maintenance depends heavily on how clean the production layer is.

**Changes and features.** Every product changes after launch. With an agency or freelancer, each change is billable. With AI tools, you make many changes yourself, and pay only for the ones that touch security, payments or data.

**Your own time.** The cost nobody invoices. Managing an agency, briefing a freelancer or building with AI tools all take time. The difference is what kind of time.

## What the Numbers Do Not Show

- **Speed.** Weeks instead of months matters when you are testing whether anyone will pay.
- **Control.** In the AI route you keep building yourself. Some founders love that; others prefer to delegate entirely.
- **Complexity ceiling.** Heavy integrations, real-time systems or complex algorithms may still need a full development team. At that point, Manifera's [offshore development teams](https://www.manifera.com/services/offshore-software-development/) or an agency become the better fit.
- **Risk.** Cheap builds that skip security cost more later. The point of a launch partner is to avoid that trade-off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to develop an app in 2026?

Anywhere from under €1,000 to well over €150,000, depending on the app type and route. A useful rule: AI tools have made building the interface cheap, but production work such as security, payments and hosting still needs expertise. Budget for both, and for running costs afterwards.

### What are the yearly maintenance costs of an app?

For professionally built apps, a common estimate is 15-20% of the build cost per year, plus hosting. AI-built apps with a solid production layer can be cheaper to maintain because founders handle many changes themselves, paying specialists only for sensitive areas.

### When is hiring an in-house developer cheaper than outsourcing?

When you have steady, ongoing development work and the revenue to support a salary of roughly €60,000-€80,000 per year plus costs in the Netherlands. Before that, outsourced or partner models usually cost less over the same period.
