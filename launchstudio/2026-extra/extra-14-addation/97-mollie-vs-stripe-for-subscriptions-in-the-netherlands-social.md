📖 One SaaS, two pricing models, two chapters

CHAPTER 1: A fixed price per store
Annemiek Smit built Winkelrooster in Lovable: shift planning for small retail chains. She started on Mollie, with a fixed monthly price per store. Simple subscriptions, and Mollie handled them well.

CHAPTER 2: A price per employee
Then she moved to per-employee pricing, with a free trial and mid-month upgrades as stores added staff. That's where the plot thickened:
• Her Mollie integration couldn't easily handle proration or seat changes, so she made manual adjustments every month
• Customers asked for a portal to update payment details and download invoices, which didn't exist

THE TURNING POINT
LaunchStudio compared both options with Annemiek. With seat-based pricing, trials and proration, the question had changed. She needed a billing system, not just a payment provider. Stripe Billing fitted the new chapter.

THE REWRITE (13 business days, €4,700, plus €49/month managed hosting)
✓ Subscriptions migrated to Stripe, with iDEAL-initiated SEPA mandates
✓ Per-seat prices, trials and proration
✓ The Customer Portal
✓ Stripe Tax
✓ Invoices synced to her bookkeeping
✓ Existing Mollie customers invited to re-confirm with a short iDEAL payment

THE ENDING
Monthly manual billing work: gone. Existing customers who completed the migration within three weeks: 91%.

"Mollie was perfect for my first pricing model. My second pricing model needed a billing system, not just a payment provider." - Annemiek Smit
