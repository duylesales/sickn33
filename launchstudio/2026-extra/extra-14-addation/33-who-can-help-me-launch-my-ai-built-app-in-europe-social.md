🇩🇪 🇳🇱 🇧🇪 One app, three countries, one launch day

Julia Nowak, a Polish localisation manager living in Berlin, built Lingoflow with Cursor: a translation workflow tool for small agencies, covering project intake, translator assignment, review rounds and client approval. Her first three agency customers were in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium, so she wanted to launch in all three at once.

The quotes she got: a Berlin freelancer at ten weeks and €16,000, mostly to "restructure" her code, and an agency starting at €45,000. Neither mentioned VAT, iDEAL or data location. Julia's own checklist showed the gaps: US hosting, cards only, German VAT charged to everyone, and no data deletion or processor documentation for her agency customers.

What each country needed, and what LaunchStudio built under her existing interface (16 business days, €6,900, plus €49/month managed hosting):

🇩🇪 GERMANY
Cards and SEPA Direct Debit, German email notifications, and reverse charge for business customers through Stripe Tax

🇳🇱 THE NETHERLANDS
iDEAL, Dutch email notifications, and the right VAT treatment through Stripe Tax and OSS

🇧🇪 BELGIUM
Bancontact, and the same VAT logic

🇪🇺 EVERYWHERE
• Migration to an EU Supabase region
• Tenant isolation with Row Level Security per agency
• Account and organisation deletion
• A sub-processor list for customers
• English notifications too

Lingoflow launched in all three countries sixteen business days after the first call. Within four months: 19 paying agencies, about a quarter of them paying with iDEAL or Bancontact.

"Everyone offered to rebuild my app. Nobody asked how my Dutch customers wanted to pay." - Julia Nowak
