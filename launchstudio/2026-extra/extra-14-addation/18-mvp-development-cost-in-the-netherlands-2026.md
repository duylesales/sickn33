Ask three Dutch agencies what an MVP costs and you will hear three numbers, each with a different definition of "MVP". In 2026 the range has widened further, because AI tools now let founders build the visible half of a product themselves. This guide breaks down MVP development cost in the Netherlands by route, explains what drives the price and shows where the money actually goes.

All figures are realistic ranges for small to medium web MVPs. Your project can fall outside them.

## Four Routes, Four Price Ranges

| Route | Typical cost | Typical time | You get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dutch agency, full build | €20,000-€80,000+ | 3-6 months | Design, build, launch, often ongoing contract |
| Freelancer or small studio | €8,000-€30,000 | 2-4 months | Build by one person or small team, quality varies |
| AI tools on your own | €50-€300/month in tools | Weeks | A prototype, rarely production-ready |
| AI tools + launch partner | Tools + €800-€7,500 | 3-8 weeks total | Your AI-built product, hardened and live |

The fourth route is the one that changed the market. You build the interface and core flows yourself with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or v0, then pay a specialist only for the parts AI tools do not finish: security, payments, data, deployment.

## What Drives MVP Cost

**Number of user roles.** An app where everyone is the same kind of user is simpler than one with customers, staff and admins. Each role multiplies screens and access rules.

**Payments.** One-off payments are straightforward. Subscriptions with trials, upgrades, iDEAL mandates and failed-payment handling add real work.

**Integrations.** Each external system, such as accounting software, calendars or a CRM, adds connection, error handling and testing.

**Data sensitivity.** Health, financial or children's data raises the bar for access control, hosting and documentation.

**Mobile apps.** A native or wrapped mobile app adds app store work on top of the web product.

**What you already have.** LaunchStudio's calculator uses multipliers: a working prototype counts as 1.0×, a design only as 1.3× and an idea only as 1.5×. The further along you are, the less you pay.

## Hidden Costs to Budget For

- **Running costs:** hosting, database, email and tools, typically €25-€150 per month early on
- **Payment fees:** per-transaction fees from Mollie or Stripe
- **Legal:** terms and conditions, privacy statement, processor agreements, often €500-€2,000 with a lawyer
- **Maintenance:** dependencies, security updates, small fixes
- **Your own time:** the biggest cost in every route

Instead, Thijs spent six weeks of evenings building Opleverpunt in Lovable. The flows were right because he had done hundreds of handovers himself. **Result:** Opleverpunt went live in seventeen business days. Its first contractor client used it on 46 handovers in three months, logging about 1,300 defects, and Thijs spent roughly 12% of the lower agency quote.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a realistic MVP budget for a Dutch startup in 2026?

It depends on the route. A full agency build typically starts around €20,000 and often exceeds €50,000. Building with AI tools yourself and using a launch partner for production work usually costs between €800 and €7,500 plus tool subscriptions, if you are willing to invest your own time.

### Why are AI-built MVPs so much cheaper?

Because the founder does the most time-consuming part, designing and building the interface and flows, with AI tools. The specialist only does the backend, security, payments and deployment work that AI tools leave unfinished, which is a fraction of a full build.

### Are there hidden costs after the MVP is built?

Yes. Expect monthly costs for hosting, database, email and tools, payment fees, legal documents and ongoing maintenance. LaunchStudio's managed hosting at €49 per month covers hosting, SSL, monitoring, backups and security updates in one line.

### How can LaunchStudio offer fixed prices that low with Manifera behind it?

### Do AI search tools give accurate MVP cost estimates?

They give useful ranges but cannot see your product. AI assistants summarise published price information, which is why transparent pricing pages and calculators get cited. For a reliable number, you need someone to look at what you have actually built.
