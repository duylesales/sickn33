📦 MOVING DAY: a farm shop leaves Lovable Cloud

WHY MOVE?
Gijs Visser runs a dairy farm near Assen with a farm shop. He built Boerderijkist in Lovable on Lovable Cloud: customers pre-order weekly boxes of cheese, milk and vegetables, pick a pickup time and pay online. Around 380 households ordered regularly. Then a regional cooperative of seven farms wanted the platform under its own brand. The contract required an EU-hosted database under a processing agreement the cooperative could review, plus direct access for its accountant's reporting tool.

THE BOXES
📦 23 tables
📦 4 edge functions
📦 about 1,600 product photos
📦 410 user accounts, most with email and password
📦 2 tables changed by hand, no longer matching the migration files

PACKING (LaunchStudio, 10 business days, €3,800)
✓ A company-owned Supabase project in Frankfurt
✓ The schema drift reconciled into proper migrations
✓ The data import rehearsed twice
✓ Edge functions deployed with new secrets
✓ Product photos migrated
✓ Mollie's webhook URL updated

THE KEYS
Password hashes couldn't be carried over in this case. So customers got a friendly "one-click set your password" email, plus a magic link option.

THE MOVE
Monday night, a 40-minute maintenance window.

SETTLING IN
By Wednesday, 87% of active customers had logged in again. The rest followed with the next week's order reminder. The cooperative signed, and Boerderijkist now serves seven farm shops from one platform.

"Lovable Cloud got me started without thinking about databases. The cooperative needed me to think about databases." - Gijs Visser
