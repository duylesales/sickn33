Niels Postma built Kappersagenda in v0: online booking for independent hair salons, sold to salon owners on a monthly plan. Salons liked the demo. But conversion from the website was low.

What was missing:
• A company name beyond the product name
• An address
• A KvK number
• A VAT number
• A privacy statement about the tools Niels actually used, instead of a generic one
• Consent before analytics loaded
• Whether prices included VAT or not

What LaunchStudio added (3 business days, €950):
✓ A footer and contact page with company details, KvK and VAT numbers
✓ A privacy statement restructured around the actual processors, for Niels's lawyer to finalise
✓ Cookie consent that blocks analytics until accepted
✓ "excl. BTW" on pricing for business customers
✓ Organization structured data

The new footer: a real company, with a real address and registration numbers.

Over the following months, website-to-demo conversion rose noticeably. And prospects stopped asking whether the company was real.
