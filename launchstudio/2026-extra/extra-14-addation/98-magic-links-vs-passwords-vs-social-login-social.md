🚨 Gerrit Hoekman runs a campsite near Ommen and built Kampeerplek in Lovable: guests book pitches, return every year and manage their reservations. Lovable generated email and password login. Every spring, when guests came back to book, a large share had forgotten their passwords - and Gerrit spent weeks answering phone calls about logging in. 😳

His guests log in once a year. Asking them to remember a password was asking for a phone call. Here's what wasn't working: 🧠

❌ Passwords for users who log in only once a year
❌ Slow password reset emails, right when guests wanted to book
❌ One sign-in method for guests and staff alike
❌ A login screen that didn't speak to his many German guests

✅ Email one-time codes as the primary method for guests
✅ Password login kept for staff, with MFA
✅ Google login added as an option, with identity linking so returning guests keep their history
✅ A reliable SMTP provider and a rewritten login screen in Dutch and German
