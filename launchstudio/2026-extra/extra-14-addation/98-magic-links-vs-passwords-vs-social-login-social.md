☎️ Every spring, at a campsite near Ommen (dramatized)

GUEST: Hello, we'd like to book our usual pitch again.
GERRIT: Lovely! You can do it online.
GUEST: I tried. What was my password again?
GERRIT: You can reset it.
GUEST: I did. The email hasn't come.
GERRIT: (makes a note. Phone rings again.)

Gerrit Hoekman runs a campsite near Ommen and built Kampeerplek in Lovable: guests book pitches, return yearly and manage their reservations. Lovable generated email and password login. And every spring, when guests came back, a large share had forgotten their password, reset emails were slow, and Gerrit spent weeks answering login calls.

His guests log in once a year. A password was never going to work.

What LaunchStudio changed (4 business days, €1,150):
✓ Email one-time codes as the main login method for guests
✓ Password login kept for staff, with MFA
✓ Google login as an extra option
✓ Identity linking, so returning guests keep their booking history
✓ A reliable SMTP provider
✓ A rewritten login screen with clear instructions in Dutch and German, for his many German guests

The same call, now:
GUEST: Hello, we'd like to... oh, never mind, I just got a code. We're booked.

During the next spring booking period, login calls dropped to a handful. And online rebookings by returning guests rose noticeably compared with the previous season.
