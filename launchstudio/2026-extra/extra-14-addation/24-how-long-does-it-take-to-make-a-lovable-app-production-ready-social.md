⏳ 19 business days to a start date that couldn't move

Martijn Post, an estate agent in Gouda, built Kijkdag in Lovable: buyers book viewing slots for listed homes, agents manage availability and get reminders, and sellers see how many viewings are planned. A regional agency group with nine offices agreed to a trial, with a start date tied to the spring listing season. When Martijn contacted LaunchStudio, nineteen business days were left.

DAY 1: Agree what NOT to build
Launch scope: secure booking, agent and seller roles, email and SMS reminders, deployment on his domain. Analytics dashboards moved to "after launch."

DAY 2: A blocker
The domain was registered by a web designer Martijn had worked with years earlier. Transferring it took four days, running in parallel with the security work.

DAYS 2 TO 13: The work
• Row Level Security per office and listing
• Atomic slot booking, so double bookings can't happen
• Email and SMS reminders through server-side functions
• Staging and production environments
• Testing with agents from two offices

DAY 14: Deployment
Total so far: €3,900.

DAY 19: The agency group's start date
Kijkdag had been live for five business days already.

THE SPRING SEASON
About 3,400 viewings scheduled across the nine offices. The post-launch dashboard came later, in a second, smaller engagement.

"I had a deadline I couldn't move. What made it work was agreeing on day one what we would not build." - Martijn Post
