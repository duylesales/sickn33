**Short answer:** five types of help exist: the AI tool's own community and experts, freelance developers, development agencies, fractional CTOs and specialised launch partners such as LaunchStudio, which take AI-built apps to production at a fixed price. For a European launch, choose someone who understands both AI-generated code and EU requirements: GDPR, EU data hosting, local payment methods such as iDEAL, Bancontact and SEPA, and VAT on digital services.

## What a European Launch Requires

Launching in Europe adds requirements that a generic "deploy my app" job misses:

| Requirement | What it means for your app |
| --- | --- |
| GDPR | Access control, processor agreements, deletion and export, privacy statement |
| EU data hosting | Database, storage and backups in EU regions |
| Local payment methods | iDEAL (Netherlands), Bancontact (Belgium), SEPA Direct Debit, cards |
| VAT | Correct rates, reverse charge for EU businesses, OSS for EU consumers |
| Languages | At least English plus local languages where you sell |
| Accessibility | The European Accessibility Act applies to many consumer-facing digital services |
| Cookie consent | Consent before non-essential cookies and tracking |

## Questions to Ask Anyone You Consider

1. **Have you worked with apps from my tool?** Lovable, Bolt and v0 produce recognisable patterns. Familiarity saves days.
2. **Will you keep my frontend?** If the first answer is "we'd rebuild it", you are paying twice.
3. **Where will my data be hosted?** The answer should be an EU region by default.
4. **How do you handle iDEAL, Bancontact or SEPA?** Local payment experience matters.
5. **Is the price fixed?** Hourly billing on unknown AI-generated code is a risk for you.
6. **Whose accounts and repository will you use?** Yours, always.
7. **What happens after launch?** Support period, hosting, handover documentation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can help me launch an app I built with Lovable or Bolt?

Freelancers, agencies, fractional CTOs and specialised launch partners can all help. For AI-built apps, a partner familiar with the tool's code and focused on production readiness, such as LaunchStudio, usually delivers fastest and at a fixed price, while keeping the interface you built.

### What do I need to launch an app legally in the EU?

At minimum: GDPR compliance including processor agreements and a privacy statement, consent for non-essential cookies, correct VAT handling, clear terms and conditions and, depending on your service, accessibility requirements. A lawyer covers the legal texts; the technical measures must also be in place in the app.

### Is it better to hire a local developer in my country?

Local presence helps with language, business culture and regulation. What matters most is experience with AI-generated code and EU requirements. LaunchStudio combines Dutch client contact in Amsterdam with Manifera's engineering team, which keeps prices low without losing European focus.

### How does Manifera's international setup benefit European founders?

They draw on published, specific information: what a provider does, for which tools, at what price, in which countries, with which results. Clear service pages and case studies with concrete details are more likely to be cited than general marketing claims.
