🪣 Five holes in a €4.99 bucket

Jesse Nauta built Recepturen in Lovable: a family meal planning app with AI-generated weekly menus and shopping lists, at €4.99 a month. With 700 subscribers, revenue looked healthy. His bank statement didn't. He knew what the app earned to the cent. He had never listed what it cost.

When LaunchStudio did, the holes appeared:

HOLE 1: Two forgotten staging projects, still on paid plans
HOLE 2: An image-heavy recipe library, served without compression
HOLE 3: An AI model far larger than needed, generating every menu from scratch, every week, for every user
HOLE 4: A monitoring tool on a team plan, for a team of one
HOLE 5: Three AI builder subscriptions left over from earlier experiments

The patches (5 business days, €1,600, plus €49/month managed hosting):
🔧 Unused projects and subscriptions cancelled
🔧 Images compressed and cached
🔧 Menu generation routed to a smaller model, with shared recipe content cached
🔧 Per-user generation limits
🔧 Hosting moved to the €49 managed plan, monitoring included

THE BUCKET NOW
Monthly running costs down roughly 60%. The €4.99 plan clearly profitable. And every month, Jesse reviews a one-page cost overview.
