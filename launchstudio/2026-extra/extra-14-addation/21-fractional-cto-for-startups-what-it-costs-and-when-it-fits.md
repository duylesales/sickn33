A fractional CTO for startups sounds like the perfect compromise: senior technical leadership without a senior salary. For some companies it is exactly right. For others, especially founders whose product was built with AI tools and now needs to go live, it is an expensive way to receive advice they cannot yet act on. The difference lies in what problem you are solving.

## What a Fractional CTO Does

A fractional CTO works with your company part-time, typically one to four days a month, and takes responsibility for technical direction. Common activities:

- Setting architecture and technology choices
- Reviewing code and pull requests from developers or agencies
- Hiring and managing the first engineers
- Representing the company in technical due diligence
- Defining security, data and reliability standards
- Translating business plans into technical roadmaps

What a fractional CTO usually does not do is write most of the code. They lead; someone else builds.

## Typical Costs in the Netherlands

| Model | Typical price range | What you usually get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day rate | €800-€1,500 per day | Ad-hoc advice, reviews, workshops |
| Monthly retainer, light | €1,500-€3,000 per month | 1-2 days, regular check-ins |
| Monthly retainer, heavy | €3,000-€8,000 per month | 3-6 days, team leadership |
| Equity component | Varies, often 0.5-2% | Longer commitment, aligned incentives |

Over six months, a light retainer costs €9,000-€18,000. Over a year, a heavier engagement easily reaches €40,000-€90,000.

## When a Fractional CTO Fits

- **You have developers or an agency** and need someone senior to direct and review their work.
- **You are preparing for investment** and need credible technical leadership in the room.
- **You are hiring engineers** and cannot assess candidates yourself.
- **The product is technically complex** and early architecture choices will be hard to reverse.
- **You have recurring technical decisions** every month, not just one launch.

## When It Doesn't Fit Yet

- **Nobody is building.** Advice without execution produces documents, not a product.
- **The main problem is finishing a prototype.** If you built in Lovable or Bolt and need security, payments and deployment, you need execution, not direction.
- **The budget is tight.** A retainer is a recurring cost; a launch engagement is a one-off.
- **The decisions are already made by your tools.** AI builders have chosen React, Supabase and a hosting platform. The architecture question is mostly "how do we make this safe and reliable", which is execution.

## Fractional CTO vs Launch Partner

| | Fractional CTO | Launch partner (LaunchStudio) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main output | Decisions, direction, reviews | Working production app |
| Builds code | Rarely | Yes, the production layer |
| Pricing | Recurring retainer | Fixed price per scope, €800-€7,500 |
| Duration | Months to years | 1-3 weeks per engagement |
| Best moment | Team exists, decisions recur | Prototype exists, needs to go live |

The two are not mutually exclusive. A common sequence: launch with a partner, gain customers and revenue, then bring in a fractional CTO when you hire your first developers.

## Questions to Ask a Fractional CTO Candidate

1. How many hours per month will you actually spend on our company?
2. Will you review code, or only architecture?
3. Have you worked with AI-generated codebases?
4. What will you hand over if the engagement ends?
5. Who builds what you design?

## Real example

### An AI-Native Founder in Action: The EdTech Founder Who Hired Advice Before Execution

Esther Meijer, a language teacher in Enschede, built Praatmaatje with Lovable: a conversation practice app where Dutch learners book short video sessions with native speakers and receive AI-generated feedback on their vocabulary. Unsure about the technical side, she hired a fractional CTO at €2,500 per month.

After three months and €7,500, Esther had an architecture document, a recommended stack and a list of security concerns. The app itself had not changed: nobody was implementing the recommendations, and the CTO's two days a month were not meant for building. Meanwhile, learners on the waiting list were drifting away.

Esther paused the retainer and brought the architecture document to LaunchStudio. Engineers implemented the critical items: Row Level Security for learner and tutor data, secure video room tokens generated server-side, Stripe for session credits, AI feedback calls moved to edge functions with transcripts minimised before sending, and deployment with monitoring. The architecture document turned out to be useful as a specification.

**Result:** Praatmaatje launched within thirteen business days. In its first quarter, about 260 learners booked roughly 1,900 sessions. Six months later, with two part-time developers hired, Esther brought a fractional CTO back in, this time with a team to direct.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a fractional CTO cost in the Netherlands?

Typically €800-€1,500 per day, or €1,500-€8,000 per month on a retainer depending on days included. Some fractional CTOs also take a small equity stake. Over a year, total costs commonly range from €18,000 to €90,000.

### What is the difference between a fractional CTO and a technical co-founder?

A fractional CTO is a paid, part-time leader who may work with several companies. A technical co-founder is a full-time owner who shares the company's risk and rewards. Fractional CTOs bring experience quickly; co-founders bring long-term commitment.

### Do I need a fractional CTO to launch an AI-built app?

Usually not. Launching an AI-built app mainly requires execution: security, payments, data and deployment. A fractional CTO becomes valuable once you have a team to lead and recurring technical decisions to make.

### How does Herre Roelevink's experience relate to fractional leadership?
