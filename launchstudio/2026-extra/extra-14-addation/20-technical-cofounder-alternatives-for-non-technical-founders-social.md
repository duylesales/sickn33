📢 WANTED: Technical co-founder

For: Laadslot, a dock scheduling tool where carriers book loading slots at distribution centres, so trucks stop queueing at the gates
Location: Venlo
Search time so far: eleven months
Candidates who came close: two
Candidates who took jobs elsewhere: two

That was Ruben Hendriks, a warehouse operations manager, for most of a year. Startup events, platforms, coffees. No co-founder.

So he stopped waiting and built Laadslot himself in Bolt, in seven weeks. The booking flow, carrier portal and gate view worked in the preview. What was missing was everything a technical co-founder would have handled:
• Carriers could see each other's bookings
• Two carriers clicking at the same moment created double bookings
• No email confirmations
• Nothing running outside the preview

What LaunchStudio filled in (12 business days, €4,300):
✓ Company-level access control with Row Level Security
✓ A database constraint and transaction, so slot booking is atomic and double bookings are impossible
✓ Email confirmations and reminders
✓ An audit log for gate staff
✓ Deployment with monitoring and EU hosting

Laadslot went live at one Venlo distribution centre within twelve business days. After three months, average truck waiting time at that site had dropped by roughly a third.
