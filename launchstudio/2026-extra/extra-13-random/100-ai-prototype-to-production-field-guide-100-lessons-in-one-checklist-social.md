Sanne Groot, a community organiser in Amsterdam Nieuw-West, built Buurtkookt in Lovable: neighbours who cook too much offer portions to others nearby for a small contribution, with pickup times, allergen information and ratings. Through local WhatsApp groups, it grew to about 2,300 members. Then a local newspaper planned an article. Sanne printed an earlier version of the checklist, ticked what she could, and brought it to the intro call.

A hundred articles sounded overwhelming. One checklist with her own ticks on it didn't.

Ticking the empty boxes (LaunchStudio, 10 business days, €2,800):
✓ Addresses revealed only after a confirmed pickup
✓ Admin roles enforced on the server
✓ Key rotation, and Stripe webhooks
✓ EU backups, with a tested restore
✓ Allergen data kept to portion listings
✓ Staging and monitoring
✓ Authenticated email
✓ A data export and deletion flow
Sanne wrote the privacy notice and house rules herself, and named a volunteer as second responder for alerts.

The newspaper article brought 1,800 new members in a week, with no outage and no privacy complaints. Buurtkookt now has around 5,000 members, and Sanne revisits the checklist every quarter.

"A hundred articles sounded overwhelming. One checklist with my own ticks on it didn't." - Sanne Groot
