Six Belgian and two Dutch contractors piloted it. To make it production ready, he hired a small offshore team at €24 an hour.

The team did every ticket he gave them. But he was the one writing the tickets, and he didn't know what was missing. After four months, the pilot contractors still didn't trust Werfmap.
