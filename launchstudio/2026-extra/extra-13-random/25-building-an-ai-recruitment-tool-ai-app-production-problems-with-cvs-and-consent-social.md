🚨 Sophie Lambrecht ran a staffing agency for technical trades in Leeuwarden before building Vakkracht in Lovable: installation, welding and construction firms post vacancies, and AI matches CVs to certifications and driving licences. Twelve employers, two agencies, 1,400 candidates in one quarter. Then a candidate asked why a company he'd never applied to had called him. 😳

Every recruiter could search every candidate through the API. CVs sat in a public bucket with predictable file names and were never deleted. The talent-pool checkbox was pre-ticked and stored nowhere. Candidates scoring under 40% were auto-rejected with no human review, and full CV text landed in the error tracker whenever parsing failed. Here's what AI recruitment tools must fix before launch: 🧠

❌ All candidates visible to all recruiters
❌ Public CV files, kept forever
❌ Pre-ticked, unrecorded consent
❌ Automatic rejection by AI score — and CVs in the logs

✅ Organisation-scoped access with row level security
✅ Private CV storage with signed links, malware scanning and deletion four weeks after a vacancy closes (twelve months with recorded consent)
✅ Explicit, versioned consent with one-click withdrawal
✅ A recruiter review queue with match explanations, CV text scrubbed from logs, and per-candidate export and deletion — embeddings included
