Ricardo Santos, a letting agent in Diemen, built Kijkmoment in Lovable: landlords publish rental listings, applicants book viewing slots, and agents manage applications through to contract. Three small letting agencies and about 40 private landlords used it, and every popular listing drew hundreds of applicants.

To "save time," the booking form required a passport copy, three payslips and an employer statement before anyone could even book a viewing. In eight months, that added up to identity documents and financial records from about 9,000 people. And agents at one agency could view applicants for another agency's properties through the API.

The new numbers (LaunchStudio, 12 business days, €3,600):
1 step to book a viewing: contact details only
Documents requested only from shortlisted applicants
BSN-redacted documents supported, with verification recorded instead of documents stored long-term
Files in private, EU-hosted storage with signed links
Agency and landlord separation enforced in the database
Nationality and similar fields removed from scoring, and auto-hiding replaced by a transparent shortlist agents review
Retention rules in place
The backlog of unnecessary documents deleted, after Ricardo confirmed the approach with a lawyer
Access logging and alerts added

Two larger letting agencies joined after reading Kijkmoment's data-handling description. And anxious support questions from applicants about their documents largely stopped.
