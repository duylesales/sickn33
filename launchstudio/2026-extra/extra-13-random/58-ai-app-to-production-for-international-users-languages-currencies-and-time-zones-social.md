🚨 Mark Hermans, a former university careers adviser in Heerlen, built Stagebank in Bolt: international students at Dutch universities find internships in Limburg and across the border, with applications, interview scheduling and a placement fee paid by companies. Students from 46 countries signed up in year one. Then three interviews were missed around the October clock change. 😳

Signup demanded a first and last name in Latin characters, rejecting students with one name or other scripts. Addresses required a Dutch postcode. Interview times had no time zone. The interface was English but every email was Dutch. Fees had no currency, so Swiss and British companies got wrong invoices - and searching "Müller" didn't find "Mueller". Here's what taking an AI app to production for international users requires: 🧠

❌ Name fields that reject real people
❌ Times stored without a time zone
❌ One language in the app, another in the inbox
❌ Money without a currency

✅ All text and emails externalised into English, Dutch and German
✅ An inclusive full-name model, country-aware addresses and E.164 phone numbers
✅ Interview times stored with explicit time zones and shown in each participant's zone, labelled
✅ Currency codes and minor-unit storage for fees, locale formatting and accent-insensitive search - with existing records carefully migrated
