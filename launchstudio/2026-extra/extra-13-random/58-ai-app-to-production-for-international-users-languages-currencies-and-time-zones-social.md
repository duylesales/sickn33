📝 THE SIGN-UP FORM, before (illustrative, for students from 46 countries)

First name* (Latin characters only)

Postcode* (format: 1234 AB)
Interview time: 14:00 (in which time zone? It didn't say)
Emails: in Dutch (the interface was in English)
Fee: 500 (in which currency? It didn't say)
Search "Müller": no results for "Mueller" or "Muller"

Mark Hermans, a former university careers adviser in Heerlen, built Stagebank in Bolt: it connects international students at Dutch universities with internships at companies in Limburg and across the border, with applications, interview scheduling and a small placement fee paid by companies. In its first academic year, students from 46 countries registered. And the app assumed they were all from Heerlen.

What went wrong:
• Students with a single name, or names in other scripts, couldn't sign up
• Interview times were stored without time zones, so students still abroad and German companies saw different times. Three interviews were missed around the October clock change.
• Swiss and British companies paying in their own currency got invoices with wrong amounts

📝 THE SIGN-UP FORM, after (LaunchStudio, 10 business days, €2,900)
✓ Full name, in any script, as one inclusive field
✓ Country-aware addresses, and E.164 phone numbers
✓ Interview times stored with explicit time zones, shown in each participant's own zone, with labels
✓ All text and emails in English, Dutch and German
✓ Fees with currency codes, stored in minor units, with locale formatting
✓ Accent-insensitive search
✓ Existing records migrated carefully

Registration completion for international students rose from about 71% to 94%. No interviews were missed at the next clock change. And Stagebank expanded to three more universities the following year.

"My users were international by definition, and my app assumed they were all from Heerlen." - Mark Hermans
