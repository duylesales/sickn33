🚨 Emre Yilmaz, a career coach in Nieuwegein, built CVcoach in Lovable with OpenAI: job seekers upload a CV and a vacancy, and an AI assistant suggests improvements. About 3,000 users. Next on his roadmap: a tool for recruitment agencies to upload fifty CVs and get them ranked. One extra button - and, as it turned out, a completely different product under the EU AI Act. 😳

Ranking candidates for employers would likely be high-risk recruitment AI, with obligations a small company wasn't ready for. The existing app had gaps too: the chat didn't say it was an AI, the privacy notice didn't mention OpenAI, full CVs with birth dates and photos went to the model, vacancy texts could carry prompt injections, and chat logs were kept forever. Here's what founders must disclose - and secure - under the AI Act: 🧠

❌ A chatbot that never says it's an AI
❌ An AI provider missing from the privacy notice
❌ More personal data sent to the model than needed
❌ A planned feature drifting into high-risk territory

✅ A clear AI disclosure in the chat
✅ Photos, birth dates and contact details stripped before CVs reach the model
✅ Vacancy and CV content delimited as untrusted data, outputs sanitised, 30-day retention on chat logs
✅ An accurate processor list - and, with legal advice, the ranking feature postponed for an inclusive-vacancy tool instead
