🗂️ 19 worries, €1,500. Sort them.

Hanna de Boer, a mother of three and former event planner in Harderwijk, built Stoepkrijt in Lovable: local organisers list children's activities, like craft workshops, sports camps and nature walks, and parents book and pay. She had €1,500 for launch, and a list of 19 concerns from reading security articles. She was ready to delay launch until autumn to afford all of them.

LaunchStudio's review sorted the list.

NON-NEGOTIABLE: fix before launch (7)
🔴 Parents could see other parents' bookings, with children's names and ages, through the API (not even on her list)
🔴 Payments confirmed by the browser redirect
🔴 The Mollie key in the frontend
🔴 Backups never restored
🔴 The database in a US region
🔴 Scattered account ownership
🔴 No uptime or error alerts

CAN WAIT: with a trigger and a target month (12)
🕓 Performance (the app had 200 users)
🕓 A full test suite
🕓 Admin tooling
🕓 Branded emails
🕓 Organiser analytics
🕓 ...and seven more

The non-negotiables, fixed for €1,400 in 6 business days:
✓ Server-side access control on bookings and children's data
✓ Verified Mollie webhooks
✓ The key rotated
✓ An EU migration, with a tested restore
✓ Accounts consolidated
✓ Uptime and error alerts
And the other twelve? A written roadmap.

Stoepkrijt launched for the summer holidays instead of autumn: 830 bookings across 45 organisers. By month three, one postponed item became urgent, exactly as planned: the activity search slowed as listings grew. Hanna fixed it with revenue from the summer.

"I had nineteen worries and fifteen hundred euros. What I needed was someone to tell me which seven would actually hurt someone." - Hanna de Boer
