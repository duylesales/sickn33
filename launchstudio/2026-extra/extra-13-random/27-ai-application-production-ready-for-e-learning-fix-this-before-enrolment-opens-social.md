🚗 THEORY EXAM, for an e-learning platform. Five questions.

QUESTION 1: Where are your premium video lessons stored?
Wrong answer: in a public storage bucket, with a lesson API that returns video URLs whatever the licence.
(A school owner found links to every premium lesson, freely shared, in a student group chat.)

QUESTION 2: Where is a student's practice exam progress saved?
Wrong answer: in local storage, lost whenever the student changes phones.

QUESTION 3: Who can see which students?
Wrong answer: instructors could see students from other driving schools through the API.

QUESTION 4: What about students under 16?
Wrong answer: parental consent wasn't considered.

QUESTION 5: Do your confirmation emails arrive?
Wrong answer: sent from the default Supabase sender, often in spam during exam season.

Bonus: completion certificates were generated in the browser.
