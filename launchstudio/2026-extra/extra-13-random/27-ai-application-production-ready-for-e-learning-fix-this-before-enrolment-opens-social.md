🚨 Marloes Hoekstra, a driving instructor in Heerenveen, built TheorieTrack in Bolt: theory-exam prep that driving schools license for their students, with video lessons, practice exams and instructor reports. Forty schools in Friesland and Groningen, about 3,500 students — many aged 16 and 17. Then a school owner forwarded a screenshot from a student group chat: links to every premium video, shared freely. 😳

The videos sat in a public bucket, and the lesson API returned URLs regardless of licence. Progress lived in local storage and vanished when students changed phones. Instructors could see other schools' students through the API, certificates were generated in the browser, parental consent for under-16s wasn't considered, and confirmation emails landed in spam during exam season. Here's what e-learning platforms must fix before enrolment opens: 🧠

❌ Paid content protected by a button, not by access control
❌ Progress stored in the browser
❌ School separation only in the interface
❌ Fakeable certificates and no plan for minors

✅ A video platform with signed playback tokens tied to active licences, and a secured lesson API
✅ Progress saved on the server with confirmed saves
✅ School-level separation with row level security
✅ Server-side certificates with a public check page, age-aware sign-up with parental consent, an authenticated email provider, pooling, backups and monitoring
