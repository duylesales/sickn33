📄 CLAIM FILE: the tent that never arrived

THE INCIDENT
A Saturday wedding in the Achterhoek. The couple's party tent never arrived. Their booking had been overwritten by a second booking for the same tent, because availability wasn't locked. Neither the couple nor the rental company was notified.

THE CLAIM
The couple demanded compensation for an emergency replacement tent.

THE PLATFORM
Verhuurkalender, built in Lovable by Marco Heijnen, who rents out party tents, tables and lighting in Winterswijk. Customers book equipment for dates, pay a deposit and choose delivery slots. Three other rental companies in the Achterhoek had joined.

WHAT MARCO FOUND WHEN HE CHECKED HIS POSITION
✗ Terms of service, generated by an AI tool, promising "guaranteed availability," with no limitation of liability
✗ No written agreement with the freelancer who had adjusted the booking logic
✗ No insurance covering the platform
✗ No logs, so no way to prove what had happened

THE SETTLEMENT
Paid by Marco, out of his own pocket.

THE ENGINEERING SIDE (LaunchStudio, 7 business days, €1,800)
✓ Availability enforced in the database, per item and per date
✓ Confirmation and change notifications to both the customer and the rental company
✓ An audit log of every booking change
✓ Verified deposit payments
✓ Monitoring and documentation

THE LEGAL SIDE (Marco, with a lawyer)
✓ New terms that describe the service accurately, and limit liability appropriately for business partners
✓ Signed agreements with the partner rental companies
✓ Professional indemnity and cyber insurance, for which the insurer asked for exactly the documentation LaunchStudio had produced

CASE STATUS
No double bookings since. The audit log has twice settled customer disputes in minutes. And Verhuurkalender added two more rental companies, under clear partner agreements.
