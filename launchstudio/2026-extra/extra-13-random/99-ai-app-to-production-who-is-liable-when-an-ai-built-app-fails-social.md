Marco's AI-generated terms promised "guaranteed availability" with no limitation of liability, there was no agreement with the freelancer who'd adjusted the booking logic, no insurance and no logs to prove what happened. He paid the settlement himself. Here's who is liable when an AI-built app fails — and how to reduce the risk: 🧠

❌ No availability locking for physical items
❌ No notifications when bookings change
❌ AI-written terms promising what the app couldn't deliver
❌ No logs, agreements or insurance

✅ Database-enforced availability per item and date
✅ Confirmation and change notifications to both customer and rental company
✅ An audit log of every booking change, verified deposit payments and monitoring
