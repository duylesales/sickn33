🚨 Gerrit Zijlstra, a retired teacher and board member in Sneek, built Vrijwilligersplein in Lovable: a shared platform where small Frisian foundations — a food bank, a visiting service for isolated elderly people, a sports foundation for children with disabilities — recruit volunteers, schedule shifts and collect donations. Eleven foundations, about 700 volunteers. Then a new treasurer asked how recurring donations worked. 😳

They didn't. "Monthly donors" got a monthly email with a payment link, and half stopped within three months. Worse: food bank volunteers could see the visiting service's client list — names and addresses of isolated elderly people — through the API. VOG and passport scans sat in a public bucket, several foundations shared one admin login used by past and present board members, and confirmations lacked ANBI details. Here's what nonprofit AI apps must get right: 🧠

❌ "Recurring" donations that were really monthly reminders
❌ Vulnerable clients' addresses visible across foundations
❌ Screening documents in public storage
❌ Shared admin logins that outlived board terms

✅ SEPA recurring donations via Mollie mandates, with failed-payment handling and self-service cancellation
✅ Data separated per foundation, client data limited to coordinators with 2FA and logged access
✅ VOG scans replaced by a recorded check date — existing scans deleted
✅ Personal accounts for every board member, corrected confirmations and annual donor summaries
