👀 WHO COULD SEE WHAT? A volunteer platform for Frisian foundations, before and after

BEFORE
Food bank volunteers ➜ the visiting service's client list: names and addresses of isolated elderly people
Anyone with a link ➜ scans of VOG certificates and passports, in a public bucket
Board members past and present ➜ one shared admin login for several foundations
"Monthly donors" ➜ a monthly email with a payment link, and about half stopped paying within three months
Donors ➜ confirmations showing amounts, but not the foundation's ANBI details

AFTER (LaunchStudio, 9 business days, €2,400)
Food bank volunteers ➜ only their own foundation's data
The visiting service's clients ➜ visible only to its three coordinators, with two-factor authentication and logged access
VOG checks ➜ a recorded check date instead of stored scans, and existing scans deleted
Every board member ➜ their own personal account, and the shared login removed
Monthly donors ➜ SEPA recurring donations through Mollie mandates, with failed-payment handling and self-service cancellation
Donors ➜ corrected confirmations, plus an annual donor summary

Gerrit Zijlstra, a retired teacher and board member of several local foundations in Sneek, built Vrijwilligersplein in Lovable: one shared platform where small Frisian foundations, such as a food bank, a visiting service for isolated elderly people and a sports foundation for children with disabilities, recruit volunteers, schedule shifts and collect donations. Eleven foundations, about 700 volunteers. It started with a new treasurer asking a simple question: how are recurring donations handled? They weren't.

Six months later, recurring donor retention across the foundations had risen from roughly 50% to over 90%. And two more foundations joined after seeing how access was set up.

"We were asking people to trust us with money and with their neighbours' addresses. The software had to deserve that as much as the volunteers do." - Gerrit Zijlstra
