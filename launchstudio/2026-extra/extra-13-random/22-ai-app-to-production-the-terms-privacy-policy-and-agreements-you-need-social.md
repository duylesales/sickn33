📸 SPOT THE DIFFERENCE: what the privacy notice said vs. what the app did

THE NOTICE SAID: we use Google Analytics.
THE APP DID: not use Google Analytics.

THE NOTICE SAID: (nothing about Supabase, the print partner, the email provider or the AI layout service)
THE APP DID: send customers' photos and addresses to all of them, and every uploaded image to the AI service.

THE NOTICE SAID: your data is stored in the EU.
THE APP DID: store the database and files in a US region.

THE NOTICE SAID: we delete your data on request.
THE APP DID: have no deletion function, and kept uploaded photos, many showing children, forever.

THE NOTICE SAID: (nothing about tracking before consent)
THE APP DID: load a marketing pixel before consent.

THE TERMS SAID: no withdrawal rights on any order.
THE LAW SAYS: that exception only applies to personalised products, like the printed book itself.
