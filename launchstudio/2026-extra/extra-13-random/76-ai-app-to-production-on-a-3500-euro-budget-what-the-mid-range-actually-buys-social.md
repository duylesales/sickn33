🧾 RECEIPT: €3,500, for one music school

Ilse Dekkers, a piano teacher who runs a small music school in Emmen with six teachers, built Muziekmoment in Lovable: students book lessons with teachers, buy lesson cards, get reminders and reschedule within the school's rules. Around 180 students used it informally, paying by bank transfer, which meant hours of reconciliation every month for Ilse. She had €3,500 set aside.

What the review found:
• Students could see each other's lesson notes by changing an ID

• Lesson cards had no payment integration at all
• A US-region database, with untested backups
• Reminder emails landing in spam
• The app on a Lovable preview link
