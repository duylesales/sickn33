Imagine starting with a new personal trainer in January. 🏋️

On the intake form, you share your medical conditions, your medication, and a past eating disorder you rarely talk about. You trust it stays between you and your coach. Every month, you upload a progress photo.

In this app, before the fix: every coach in the studio could read your intake, not just yours. Your progress photos sat in a public bucket with sequential file names. And when you asked for an AI meal plan, your full intake answers, medical history included, went to the model provider.

Dewi Pranoto runs a small personal training studio in Capelle aan den IJssel with three coaches, and built Coachkaart in Bolt: clients complete an intake, get weekly programmes, log workouts and meals, upload monthly progress photos, book sessions and buy session packages. About 140 clients, with January expected to double that. In December, Dewi asked for a review.

It found more:
• Coaches could see all clients, including colleagues'
• Package credits were deducted by the browser, so a refresh at the right moment kept a session free
• Payments confirmed by redirect
• Late cancellations judged by hand

What LaunchStudio changed before January (10 business days, €2,700):
✓ Health answers moved to a restricted, access-logged table, with explicit consent
✓ Photos in private storage, with signed links and metadata stripped
✓ Coach-client relationships enforced in the database
✓ Credit deduction made transactional at booking, with Mollie webhooks confirming purchases
✓ Cancellation rules enforced in Europe/Amsterdam time
✓ The AI meal plan using only goals and preferences, labelled as AI-generated and approved by a coach

The January intake brought 170 new clients, with no incidents or payment disputes. Clients who asked how their photos were stored got a clear answer. And two of Dewi's coaches now run their own client groups inside the app.

"My clients tell me things they don't tell their friends. The app had to be as discreet as I am in the studio." - Dewi Pranoto
