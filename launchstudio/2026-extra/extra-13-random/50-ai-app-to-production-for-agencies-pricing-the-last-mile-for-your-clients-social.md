🤝 Who did what? An agency, a gym chain, and a white-label partner

THE CLIENT
The owner of four boutique gyms, a long-standing client of Pixelpolder, arrived with a member app she'd built in Bolt over a weekend: class booking, membership management, a workout log and a supplements shop. She wanted it live for 1,100 members within a month, and assumed the agency could "just publish it."

THE AGENCY
Roel Stam runs Pixelpolder, a six-person design and marketing agency in Heemstede. His developers were front-end specialists, fully booked, and uneasy about payments and member data. So Roel brought in LaunchStudio as a white-label partner.

THE REVIEW (delivered to Roel within three days)
• Members could view other members' profiles and workout logs through the API
• Class capacity enforced only in the browser
• Mollie membership payments confirmed by redirect
• The Mollie live key in the frontend

WHO DID WHAT
Roel ➜ presented the findings under Pixelpolder's name, with a fixed price that included his margin. The client approved the same day. Roel stayed the single point of contact.
LaunchStudio ➜ fixed access control, capacity, payments and secrets, migrated data to an EU region, and set up hosting, staging, monitoring and backups (Launch & Grow, 14 business days, €4,400, plus €49/month)
Pixelpolder ➜ ran the launch campaign, the onboarding screens and the member emails

THE RESULT
On schedule. 780 members activated accounts in the first month, and class no-shows fell once reminders came in. Pixelpolder added managed hosting to the client's retainer, and has since used the same setup for two more client apps.

"My client didn't need to know who did the backend. She needed it to work and to come from someone she trusted. We could be that someone without pretending to be engineers we're not." - Roel Stam
