Fabienne Schuurmans runs a two-person studio in Utrecht building scheduling software for healthcare practices. Pitching to a dental group, she demoed the project she'd built for an existing physiotherapy client, because it was populated and looked convincing. She shared the project link, not a deployment link.

That database held around 900 real patients of the physiotherapy practice: names, dates of birth, appointment histories, treatment notes. And the same link exposed the whole codebase of a product the physiotherapy client had paid for. 🦷

The physiotherapy practice stayed a client, which Fabienne puts down entirely to telling them within a day, in full. The dental group didn't go ahead.
