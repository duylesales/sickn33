✏️ Line edits to one founder's legal pages. Each old line is real. Each new line is what replaced it.

OLD: Governed by Delaware law, disputes before a Delaware court.
NEW: Dutch law and Dutch jurisdiction.

OLD: All liability excluded, without limit.
NEW: Liability capped at twelve months of fees, excluding intent and gross negligence.

OLD: (nothing about the right of withdrawal)
NEW: The withdrawal right implemented properly, including customers who ask for immediate access and how the used period is handled.

OLD: "No refunds under any circumstances."
NEW: A refund policy stated plainly and applied consistently.

OLD: Data deleted within 30 days of cancellation.
NEW: The same promise, now true: a deletion function built, retention periods set, and a job enforcing them.

OLD: (no company details anywhere)
NEW: Legal name, KvK and VAT numbers in the footer.

Marloes Steegh built Boekhoudbuddy in Lovable: simple bookkeeping for Dutch freelancers and sole traders, €12 a month, 900 subscribers. The pages were generated at launch and never questioned, until a subscriber who cancelled within a week asked for a refund under the withdrawal right. She refused, citing her terms. He was correct, and her terms weren't enforceable. 📚

The dispute grew far beyond €12, including a post in a 4,000-member freelancers' forum, followed by eleven cancellations over the next fortnight.

What else came with the edits (LaunchStudio, 3 business days plus legal review, €3,900):
✓ Renewal and cancellation terms aligned with consumer rules, with cancelling as easy as signing up
✓ The privacy policy rewritten from the actual data inventory
✓ Agreement recorded per user, with version and timestamp
✓ A Dutch lawyer's review of the final documents

The dispute was settled with a refund and an apology. Marloes published a short note on what had been wrong and what changed, and credits it with stopping the cancellations. The deletion function, built only to make the privacy policy true, has been used by 140 customers and gets mentioned positively in her reviews.

"My terms said no refunds under any circumstances. It turned out that sentence was not only unenforceable, it was the most expensive sentence in my business." - Marloes Steegh
