🚨 Marloes Steegh's Boekhoudbuddy offers simple bookkeeping to 900 Dutch freelancers at €12/month. Her legal pages were generated at launch: Delaware law, unlimited liability exclusions, "no refunds under any circumstances", and a privacy policy promising deletion within 30 days - on a product with no deletion function. 😳

A subscriber who cancelled within a week claimed his 14-day withdrawal right. She refused, citing her terms. He was right. The dispute ended up on a 4,000-member freelancers' forum - and eleven more people cancelled. Here's what legal pages an app actually needs: 🧠

❌ Foreign law and courts for a Dutch company with Dutch customers
❌ "No refunds" terms that ignore the European withdrawal right
❌ Liability exclusions that are void in the Netherlands
❌ A privacy policy promising things the product couldn't do - and no KvK or VAT number anywhere

✅ Terms rewritten under Dutch law, with liability capped at 12 months of fees (not excluding intent or gross negligence)
✅ The withdrawal right implemented properly, with cancellation as easy as signing up
✅ A real deletion function and retention job built - so the privacy policy became true
✅ Company details in the footer, per-user versioned acceptance, and one review by a Dutch lawyer
