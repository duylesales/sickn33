🚨 Sylvia Grimberg's Evenementplanner coordinates around 300 events a year for 19 conference organisers. She hired a developer in Vietnam - good rate, frustrating start. In three weeks, just four small features shipped. 😳

Her tasks were one line each. Every task produced a question, every question waited a day, and half her working days ended with a question in her inbox and no progress. Here's how to make async work across time zones actually fast: 🧠

❌ One-line tasks like "fix the venue capacity bug"
❌ Every ambiguity becoming a question - and a lost day
❌ Reviews done "when she got to it", sometimes two days later
❌ No conventions file, so work arrived in the developer's own idiom

✅ Tasks rewritten with the outcome, the customer context, explicit boundaries and the obvious questions pre-answered
✅ A 15-minute daily call at the start of his day, plus a written update: done, next, blocked
✅ A standing rule: when blocked, proceed on a documented assumption - and keep a list of the next five tasks
✅ A conventions file from the existing code, and review moved to the first 20 minutes of her morning
