🚨 Naomi Dekker's Verzuimregistratie handles sick leave registration for 160 companies. During the build, an analytics script went in with default settings: autocapture of every click, and session replay switched on. The replays recorded managers' free-text notes about employees' health — and sent them to a provider outside the EU, with no processing agreement. 😳

It surfaced when a customer's data protection officer asked for the subprocessor list. And the tool still couldn't answer the board's simple question: which of the 160 companies actually used the product each week? Here's how to track the right product events under GDPR: 🧠

❌ Session replay recording screens full of health-related notes
❌ Page URLs containing employee names
❌ 140 inconsistently named events and no reliable organisation ID
❌ A non-EU provider, no processing agreement, nothing in the privacy statement

✅ Session replay removed from the app, autocapture disabled, and old data deleted at the previous provider with written confirmation
✅ A tracking plan of 22 server-side events with pseudonymous organisation and user IDs — no names, notes or identifying URLs
✅ Events stored in the product's own database and forwarded to EU-hosted analytics under a processing agreement, configured to Dutch DPA low-impact conditions
✅ A per-customer opt-out, updated processing register and subprocessor list, plus activation and account health views
