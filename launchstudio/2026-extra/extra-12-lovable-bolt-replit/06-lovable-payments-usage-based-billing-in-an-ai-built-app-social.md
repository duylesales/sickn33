Reinier Slootweg built Dossierlezer with Lovable: it reads tender documents and produces a structured summary for construction and engineering firms bidding on public work. Model calls were his biggest variable cost, and he'd never measured them per customer. In month seven, his provider invoice passed his subscription revenue.

He wasn't losing money on the product. He was losing money on four accounts he couldn't see, because everything was one number. And raising the flat price would have punished 92 customers to subsidise four, two of them reference customers.

The new numbers (LaunchStudio, 5 business days, €3,750):
1 billable unit: a document, not tokens or pages
1 usage event per processed document, with an idempotency key, the account, time, page count and cost
0 charges for failed processing: counting happens server-side, at successful completion
40 documents included in the €89 plan
25 more per bundle, at €35
80% and 100%: notifications to the billing contact, with a hard stop by default and opt-in top-up
6 past periods visible on a usage page in the product
12 accounts getting the monthly per-project statement they'd asked for

What happened:
4 heavy accounts: now about €411 a month each, and all 4 stayed (one said bundles were cheaper than the internal process they replaced)
71 of 92 ordinary customers: never exceeded the allowance, so nothing changed
Gross margin the next quarter: from negative to 68%
