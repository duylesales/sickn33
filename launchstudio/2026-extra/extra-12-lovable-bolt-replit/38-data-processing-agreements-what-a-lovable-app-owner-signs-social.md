❌ Signing customer templates without checking what the product can do
❌ No per-customer deletion, and backups with no retention limit
❌ 24-hour breach notification promised with no way to detect a breach that fast
❌ No register of who signed what - four deletion windows, three notification periods, two audit rights

✅ Per-organisation deletion across all tables - deleted, anonymised for statistics, or kept for tax - with an automatic completion certificate
✅ 90-day backup retention, plus a procedure so restores never bring deleted data back
✅ Structured export, her own Dutch processing agreement reviewed by a privacy lawyer, and a register of every signed version
