🚨 Vincent Doeleman's Kandidaatmatch helps 23 recruitment agencies match about 30,000 candidates to vacancies. It started as search and filter — clearly minimal risk. Over a year, one reasonable request at a time, a model began scoring candidates, sorting by that score, and hiding anyone below a threshold. 😳

Then an agency's client — a large employer with its own compliance team — asked how it complied with the EU AI Act. The product was now filtering access to work automatically: high risk, with no documentation at all. Here's what the AI Act means for one small feature: 🧠

❌ A score, then a sort, then a hidden threshold — a category change nobody decided
❌ A number with no explanation of why
❌ No record of what was suggested versus what a recruiter decided
❌ No data processing agreement with the model provider, and full candidate records sent to the model

✅ The threshold removed — every matching candidate stays visible, with the score just one sortable column
✅ Visible reasoning per score: which requirements matched and which didn't
✅ Recruiter overrides logged, a clear note that scores mustn't be the sole basis for a decision, and a written categorisation
✅ A provider agreement in place, and only the relevant experience text sent to the model
