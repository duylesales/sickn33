Brand strategy agencies occupy a position of immense strategic trust: when founders and corporate innovation teams launch new ventures, they turn to brand agencies first to define their naming, positioning, visual identity, and customer journeys. But historically, once the Figma brand deck and marketing website were delivered, agencies had to step aside and watch outside development firms capture the largest share of the client's budget to build the actual application. For Merkvast — an 11-person creative brand agency based along the Keizersgracht in Amsterdam — partnering with LaunchStudio unlocked the ability to deliver complete, working software products under their own brand, generating record quarterly margins with zero in-house developer hiring.

## The Strategy: From Brand Strategy to Full Product Delivery

Merkvast's Managing Partner, Sander van Loon, noticed that client timelines were consistently breaking down during development handoffs:
- Clients spent €20,000 with Merkvast on branding and UX strategy, and then spent €60,000+ with slow traditional dev shops that took 6 months to build basic MVPs.
- The resulting live products frequently diluted the carefully crafted brand experience — a beautiful Figma prototype would come back from a dev shop with generic form components, broken responsive layouts, and none of the interaction polish the brand team had specified.
- Merkvast's revenue was capped by billable design hours rather than high-value strategic product launches, meaning the agency's growth ceiling was directly tied to headcount rather than to the value it created for clients.
- Referring clients to outside dev shops meant losing visibility into the build entirely — Sander had no way to guarantee delivery quality on work his agency's name was still attached to in the client's mind.

Sander partnered with LaunchStudio to create a productized "Venture Launchpad" service: Merkvast designs the brand, UX, and AI frontend prototype (using Lovable, Figma, and React), while LaunchStudio's Manifera engineering team powers the entire backend architecture invisibly behind the scenes. Every deliverable — the repository, the staging environment, the client-facing communication — is white-labeled under Merkvast's name, so from the client's perspective, Merkvast simply became a full-service digital product studio overnight.

## One Quarter, Three Successful Enterprise MVPs

In Q2 2026, Merkvast sold and delivered three distinct client digital products through the LaunchStudio partnership, each with its own regulatory and architectural complexity that Merkvast's design team was never expected to solve alone:

**1. ZorgMatch (Healthcare Staffing Platform — Utrecht):**
- *The Client:* A regional nursing collective needing an automated shifts-and-scheduling portal to replace a spreadsheet-and-WhatsApp process that was causing double-bookings and missed shift coverage across 14 care facilities.
- *The Build:* Multi-role authentication (agency coordinators, facility managers, individual nurses), GDPR-compliant health credential verification with expiry tracking, SMS dispatch via Twilio for last-minute shift openings, and automated monthly billing reconciled against actual logged hours rather than scheduled hours.
- *Timeline:* Delivered in 3 weeks.
- *Client Fee:* €32,000 (LaunchStudio backend cost: €6,800).

**2. LeaseVergelijk (B2B EV Fleet Comparison Tool — Rotterdam):**
- *The Client:* A sustainable mobility consultancy offering real-time fleet total cost of ownership (TCO) comparisons for companies transitioning combustion fleets to electric vehicles ahead of Dutch emissions-zone deadlines.
- *The Build:* A complex server-side calculation engine modeling depreciation, charging infrastructure cost, subsidy eligibility, and fuel-versus-electricity cost curves; dynamic PDF fleet reports generated on demand for client presentations; and Mollie corporate subscription billing with multi-seat account management for consultancy teams.
- *Timeline:* Delivered in 2.5 weeks.
- *Client Fee:* €26,000 (LaunchStudio backend cost: €5,400).

**3. BouwHub (Subcontractor Bidding Marketplace — Eindhoven):**
- *The Client:* A construction technology startup connecting general contractors with certified electricians and plumbers for competitive project bidding, replacing an informal network of phone calls and personal relationships.
- *The Build:* Stripe Connect split-payments routing platform fees automatically on every transaction, escrow milestone payouts that release funds to subcontractors only after a contractor confirms work completion, and Supabase Row-Level Security ensuring competing contractors could never see each other's bid amounts.
- *Timeline:* Delivered in 3 weeks.
- *Client Fee:* €34,000 (LaunchStudio backend cost: €7,200).

Each engagement followed the same handoff rhythm: Merkvast's design team delivered a clickable Lovable or Figma prototype and brand system, LaunchStudio scoped the backend within 48 hours, and Manifera's engineers built against that prototype directly — meaning the visual design Merkvast's clients had already approved shipped to production essentially unchanged, rather than being reinterpreted (and diluted) by a second agency partway through the build.

## The Commercial Impact: 68% Gross Margins

Over the 90-day quarter, Merkvast generated **€92,000 in total project revenue across the three product launches**, with total LaunchStudio engineering costs of **€19,400**. Merkvast captured **€72,600 in gross profit**, while elevating their brand positioning from a graphic design shop to an elite venture studio — a repositioning that let Sander raise the agency's own strategy-and-branding day rate by 15% for new prospects who now saw Merkvast as an end-to-end product partner rather than a pure design vendor.

All three clients retained Merkvast for ongoing marketing, growth design, and managed product support, creating an additional €4,500/month in recurring agency retainers. Because Manifera's Launch & Grow plan handled the underlying hosting, backups, and security patching for all three products, Merkvast could package a managed-product retainer on top without needing to hire a single developer or DevOps engineer internally — the recurring technical burden stayed with LaunchStudio while the recurring revenue accrued to Merkvast.
