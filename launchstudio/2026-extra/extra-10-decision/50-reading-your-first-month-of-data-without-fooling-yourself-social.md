A scary percentage at small sample sizes feels like a verdict. Here's why it's usually a coincidence in disguise: 🧠

❌ 11 of the 15 "non-activated" accounts had actually completed the real activation action
❌ A duplicate event, firing twice whenever a second browser tab was open, quietly inflated the denominator
❌ With 19 signups, one converting customer moves your headline rate by a third — not a trend, a rounding accident
❌ A six-week rewrite was nearly launched to fix a problem that was a tracking bug, not a product one

✅ Below roughly 100 events per step, read counts and individual sessions, not percentages
✅ Go through non-converting accounts one at a time before rebuilding anything around a rate
✅ Read your error tracker before your analytics dashboard — one real defect matters at any sample size
✅ Leave pricing, positioning and roadmap alone in month one until measurement itself is trustworthy
