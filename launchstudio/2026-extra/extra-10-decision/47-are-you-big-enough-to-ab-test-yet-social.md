A "significant" result at 11pm feels like proof. At small sample sizes, it rarely is. Here's the trap: 🧠

❌ 95% confidence from 94 visitors and 95% confidence from 10,000 look identical on a dashboard — they are not remotely equivalent
❌ At Verso's baseline conversion rate, the test would have needed several times that traffic to reliably detect a difference that size
❌ Checking results daily and stopping the moment it looks "significant" inflates false positives well past the 5% everyone assumes
❌ Testing a button color at low volume is close to unfalsifiable — you'll never gather enough data to trust either result

✅ Run the power calculation before launching a test, not after seeing a number you like
✅ Below a few thousand relevant actions per variant, switch methods instead of forcing an A/B test
✅ Roll changes out to everyone with a guardrail metric and a pre-set rollback threshold
✅ Pair the rollout with a handful of structured customer calls to find what a thin sample can't isolate
