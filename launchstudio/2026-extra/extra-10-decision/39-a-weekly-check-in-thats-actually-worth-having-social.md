🚨 Two "on track" check-ins in a row told Lotte everything was fine. Four days before launch, she learned the email-confirmation service had been silently failing since week two. 😳

"How's it going" is the wrong question — it produces an averaged answer, not the one outlier that actually matters. 🧠

❌ "Good, making progress" hides a week with four smooth days and one seriously blocked one
❌ Group calls where individual contributors soften risk in front of their peers
❌ Asking "how many hours did the migration take" turns a partnership meeting into an audit
❌ Letting a mentioned risk repeat for two weeks running without ever escalating it

✅ Run a 30-minute agenda: what shipped, what's blocked and by whom, what's likely to go wrong next week
✅ Ask "if something's going to go sideways next week, what's your best guess" before it's a real blocker
✅ Batch your open decisions into one slot instead of dribbling them out across the week
✅ Keep it to one point of contact; distribute a written summary widely instead of adding people to the call
