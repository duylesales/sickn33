🚨 Jasmijn Verstraete opened her feature-request spreadsheet every Monday with a sigh — 211 rows, nine months old, zero clear link to anything her team had actually built. 😳

Collecting every idea feels responsible. Here's why it usually just produces a graveyard: 🧠

❌ A 211-row spreadsheet doesn't tell you what to build next any more clearly than having no spreadsheet at all
❌ Public voting boards reward whichever request is easiest to click in ten seconds, not whichever moves a real metric
❌ A quarterly NPS score with no follow-up question can't tell "I love it but the price bothers me" from indifference
❌ Silence isn't satisfaction — most unhappy customers just stop logging in instead of complaining

✅ Cut down to three owned channels: a targeted in-app prompt, a small invited customer group, a mandatory cancellation survey
✅ Ask "what almost stopped you finishing that?" right after a meaningful action, not a generic "how's it going?"
✅ Run one fixed 30-minute weekly review: act on it, park it with a reason, or discard it
✅ Close the loop publicly — tell the customer who asked in March that it shipped last week
