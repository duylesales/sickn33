"I don't even know what to ask for" feels like a disqualifying gap. It's actually a translation problem, and the translation isn't your job: 🧠

❌ She'd been treating vocabulary as a prerequisite to even starting the conversation, delaying it for months
❌ A screenshot of a strange Supabase dashboard message, taken once and never investigated, turned out to describe a failed database migration
❌ That missing migration had left one table without the ownership field the rest of the schema used — precisely the mechanism behind her worst fear
❌ Translating "make it work" into a checkable scope is the engineer's job, not something you're supposed to arrive already knowing

✅ Describe what you're afraid of in plain language — "what if another artist sees a client's booking" is a complete, usable input
✅ Bring screenshots of anything confusing you noticed, even without understanding it
✅ List every login and third-party service the product touches — that inventory alone answers most technical questions
✅ Ask the questions that don't require vocabulary: "if I fix one thing on this list, which one, and why?"
