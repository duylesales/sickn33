🚨 Roos Hendriksen spent six weeks building a full Slack app with OAuth, action buttons, and a slash command — for two customers who asked. 58 others never touched it. 😳

"Can you send this to Slack?" hides an enormous range of effort, and founders usually build the expensive end first: 🧠

❌ Of 60 accounts, only the two who originally asked ever connected the finished Slack app
❌ Most of the other 58 accounts turned out to run on Microsoft Teams, or nothing at all
❌ Two Slack platform changes forced deadline-bound updates, and a signature verification bug broke the buttons silently for three weeks
❌ The real, most common request underneath it all was simply "let me know without keeping the product open"

✅ Start with an incoming webhook the customer pastes in themselves — no OAuth, no review, an afternoon of work
✅ Build an in-app notification centre before any external channel — it's the one durable record customers can return to
✅ Let customers choose which events go to a channel, separate from email, and group bursts into one message
✅ Ask which tool is actually open on your customers' screens all day before building for a specific platform
