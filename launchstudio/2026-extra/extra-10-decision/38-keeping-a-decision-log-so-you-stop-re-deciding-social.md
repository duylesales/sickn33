"Didn't we already decide this?" is one of the most expensive sentences a growing team says out loud. 🧠

❌ Re-litigating a settled decision from scratch because the reasoning only ever lived in memory
❌ A Slack thread nobody can find, three weeks later, "for reasons that made sense at the time"
❌ Writing revisit conditions as dates ("reassess in three months") instead of numbers or events
❌ Logging "because it felt right" instead of something measurable or checkable later

✅ Log a decision only if reversing it costs real time/money, or someone else needs the reasoning without asking you
✅ Use five columns: date, decision, reasoning, who decided, revisit condition
✅ Attach logging to a moment that already happens — the end of the call where the decision was made
✅ Pull up the log instead of re-arguing: confirm the revisit condition hasn't been met and move on
