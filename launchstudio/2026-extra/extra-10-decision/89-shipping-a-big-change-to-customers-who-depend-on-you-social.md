🚨 Anouk shipped a better calendar to all 140 practices at once, with no way back. Support went from 4 messages a week to 60 in three days. 😳

A redesign that's objectively better still tempts founders to just ship it to everyone. Here's why that backfires: 🧠

❌ The new calendar moved the day view — used several hundred times a day by experienced receptionists — behind an extra click
❌ A familiar keyboard shortcut changed, and there was no old version left to fall back to; it had been removed from the code
❌ Nothing was actually broken — people simply couldn't work at their previous speed and had no alternative
❌ Two practices cancelled within the first fortnight, and a third asked for the old version back, which no longer existed

✅ Roll out to yourself first, then volunteers who opted in, then a small share of accounts, before everyone
✅ Keep the old version available for a defined 4-8 week window — a way back turns panic into patience
✅ Watch task completion rate and switch-back rate between groups, not just complaints — most unhappy customers say nothing
✅ Write down what must not break before you start, in the customer's terms, and decide your stop condition in advance
