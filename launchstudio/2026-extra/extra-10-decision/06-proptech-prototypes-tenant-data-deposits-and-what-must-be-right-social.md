PropTech prototypes look simple: a listing, an application, a lease, a deposit. Each one hides an obligation an AI tool never asks about: 🧠

❌ Deposits paid straight into the same Stripe account as platform subscription revenue, with no separation
❌ No way to even identify which transactions were deposits versus subscription payments
❌ Rejected tenant applications persisted indefinitely, "just in case"
❌ A landlord asked if deposits were registered with the Netherlands' rent deposit protections — nobody knew

✅ Deposits are the tenant's money held in trust, not platform revenue — they need their own ledger
✅ Move deposit funds into a distinctly labeled holding structure, separate from operating funds
✅ Build a reporting view so a landlord can confirm exactly which deposits are held, for whom
✅ Purge rejected applications after a defined retention window instead of keeping everything forever
