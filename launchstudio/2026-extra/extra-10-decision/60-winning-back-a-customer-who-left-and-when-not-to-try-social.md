Losing customers tempts founders to blast the whole churned list. Here's the better read: 🧠

❌ 23 customers cancelled in eight months; a scheduled, one-size-fits-all win-back would have reached all of them regardless of why they left
❌ Seven had given the exact same reason: her tool couldn't handle partial payments against an invoice, routine in construction
❌ A discount on a plan someone already rejected mostly teaches them the price is negotiable
❌ A fixed 30/60/90-day send schedule sends the same message to everyone whether or not anything has actually changed

✅ Sort churned customers by their stated reason for leaving, not by how long ago they cancelled
✅ Send event-driven win-backs — the day you ship the thing they asked for, not on a timer
✅ Name the specific fix and reference what they said when they left; skip the generic apology
✅ Keep cancelled accounts read-only so "your workspace is exactly as you left it" is literally true
