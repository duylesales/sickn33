🧠 "Best AI app" rankings measure how pleasant a tool is to build with. They say nothing about whether what you build is safe to launch.

❌ Ranking criteria are almost always polish, interface feel, features, and buzz — not production-readiness track record
❌ A tool can produce a beautiful demo and still scaffold a database that breaks under concurrent access
❌ None of the real gaps show up in the thirty minutes a reviewer spends clicking through a demo
❌ Nobody tracks "how often apps built with this tool make it to a stable, secure launch" — the number that actually matters

✅ Look for case studies of real, launched products, not just demos
✅ Check what the tool's own docs say about security and production deployment, not just speed
✅ Find communities discussing what happened *after* building, not just the building experience
