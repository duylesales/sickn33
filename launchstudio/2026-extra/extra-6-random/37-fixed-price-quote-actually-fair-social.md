📋 Femke Oosterhuis built AgendaKoppel, a scheduling SaaS in Blaricum, using Lovable. Ahead of a production push, she asked three vendors to quote the exact same scope — authentication hardening, a double-booking fix, and calendar data security. The prices came back at roughly €900, €3,200, and €6,000, for what was supposed to be the same job. 😳

🧠 A number without a breakdown isn't a quote, it's a guess dressed up as one.

❌ The €900 quote quietly omitted testing and data migration entirely
❌ The €6,000 quote included a broader authentication rebuild nobody asked for
❌ A vendor unwilling to itemize their price usually means it was set by instinct, not scope
❌ A lower total missing a whole component isn't cheaper — it's incomplete

✅ Ask every vendor to itemize: specific tasks, and roughly how many hours or days each one takes
✅ Write your own independent itemized list before you ever compare vendor breakdowns
✅ Negotiate specific line items instead of asking "can you come down on price"
