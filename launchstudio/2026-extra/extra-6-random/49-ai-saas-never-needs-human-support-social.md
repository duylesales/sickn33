🤖 Merel Timmermans built AfspraakSlim, a scheduling SaaS, using v0 with a connected backend — and assumed her AI chat assistant would fully replace human support. For a while, that looked like a reasonable bet. 😳

Then real usage hit the edge cases the bot couldn't touch. 🧠

❌ Two customers got double-booked into the same slot after a syncing delay
❌ A refund dispute over a cancelled service had no framework the assistant could evaluate
❌ The bot gave generic, unhelpful answers, with no human anywhere in the loop to catch the frustration
❌ Several affected users left negative reviews before Merel even knew there was a problem

✅ Define in advance which categories of requests get handed off, not attempted — anything involving money or a dispute
✅ Build a handoff system that detects double-booking conflicts and refund disputes automatically
✅ Route flagged requests straight to a real inbox with full context attached, instantly
