📓 Three weeks between "start the project" and a national trade fair

Rob Jacobs runs Verhuurplanner from Eindhoven, a SaaS for event equipment rental companies built with Cursor and Supabase. He wanted to present it at a national trade fair, three weeks away. So he started a Launch & Grow project with LaunchStudio, quoted at €5,100 (plus €49/month afterwards).

WEEK 1: find out
• Day one: the inventory shows the domain is registered by his former business partner. The transfer starts the same day.
• The review finds loose policies between rental companies, Stripe subscriptions updated on the success page, and no staging.
• A service key in a client-side function: rotated that afternoon.
• In the findings call, Rob decides: staff at a rental company see equipment and bookings, but invoices only with a finance role.

WEEK 2: build
• Policies rewritten, with tests
• Staging and a pipeline set up
• Stripe moved to verified webhooks, with past subscriptions reconciled
• Email authentication configured
• Rob gets an update every other day, and answers two questions about failed payment rules.

WEEK 3: ship
• Rob spends one hour checking staging
• Release on Tuesday morning
• Domain transfer completes on Wednesday, two days before the fair
• Handover call on Thursday

THE FAIR
Rob demonstrated the live product, and signed up nine rental companies during and after the event, without a single technical issue during onboarding.

Total project time: 15 business days.

"I expected three weeks of chaos. It was three weeks of short messages, two calls and one hour clicking through staging. Then I went to the fair with an app I trusted." - Rob Jacobs
