📝 THE PRE-CALL TEMPLATE, as one founder filled it in (about twenty minutes)

Tools and services: Bolt, Supabase, Netlify, Mollie, Postmark
Who created what: "The Supabase project was set up by a freelancer who helped me early on."
Who should see what: "Interpreters see only their own jobs and rates. Agencies see their own interpreters and invoices."
Known problems: "Once an interpreter told me she could see another interpreter's hourly rate. I think I fixed it with a prompt but I am not sure."
Attachments: a security questionnaire from a hospital interpreting service
Deadline: a pilot starting in five weeks
Code: read access to the GitHub repository

Nadia Bouzid runs Tolkplanner from Rotterdam, built with Bolt and Supabase: agencies assign interpreting jobs, interpreters accept them, and invoices are generated monthly. She thought she'd need to understand her app technically before talking to anyone. She only needed to describe it honestly.

WHAT HAPPENED NEXT
The intro call: fifteen minutes.
The short look at the code confirmed two things from her own notes:
• Her earlier fix had hidden the hourly rate in the interface, but it was still readable through the API
• The Supabase project was still in the freelancer's account

The written quote arrived the next morning: €3,100 within Launch Ready, with eighteen specific scope items, including the ownership transfer, policies for agencies and interpreters with tests, payment and email checks, and support for the hospital's questionnaire.

The project started three days later and took 8 business days, finishing a week before the pilot. The hospital accepted the questionnaire answers. And Nadia said the preparation made her feel in control from the very first conversation.
