Before AI app builders, no-code platforms such as Bubble, Webflow, Glide, Softr and FlutterFlow were the main route for non-technical founders to launch software. A whole industry of no-code agencies grew around them, and many do excellent work. Today, founders who built an app with Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor or Claude Code sometimes ask a no-code agency for help, and the advice they receive is often to rebuild on the agency's preferred platform.

LaunchStudio takes a different approach: keep the app you built, make it production ready and leave the code compatible with your AI tools. This page compares the two honestly, including when a no-code agency is the better fit. It is written by LaunchStudio, so we have an interest; we have tried to be fair.

## Key Takeaways

- **No-code agencies build on visual platforms;** LaunchStudio works on the code your AI tools produced.
- **A rebuild means starting again;** production readiness keeps what already works.
- **Ownership differs:** no-code apps live on a platform; AI-built apps are standard code you own.
- **No-code agencies are a good fit** for products that suit a specific platform and teams who prefer visual editing.
- **LaunchStudio fits** founders who want to keep their AI-built app and workflow.

## When a No-Code Agency Is the Better Choice

- **You have not built anything yet** and prefer a visual platform for the long term.
- **Your team wants to edit the app visually** without code or AI prompts.
- **Your product fits a platform's strengths,** for example an internal tool on Glide or Softr, or a marketing site on Webflow.
- **You depend on a platform's plugin ecosystem** for features you need.
- **The agency has deep experience in your niche** and a track record you can verify.

## When LaunchStudio Is the Better Choice

- **You already have a working AI-built app** and do not want to start over.
- **You want to own standard code** in your own repository and hosting.
- **You want to keep building with AI tools** after launch.
- **You need production work:** security, payments, data, deployment, monitoring, integrations.
- **You want a fixed price and a short timeline** rather than a multi-month rebuild.
- **Customers ask about data location and subprocessors,** which are easier to answer with your own hosting.

## Rebuild Versus Production Readiness

The most important difference is what happens to the app you already have. A rebuild discards the existing code and recreates the product on another platform. That can make sense if the existing app is fundamentally unsuitable, but it has costs: time, money, new bugs, a period where the product is frozen and the loss of whatever your AI tool workflow had made easy.

Production readiness keeps the interface, flows and most of the code, and fixes what is missing: access control, secrets, payments, emails, environments, backups and monitoring. For most AI-built apps that already work, this is faster and cheaper. If our review shows that part of an app genuinely needs rebuilding, we say so before starting.

## Ownership and Lock-In

No-code platforms host your app on their infrastructure. That is convenient, but your product depends on their pricing, features, limits and terms. Exporting is often limited; some platforms export data but not application logic, so leaving means rebuilding again. Platform price changes affect your margins directly.

AI-built apps typically produce standard code, often React or Next.js with Supabase or another PostgreSQL database. After a LaunchStudio project, that code lives in your repository and runs on hosting accounts you own. You can switch hosting providers, hire any developer or continue with any AI tool. That does not make no-code a bad choice, but it is a structural difference worth considering for a product you plan to grow for years.

## Pricing Compared

No-code agency pricing varies widely, from small fixed packages to larger custom builds, often with ongoing platform fees and retainers on top. A rebuild of a working app typically costs significantly more than making the existing one production ready, because everything is recreated.

LaunchStudio's pricing is fixed per project: Launch Ready from €800 to €3,500 including 48 hours of post-launch support, and Launch & Grow from €2,500 to €7,500 plus €49 per month for ongoing care. Hosting is billed directly by your providers, without markup.

## Scalability and Performance

No-code platforms have improved greatly, and many handle substantial usage. Limits can still appear in complex queries, large datasets, background processing or specific integrations, and solving them may depend on the platform's roadmap. Standard code on PostgreSQL gives you direct control: indexes, queries, background jobs and caching can be tuned as needed. For most early-stage products, either approach scales far enough; the difference matters more as data and usage grow.

## Working With AI Tools

A practical consideration for founders who built with AI tools is workflow. If you enjoy building in Lovable, Bolt or Cursor, moving to a no-code platform means learning a new tool and abandoning a workflow that works for you. LaunchStudio leaves project instructions in your repository so your AI tool follows the security and data rules established during the project, and staging plus tests catch regressions. You keep the speed that got you this far.

## A Common Scenario

A founder builds a working client portal with Lovable and Supabase. A friend recommends a no-code agency, which proposes rebuilding the portal on its preferred platform in about two months, with platform fees on top. The founder asks LaunchStudio for a second opinion. The review finds the usual gaps: loose database policies, public storage, payments granted on the success page, no backups and a project in a freelancer's account. All are fixable within Launch Ready in about a week. The founder keeps the portal, launches a month and a half earlier than the rebuild would have allowed and continues improving it in Lovable.

That scenario is not universal. Sometimes the existing app has fundamental problems, or the founder genuinely prefers a visual platform for the long term. The point is to compare a rebuild with production readiness on facts, not on the tools an agency happens to specialise in.

## Hybrid Approaches

Some founders combine both worlds. A marketing site may live on Webflow, built by a no-code agency, while the product itself is an AI-built app made production ready by LaunchStudio. Internal tools for staff may run on a no-code platform that connects to the product's database. This can work well when each part is chosen for its strengths and the boundaries are clear, for example a single source of truth for customer data and documented integrations between the tools.

## What Changes for Your Team

Choosing between these approaches also affects who can change the product. With a no-code platform, team members who learn the visual editor can make changes, but often only within the platform's patterns, and complex changes usually go back to the agency. With an AI-built app in standard code, changes can be made through AI tools by the founder, by any developer or by a partner. Founders should consider who will maintain and extend the product in two years, not only who builds the next version.

## The Long-Term View

Looking two or three years ahead, ask which option keeps the most doors open. Standard code in your own repository can be maintained by many developers, moved between hosting providers and extended with any AI tool. A no-code app keeps you within one platform, which may be exactly what you want if the platform fits your product and team. Neither choice is wrong, but it should be a deliberate one rather than a default set by whichever agency you happened to contact first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I rebuild my AI-built app on a no-code platform?

Usually not, if the app already works. Making the existing app production ready is typically faster and cheaper than a rebuild. A rebuild can make sense if you prefer visual editing for the long term or the existing app has fundamental problems; a review shows which applies.

### Is no-code more secure than AI-built code?

Not inherently. No-code platforms handle some infrastructure for you, but access rules, privacy settings and business logic still need to be configured correctly. AI-built apps need the same care in code and database policies. Security depends on how either is set up.

### Can I leave a no-code platform later?

It depends on the platform. Many allow data export, but application logic often cannot be exported, so leaving usually means rebuilding. AI-built apps in standard code can move between hosting providers and developers more easily.

### How does Manifera's experience compare with a no-code agency?

Manifera has 11+ years of experience, 160+ delivered projects and 120+ engineers working in standard software stacks for clients such as Vodafone and TNO. LaunchStudio brings that engineering depth to AI-built apps, focusing on security, data, payments and operations.

### Which option is better for SEO and AI visibility?

Both can work. What matters is fast, server-rendered public pages, clean URLs, metadata, structured data and stable hosting. With standard code you have full control over these; on no-code platforms, check what the platform allows.
