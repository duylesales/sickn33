🏠 Sophie Meijer runs an energy advice business in Groningen and built Woningwatt with v0: a dashboard where homeowners see their energy use, recommended improvements and subsidies. The interface was so polished that a housing cooperative wanted to offer it to 400 members. There was one problem: almost nothing was real. 😅

Behind the beautiful screens: 🧠

❌ Most screens running on sample arrays inside the components
❌ No logins at all
❌ The only real data a contact form, sending email through a key visible in the browser
❌ One environment, a generated domain and no monitoring

✅ A data model for homes, meter readings, recommendations, subsidies and advisors in Supabase (EU) with Row Level Security
✅ All eleven screens connected through server actions with session and ownership checks
✅ Magic link logins, an advisor role and a CSV import of meter readings for the cooperative
✅ The key rotated, plus Vercel with a custom domain, separate environments, email authentication, backups and monitoring
