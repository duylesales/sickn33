After launch, an app needs ongoing care. Dependencies receive security updates. Hosting providers change plans and limits. Backups need checking. Monitoring sends alerts that someone must look at. Supabase, Vercel and payment providers deprecate features. Most founders do not want to become part-time system administrators, but they also cannot afford a full-time developer just to keep the lights on.

LaunchStudio's Launch & Grow package includes an ongoing service at €49 per month for exactly this. It is not a hosting plan in the traditional sense, because your app stays in your own accounts with providers such as Vercel, Netlify and Supabase. It is a managed care service on top of that hosting: someone watches the app, keeps it updated and steps in when something needs attention.

This page explains what the €49 per month typically covers, what it does not and how it relates to the costs your hosting providers charge directly.

## Key Takeaways

- **Your app stays in your accounts.** The €49 per month pays for care, not for servers.
- **Monitoring and alert follow-up** mean problems are seen and triaged, not just logged.
- **Updates and backup checks** keep the app secure and recoverable over time.
- **Small fixes get priority,** while larger changes are quoted separately.
- **Hosting provider costs are separate** and billed directly to you by those providers.

## Why Apps Need Ongoing Care

An app that is not maintained slowly becomes riskier. Libraries in your project receive security fixes that are not applied. A provider changes how authentication tokens work and an old integration stops. A database plan reaches its limits without warning. Backups keep running but nobody notices when one fails. Error rates creep up after an update to a third-party script.

None of this is dramatic on any given day. Over months it adds up to the kind of incident that happens at the worst moment: during a launch campaign, on the day of an investor demo or on a weekend when nobody is watching. Ongoing care turns those surprises into routine work done in advance.

## How Hosting Costs Work

Because your app stays in your own accounts, hosting providers bill you directly. For many early-stage AI-built apps, typical monthly provider costs range from a free tier up to a few tens of euros, for example a Supabase Pro plan and a Vercel or Netlify plan, plus email, monitoring and payment fees. Costs grow with usage: database size, compute, bandwidth, function executions and stored files.

Keeping hosting in your accounts has several advantages. You own everything, so you can switch providers or support partners at any time. Costs are transparent, because you see the provider's invoice. And there is no markup hidden in a bundled hosting fee. Part of the monthly service is watching those costs and suggesting changes when a plan no longer fits, such as upgrading compute before performance suffers or reducing usage that inflates the bill.

## What a Monthly Health Note Looks Like

The health note is deliberately short, so you actually read it. A typical note covers:

- **Availability:** uptime for the month and any incidents, with duration and cause
- **Errors:** the number of new error types and whether rates went up or down
- **Updates:** which dependencies and security patches were applied
- **Backups:** confirmation that backups ran and the result of the latest restore test
- **Usage and costs:** database size, storage, bandwidth and function usage compared with plan limits
- **Upcoming changes:** provider deprecations or renewals that need attention
- **Recommendations:** two or three suggestions, each marked as urgent, soon or when convenient

Founders often forward the note to a co-founder, investor or board member as a simple way to show that the product is looked after. It is also useful during due diligence, because it gives a factual history of how the app has been run.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the €49 per month a hosting fee?

No. Your app stays in your own accounts with providers such as Vercel, Netlify and Supabase, which bill you directly. The €49 per month pays for ongoing care: monitoring, alert follow-up, updates, backup checks, platform watch, a monthly health note and priority for small fixes.

### Can I get the monthly service without the Launch & Grow project?

The monthly service is part of Launch & Grow, which starts with a project in the €2,500 to €7,500 range to prepare the app properly. For apps that were already prepared by LaunchStudio or are in good shape, onboarding can be shorter. We confirm what is needed after a short review.

### Can I cancel the monthly service?

Yes. Because everything is in your accounts, you can stop the service without losing access to your code, hosting or data. We remove our access and hand over any notes, so you or another partner can continue.
