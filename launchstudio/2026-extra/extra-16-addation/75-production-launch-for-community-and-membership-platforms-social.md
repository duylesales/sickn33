🎨 Lotte de Kok runs a coaching business for independent designers from Amsterdam and built Studiokring with Lovable and Supabase: a paid community with memberships, recorded masterclasses, a member directory and discussions. With about 450 members, recordings began circulating outside the community, former members kept access for months and one member found her phone number in the public directory. 😳

The assessment found: 🧠

❌ Videos in a public bucket with permanent links
❌ Membership status set at sign-up and never updated from Stripe events
❌ Every profile field shown to every member, and profile pages indexed by search engines
❌ Messages readable by changing a conversation ID, no reporting, and full admin rights for every moderator

✅ Membership driven by verified, idempotent Stripe webhooks, with a short grace period and access ending with the paid period
✅ Recordings on protected video hosting with signed playback, documents in private storage with expiring links
✅ Profile privacy settings with private defaults, public indexing off and exports limited to Lotte
✅ Messages restricted to participants with tested policies, plus reporting, a moderation queue and a separate moderator role
