📖 Annemiek Boersma is a remedial teacher in Assen who built Leesladder with Lovable and Supabase: pupils practise reading at their level, teachers track progress, parents see weekly summaries. Her school loved it, and a board with eleven primary schools wanted it too. Then the privacy officer sent the model processing agreement and a list of questions. 📝

The assessment showed why the board almost said no: 🧠

❌ The database hosted outside the EU, and every teacher able to see every pupil
❌ Parents able to open other children's progress pages by changing the address
❌ Free-text notes about dyslexia and home situations
❌ The AI feedback feature sending pupils' full names and notes to a model provider, with no retention or deletion

✅ An EU region, schools separated and roles for teachers, remedial teachers, parents, pupils and admins enforced and tested
✅ Parents limited to their own children, with support for two parents per child
✅ Structured support categories instead of free text, and AI receiving only the reading text and level
✅ School account login, retention jobs, export and deletion, accessibility checks and input for the privacy annex
