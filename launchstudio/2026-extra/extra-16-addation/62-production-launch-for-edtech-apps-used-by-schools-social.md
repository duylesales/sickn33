📝 SCHOOL REPORT: Leesladder, before the privacy annex

Reading practice at each pupil's level ........ A
Progress tracking for teachers ................ A
Weekly summaries for parents .................. A
Privacy annex readiness ....................... F

Annemiek Boersma is a remedial teacher in Assen who built Leesladder with Lovable and Supabase. Pupils practise reading at their own level, teachers track progress, and parents get weekly summaries. Her own school loved it, and a school board with eleven primary schools wanted to adopt it. The board's privacy officer sent the model processing agreement, its privacy annex and a list of questions.

TEACHER'S COMMENTS
• The database was outside the EU
• Every teacher could see every pupil in the school
• Parents could open other children's progress pages by changing the address
• Free-text notes contained remarks about dyslexia and home situations
• The AI feedback feature sent pupils' full names and notes to a model provider
• No retention policy, and no way to remove a pupil's data after they left

EXTRA WORK (LaunchStudio, Launch & Grow, 12 business days, €4,600, plus €49/month)
✏️ Moved to an EU region
✏️ Schools separated, with roles for teachers, remedial teachers, parents, pupils and administrators, enforced by Row Level Security and tested per role
✏️ Parents see only their own children, with support for two parents per child
✏️ Free-text notes replaced by structured support categories, plus an optional note for remedial staff only
✏️ The AI feature receives only the reading text and level, no names or notes
✏️ Login through the board's school accounts for staff and pupils
✏️ Data removed a year after a pupil leaves, with export and deletion for schools
✏️ Accessibility checks on key journeys, and technical input for the annex plus a subprocessor list

NEW REPORT
Privacy annex readiness ....................... A

The privacy officer approved Leesladder for all eleven schools. Around 1,400 pupils started after the autumn holiday, and two more school boards used the same documentation in their own reviews.

"I built the app for children who struggle with reading. The privacy officer reminded me that their data deserves the same care. Now it gets it." - Annemiek Boersma
