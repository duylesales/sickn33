**Short answer:** Choose Launch Ready (€800 to €3,500, fixed) if your app mainly needs to be made safe and live, and you will manage it yourself afterwards. Choose Launch & Grow (€2,500 to €7,500 fixed, plus €49 per month) if your app needs structural work, such as multi-tenancy, migrations, enterprise features or AI agents, or if you have paying customers and want someone watching the app after launch. If you are unsure, the intro call will tell you; the quote states which package fits and why.

LaunchStudio offers two packages, and most founders know quickly which one suits them. For those who do not, this page offers a practical way to decide: ten questions, typical scenarios and a look at total cost over a year. It complements the detailed explanations of each package elsewhere on our site.

## Key Takeaways

- **Launch Ready** makes an existing app safe, owned, deployed and monitored, with 48 hours of support.
- **Launch & Grow** adds structural work and ongoing care at €49 per month.
- **The overlap zone** between €2,500 and €3,500 is decided mainly by whether you want ongoing care.
- **You can start with Launch Ready** and move to Launch & Grow later.
- **The quote recommends a package,** with reasons, after a short look at your app.

## Ten Questions to Decide

Answer each question with yes or no.

1. Do you have, or expect within three months, customers who pay you?
2. Would a day of downtime cost you more than a few hundred euros or damage an important relationship?
3. Does your app need organisations, teams or roles that do not exist yet?
4. Do you need to move from one platform to another, such as Firebase to Supabase or off an all-in-one builder?
5. Has a larger customer asked for SSO, audit logs or a security questionnaire?
6. Does your app include AI agents that take actions, or AI features with significant costs?
7. Do you lack a developer or technical person who reads provider emails and applies updates?
8. Do you plan to keep building frequently with your AI tool after launch?
9. Do integrations with accounting, CRM or other systems need to be built or made reliable?
10. Do you handle sensitive data, such as health, financial, children's or HR data?

**Mostly no:** Launch Ready is likely the right fit.
**Yes to questions 3, 4, 5, 6 or 9:** structural work suggests Launch & Grow.
**Yes to questions 1, 2, 7 and 8, but no structural work:** Launch Ready with a possible move to Launch & Grow for ongoing care, or Launch & Grow from the start if the scope reaches its range.
**Yes to question 10:** usually Launch & Grow, because sensitive data typically needs more work and ongoing attention.

## The Overlap Zone

Both packages can cost between €2,500 and €3,500. In that zone, the scope of the project itself is similar; the difference is what happens afterwards. With Launch Ready, you receive 48 hours of post-launch support and then manage the app yourself, with ad-hoc help when needed. With Launch & Grow, ongoing care at €49 per month starts: monitoring with alerts to our team, dependency and security updates, backup checks, platform watch, a monthly health note and small fixes within an allowance.

For many founders, the difference in annual cost between the two paths is small compared with the cost of a single incident: a weekend of downtime, a payment bug that runs for weeks or a missed provider deprecation. Hosting and provider costs are separate in all cases and billed directly to you.

## What Launch Ready Always Includes

Production readiness review, security basics, ownership check, production setup with domain and HTTPS, backups, monitoring, the agreed fixes, a written summary, project instructions for your AI tool and 48 hours of post-launch support. That is enough for many apps to launch and run safely, especially when the founder is comfortable handling routine updates.

## What Launch & Grow Adds

Everything in Launch Ready, plus structural work such as multi-tenancy, migrations, enterprise readiness, AI agent hardening, integration foundations or mobile releases, and the ongoing care service. It is designed for apps that are becoming central to a business and for founders who want a partner who knows the app when the next question arises.

## Switching Later

Starting with Launch Ready does not close the door. Many founders launch with Launch Ready, run the app for a few months, then move to Launch & Grow when revenue grows, a larger customer appears or they get tired of reading provider emails. Because the app was prepared by us, onboarding is short. The reverse also happens: founders on Launch & Grow can stop the monthly service at any time, since everything is in their own accounts.

## When Neither Package Fits

Sometimes neither package is the right answer. A prototype that will be thrown away after user testing may not need production work at all. A large product with a full engineering team may need a dedicated team rather than a project. A product with a hard regulatory requirement may need specialist advisers before any technical work. We tell you honestly in the intro call, and point you to alternatives where they make more sense, including Manifera's dedicated teams for larger roadmaps.

## How the Recommendation Is Made

After the intro call and a short look at your app, the quote names the package and explains why: the scope items, the assumptions and whether ongoing care is recommended. If the scope sits in the overlap zone, the quote may offer both options so you can choose. You are never pushed into ongoing care; it is a recommendation based on your situation.

## Common Misconceptions

**"Launch & Grow is just Launch Ready with a subscription."** Not quite. Launch & Grow projects usually include structural work that Launch Ready does not cover, and the ongoing service is real work every month, not a maintenance fee for nothing.

**"Launch Ready is only for tiny apps."** Many Launch Ready projects involve SaaS products with payments, roles and paying customers. What they do not need is structural change.

**"If I choose Launch Ready, I am on my own afterwards."** You have 48 hours of support, issues caused by our work remain our responsibility and you can request ad-hoc help or move to Launch & Grow whenever you want.

**"The monthly fee will grow over time."** The ongoing service stays at €49 per month. Larger work is quoted separately, at a fixed price, and only when you ask for it.

## Thinking Six Months Ahead

A useful exercise is to picture your app six months from now. Will it have significantly more customers, a larger customer with formal requirements, a new integration or a team instead of individual users? If yes, consider whether doing structural work now, within Launch & Grow, saves you a second project later. If the next six months look similar to today, Launch Ready now and a decision about ongoing care later is often the more sensible order.

## Who Usually Chooses Each Package

Founders who choose Launch Ready are often in an early phase: they have built something that works, want to put it in front of real users safely and prefer to learn how the app behaves before committing to ongoing support. Some have a technical friend, partner or part-time administrator who can handle routine updates. Others run a small, stable tool that rarely changes after launch.

Founders who choose Launch & Grow are usually one step further. Customers depend on the app, revenue is starting or growing, a larger customer has arrived with requirements, or the app has outgrown its first structure. Many of them keep building every week with their AI tool and want a safety net that catches regressions and watches the platforms they depend on. For them, €49 per month is less a cost than a way to stop worrying about things they do not have time to check.

## What Happens if Your Needs Change Mid-Project

Occasionally, the review in week one reveals that the app needs more than expected, for example an organisation model nobody had mentioned, or that it needs less. If the scope grows, we explain the options and costs, and you decide whether to extend the project, move to Launch & Grow or plan the extra work for later. If it turns out smaller, we tell you and discuss how to adjust the scope or quote. Either way, nothing changes without your agreement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Launch Ready and Launch & Grow?

Launch Ready (€800-€3,500) makes an existing app safe, owned, deployed and monitored, with 48 hours of support. Launch & Grow (€2,500-€7,500 plus €49 per month) adds structural work, such as multi-tenancy, migrations or enterprise features, and ongoing care after launch.

### Can I start with Launch Ready and upgrade later?

Yes. Many founders do. Because we already know the app, moving to Launch & Grow later is quick, either for ongoing care alone or with a new structural project.

### Is the €49 per month mandatory?

Only as part of Launch & Grow, and you can stop it at any time without losing access to anything, since all accounts are yours. Launch Ready has no monthly fee.

### How does Manifera's experience help in choosing a package?

Manifera has delivered fixed projects and long-term support for 11+ years across 160+ projects with 120+ engineers, for clients such as Vodafone and TNO. That experience lets LaunchStudio recommend the package that matches the actual risks and plans of your app.

### Do both packages cover SEO and AI visibility basics?

Yes. Both include custom domain, HTTPS, redirects, exclusion of private pages from indexing and fast, stable hosting. Launch & Grow keeps these healthy over time through monitoring and updates, which helps search engines and AI answer engines continue to read your site reliably.
