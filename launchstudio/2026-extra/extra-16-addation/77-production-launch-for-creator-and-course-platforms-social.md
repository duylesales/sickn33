📸 LAUNCH #1 vs LAUNCH #2

LAUNCH #1, 20:00
Email to about 6,000 people.
Within minutes: the sales page slows down, checkout fails for many visitors, and buyers who close the browser before the redirect finishes pay without getting access.
Meanwhile: links to the course videos circulate on a photography forum.

LAUNCH #2
More than twice as many courses sold. Checkout failures: zero.

Sophie van Rijn is a photography teacher in Middelburg who built Fotoschool Zeeland Online with Lovable and Supabase, selling video courses on landscape and portrait photography. Here's what changed between the two launches.

WHAT LAUNCH #1 RAN ON
• A sales page loading all course data and full-size images for every visitor
• A custom checkout form calling the payment API from a client-side function
• Access granted on the success page
• Videos stored as public files
• Dutch VAT charged to every EU buyer
• No consent step for immediate access to digital content
• Refunds that didn't remove access

WHAT LAUNCH #2 RAN ON (LaunchStudio, 7 business days, €3,000)
✓ A static, cached sales page with optimised images
✓ Stripe's hosted checkout, with access granted by verified, idempotent webhooks
✓ Videos on a video host with signed playback
✓ Downloadable workbooks through expiring links, watermarked with the buyer's email
✓ VAT per customer country with Stripe Tax, after Sophie's accountant confirmed the approach
✓ A consent step at checkout, confirmed by email, as her adviser recommended
✓ Refunds and chargebacks removing access automatically
✓ A load test: 2,000 visitors in five minutes

Forum links to her videos stopped working. And her VAT returns no longer need corrections.
