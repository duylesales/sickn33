Launch Ready is LaunchStudio's fixed-price package for founders who built an app with AI tools and want it safe, stable and ready for real customers. The price range is €800 to €3,500, and the question we hear most is simple: what exactly do I get, and why would my app be at the lower or upper end?

This page answers that in detail. It explains what every Launch Ready project includes, what is added as scope grows, how we decide on the fixed price, how long it takes and when the other package, Launch & Grow, is a better fit. The aim is that you can estimate where your app falls before you talk to us.

## Key Takeaways

- **Launch Ready is fixed price:** €800 to €3,500, agreed before work starts.
- **Every project includes a review, fixes, a production setup and 48 hours of post-launch support.**
- **The price depends on scope,** mainly security, payments, data and integrations.
- **Most projects take three to ten business days.**
- **Launch & Grow fits** larger structural work and founders who want ongoing support at €49 per month.

## What Every Launch Ready Project Includes

Regardless of price, every Launch Ready project covers the essentials that make an AI-built app ready for real users:

- **Production readiness review** of the app, its accounts and its configuration
- **Security basics:** database access rules checked, secrets out of the browser, admin access verified
- **Ownership check:** code, hosting, database and domain in accounts you control
- **Production setup:** custom domain, HTTPS and environment variables configured correctly
- **Backups** enabled and verified
- **Monitoring** for uptime and errors, with alerts to you
- **Fixes** for the issues agreed in the quote
- **Handover** with a short written summary and project instructions for your AI tool
- **48 hours of post-launch support** after release

Your interface and features stay the same. Launch Ready is about making what you built work safely in production, not about redesigning it.

## What Determines the Price

Five factors explain most of the difference between an €800 and a €3,500 project:

1. **Data and access complexity.** The number of tables, user roles and organisations determines how much work database policies and access control require.
2. **Payments.** Apps that take money need verified webhooks, correct subscription states and reconciliation, which adds work.
3. **Sensitive data.** Health, financial, children's or HR data needs more careful access control, storage and documentation.
4. **Integrations and AI features.** Each external system or AI feature adds connections, error handling and limits.
5. **State of the code.** Apps with many duplicated components, abandoned features or conflicting configurations need more clean-up before fixes are safe.

The tool you used matters less than people expect. Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor and Claude Code apps each have typical patterns, but the five factors above drive the price.

## Why a Fixed Price?

Hourly billing puts the risk on you: if the work takes longer, you pay more. A fixed price puts it on us, which is only possible because we have done this type of work many times and know the common issues in AI-built apps. It also changes the conversation. Instead of discussing hours, we agree on outcomes: which issues are fixed, what is set up and what you receive.

## What We Need From You

- A 15-minute intro call
- Read access to your code and configuration for the quote
- Access to your accounts during the project, or invitations for our team
- A contact who can answer questions within a day
- Time for a short check on staging before release

## How to Prepare Before the Call

You do not need technical knowledge to prepare. A few things help us quote accurately and quickly: a list of the tools you used, such as Lovable plus Supabase or Cursor plus Vercel; a short description of who uses the app and what they can do; any deadline you have; and any problems you have already noticed, such as slow pages, emails in spam or a test account that could see another user's data. If you have received questions from customers about security or data, bring those too.

## Common Questions Before Signing

Founders often ask whether they can keep building during the project. Usually yes, with a short agreement about which parts of the app are being changed, so work does not collide. They ask whether we will change the look of the app. We will not, unless you ask. And they ask what happens if the review reveals that the app needs a rebuild. In that case, we tell you before starting, explain why and what the alternatives are, and you decide. Most apps do not need a rebuild.

## What the 48 Hours of Support Cover

The support window starts when the app is released to production. During those 48 hours, our team watches monitoring and error tracking closely, responds quickly to anything you report and fixes problems related to the delivered work. We plan releases on working days so the window does not fall over a weekend. Issues caused by our work remain our responsibility after the window as well; the 48 hours describe the period of heightened attention, not the end of accountability. New requests after that are quoted separately or handled through Launch & Grow.

## Comparing Launch Ready With the Alternatives

Founders sometimes compare Launch Ready with hiring a freelancer by the hour or asking a friend who is a developer. Both can work. The differences are predictability and completeness. A freelancer bills hours and usually fixes what you point them to; Launch Ready starts with a structured review of the areas where AI-built apps commonly fail, agrees a fixed scope and ends with documentation and support. A friend may help for free but rarely has time for backups, monitoring and ownership checks. For many founders, a fixed-price package is the simplest way to know the app is ready without managing the work themselves.

## Real example

### An AI-Native Founder in Action: From Preview Link to Paying Pilot in a Week

Esther van den Heuvel runs a speech therapy practice in Tilburg and built Praatmaatje with Lovable and Supabase, an app where children practise speech exercises at home and therapists track their progress. A group of four practices wanted to start a paid pilot, but asked where the children's data was stored and who could see it. Esther had a preview link, a Supabase project in her personal account and no idea how to answer.

The intro call and a short look at the project led to a fixed quote of €2,400. The review found tables where any logged-in therapist could read all children's records, audio recordings in a public bucket, the database in a non-EU region and no backups configured. LaunchStudio moved the project to an EU region in a business account, rewrote the database policies so therapists saw only their own patients and parents only their own children, moved recordings to private storage with signed links, configured the domain and email, enabled backups with a restore test and set up monitoring. A written summary explained the measures in language Esther could share with the practices.

**Result:** The four practices started the pilot one week later with 85 children. During the 48 hours of post-launch support, one issue with audio uploads on older iPads was found and fixed. Esther used the summary to answer the practices' data questions in a single email.
