👩‍💻 A first hire's first six months, on solid ground

BEFORE THE START DATE
Thomas Verhoeven runs Liftlog from Eindhoven, a field inspection SaaS for elevator maintenance companies, built with Cursor and Supabase. 25 paying companies. He hired his first developer: a talented graduate with Next.js side projects. Then an enterprise prospect sent a security questionnaire. Neither Thomas nor his new hire could answer most of it.

THE FOUNDATION (LaunchStudio, Launch & Grow, 12 business days, €4,800)
✓ Organisation-based access policies, with tests
✓ Two authentication approaches consolidated into one
✓ Verified Stripe webhooks
✓ Staging, and a pipeline with preview deployments
✓ Migrations, backups and monitoring
✓ An evidence pack for the questionnaire
✓ Project rules for Cursor and Claude Code, documenting the architecture and conventions

DAY ONE
LaunchStudio walks the new developer through the codebase, the tests and the release process.

WEEK TWO
The developer ships their first customer-facing feature.

MONTHS ONE TO THREE (€49/month, with change reviews)
LaunchStudio reviews the developer's pull requests that touch access control, payments or migrations, explaining the issues instead of just fixing them.

THE ENTERPRISE PROSPECT
Signed, after receiving the questionnaire answers.

MONTH SIX
Production incidents caused by the junior developer's changes: none. Thomas says the reviews are what made the hire work.

"I hired someone to build features, not to discover that our payments double-counted. Fixing the foundation first meant my developer could do the job I hired them for." - Thomas Verhoeven
