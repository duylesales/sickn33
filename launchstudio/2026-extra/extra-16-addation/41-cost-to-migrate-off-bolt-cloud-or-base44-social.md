📦 MOVING DAY: a food bank's volunteer platform gets its own home

WHY MOVE?
Anouk de Graaf coordinates volunteers for a network of food banks in Dordrecht. She built Vrijwilligerspunt on an all-in-one AI platform, where volunteers sign up for shifts and coordinators manage locations. Within a year, around 900 volunteers used it. Then the municipality, which funds part of the network, asked: where is volunteer data stored, who processes it, and can it be exported on request? Anouk also wanted to connect to the municipality's reporting, and let a volunteer developer contribute through GitHub.

WHAT COULD BE PACKED
📦 The frontend code: exportable
📦 The data: exportable through the platform's export features
📦 Password hashes: not exportable
📦 Shift reminders: running as platform automations

THE NEW HOME (LaunchStudio, 13 business days, €4,300, plus €49/month)
✓ Supabase in an EU region, with the frontend on Vercel
✓ Data mapped into tables for volunteers, locations, shifts and sign-ups
✓ Row Level Security for volunteers, coordinators and administrators
✓ Reminders rebuilt as scheduled functions
✓ A reporting export for the municipality

THE KEYS
Since passwords couldn't move, volunteers got a magic link email the day before cutover.

THE MOVE
Sunday morning. Less than an hour.

SETTLING IN
About 85% of volunteers logged in within the first week, without contacting support. Anouk answered the municipality with a one-page overview and a subprocessor list. And the volunteer developer started contributing through pull requests.

"The platform got us started faster than anything else could have. Moving gave us a home we could explain to the municipality, and a codebase other people could help with." - Anouk de Graaf
