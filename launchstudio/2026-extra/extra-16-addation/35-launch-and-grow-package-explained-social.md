📅 A YEAR IN REVIEW: Afspraakjes, from 10 clinics to around 60

THE STARTING POINT
Rik Dekker runs a dental practice management start-up in Arnhem and built Afspraakjes with Lovable and Supabase: booking and reminders for dental clinics. After a Launch Ready project, ten clinics were happy users. Then a regional group of 50 clinics wanted in, with central management, roles per clinic, invoicing per location and an audit trail of appointment changes. Rik had no developer, and spent his evenings on provider emails and testing updates.

THE PROJECT (Launch & Grow, 16 business days, €6,200)
✓ A multi-tenant model with groups, clinics and roles, enforced through Row Level Security and covered by isolation tests
✓ Stripe billing per location, under a group account
✓ An audit log of appointment changes and cancellations
✓ An admin panel, so Rik's support colleague can help clinics without database access
✓ The ten existing clinics migrated without downtime

THE REST OF THE YEAR (€49 a month)
• Dependency updates every week, through staging
• An SMS provider deprecation spotted three months ahead
• A warning that database storage would hit the plan limit within six weeks
• A dozen small fixes
• Two regressions from Rik's own AI-generated changes, caught by tests before reaching any clinic
• Plus a separate fixed-price project for an integration with a dental records system

THE YEAR'S HEADLINES
Around 60 clinics. No outage longer than a few minutes. And Rik's evenings went back to product and sales.

"Launch Ready got me live. Launch & Grow got me a team that knows my app, for less than I spend on coffee for the office." - Rik Dekker
