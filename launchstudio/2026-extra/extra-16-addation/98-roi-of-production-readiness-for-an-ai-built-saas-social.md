🧮 The ROI math of one "the platform works fine" project

THE COST
€3,200, for a Launch Ready project (7 business days)

WHAT IT FOUND
31 members who kept access after failed renewals, some for months
0 dunning: failed payments simply cancelled after the first failure
3 paid courses shared on a forum, because the videos were in public storage
1 database plan close to its limit, because the course catalogue loaded every lesson for every visitor

THE RETURNS
+ 19 of those 31 members updated their payment details and stayed
+ roughly half of failed payments recovered in the following months, thanks to dunning with retries and reminders
+ forum links to the courses stopped working, after videos moved to signed playback
− 1 planned database upgrade, no longer needed after the catalogue query was optimised
+ the time Kees no longer spends on payment questions (not counted)

= recovered and retained revenue covering the project in about five months

Kees Smulders runs Houtwerkplaats Online from Zwolle, a subscription platform for hobby woodworkers built with Lovable and Supabase. Around 700 members pay monthly or yearly through Stripe for video courses and project plans. He hesitated to spend €3,200, because the platform seemed to work.

After the project, access followed verified webhooks, dunning was switched on, videos moved to signed playback, the catalogue query was optimised and monitoring was in place. And Kees tracked the numbers himself.

"I saw the project as a cost. My own numbers showed it was the best-paying investment I made that year." - Kees Smulders
