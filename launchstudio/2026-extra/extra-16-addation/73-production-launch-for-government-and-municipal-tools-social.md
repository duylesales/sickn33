🎮 FROM ONE NEIGHBOURHOOD TO THREE MUNICIPALITIES, in three levels

LEVEL 1: THE PILOT
Ingrid Wolters, a former community worker in Almere, built Buurtmelder with v0, Cursor and Supabase. Residents report issues like broken lighting or illegal dumping, follow up on their reports, and neighbourhood teams coordinate the response. The pilot neighbourhood loved it.
Status: complete ✓

LEVEL 2: THE BOSS FIGHT
The municipality considered a citywide rollout, and sharing it with two neighbouring municipalities. Its security officer and privacy officer sent questions based on the BIO, and asked for an accessibility statement, a processing agreement and the data location. The assessment found:
⚠️ The database outside the EU
⚠️ Residents' names and addresses visible to every neighbourhood team member
⚠️ Photos with location metadata in a public bucket
⚠️ One shared login for municipal staff, and no logging
⚠️ A report form that couldn't be completed with a keyboard, among other accessibility failures
⚠️ Reports never deleted

POWER-UPS (LaunchStudio, Launch & Grow, 16 business days, €6,300, plus €49/month)
✓ Moved to an EU region
✓ Municipalities separated, with roles for residents, neighbourhood teams, coordinators and administrators
✓ Residents' personal details visible only to the team handling their report
✓ Photo metadata stripped, and photos stored privately
✓ Civil servants logging in through the municipality's SSO, with administrative actions logged and backups tested
✓ Every key journey usable with keyboard and screen reader, plus input for the accessibility statement
✓ Configurable retention for closed reports, and a full export
✓ A security overview mapped to the BIO topics the officer raised

LEVEL 3: THE CITY AND BEYOND
Both officers approved the citywide rollout. The two neighbouring municipalities started their own pilots on the same platform, each with separated data. Residents submitted over 2,000 reports in the first three months.

"The pilot proved residents wanted the app. The documentation proved the municipality could trust it. You need both to go from one neighbourhood to a whole city." - Ingrid Wolters
