❌ The database hosted outside the EU
❌ Residents' names and addresses visible to every neighbourhood team member
❌ Photos with location metadata in a public bucket, one shared staff login and no logging
❌ A report form that couldn't be completed with a keyboard, and reports never deleted

✅ An EU region, with municipalities separated and residents' details visible only to the team handling their report
✅ Photo metadata stripped and storage made private, SSO for civil servants, logged admin actions and tested backups
✅ Accessibility fixes for all key journeys and input for the accessibility statement
✅ Configurable retention, a full export and a security overview mapped to the BIO topics raised
