Mark Huisman runs a renovation company in Apeldoorn and built Bouwlog with Bolt and Supabase: his teams and subcontractors log daily progress, take photos, record snags and request extra work, while homeowners follow their project. After one dispute over extra work, and a project where several photos needed for the quality file were simply missing, Mark wanted the app to be dependable before offering it to other contractors.

What the assessment found:
• Every action needed a live connection, so photos taken in basements and new-build shells often failed to upload, sometimes silently
• Retries created duplicates
• Subcontractors could see every project, including other subcontractors' prices
• Drawings uploaded as new files, with no way to tell which was current
• Extra work as free-text messages, "approved" with a thumbs-up
• Earlier entries editable, without any history

What LaunchStudio built (Launch & Grow, 14 business days, €5,400, plus €49/month):
✓ An offline-capable field app that preloads the day's projects and queues photos, checklists and notes, with idempotent sync and a visible pending indicator
✓ Photos compressed and stored privately, with capture times
✓ Projects with participating companies and roles: subcontractors see only their own work, homeowners only their own project
✓ Document versions, with a clear current revision and notifications
✓ Change orders as a structured flow, with description, photos, price and time impact, approved by the client in the app, and locked after approval
✓ A quality file export collecting evidence per building element

No photos lost in the months that followed. The next extra work request was approved and invoiced without discussion. And Mark started offering Bouwlog to three other renovation companies, each with its own separated projects.
