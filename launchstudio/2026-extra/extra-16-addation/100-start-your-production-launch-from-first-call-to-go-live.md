**Short answer:** Starting a production launch with LaunchStudio takes four steps: send a short description of your app, have a 15-minute intro call, give read access for a short look and receive a written fixed quote, usually within one or two business days. After you approve, the project runs for three business days to four weeks, depending on scope, and ends with a launch readiness sign-off, a release on a working day, post-launch support and a handover of everything you need to continue without us. Launch Ready costs €800 to €3,500; Launch & Grow costs €2,500 to €7,500 plus €49 per month.

You built something real with AI tools: an app that customers want, maybe already use. The last step between that app and a business you can grow with confidence is making it production ready: secure, owned by you, reliably deployed, monitored and documented. This page walks through the whole journey from first contact to go-live, with links to detailed guides for each step, so you know exactly what to expect.

## Key Takeaways

- **Starting is easy:** a short description, a 15-minute call and read access are enough for a quote.
- **The quote is fixed,** with scope, assumptions, exclusions and timeline in writing.
- **Your app stays live** during the project; work happens on staging.
- **Launch is a decision based on evidence,** with a sign-off checklist.
- **You end with ownership, documentation and a workflow** that keeps your AI tool productive.

## Step 1: Prepare a Short Description

Before the first call, write a page about your app: what it does, who uses it, which tools and services it relies on, what each type of user may see, whether it takes payments or stores sensitive data, known problems, deadlines and any customer requirements. You do not need technical knowledge; your own words are best. Our guide on [what to send before your first call](https://launchstudio.eu/en/blog/what-to-send-before-your-first-call-about-production) includes a template.

## Step 2: The Intro Call

The intro call takes about 15 minutes. We confirm what you sent, ask a few questions, explain what we would look at and indicate whether Launch Ready or Launch & Grow is likely to fit. If your app does not need us, or needs something we do not do, we say so. You are welcome to ask anything, including who will work on your app and how data is handled.

## Step 3: A Short Look and a Fixed Quote

With read access to your code and configuration, we take a short look at the project. Within one or two business days, you receive a written quote listing the scope items, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, what we need from you, the acceptance process, post-launch support and the fixed price. Our guide on [what a fixed-price quote includes and excludes](https://launchstudio.eu/en/blog/what-a-fixed-price-quote-includes-and-excludes) explains each part.

## Step 4: Start the Project

After you approve the quote, we agree a start date and, if personal data is involved, sign a processing agreement. You invite our team to your accounts with individual roles. If anything sits in someone else's name, such as a database in a former freelancer's account or a domain registered by a past partner, we start moving it into your company's ownership on day one.

## The Project Weeks

| Phase | What happens | Your involvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access and review | Account inventory, staging, review of security, payments, data and operations | Inviting us, answering a few questions |
| Findings call | Plain-language walk-through of findings and priorities | About 30 to 45 minutes |
| Fixes and setup | Fixes in priority order, tests, staging, pipeline, email, backups, monitoring | Reading short updates, occasional decisions |
| Verification | Tests, end-to-end checks and the launch readiness checklist | About an hour checking staging |
| Release | Planned release on a working day, checks in production, rollback ready | Being available |
| Handover | Summary, inventory, procedures and AI tool instructions | About an hour |

Shorter Launch Ready projects compress these phases into three to five business days. Our guide on [what happens in week one, two and three](https://launchstudio.eu/en/blog/what-happens-in-week-one-two-and-three-of-a-production-launch) describes them in detail.

## Sign-Off and Go-Live

Before release, we complete a launch readiness checklist with you: ownership, access control, secrets, authentication, payments, email, data protection, environments, backups, monitoring, performance, accessibility basics, legal pages, support and SEO basics. Each item has evidence. Items that are not ready are either fixed or recorded as accepted risks with a date. The decision to go live is yours, based on facts. See our [launch readiness sign-off checklist](https://launchstudio.eu/en/blog/launch-readiness-sign-off-checklist-before-going-live).

## After Release

Launch & Grow continues with ongoing care at €49 per month: monitoring with alerts to our team, updates, backup checks, platform watch, a monthly health note and small fixes. Our guide on [what happens if something breaks after launch](https://launchstudio.eu/en/blog/what-happens-if-something-breaks-after-launch) explains responsibilities clearly.

**Will my app look different?** No, unless you ask. The work happens underneath the interface.

**Will my app be offline?** No. We work on staging and release at a planned moment.

**Do I need to understand the technical details?** No. Findings, updates and documentation are written in plain language, and decisions are framed in business terms.

**What if you find something unexpected?** We tell you, explain options and costs, and nothing extra is done without your approval.

**Can I stop ongoing care later?** Yes. Everything is in your accounts, so you can stop at any time.

## Real example

### An AI-Native Founder in Action: From First Email to Go-Live in Twelve Days

Mila Hoogland runs a SaaS for flower shops from Aalsmeer, built with Lovable and Supabase, where florists manage orders, deliveries and subscriptions for weekly bouquets paid through Mollie. With about 45 shops using it and a regional florist association interested in recommending it to members, she wanted the app ready before the association's spring meeting, three weeks away.

On a Monday, she sent a one-page description using the template: tools, account owners, roles for shop owners, staff and couriers, Mollie subscriptions, a worry that couriers could see more customer details than needed and the meeting date. The intro call took place on Tuesday, she gave read access the same afternoon and the fixed quote of €2,900 within Launch Ready arrived on Wednesday, with sixteen scope items and a note that the domain transfer from her former web designer should start immediately.

The project started the following Monday. The review confirmed the courier issue and found subscription renewals that kept access after failed payments, emails from the default provider and no staging. Fixes and setup followed in priority order, with updates every other day. On day eight, Mila checked staging for an hour. The sign-off checklist showed one accepted risk, an accessibility issue on a rarely used settings page, scheduled for the following month. The release took place on a Thursday morning, and the handover call followed on Friday.

**Result:** Mila presented a production-ready app at the association's meeting, with a one-page summary of how shops' and customers' data was protected. Twelve new shops signed up in the following month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I start a production launch with LaunchStudio?

Send a short description of your app, book a 15-minute intro call and give read access for a short look. You receive a written fixed quote, usually within one or two business days. After approval, the project starts on an agreed date.

### How long does it take from first call to go-live?

Small Launch Ready projects can go live within one to two weeks of the first call. Larger Launch & Grow projects usually take three to five weeks in total, including quoting. The quote states the timeline for your app.

### What does it cost?

Launch Ready costs €800 to €3,500, fixed, including 48 hours of post-launch support. Launch & Grow costs €2,500 to €7,500, fixed, plus €49 per month for ongoing care. Hosting and provider costs are billed directly by the providers.

### Why does Manifera's experience matter for my launch?

Manifera has delivered 160+ projects over 11+ years with 120+ engineers, for clients such as Vodafone and TNO. That experience makes fixed prices, clear processes and reliable results possible for founders with AI-built apps.

### Does the launch cover SEO and AI visibility basics?

Yes. The production setup and sign-off checklist include custom domain, HTTPS, redirects, page metadata, exclusion of private pages from indexing, a sitemap and fast, stable hosting, so search engines and AI answer engines can read your site properly from launch day.
