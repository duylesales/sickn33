BEFORE ➜ AFTER: a driving school's Sunday evenings 🚗

Bram Visser runs a driving school in Zwolle and built Rijlesplan with Lovable and Supabase, so students can book lessons, pay for packages and track their progress. After a Launch Ready project and its 48 hours of included support, things ran smoothly for two months. Then Bram chose ad-hoc support and kept improving the app himself.

BEFORE: Problems reached students before Bram knew about them.
Over four months: a booking page change that hid evening slots on phones, an expired email setting that stopped lesson reminders, and a failed payment webhook after he changed one package's price.
AFTER: Monitoring and error tracking routed to LaunchStudio, and payment webhooks with alerts.

BEFORE: Changes went straight to students.
AFTER: Every change flows through staging first, with updates applied weekly.

BEFORE: Every fix was quick, but cost students' trust and a few stressful evenings.
AFTER: In six months, two problems were caught on staging, and one alert, a provider outage, was explained to Bram before any student got in touch.

BEFORE: Big changes meant figuring it out alone.
AFTER: For the new exam-preparation packages, Bram sent a short description and got a fixed quote for the parts he didn't want to build himself.

BEFORE: Reading error logs on Sunday evenings.
AFTER: Reading one monthly note.

The switch: a €2,800 Launch & Grow project for staging, monitoring, webhook alerts and handover, then €49 a month. Onboarding took 6 business days. Since then, lesson reminders and payments have run without interruption.

"Ad-hoc help was fine until the app became the way my business runs. Now someone notices problems before my students do." - Bram Visser
