📄 THE ONE-PAGE PROPOSAL that got a board to say yes in one meeting

TO: my co-founder and our two-person advisory board
FROM: Marloes van Dijk
RE: €4,800 on production work

THE QUESTION YOU ASKED
"The customers are happy. Why now?"

WHAT A SHORT REVIEW FOUND
1. Care workers at one organisation could read client notes from another, through a broad policy
2. The database was hosted outside the EU
3. The Supabase project was in my personal account

WHY IT MATTERS NOW
Our largest prospect, a regional care group with 300 clients, sent a security questionnaire. We can't answer it honestly today. That deal is worth about three times the project cost, per year.

THE PROPOSAL
A fixed Launch & Grow quote: €4,800, plus €49/month. Two phases, with data separation and EU hosting first.

WHAT WE GET
Data separation, EU hosting, ownership moved to the company, support for the questionnaire, and ongoing care.

HOW WE'LL MEASURE SUCCESS
Risks closed, the questionnaire answered, no incidents after release.

Marloes is the product-minded co-founder of Thuiszorgrooster, a home care coordination SaaS in Apeldoorn built with Lovable and Supabase, used by 15 small home care organisations. Her co-founder handled the finances. He didn't need to understand database policies. He needed to know what the biggest deal depended on, and what fixing it would cost.

The board approved both phases in one meeting. The project took 13 business days. The care group signed six weeks later, after receiving the questionnaire answers. And at the next board meeting, Marloes reported the closed risks, the signed deal and zero incidents since release.

"My co-founder did not need to understand database policies. He needed to know that our biggest deal depended on them, and what it would cost to fix." - Marloes van Dijk
