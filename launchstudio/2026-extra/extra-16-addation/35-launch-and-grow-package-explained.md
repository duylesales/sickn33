Launch & Grow is LaunchStudio's package for founders whose AI-built app is becoming a real business. It combines two things: a fixed-price project of €2,500 to €7,500 that handles the structural work a growing product needs, and an ongoing service at €49 per month that keeps the app monitored, updated and supported afterwards.

Where Launch Ready gets an app safely into production, Launch & Grow is designed for what comes next: more customers, larger customers, more data, more integrations and a founder who wants someone watching the app while they focus on selling and building. This page explains what the package includes, which projects typically fall within it and how to decide whether it fits.

## Key Takeaways

- **Two parts:** a fixed-price project (€2,500-€7,500) and ongoing care (€49 per month).
- **The project covers structural work,** such as multi-tenancy, migrations, enterprise features or AI agents.
- **The monthly service covers monitoring, updates, backups, platform watch and small fixes.**
- **Everything stays in your accounts,** and you can stop the monthly service at any time.
- **It suits founders with paying customers** and no in-house developer, or a small team that needs backup.

Every Launch & Grow project includes everything in Launch Ready, such as the production readiness review, security basics, ownership check, production setup, backups, monitoring and handover, and adds larger work agreed in the quote. Typical project types:

These are typical ranges. The fixed quote is based on an assessment of your app and agreed before work starts.

## Part Two: Ongoing Care at €49 per Month

After the project, the monthly service begins. It typically includes:

- **Uptime monitoring and error tracking,** with alerts to LaunchStudio and you
- **Alert follow-up** during support hours, with a clear next step
- **Dependency and security updates,** reviewed and released through staging
- **Backup checks** with periodic restore tests
- **Platform watch** for deprecations and changes from providers such as Supabase, Vercel, Stripe and Mollie
- **Usage and cost alerts** before plan limits are reached
- **A monthly health note** with uptime, errors, updates, usage and recommendations
- **Small fixes** handled with priority within an allowance
- **Advice** on providers, features and next steps

Larger work is quoted separately, which keeps the monthly fee low and predictable. Hosting and provider costs stay in your own accounts and are billed by the providers.

## How a Launch & Grow Project Runs

1. **Intro call** about your app, customers, plans and constraints.
2. **Assessment** of the code, data model, accounts and the structural change needed.
3. **Fixed quote** with scope, deliverables, timeline and what is excluded.
4. **Design** of structural changes, such as the tenant model or migration plan, agreed with you.
5. **Implementation** on staging, in short cycles with regular updates.
6. **Testing** including automated tests for access, payments and critical flows.
7. **Cutover or release** in a planned window with rollback.
8. **Handover** of documentation, inventory and AI tool instructions.
9. **Ongoing care** starts, with the first monthly health note after about four weeks.

Most Launch & Grow projects take two to four weeks.

## Why Ongoing Care Matters for AI-Built Apps

AI-built apps change quickly. Founders keep prompting new features, providers update their platforms and dependencies receive security fixes. Each change can introduce a regression that nobody notices until a customer does. Ongoing care turns that risk into routine work: updates are tested, alerts are followed up and the health note shows trends before they become incidents.

There is also a continuity benefit. When something unexpected happens, the team that responds already knows your app, its data model and its history. That makes responses faster and cheaper than starting from scratch with someone new every time.

Because everything stays in your accounts, you can stop the monthly service at any time. We remove our access, hand over notes and make sure monitoring alerts still reach you. Founders who later hire an in-house developer often keep the service for a few months during the transition, then stop or reduce it once the new developer is settled.

## Not Included

Launch & Grow does not include a full-time developer, unlimited changes, 24/7 on-call response or provider costs. Extended coverage for peak periods and development retainers are available separately. Legal advice, certifications and formal penetration tests are outside the package, although we prepare the technical groundwork for them.

## What the Monthly Health Note Tells You

Each month you receive a short note that summarises the state of your app in plain language. It covers availability and any incidents, new or rising errors, updates applied, backup and restore results, usage against plan limits, upcoming provider changes and two or three recommendations marked by urgency. Founders often forward it to co-founders, investors or board members as a simple record that the product is looked after. Over a year, the notes form a factual history of how the app has been run, which is useful during due diligence or when hiring a first engineer.

## How Small Fixes Work

Small fixes are the everyday requests that keep a product polished: a broken link, an email template that needs a new line, a form validation that is too strict, a minor bug reported by a customer. Within the monthly allowance, these are handled with priority, tested on staging and released. If a request turns out to be larger, for example because it touches the data model or payments, we explain why and send a fixed quote before starting. That way, nothing grows into an unexpected bill, and you always know whether a request falls inside or outside the monthly service.

## Working Alongside Your AI Tools

Launch & Grow is built for founders who keep building themselves. The project instructions in your repository tell your AI tool how to handle security, migrations, tenant isolation and data access. Staging and automated tests catch regressions before customers see them. When you want a second pair of eyes on a larger AI-generated change, you can send it for a quick review. The aim is not to replace your own building, but to make it safer and faster.

## Real example

### An AI-Native Founder in Action: From Ten Clinics to Sixty Without a Developer

Rik Dekker runs a dental practice management start-up in Arnhem and built Afspraakjes with Lovable and Supabase, a booking and reminder tool for dental clinics. After a Launch Ready project, ten clinics used it happily. Then a regional group of 50 clinics wanted to join, with central management, roles per clinic, invoicing per location and an audit trail of appointment changes. Rik had no developer and was spending evenings answering provider emails and testing updates.

The Launch & Grow project converted Afspraakjes to a multi-tenant model with groups, clinics and roles, enforced through Row Level Security and covered by isolation tests. Stripe billing moved to per-location subscriptions under a group account. An audit log recorded appointment changes and cancellations, and an admin panel let Rik's support colleague help clinics without database access. The ten existing clinics were migrated without downtime.

After the project, the monthly service took over. Over the following year it applied dependency updates every week through staging, caught an SMS provider deprecation three months ahead, flagged that database storage would hit the plan limit within six weeks and handled a dozen small fixes. Twice, regressions introduced by Rik's own AI-generated changes were caught by tests before reaching clinics. Rik later added a fixed-price project for an integration with a dental records system.

**Result:** Afspraakjes grew from 10 to around 60 clinics in a year with no outage longer than a few minutes. Rik spends his evenings on product and sales instead of provider emails.

The project costs between €2,500 and €7,500, fixed before work starts, and the ongoing service costs €49 per month. Hosting and provider costs are billed directly by the providers. Additional projects later are quoted at a fixed price.
