Wessel de Bruijn runs Vaarplek, a booking platform for boat rentals in Giethoorn, built with Lovable and Supabase. A year earlier, a small agency had "made it production ready." When he wanted to add a second location and change the pricing, he found the "before" column.

He thought he had a partner. He actually had a landlord.

The first step LaunchStudio took wasn't technical. Together with Wessel, they drafted a polite request to the agency to transfer the accounts, repository and domain, pointing to the ownership clause in the original agreement. The agency complied within two weeks.

Then the review found what "production ready" had meant:
• Several database policies disabled to "fix errors"
• Payments relying on the success page
• No backups beyond the default

The fixed quote (6 business days after the transfer, €2,300):
✓ Policies restored, with tests
✓ Verified Mollie webhooks
✓ Backups, with a restore test
✓ Staging and monitoring
✓ Documentation
✓ Everything in Wessel's accounts

Hosting costs went down. The second location launched for the new season. And Wessel made the pricing change himself in Lovable, with the tests confirming nothing broke.

"I thought I had a partner. I actually had a landlord. Now the house is mine." - Wessel de Bruijn
