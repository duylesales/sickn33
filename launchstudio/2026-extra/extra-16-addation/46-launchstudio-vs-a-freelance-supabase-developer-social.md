🚌 What's your app's bus factor?

The bus factor: how many people would have to disappear before nobody knows how your app works.

Jasper Wolters found out his was 1. The freelancer didn't get hit by a bus. They just went on a three-month sabbatical, two weeks before launch. 🐶

Jasper runs a network of dog training schools in Den Haag and built Puppypad with Lovable and Supabase: booking courses, tracking homework exercises and paying through Mollie. A freelance developer had helped him for three months, billed hourly, and Jasper was happy with the work. Six new training locations were about to launch.

What lived in one person's head and accounts:
• The Supabase project
• The Netlify site
• How the payment webhook worked
• All of it undocumented

The freelancer kindly transferred the accounts before leaving. Then LaunchStudio's review found good work, like well-written policies for courses, plus gaps nobody had asked them to look at:
• Homework videos in a public bucket
• No backups beyond the default
• No monitoring
• Webhooks processing retries twice

What LaunchStudio did (5 business days, €2,100):
✓ The freelancer's good parts kept intact
✓ The gaps fixed, webhooks included
✓ Backups and monitoring
✓ The whole setup documented, with project instructions

The six new locations launched on schedule, with around 450 new members. When the freelancer came back, Jasper hired them again for new features, now on a documented foundation, while LaunchStudio handles operations under Launch & Grow at €49 a month.

Bus factor now: more than 1.
