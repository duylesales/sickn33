Both can work well. They differ in how they are priced, what they cover, who is accountable and what happens after the project.

This comparison is written by LaunchStudio, so we have an interest. We have tried to be fair, including the situations where a good freelancer is the better choice.

- **The main differences** are scope coverage, price certainty, continuity and accountability.
- **Freelancer quality varies widely;** check references and ask specific questions.

## When a Freelancer Is the Better Choice

A freelancer can be the right choice when:

- **You know exactly what needs doing,** for example "add a Mollie webhook" or "write policies for these five tables".
- **You want ongoing hands-on development,** several days per week, as part of your team.
- **You have found someone you trust,** with relevant references and a working style that suits you.
- **You have a technical co-founder or adviser** who can review the work.
- **The budget is very small** and the task is narrow.

Many excellent Supabase developers work freelance, and some are specialists who know the platform better than most agencies. If you have one of them, keep them.

## When LaunchStudio Is the Better Choice

LaunchStudio tends to fit better when:

- **You do not know what you do not know.** A structured review covers the common gaps in AI-built apps rather than only the tasks you name.
- **You want price certainty.** Launch Ready costs €800 to €3,500, fixed before work starts.
- **The work spans several areas:** security, payments, email, deployment, monitoring and ownership.
- **You need documentation** you can share with customers, investors or a future developer.
- **You want continuity,** with a team that knows your app if something goes wrong months later.
- **A customer or investor asks** who is responsible for the technical quality.

## Pricing: Hourly Versus Fixed

Freelance rates in the Netherlands vary widely by experience and specialisation. Hourly billing is fair to both sides when scope is uncertain, but it places the risk of overrun on you. A task estimated at two days can take five when unexpected issues appear, and founders without technical background find it hard to judge whether the extra time was necessary.

A fixed price moves that risk to the supplier. It is possible for LaunchStudio because production readiness for AI-built apps follows recognisable patterns, and the quote is based on a short look at your project. If something unexpected changes the scope, we tell you before doing extra work.

Neither model is better in every case. For open-ended feature development, hourly or retainer work often makes sense. For a defined launch, fixed price usually gives founders more control.

## Scope: Tasks Versus Outcomes

A freelancer usually works on tasks: you describe a problem, they fix it. That works well when you know what the problems are. Founders who built with AI tools often do not. They test with one account and do not see that another user's data is visible. They see a checkout working but not that access is granted on the success page. They do not know their Supabase project is in a freelancer's account.

LaunchStudio starts with a review of the areas where AI-built apps most often fail, then agrees the outcome: an app that is secure, owned by you, deployed properly, monitored and documented. Some freelancers work this way too; ask whether yours does.

## Continuity and Availability

The biggest practical risk with a single freelancer is availability. Holidays, illness, a large new client or a career change can leave you without help exactly when your app breaks. Knowledge of your app sits in one person's head unless it is documented. When they leave, the next developer starts from scratch.

LaunchStudio works as a team backed by Manifera, with documentation as part of every project. If one engineer is unavailable, another can pick up the work. That does not make individual freelancers unreliable; it is simply a structural difference to consider, especially once customers depend on the app.

## Quality and How to Judge It

Freelancer quality varies more than agency quality, in both directions. Some freelancers are outstanding; others learned Supabase last month. Founders without technical background find it difficult to tell the difference from a profile or a conversation. Useful questions to ask any candidate, freelancer or agency:

- How do you test that users cannot see each other's data?
- How do you handle payment webhooks, and how do you prevent duplicates?
- Where will the code, database and hosting live, and who will own them?
- How do you handle secrets and environments?
- What do I receive at the end, in writing?
- What happens if something breaks a month after you finish?
- Can I speak to two founders you have worked with?

Clear, specific answers are a good sign. Vague reassurance is not.

## Ownership and Lock-In

With either option, insist that code, database, hosting and domain live in accounts you own. Some freelancers create projects in their own accounts for convenience, which causes problems later. LaunchStudio works in your accounts by default and documents every service in an inventory, so you can switch partners at any time.

## Contracts and Practicalities

Hiring a freelancer in the Netherlands involves a few practical points: a clear assignment agreement, agreements on intellectual property so the code belongs to you, confidentiality for customer data and, where personal data is processed, a processing agreement. Founders also need to consider the rules on self-employment and false self-employment when a freelancer works for a long period in a role similar to an employee; the KVK and the Belastingdienst publish guidance, and an adviser can help. None of this is a reason to avoid freelancers, but it is work to arrange properly.

With LaunchStudio, the quote, terms and processing arrangements are part of the standard engagement. Ownership of code and accounts stays with you, and the scope is written down before work starts.

## Communication and Working Style

Freelancers often offer very direct communication: you talk to the person writing the code, sometimes daily. That can be a big advantage for fast iteration. The trade-off is that communication depends on one person's availability and habits. LaunchStudio uses a fixed contact for each project, short written updates and a shared channel, with engineers joining calls when technical detail is needed. Founders who like to work closely with a developer every day often prefer a freelancer for feature work; founders who prefer to delegate a defined outcome often prefer a service.

## What Founders Tell Us After Using Both

Founders who have worked with both often describe a similar pattern. Freelancers were helpful for building features and solving specific bugs, especially when the founder knew what to ask. The production launch went more smoothly with a structured review, because it surfaced issues nobody had thought to ask about, and because the result was documented. The most satisfied founders used each for what it does best, instead of expecting one option to cover everything.

## A Simple Decision Rule

If you can write down every task that needs doing and someone technical can check the result, a good freelancer is often enough. If you cannot, because you are not sure what is missing, start with a structured review and decide afterwards who does the follow-up work.

## Combining Both

Many founders use both. LaunchStudio handles the production launch: review, security, payments, deployment, monitoring and documentation, at a fixed price. Afterwards, a freelancer builds new features on a clean, documented foundation, with project instructions and tests that make their work faster and safer. Under Launch & Grow, LaunchStudio continues with monitoring and updates while the freelancer focuses on product work. This combination often costs less than either option alone over a year.

Jasper Wolters runs a dog training school network in Den Haag and built Puppypad with Lovable and Supabase, an app for booking courses, tracking homework exercises and paying through Mollie. A freelance developer had helped him fix several issues over three months, billed hourly, and Jasper was happy with the work. Then the freelancer took a three-month sabbatical, two weeks before Jasper's planned launch to six new training locations.

When Jasper contacted LaunchStudio, there was no documentation, the Supabase project and Netlify site were in the freelancer's accounts and nobody else knew how the payment webhook worked. The freelancer kindly transferred the accounts before leaving. LaunchStudio's review found good work in several places, including well-written policies for courses, but also gaps the freelancer had not been asked to look at: homework videos in a public bucket, no backups beyond the default, monitoring missing and webhooks processing retries twice.

LaunchStudio quoted €2,100 for the remaining work, kept the freelancer's good parts intact, fixed the gaps, documented the setup and added project instructions. When the freelancer returned, Jasper hired them again for new features, working on the documented foundation, while LaunchStudio continued monitoring under Launch & Grow.

**Result:** The six new locations launched on schedule with around 450 new members. Jasper now has both a freelancer for features and a team for operations, and neither depends on the other's memory.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a freelancer cheaper than LaunchStudio?

Sometimes, for narrow tasks. For a complete production launch, the total cost is often similar, but LaunchStudio's price is fixed in advance and includes a structured review, documentation and post-launch support. With hourly work, the final cost depends on how long the tasks take.

### Can LaunchStudio work with my existing freelancer?

Yes. We often coordinate with freelancers: LaunchStudio handles production readiness and operations, while the freelancer builds features. Clear documentation and project instructions make that collaboration easier.

### How do I check whether a freelancer is good with Supabase?

Ask how they test data access between users, how they handle webhooks and secrets, where the accounts will live and what you receive at the end. Ask for references from founders with similar apps. Specific, concrete answers are the best signal.

### How does Manifera's backing make a difference?

Manifera has 11+ years of experience, 160+ delivered projects and 120+ engineers, working for clients such as Vodafone and TNO. That gives LaunchStudio team depth, continuity during holidays and illness, and company accountability that a single person cannot offer.

### Does the choice affect my SEO and AI visibility?

Indirectly. Whoever you choose should set up a custom domain, HTTPS, redirects, fast pages and monitoring, which help search engines and AI answer engines. Ask any provider whether these are included, because they are easy to overlook in task-based work.
