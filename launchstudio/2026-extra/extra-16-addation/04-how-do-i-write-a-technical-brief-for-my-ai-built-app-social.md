Three developers, three answers, because nobody had asked them the same question. 📝

Ellen Brouwer built Inspectiepunt with Bolt and Supabase: building inspectors complete checklists, add photos and generate reports for property owners. A housing corporation was ready to pilot it, so she asked three developers to "make it ready for production." Each one guessed what she meant.

So Ellen wrote a three-page brief. Here's its table of contents:

1. Product summary
2. Roles: inspectors, planners, property owners
3. Current state and known issues (including inspectors seeing each other's reports, and photos in a public bucket)
4. Must-haves for the pilot
5. What must not change: the inspector interface
6. Requirements: EU hosting and a processing agreement
7. Integrations
8. Budget range
9. Success criteria you can check
+ A screen recording of the inspection flow, and read access to the repository

With the brief, she asked for new proposals. This time they were comparable, all covering the same scope, and the price range narrowed considerably.

LaunchStudio's fixed-price proposal (10 business days, €3,400) kept the interface and covered:
✓ Row Level Security per role
✓ Private photo storage
✓ EU hosting
✓ Report generation fixes
✓ Deployment and monitoring

The pilot started on schedule. The housing corporation's privacy officer approved the setup from the documentation. And Ellen now updates the brief before every major development phase, and uses it to onboard new developers.
