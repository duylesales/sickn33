❌ A one-line request: "make it production ready"
❌ Known issues not mentioned (inspectors could see each other's reports)
❌ No constraints, no budget range, no success criteria
❌ Nothing said about keeping the interface

✅ A three-page brief: users, roles, current state and known issues
✅ Must-haves vs nice-to-haves, plus "the inspector interface stays"
✅ EU hosting, privacy requirements and a budget range
✅ Checkable success criteria, a screen recording and read access to the repo
