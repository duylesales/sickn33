Jeroen Pauwels runs Dossierkluis from Maastricht, a SaaS for small accounting firms built with Bolt and Supabase, where firms collect documents from clients and share reports. Before launching to twenty firms, he ran every free check he could find. All green. He booked a LaunchStudio intro call mainly to confirm he was ready.

During the call, Jeroen described the roles: firm staff see only their own firm's clients, and clients only their own documents. LaunchStudio suggested a quick test with two firm accounts. Within minutes, a staff member of one firm listed another firm's documents through the API. The policy existed. It just only checked that the user was logged in.

The Launch Ready project that followed (6 business days, €2,500) found more:
• Client documents in a bucket with public read access
• The Supabase project on Jeroen's personal account
• Backups never restored

And fixed it all:
✓ Firm and client policies, with tests
✓ Private storage with signed links
✓ The ownership transfer
✓ A restore test
✓ Staging and monitoring

Jeroen kept every free tool running afterwards. The twenty firms launched a week later than planned, without any client documents exposed. His advice to other founders now: "do the free checks, then pay someone to test the roles."
