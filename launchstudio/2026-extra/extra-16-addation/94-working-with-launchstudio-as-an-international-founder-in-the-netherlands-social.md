Then Dutch activity providers asked to list their courses and take payments through the app. Priya registered with the KVK and wanted to turn a side project into a real Dutch business. 🇳🇱

The review found: 🧠

❌ The database hosted outside the EU
❌ Children's names and ages visible to every provider
❌ Card-only payments through a link, with no webhooks
❌ No company details or legal pages, and English-only emails

What changed, with the whole project run in English: ✅

✅ Moved to an EU region, with providers separated and children's data limited to what each provider needs
✅ Mollie configured with iDEAL and cards, verified webhooks and refunds
✅ Invoices with KVK and VAT details, plus a footer with company information, terms, privacy policy and cookie consent (texts from a Dutch legal service)
✅ Interface and email texts in English and Dutch, translated by a freelancer
