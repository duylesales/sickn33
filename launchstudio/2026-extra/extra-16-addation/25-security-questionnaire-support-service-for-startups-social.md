A national logistics company with around 2,000 employees wanted it for its warehouse staff, and sent a 180-question security questionnaire due in ten days, before its quarterly vendor review.

THE START: about a quarter answerable
Annelies could answer around 45 questions herself.

FACT-FINDING
Already in place: database and storage in an EU region, HTTPS enforced, provider encryption.
Not in place:
• No MFA on admin accounts
• Certificates in a public bucket
• Backups never restored
• The AI quiz generator sending learner names to the model provider
• No written incident procedure, no subprocessor list

THE WORK (LaunchStudio, 7 business days, €2,400)
✓ Draft answers for all 180 questions, with 14 flagged for Annelies to decide
✓ MFA enforced for admins
✓ Certificates moved to private storage, with signed links
✓ A backup restore tested and documented
✓ Learner names removed from AI prompts
✓ A short incident response procedure and a subprocessor list
✓ For penetration testing and certification: honest answers describing the internal review, the fixes made and a dated plan for an independent test
✓ A 45-minute call with the customer's security officer to walk through the architecture
