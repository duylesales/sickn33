🏘️ Niels Schouten runs a letting agency in Utrecht and built Huurloket with Lovable and Supabase: applicants apply for rental homes, landlords review candidates. A larger investor with around 400 homes wanted to switch. Its compliance officer asked how the platform handled applicants' data under the rules on good landlordship and privacy. 📋

The honest answer wasn't good: 🧠

❌ Identity documents, three payslips, an employer statement and bank statements required up front
❌ All stored indefinitely in a public bucket with predictable names
❌ Every landlord able to see every applicant, and rejected applicants never deleted
❌ An AI "suitability score" that landlords used to filter people

✅ Staged applications, with his lawyer's guidance: contact details and income range first, documents only for shortlisted candidates
✅ Income verification and identity checks returning results instead of documents, with stored copies removed
✅ Private storage, automatic deletion for unsuccessful applicants and the backlog cleaned up
✅ Landlords seeing only their own applicants, and the AI score replaced by published criteria, with people deciding
