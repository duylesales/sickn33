📄 RENTAL APPLICATION FORM (before)

To apply, upload:
☑ A copy of your identity document
☑ Three payslips
☑ An employer's statement
☑ Bank statements

Stored: indefinitely, in a public bucket with predictable names
Visible to: every landlord on the platform
If you're rejected: kept anyway
Bonus: an AI "suitability score" landlords use to filter you

Niels Schouten runs a letting agency in Utrecht and built Huurloket with Lovable and Supabase: applicants apply for rental homes, and landlords review candidates. Several small landlords used it. Then an investor with around 400 homes wanted to switch, and its compliance officer asked how the platform handled applicants' data under the rules on good landlordship and privacy.

The form could ask for everything, so it did.

📄 RENTAL APPLICATION FORM (after, with guidance from Niels's lawyer)

Stage 1: contact details and income range
Stage 2, only if shortlisted: the documents actually needed
Income: verified through a service that returns a result, not documents, where the applicant agrees
Identity: checked in a verification flow, with stored copies removed
Stored: private storage, short-lived links
If you're unsuccessful: documents deleted automatically after the agreed period
Visible to: only the landlords and agents for the listings you applied to
AI score: removed. The platform shows whether you meet the landlord's published criteria, and people decide.

The work: LaunchStudio, Launch & Grow, 13 business days, €4,700, plus €49/month, including clean-up of the existing backlog of documents.

The compliance officer approved Huurloket, and the 400 homes moved to the platform. Applicants now get clear information on what they share and when, and the number of documents stored on the platform dropped dramatically.

"We were collecting everything because the form could. Now we ask for what we need, when we need it, and forget it when we should." - Niels Schouten
