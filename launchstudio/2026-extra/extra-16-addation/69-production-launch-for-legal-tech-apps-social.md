⚖️ OBJECTION: an AI assistant in a law firm, on trial

THE PLATFORM
Dossierwijs, built by Eva Molenaar, a former employment lawyer in Den Haag, with Claude Code and Supabase. Small employment law firms manage matters, share documents with clients, and use an AI assistant to summarise files and draft letters. Three firms piloted it.

EXHIBIT A
A draft letter citing a court decision that did not exist. Spotted by an associate.

EXHIBIT B
A secretary in another team able to open documents in a sensitive dismissal case. Questioned by a partner.

FURTHER EVIDENCE
• All users in a firm could access all matters
• Client document links never expired, and were shared by email
• The assistant answered from general model knowledge as well as the file, without showing sources
• Every document in a matter went to the model provider with each question
• No way to switch AI off for a matter
• Closed matters never archived or deleted

THE RULING (LaunchStudio, Launch & Grow, 17 business days, €6,600, plus €49/month)
✓ Matter teams with partner, lawyer and support roles, plus explicit ethical walls, enforced by Row Level Security and tested with walled-off accounts
✓ Client sharing inside the platform, with expiring access and download logging
✓ The assistant rebuilt to answer only from retrieved passages of the matter's own documents, each statement linked to its source
✓ Case law references shown only when linked to a verified source, and drafts marked as requiring review
✓ AI switchable off per matter
✓ Configurable retention that archives and later deletes closed matters, plus a full export

AFTER THE TRIAL
The pilot firms expanded to all their matters. No unsupported citations appeared in drafts in the following months. And Eva used the documentation to win two more firms whose partners had initially refused AI tools.
