A launch-day incident is a communication problem first, an engineering one second: 📞

❌ Reassuring the client before you actually know anything, then being proven wrong minutes later
❌ Going quiet while you wait for a complete picture — silence reads as incompetence
❌ Assigning blame mid-crisis, even implicitly, when the client needs it solved
❌ Over-committing to a fix timeline you're not actually confident in

✅ Acknowledge specifics, get engineering on it in parallel, promise a real update in 15 minutes
✅ Resolve customer-facing fallout — refunds especially — without waiting for the root cause
✅ Send a short debrief within days: what happened, what's fixed, what prevents repeats
✅ Ask the client directly, after the dust settles, how they're feeling about the relationship
