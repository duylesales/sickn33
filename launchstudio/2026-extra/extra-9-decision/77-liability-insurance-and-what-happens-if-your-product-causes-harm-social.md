Insurance gets treated as a binary switch: have it or don't. That's not how policy reads: 🧠

❌ A claim gets denied if the incident traces to a neglected control, like plaintext passwords
❌ EU jurisdictions restrict insuring the fine — defense costs covered, the fine often isn't
❌ Insurance covers future incidents from the start date, not ones you know about
❌ Enterprise procurement asks for a certificate of coverage as standard onboarding

✅ Professional indemnity and cyber insurance are separate policies — evaluate both
✅ For ordinary business data, indemnity coverage often starts around €300-800/year — modest against incident cost
✅ Pre-revenue is fine to defer coverage, as long as the security work isn't deferred too
✅ Documentation habits — a data inventory, an incident write-up — are what make a claim go smoothly
