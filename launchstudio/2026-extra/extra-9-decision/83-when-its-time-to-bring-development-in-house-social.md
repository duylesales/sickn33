The pressure to hire is usually social, not financial: 🧮

❌ Investors ask about team size as a proxy for seriousness, not readiness
❌ A €75,000 salary quietly becomes €110,000–€130,000 with benefits, recruiting, and ramp-up
❌ A salaried hire needs to be kept busy, which invents work during slow quarters
❌ Ending a permanent Dutch contract is far harder than scaling down a partner engagement

✅ Compare trailing twelve months of contract spend against the fully-loaded hire cost
✅ The crossover arrives once work is continuous, not project-shaped, with no natural gaps
✅ A hybrid path works: hire in-house while your existing partner onboards them
✅ Run the numbers on real invoices, not a vague feeling that "we spend a lot"
