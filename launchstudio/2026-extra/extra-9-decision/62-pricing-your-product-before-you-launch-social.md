🚨 Two nights before launch, Femke was choosing flat vs. tiered pricing — only to realize her Lovable app never built the limits her tiers were meant to enforce. 😳

Founders treat pricing as a whiteboard decision handed off as copy. It isn't: 🧠

❌ Tiered pricing needs plan enforcement at the moment of use, not just at checkout
❌ Proration — crediting a mid-cycle upgrade correctly — is what AI checkouts skip
❌ A one-time-purchase system often has no "account" concept, which bites later
❌ VAT-inclusive vs. VAT-added gets decided silently, usually by accident

✅ Settle the shape of pricing — flat, tiered, usage, one-time — before backend work starts
✅ Ask: "if I invoiced tomorrow, what sets the amount?" — the answer reveals what to build
✅ Two tiers with limits checked in code beat three tiers only shown on a page
✅ Write the pricing logic down for whoever builds payments
