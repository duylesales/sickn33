Everyone says have a lawyer check the contract. The real argument lives in the scope: 🧠

❌ "Implement user authentication" names a topic, not a proof anyone can check
❌ Migration gets one sentence with no quantity, though 340 real records exist
❌ Hosting never says whose account it lives in, or who pays monthly
❌ "Post-launch support included" is a mood, not days or a response time

✅ Write who does it, what proves it, what happens if not, in the margin
✅ Turn every deliverable into an "I will be able to" sentence you can click through
✅ Ask for a named account owner for hosting, database and payment provider
✅ Replace weasel verbs — "assist," "as needed," "best effort" — with something gradable
