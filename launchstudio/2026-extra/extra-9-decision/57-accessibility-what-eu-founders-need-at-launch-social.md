🚨 Tab to your own signup button right now. No outline, no highlight, just a cursor jumping somewhere invisible? That detail alone can make a product unusable by keyboard. 😳

Modern, minimal design and accessible design often pull in different directions, and most fixes are cheaper than founders assume: 🧠

❌ Soft gray text on white that fails contrast requirements on nearly every page
❌ A custom dropdown that only opens with a mouse click, never a keyboard
❌ Focus states stripped out by a CSS reset applied purely for aesthetic reasons
❌ Months of user testing that missed it all, because every tester used a mouse

✅ Tab through your product yourself and note every place focus becomes invisible
✅ Run a free checker like WAVE or axe DevTools, then fix what it flags
✅ Add real labels to form fields and real alt text to every meaningful image
✅ Write descriptive link text — "click here" tells a screen reader user nothing
