Any user in the same company could read notes - including HR. Here's what a coaching platform needs for sessions, packages and payments: 🧠

❌ Session notes readable by HR users
❌ Package credits as one counter that went negative on simultaneous bookings
❌ Late cancellations handled manually, and coaches double-booked outside their calendars
❌ Intake answers about stress and burnout emailed as plain text

✅ Notes visible only to the writing coach, with explicitly shared action points for the manager
✅ HR limited to budget and usage reports, notes encrypted and access logged
✅ A credit ledger with reservations, consumption, a 48-hour cancellation rule and expiry
✅ Google and Outlook calendar sync, and consented intakes kept in-platform for coaches only
