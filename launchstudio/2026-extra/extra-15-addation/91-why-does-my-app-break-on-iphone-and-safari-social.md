🚨 Sanne Bakker built Knipklok with Bolt and Supabase: online booking for hair salons, with customers picking a stylist and paying a deposit. Twelve salons signed up, and most bookings came from links in the salons' Instagram profiles. Then bookings dropped. Customers said it "didn't work". 😳

On Sanne's Android phone and laptop, everything worked. On an iPhone, the problems were obvious. Here's why apps break on iPhone and Safari: 🧠

❌ Times sent as 2028-03-12 14:30:00, which Safari couldn't parse - empty slots
❌ The "Confirm booking" button hidden behind the keyboard
❌ Google sign-in failing in Instagram's in-app browser
❌ Deposit receipts blocked as pop-ups

✅ ISO timestamps with time zones and a date library
✅ A booking form with the button above the keyboard, dynamic viewport units and 16px inputs
✅ Guest booking with email and a one-time code, plus "Open in Safari" for Google sign-in
✅ Server-generated receipt links, error monitoring and a real-device test checklist
