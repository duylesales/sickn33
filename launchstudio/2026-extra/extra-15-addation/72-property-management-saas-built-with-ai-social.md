📦 STOCK LIST: what was stored in one tenant portal

☐ Payslips from applicants
☐ Employer statements
☐ Full passport copies
☐ Stored in: a public bucket, with predictable file names
☐ Readable by: thanks to one policy, any logged-in user could read all applications, so applicants could see each other's income
☐ Contractors' view: full tenant profiles with phone numbers and payment status
☐ Rejected applications: never deleted
☐ Rent: tracked in a spreadsheet, exported to the portal by hand

Rachid Amrani manages around 600 rental homes for private owners in Den Haag. He built Huurdersloket with Bolt and Supabase so applicants can apply for homes, tenants can report maintenance issues and owners get monthly reports. It saved his team hours every week, and two other management companies wanted to use it. The review before onboarding them produced the list above.

The clean-up (LaunchStudio, 18 business days, €7,200):
✓ Income details collected only from shortlisted applicants, identity verified without storing full passport copies, and rejected applicants' documents deleted after four weeks
✓ Private storage with signed links
✓ Row Level Security separating management companies, owners, tenancies, applications and work orders, with contractors seeing only work order details and access instructions
✓ Rent by SEPA direct debit through Mollie Connect, each owner's account connected, reminders on failed collections
✓ Service cost statements generated from recorded costs, with an audit trail
✓ Location metadata stripped from photos, and MFA for staff and owners

The result: both management companies onboarded after a privacy review, taking the platform to around 1,900 homes. Arrears dropped noticeably. And the deletion policy for applications became a selling point with owners.

"I built the portal to save my team time. I hadn't realised I'd also built a warehouse of other people's payslips that anyone could walk into." - Rachid Amrani
