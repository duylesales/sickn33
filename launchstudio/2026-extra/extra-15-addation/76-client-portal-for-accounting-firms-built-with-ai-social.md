That's what an approval looked like in Aangifteportaal before the fix. A household shared one login, so one partner could approve a joint return without the other knowing, and the record didn't show which version was approved.

Marloes de Haan, a former tax assistant, built Aangifteportaal with Lovable and Supabase for a mid-sized accounting firm in Amersfoort with around 1,500 private and 300 business clients. Clients upload documents per tax year, staff track progress, and clients approve their returns online. After one tax season, three more firms wanted it. Their reviews also found:
• Documents with predictable paths, readable by any logged-in user who knew the path
• Identity documents with unmasked BSNs, kept indefinitely alongside ordinary documents
• Email subjects naming documents, such as "Payslip December 2027"
• No MFA for staff

The new form (LaunchStudio, 18 business days, €7,300):
Approved by: ☑ every required approver, each with their own login
Authenticated with MFA: ☑ yes
Version approved: ☑ a specific document version, recorded in an audit log

And behind it:
✓ Personal and shared document areas per household member
✓ Storage access based on client relationship and staff assignment, with signed links
✓ Requests as templates with items, statuses and automatic reminders
✓ Wwft onboarding as a separate flow with its own retention, and guidance to mask BSN and photo where not needed
✓ Neutral notification emails linking to the portal
✓ Mandatory MFA for staff, strongly encouraged for clients

The three firms onboarded before the next season. Across four firms, around 4,800 returns were approved, each linked to an authenticated approver and a document version. Staff made far fewer chasing calls.
