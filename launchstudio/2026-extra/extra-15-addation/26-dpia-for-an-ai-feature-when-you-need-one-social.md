Vulnerable clients, income and health data, and new technology: several high-risk criteria at once. And technically, the assistant could see far too much. Here's when an AI feature needs a DPIA: 🧠

❌ AI retrieval across the whole organisation, not the counsellor's caseload
❌ Prompts sent to a US endpoint without a processing agreement
❌ Full prompts and answers stored in logs indefinitely
❌ AI-drafted letters that could be sent without review

✅ Permission-aware retrieval enforced by Row Level Security
✅ An EU AI endpoint with no training use, and prompts stripped of unneeded identifiers
✅ Metadata-only logs with 30-day retention
✅ Mandatory counsellor review with AI labelling, and a DPIA input pack
