🚨 Thijs van der Meer runs Buurtboer, a grocery delivery service around Apeldoorn connecting local farms with households. Its webshop was built in Lovable. Then a customer who is blind emailed: she'd given up halfway through checkout because her screen reader couldn't find the delivery slot picker. 😳

With 14 employees selling to consumers, Buurtboer falls under the European Accessibility Act. And the audit showed the problem wasn't isolated. Here's what the EAA means for SaaS and webshops: 🧠

❌ A slot picker made of clickable divs with no keyboard support
❌ Icon buttons without accessible names, fields relying on placeholders
❌ Errors shown only as red borders, and a brand green failing contrast
❌ A cart drawer trapping keyboard focus, and no accessibility statement

✅ The slot picker rebuilt as an accessible radio group
✅ Visible labels and text error messages linked to every field
✅ A compliant brand green, focus management and screen reader announcements
✅ Automated checks in the pipeline and a published accessibility statement
