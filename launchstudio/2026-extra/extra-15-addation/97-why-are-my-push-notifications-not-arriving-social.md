🚨 Lars Visser built Afhaalklaar with Lovable and Supabase: a PWA for bakeries and delis where customers order ahead and get a notification when it's ready. Eleven shops used it, selling it as "no more queues". But customers still waited at the counter, saying they never got the notification. 😳

On Lars's Android phone, it worked perfectly. For most of his customers' customers, it didn't exist. Here's why push notifications don't arrive: 🧠

❌ iPhone users (~60%) ordering in Safari without adding the app to the home screen
❌ One subscription field per user, so only the last-used device got notified
❌ VAPID keys regenerated by a prompt, silently breaking older subscriptions
❌ A later prompt replacing the service worker without a push handler

✅ Stable VAPID keys in server secrets and a push handler in a Workbox service worker
✅ One subscription row per device, refreshed on open and removed on 404/410
✅ Every send logged with its result
✅ Push or SMS chosen at checkout, with an install guide and SMS default for iPhone users
