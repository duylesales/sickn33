💡 Stand on the boulevard in Vlissingen and container ships pass close to shore on their way to Antwerp — through one of Europe's most demanding shipping routes, with strong currents and shifting sandbanks.

⚓ Damen Naval builds complex naval vessels here, HZ University of Applied Sciences and the Maritime Institute De Ruyter train engineers and officers, and North Sea Port links Vlissingen with Terneuzen and Ghent.

🛰️ Data work spans model-based systems engineering and simulation for shipbuilding, onboard condition monitoring, vessel traffic prediction from AIS and radar via the Scheldt Radar Chain, pilotage planning and hydrography.

✅ Most maritime data work happens on land — in shipyards, traffic centres and port offices — but understanding how ships and crews operate is what makes it useful.
