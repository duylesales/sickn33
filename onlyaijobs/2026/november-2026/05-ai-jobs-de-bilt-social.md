💡 Every Dutch forecast, heat warning and climate scenario traces back to one place on the eastern edge of Utrecht: De Bilt, home of KNMI.

🌧️ Weather is having a machine learning moment. Data-driven models trained on decades of reanalysis now rival physics-based forecasts for many variables, and ECMWF runs one alongside its traditional system. That raises hard questions: how reliable are they for rare extremes, and how do you communicate uncertainty in a warning?

🔬 Work here spans nowcasting, climate downscaling for the KNMI'23 scenarios, seismology, observation quality and — via RIVM in Bilthoven — public health surveillance and air quality.

✅ Operational, not only academic: warnings go out on time. Salary scales are published under the central government agreement.
