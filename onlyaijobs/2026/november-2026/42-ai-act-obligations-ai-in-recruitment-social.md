💡 Recruitment is one of the most common places where organisations use AI without calling it AI: systems that rank CVs, recommend candidates, screen assessments or answer questions.

📜 Under the EU AI Act, AI used to place targeted job ads, filter applications or evaluate candidates is high-risk. AI that infers emotions in the workplace is prohibited, which makes video interview features analysing facial expressions or voice highly problematic. AI literacy obligations already apply.

🛠️ Buying a tool does not transfer responsibility. As a deployer you need competent human oversight, monitoring, log-keeping, information to candidates and workers' representatives — alongside GDPR rules on automated decisions and DPIAs, and works council consent where relevant.

✅ Start with an inventory: which tools rank, filter or evaluate candidates, including features hidden inside larger platforms?
