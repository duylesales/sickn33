💡 For every AI role you fill, dozens of candidates hear "no" — or hear nothing. In a community this small, that is part of your employer brand.

🕒 Silence damages more than rejection. State timelines in the vacancy, reject early-stage candidates promptly instead of keeping them as backup, and inform finalists within days of the decision.

📝 Give feedback proportional to effort. After a take-home assignment, fifteen minutes produces one or two strengths, the main gaps and how close the candidate was. Base it on criteria defined before the process, avoid anything touching protected characteristics, and keep notes consistent — candidates can request access under the GDPR.

✅ Ask strong runners-up whether they want to stay in a talent pool, with consent and a clear retention period. They are often your next hire.
