💡 Halfway between Rotterdam and Antwerp lies an industrial area most travellers only notice from the A16 bridge. Moerdijk combines a large chemical complex, a waste-to-energy plant, a seaport and a logistics park.

🛡️ Here, data work is safety work. Alarm floods during a process upset can hide the root cause; multivariate monitoring can detect a developing leak days before a conventional alarm; inspection and maintenance data decide when equipment is opened. The 2011 Chemie-Pack fire still shapes how the region thinks about risk.

📜 Seveso III and Brzo 2015, the Environment and Planning Act, EU ETS, NIS2 and REACH set the boundaries.

✅ Models here start as decision support and earn their way towards automation. Engineers who respect that get adopted.
