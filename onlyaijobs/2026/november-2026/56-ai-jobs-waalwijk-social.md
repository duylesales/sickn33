💡 A century ago, the Langstraat was the centre of the Dutch shoe and leather industry. Drive along the A59 today and you pass distribution centres instead.

📦 Waalwijk became one of the country's busiest logistics locations. The data work is operational and measurable: warehouse slotting and pick paths, labour planning for Black Friday peaks, returns prediction in fashion and footwear, transport routing and emissions reporting under the CSRD and ISO 14083.

🎢 A few kilometres away, one of Europe's largest theme parks turns visitor flows, queue prediction, pricing and staff planning into its own analytics challenge.

📜 Analytics involving warehouse workers touches GDPR, the Works Councils Act and the AI Act's rules on worker-related AI.

✅ No train station, but Tilburg and Den Bosch are a short drive away.
