💡 Boxmeer is a small town on the Meuse, part of Land van Cuijk. It is also the headquarters location of an international animal breeding company whose genetics are used worldwide.

🧬 Modern breeding is a data discipline. Hendrix Genetics works across layers, turkeys, pigs and aquaculture, and genomic selection means estimating an animal's potential from DNA long before performance is observed — with mixed models, genomic prediction and increasingly automated phenotyping through cameras and sensors.

📌 Genetic diversity is a quantitative problem too: selecting too narrowly raises inbreeding, so optimal contribution selection and simulation matter as much as prediction accuracy.

📜 The EU Animal Breeding Regulation, the Animal Health Law and welfare expectations frame the work.

✅ Boxmeer sits on the Maaslijn, about half an hour from Nijmegen.
