💡 Bergen op Zoom is known for its historic centre and its carnival. It also hosts one of the largest engineering plastics production sites in Europe, established by General Electric in the early 1970s and operated by SABIC since 2007.

🧪 The materials made there end up in cars, electronics, medical devices and construction. Data work covers polymer process optimisation, predicting product properties from process data before laboratory results arrive, materials informatics that cuts the number of formulation experiments, asset reliability and sustainability reporting.

♻️ Circular plastics make it harder in an interesting way: recycled feedstocks vary far more than virgin material, so process control and quality prediction must adapt.

📜 REACH, packaging and waste rules, Brzo 2015, EU ETS and the CSRD shape the work.

✅ Ten minutes from Roosendaal by train, close to Breda and Antwerp.
