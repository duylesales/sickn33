💡 From the air, Westland looks like a sea of glass. Behind it sits a technology sector that exports climate computers, breeding material and greenhouse concepts worldwide.

🌱 The AI work is specific: climate control that optimises temperature, CO2 and light for yield and energy; crop monitoring with computer vision; harvest robots that must find fruit behind leaves; and plant breeding that combines genomic prediction with automated phenotyping. The World Horti Center, Priva, Rijk Zwaan, Dümmen Orange, Royal FloraHolland Naaldwijk and WUR greenhouse research anchor the cluster.

👥 Labour shortages are the strongest driver of automation, which gives most projects a very concrete business case.

✅ No train station, but Westland borders The Hague and is twenty to thirty minutes from Delft and Rotterdam.
