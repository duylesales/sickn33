💡 A manufacturer with eighty employees has years of production data and concludes: "We need a data scientist." Sometimes that is right. Often it is premature.

📋 Check readiness first: a concrete business problem, accessible data, an internal sponsor who will use results, time from domain experts, budget beyond salary and patience for six to twelve months.

🧭 The best first hire is frequently not a data scientist. If reporting is unreliable and data is scattered, a versatile analyst or analytics engineer builds the foundation that makes modelling possible later.

💰 Budget realistically: employment costs on top of salary, tools and cloud, data extraction from existing software, training and an external mentor — plus management time.

✅ Student projects, interim professionals and SME digitalisation support are cheaper ways to test feasibility first.
