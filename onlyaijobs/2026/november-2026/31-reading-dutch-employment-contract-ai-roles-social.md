💡 After weeks of interviews and a salary negotiation, the contract arrives — and most people sign quickly, relieved the process is over. A few clauses deserve more attention than that.

📜 Check the probation period against legal limits: none for contracts of six months or less, one month up to two years, two months above. Check the non-compete clause — in a fixed-term contract it is only valid with written justification based on substantial business interests. Check whether the IP clause covers work unrelated to your job, and list existing side projects explicitly.

💰 Check whether the salary includes the 8% holiday allowance, what the pension scheme offers and which collective agreement applies.

✅ Training your employer must legally provide has to be free, and side-job bans need objective justification.
