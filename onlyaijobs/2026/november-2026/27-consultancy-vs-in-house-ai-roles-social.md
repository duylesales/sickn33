💡 A large share of Dutch AI vacancies is posted not by the organisation with the problem, but by a consultancy or secondment firm. Many professionals end up choosing that route by accident rather than deliberately.

⚖️ Three models work differently. Project consultancy gives exposure across sectors and structured training. Secondment places you inside a client team for months or years. In-house work gives depth, ownership and the lessons that only appear after deployment — drift, adoption, maintenance.

📜 Check the contract before signing: client non-solicitation clauses can block a direct move to the client where you worked, and Waadi rules apply to staff made available to others.

✅ Consultants learn to deliver; in-house professionals learn what happens after delivery. Strong careers usually contain both.
