💡 When a Dutch timetable is built, parcels are routed overnight or nurses are rostered, the decisive technology is usually not a neural network. It is mathematical optimisation.

🚆 The Netherlands has an unusually strong operations research tradition: NS is internationally known for applying OR to timetabling, rolling stock and crew planning, ProRail plans capacity on a very busy network, PostNL runs national parcel logistics and ORTEC has built optimisation software since the early 1980s.

📜 Regulation becomes constraints — EU driving time rules, working hours law, capacity allocation rules.

✅ The skill is choosing the approach: exact methods when decisions must be defensible, heuristics at scale, machine learning for forecasting inputs. And plans that planners actually accept.
