💡 For years, Dutch AI vacancies said "competitive salary" and left the rest to negotiation. The EU Pay Transparency Directive ends that practice.

📜 Candidates must receive the initial pay or its range before the interview, pay history questions are prohibited, and pay structures must rest on objective, gender-neutral criteria. Employers from 100 staff will report on gender pay gaps, and an unexplained gap of 5% in a category triggers a joint pay assessment.

💰 AI roles make this harder than average: market premiums, inconsistent titles and differences created by individual negotiation.

✅ Before publishing a range, compare it with what current employees in comparable roles earn. Many employers discover unexplained differences at exactly that moment — better then than in a reporting cycle.
