💡 The Netherlands has one of the highest shares of part-time work in Europe. Yet most data science vacancies still say 40 hours, and candidates hesitate to ask for 32.

🏠 In practice, part-time AI work is increasingly normal, especially in the public sector, healthcare, research and education. The Flexible Working Act lets employees request fewer hours, and employers may refuse only on substantial business grounds. The Equal Treatment (Working Hours) Act prohibits treating part-timers unfavourably without justification.
