💡 The best language models are trained mostly on English. Dutch municipalities write letters in Dutch, courts publish rulings in Dutch and insurers handle claims in Dutch. That gap is a job market.

🗣️ Legal, medical, tax and government Dutch differ sharply from everyday text. Compound words break tokenisation and search. Formal and informal register matters in citizen communication. And almost every benchmark you can find is English, so Dutch evaluation sets usually have to be built.

🏛️ GPT-NL, developed by TNO, SURF and the Netherlands Forensic Institute, reflects the interest in Dutch models for sensitive public applications.

✅ Most of this work is titled data scientist, AI specialist or legal knowledge engineer — or posted in Dutch as "taaltechnologie".
