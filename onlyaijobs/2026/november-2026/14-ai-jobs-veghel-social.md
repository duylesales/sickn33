💡 If you have checked in a suitcase at a major airport or received a parcel from a large webshop, it may have travelled over equipment designed in Veghel.

🤖 Vanderlande, headquartered here and part of Toyota Industries, builds automated material handling for airports, warehouses and parcel centres. Jumbo's head office and a large Mars factory sit in the same town. Work spans robotic picking, simulation and digital twins, throughput optimisation, predictive maintenance, grocery replenishment and food process control.

📜 The Machinery Regulation, the AI Act's rules on worker-related AI and the Works Councils Act shape analytics that touch employees.

✅ Robotic picking is the hard part: soft bags, shiny packaging, millions of picks, and reliability that must match human workers.
