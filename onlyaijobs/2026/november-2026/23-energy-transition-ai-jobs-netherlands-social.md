💡 In large parts of the Netherlands, companies wanting a heavier grid connection are put on a waiting list. Solar parks sometimes cannot feed in everything they produce. Grid congestion has become a national economic problem.

⚡ Building cables takes years, so operators must use existing capacity more intelligently. That is a data problem: probabilistic forecasting of load, solar and wind; estimating loads on low-voltage grids from smart meter data; optimising flexibility from batteries, EVs and industry; and asset and grid planning.

🏢 TenneT, Alliander, Stedin, Enexis, GOPACS, ElaadNL, suppliers, traders and the ACM shape the landscape.
