💡 Many international tech companies dropped the cover letter. Dutch public organisations, universities, hospitals and smaller companies did not — and there, letters are often read carefully.

✍️ A good motivation letter does what a CV cannot: it connects your experience to this role and this organisation. Answer three questions — why this organisation, why this role, why you — with one concrete example each. One page, three to five paragraphs, no "Met veel enthousiasme solliciteer ik".

🏛️ For government roles, address the vacancy's requirements in order; selection committees score against them. For research groups, show you read their recent work.

✅ Use AI tools for structure and language, not for content. Recruiters recognise generic generated phrasing quickly, and every claim must survive an interview.
