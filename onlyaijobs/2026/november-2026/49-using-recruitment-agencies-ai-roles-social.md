💡 When an AI vacancy has been open for months, an agency promising a shortlist in weeks is tempting. Sometimes that is the right call — and sometimes it means paying a fee for candidates who were already applying directly.

⚖️ Agencies add most value for senior, rare, urgent or confidential searches, where passive candidates must be approached personally. For junior and medior roles with a realistic pool, direct publication in specialist and regional channels usually reaches the same people at a fraction of the cost.

📜 Check the contract: fee basis, guarantee period, exclusivity, candidate ownership, double submissions and data protection. For secondment, verify Waadi registration in the KVK register and follow the new admission system for labour providers.

✅ Pay transparency rules apply to agencies recruiting on your behalf too — including the ban on pay history questions.
