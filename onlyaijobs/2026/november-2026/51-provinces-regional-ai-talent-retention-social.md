💡 OnlyAIJobs exists because of one observation: many job seekers from Noord-Brabant leave for the Randstad not because they want to, but because they do not know which interesting companies sit near their home.

🏛️ That applies far beyond Brabant. Provinces fund campuses, clusters and knowledge institutions, then watch graduates leave — while regional manufacturers, agrifood companies, grid operators and hospitals struggle to hire data professionals.

🔍 Part of the gap is information. SME vacancies lose the visibility contest on generalist boards, job titles avoid AI vocabulary, and city-based searches miss employers in smaller municipalities and business parks.

🚲 Housing and transport belong in talent policy too: a role is not realistic if the business park cannot be reached without a car.

✅ Labour markets cross provincial borders; cooperation beats competing with the neighbouring region.
