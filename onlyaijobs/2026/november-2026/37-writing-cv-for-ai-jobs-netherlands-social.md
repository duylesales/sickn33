💡 A hiring manager for a junior data role may receive hundreds of applications, most read for under a minute. Your CV is not a record of everything you did — it is a short argument for a conversation.

📄 Dutch conventions: one to two pages, reverse chronological, photo optional, no date of birth, no full address and never your BSN. Use CEFR levels for languages, group skills without star ratings, and keep the layout simple so screening systems can read it.

📌 Replace tasks with results. "Worked on machine learning models" says nothing; "built a model predicting delivery delays that reduced manual planning checks in a pilot region" says a lot.

✅ Link to a repository where your two best projects are easy to find — hiring managers do click. Under the AI Act, recruitment AI is high-risk, and GDPR rights apply to your application data.
