💡 A rose cut in Kenya in the morning can be sold in Aalsmeer the next day and stand in a German supermarket shortly after. Behind that sits one of the most data-intensive trading systems in agriculture.

💰 Pricing is brutal: flowers lose value by the hour, Valentine's Day and Mother's Day dominate demand, and supply depends on weather in producing countries.
