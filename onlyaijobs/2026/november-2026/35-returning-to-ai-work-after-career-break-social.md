💡 Two years away from AI work can feel like falling off a moving train. In practice, the fundamentals changed far less than the headlines suggest.

🧭 Tools, libraries and model releases move fast, and generative AI changed how many teams build. Statistics, evaluation, data quality, experimental design and explaining uncertainty did not. If anything, they matter more now that impressive-looking systems are easy to build.

🎓 A realistic refresh takes weeks to a few months: map what changed in your specialism from current vacancies, update your toolchain, learn the basics of LLM applications and the AI Act, then build one small, well-documented project.

✅ One line on your CV about the break is enough. Dutch law also protects you: equal treatment rules, the Work and Care Act, the Flexible Working Act and the Gatekeeper Improvement Act for returns after illness.
