💡 Debate about AI talent in the Netherlands usually focuses on research universities. The practical adoption of AI in regional manufacturers, logistics firms, hospitals and municipalities depends largely on universities of applied sciences.

🎓 They have campuses across the country, educate many of the future data professionals, and their graduates often stay in the region. Lectorates conduct practice-based research directly with SMEs, supported by SIA and its RAAK programmes, with Centres of Expertise and professional doctorate pilots alongside.

👥 Employers ask for a consistent profile: practical data skills, domain understanding, communication and critical thinking about when a model is unreliable or unnecessary.

📌 The gap is visibility. Many regional SMEs never offer internships because they do not know how, and students search where large employers dominate.
