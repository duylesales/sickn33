💡 Thirty applications, silence from half, fifteen rejections and two interviews that went to someone else. It is easy to conclude the market is closed. Usually the conclusion is wrong.

📊 Track where rejections happen, not how many. Rejections after applying point to targeting or your CV. Rejections after interviews point to how you explain projects. Rejections after assessments point to specific skills — baselines, validation, code clarity. Rejections after final rounds often point to nothing you can change.

✉️ Ask for feedback briefly and specifically within a few days, with one or two questions. Under the GDPR you can also request access to your personal data, which may include assessment notes.

✅ And review your target list: regional manufacturers, grid operators, hospitals, municipalities and SMEs hiring their first data professional face far less competition than Amsterdam scale-ups.
