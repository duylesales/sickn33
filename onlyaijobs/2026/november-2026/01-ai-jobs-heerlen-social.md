💡 Ask where Dutch official statistics are produced and most people say The Hague. Part of the answer is Heerlen — along with the administration of millions of pension entitlements.

🏛️ Statistics Netherlands has a large office here, APG runs pension administration and asset management, and the Brightlands Smart Services Campus concentrates data work with the Open Universiteit, Zuyd and Maastricht University nearby. The work is record linkage, data quality, methodology and actuarial analytics — under the Statistics Netherlands Act, GDPR and DNB supervision.

📜 The Future Pensions Act transition adds one of the largest data migration projects in Dutch finance, with a legal deadline in 2028.

✅ Titles rarely say "AI": methodologist, data quality specialist, actuarial analyst. Search by category, and treat Heerlen, Maastricht and Aachen as one market.
