💡 Behind almost every modern scientific result is software — often written by a PhD candidate who moved on, leaving code nobody can maintain. The research software engineer exists to fix that.

🔬 In the Netherlands the role has become a recognised career: the Netherlands eScience Center employs RSEs across disciplines, NL-RSE connects the community, SURF provides national computing, and universities have digital competence centres. Work includes turning prototypes into tested packages, scaling analyses to HPC, building ML training pipelines and making results reproducible.

🎓 Recognition is improving through the national Recognition and Rewards programme and clearer job profiles under the universities' collective agreement.

✅ Vacancies are often titled scientific programmer, research engineer or data engineer — searching only "research software engineer" misses most of them.
