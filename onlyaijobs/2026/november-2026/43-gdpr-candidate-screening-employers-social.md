💡 One AI vacancy can generate hundreds of CVs, assessment results, interview notes and emails. All of it is personal data — and candidates who work with data every day notice how you handle theirs.

📜 The Dutch Data Protection Authority indicates deleting application data no later than four weeks after the procedure, or longer with the candidate's consent. Do not ask for the BSN during selection, avoid health questions, and keep online screening relevant, proportionate and transparent.

🔒 Practical risks are mundane: CVs forwarded by email, shared spreadsheets with informal comments, and notes nobody expected a candidate to request access to.

✅ Write interview notes as if the candidate might read them — because they can ask to. And check transfer mechanisms and processor agreements for non-EU recruitment tools.
