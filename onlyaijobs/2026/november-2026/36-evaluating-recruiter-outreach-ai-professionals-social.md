💡 "Exciting opportunity for a Senior AI Engineer." "I came across your impressive profile." After a few years of experience, these arrive weekly — and most professionals respond by ignoring everything.

🔍 A quick filter helps. Worth answering: the message names the company or sector, the team and problem, the salary range, location and contract type, and shows someone read your profile. Noise: generic superlatives, no company name even after you ask, pressure to send your CV immediately, or "competitive salary" as the only pay information.

⚠️ Scam signals are different: requests for payment, identity documents or bank details before any contract, interviews only via chat apps and companies you cannot find in the KVK register.

✅ Never pay a recruiter in the Netherlands — employers pay the fees. And always agree which company gets your CV before it is sent.
