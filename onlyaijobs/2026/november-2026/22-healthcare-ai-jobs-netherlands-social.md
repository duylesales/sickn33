💡 Most people picture healthcare AI as models detecting disease in scans. That work exists, but it is a small part of the picture.

🏥 Dutch healthcare faces staff shortages and administrative burden. The urgent questions are about capacity: predicting discharges to free beds, planning operating rooms, reducing documentation time and identifying patients who could be treated at home. Employers include university medical centres, general hospitals, health insurers, Health-RI, Nictiz, medtech companies and public health institutes.

📜 GDPR special category data, the WGBO, the WMO, MDR, the AI Act, the European Health Data Space and NEN 7510 decide what is possible.

✅ Getting data access and aligning definitions usually takes longer than modelling. Clinical collaboration is the real skill.
