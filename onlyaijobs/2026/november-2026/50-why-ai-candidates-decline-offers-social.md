💡 Six weeks of interviews, five interviewers, a technical assessment — and the preferred candidate says no. The runner-up has already accepted elsewhere.

📊 The reasons are usually predictable: pay revealed only at the offer stage, a process slower than competing employers, a counteroffer, office requirements that appeared late, data maturity that looked weaker in interviews than in the vacancy, restrictive contract clauses, or a commute that becomes real once the location sinks in.

🕒 Speed and transparency fix most of it. Publish the salary range, complete interviews within a few weeks, decide within days of the final round and state hybrid and part-time policies upfront.

✅ Ask motivations early. If someone is leaving for development or impact, a salary counteroffer from their employer will not decide it — but your silence between acceptance and start date might.
