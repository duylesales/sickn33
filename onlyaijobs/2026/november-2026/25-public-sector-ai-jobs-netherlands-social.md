💡 For many people in the Netherlands, "algorithm" became associated with injustice after the childcare benefits scandal. That history shapes every public sector data job today.

🏛️ The framework is concrete: the Algorithm Register, the implementation framework for responsible algorithm use, human rights impact assessments, the Dutch Data Protection Authority's coordinating role and Court of Audit reviews. Under the AI Act, AI evaluating eligibility for benefits is high-risk, and public deployers must carry out a fundamental rights impact assessment.

📌 The work is broader than risk models: infrastructure maintenance, water management, official statistics, service improvement, geospatial data and policy analysis.

✅ Sometimes the best outcome is a model that is not deployed. Candidates need to be comfortable with that.
