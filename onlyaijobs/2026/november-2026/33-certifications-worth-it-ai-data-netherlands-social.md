💡 Search "how to become a data scientist" and you will be offered dozens of certificates. Some open doors in the Dutch market; others consume months of evenings for very little.

🎓 Most hiring managers value demonstrated skills more than credentials. The exceptions are real though: consultancies and IT service providers need certified staff for client work and partner status, public tenders sometimes require them, governance and privacy roles value recognised credentials, and career switchers can use them to show commitment.

📌 Cloud and data platform certifications help most for data engineering and MLOps roles — provided they match the platforms your target employers actually use. ISO/IEC 42001 training and AI governance credentials are rising with the AI Act.

✅ Check ten vacancies for your target role. If a certification never appears, its value is limited.
