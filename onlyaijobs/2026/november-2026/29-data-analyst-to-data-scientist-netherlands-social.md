💡 A remarkable number of Dutch data scientists started as analysts. Most of them overestimated how much deep learning they needed, and underestimated the gaps that actually held them back.

📊 Analysts already have what is hardest to teach: finding and cleaning real data, knowing which questions matter and explaining results to people who decide things. The real gaps are statistical reasoning, honest model evaluation — baselines, validation, data leakage — and programming with reproducible code.

🎓 Funding exists: employer development budgets, collective agreement training rights, sector O&O funds, and the AI Act's AI literacy obligation gives employers an extra reason to invest.

✅ The most effective route is usually internal: one predictive project, with data and stakeholders you already understand.
