💡 Drachten has no railway station, and most people in the Randstad could not place it on a map. Millions of people use products designed and made there.

🏭 Philips has developed and manufactured personal care products in Drachten for decades, on a site known for high automation. Because labour costs made automation necessary, the region built deep experience in smart manufacturing long before the term existed.

👁️ Data work: visual inspection where defects are rare, so anomaly detection beats classification; process control within micrometres; predictive maintenance; and analytics on connected products through apps.

📜 The Data Act, the Machinery Regulation, revised product liability rules and Ecodesign requirements shape how product data is handled.

✅ About half an hour from Groningen; express buses serve Groningen and Leeuwarden.
