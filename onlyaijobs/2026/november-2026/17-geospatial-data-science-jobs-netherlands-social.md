💡 Few countries are mapped as precisely as the Netherlands. Every building and parcel is registered, and the whole country has been laser-scanned into a detailed elevation model.

🗺️ That is not a curiosity in a low-lying country. Dike safety, spatial planning, energy siting, climate adaptation and agricultural policy all run on spatial data — through Kadaster, PDOK, AHN, the BGT, Rijkswaterstaat, water boards and standards from Geonovum.

🛰️ Work spans object detection in aerial imagery, point cloud classification, subsidence monitoring with satellite radar, 3D city models and spatial prediction — under INSPIRE, the Open Data Directive and the Environment and Planning Act.
