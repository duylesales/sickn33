💡 Hire AI expertise, or develop the people who already know your business? For most Dutch employers the honest answer is both — but deliberately, not by default.

🧭 Separate three levels. AI literacy for everyone using AI tools, now also an obligation under the AI Act. AI-enabled roles for analysts, domain experts and product owners. AI specialists for building and maintaining systems, where senior and narrow expertise usually has to be hired.

🎓 Analysts, BI developers, software engineers and motivated domain experts are the strongest upskilling candidates. What makes programmes work is not courses but protected time, mentoring and real projects — plus a defined role to grow into.

💰 SLIM, sector training funds and collective agreement budgets can fund it. Mandatory training must be free of charge.

✅ One experienced hire mentoring several upskilled employees often beats trying to recruit a whole team.
