💡 Data engineers move data. Analysts answer questions. Between them sits the gap where most organisations lose trust in their numbers — and that is where the analytics engineer works.

🔧 The job: transforming raw data into clean, tested, documented models; defining metrics once, clearly; writing tests that catch quality problems before dashboards do; and applying software engineering practices to analytics code. Typically SQL and dbt on Snowflake, Databricks or BigQuery.

🤖 It matters more with AI. If "revenue" means three different things in three tables, a language model answering questions about your data cannot resolve that — it will answer confidently and wrongly.

✅ Dutch vacancies often call this BI developer, data modeller or data warehouse specialist. Read the responsibilities, not the title.
