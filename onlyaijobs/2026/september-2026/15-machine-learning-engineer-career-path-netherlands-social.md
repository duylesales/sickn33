A "Senior ML Engineer" title at a 5-person startup and the same title at an enterprise can describe genuinely different jobs. Title inflation is real, and the title alone is a weaker signal than most candidates treat it as.

What actually separates levels: scope of ownership (executing a defined task vs. deciding what the task should be), production accountability (is a model's failure your problem at 2am), and breadth vs. depth (do-everything at a small company vs. specialize at a large one).

Ask about these three things in an interview. They tell you more than the word before "Engineer."
