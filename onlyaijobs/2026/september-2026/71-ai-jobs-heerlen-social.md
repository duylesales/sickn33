⛏️ Heerlen's data economy exists because of a decision, not organic growth. The coal mines closed, national government functions were relocated south, and later a campus was built specifically for data-driven services. Almost every distinctive feature follows from that.

📊 The most underrated data employer in the Netherlands is here: official statistics. Deriving publishable figures from administrative registers that were never designed for measurement. Formal disclosure control — cell suppression, perturbation, differential privacy — where the accuracy-versus-protection trade-off must be defensible because the numbers feed law. And system-wide consistency: a model that improves one estimate but breaks its consistency with another is not an improvement.

💰 Second anchor most candidates never consider: pension administration. Longevity modelling where small systematic errors compound across millions of participants and decades. Entity resolution on employment records spanning multiple administrative regimes — with legal consequence for individual entitlements.

🇩🇪 The standout geographic fact: Aachen is 30–40 minutes away, with a leading technical university and a large industrial base. No interior Dutch city of this size offers that.
