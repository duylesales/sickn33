💡 Delft has about 100,000 residents — and within a few square kilometres: a major technical university, several national research institutes, and one of Europe's most established deep-tech incubators. That density changes what an AI job search looks like here.

🔍 Job titles systematically undersell the work. A "software engineer" posting at a Delft robotics spinout may require state estimation, control theory and sensor fusion — skills an Amsterdam ad would advertise far more loudly. Filtering by title misleads you in both directions.

🤝 Most spinout hiring resolves before a public posting exists. The founder messages their old research group, mentions it at an incubator event, and has a mental shortlist within two weeks. Not gatekeeping — for an eleven-person company, a bad technical hire is existential.

🚆 The quiet advantage: The Hague in 12 minutes, Rotterdam in 15, Leiden in 25, Amsterdam in under an hour. You can change the character of your career without changing your address.
