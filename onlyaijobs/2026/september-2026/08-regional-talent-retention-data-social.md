A regional development office and a job board have almost nothing in common operationally. But both are trying to answer the same question from opposite ends: is the work that would keep someone here actually visible to them?

OnlyAIJobs exists because graduates from Noord-Brabant often move to the Randstad not because better roles exist there, but because they don't know which companies near them are hiring. That's a visibility problem, not a labor-market statistic — and visibility is a much cheaper thing to fix than attracting entirely new employers.

For universities, business associations and municipalities already working on talent retention: pointing graduates toward a visible, address-level list of current local AI/ML/Data vacancies is a low-effort addition to work you're already doing.
