A generalist recruiter screening for a backend role can reasonably judge years of experience and tech overlap. Screening an AI candidate the same way misses most of what separates a strong hire from a weak one.

Tool familiarity isn't the same as judgment — two candidates can list the same frameworks while differing enormously in whether they'd choose a simpler statistical method over a complex model. "Years of experience" compresses very different kinds of experience. And a recruiter without technical background usually can't tell sound code from a copied tutorial.

Involve a technical colleague early, even briefly. It catches what a keyword scan misses.
