A job ad for an AI role in the Netherlands will tell you the tech stack, maybe the salary range. It won't tell you how tight the housing market is near the office, or whether sponsorship at this specific employer is routine or a first-time exception.

Ask directly: is visa sponsorship a tested process here, or are they navigating it for the first time? What does the realistic commute radius around the office actually contain — sometimes a comfortable, affordable place to live is in a smaller town, not the city on the job ad. Does the 30% ruling apply to your specific contract, or are you assuming?

Do this groundwork before accepting, not after.
