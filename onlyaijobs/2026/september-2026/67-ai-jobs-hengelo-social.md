📡 Hengelo holds the most significant naval radar and sensor engineering operation in the Netherlands. If signal processing and sensor fusion interest you, this is one of the few places in the country where that work exists at scale.

⚠️ But get the framing right. This is signal processing first, machine learning second. Turning up proposing to replace a Kalman filter with a neural network — without being able to explain what the filter assumes and when those assumptions fail — signals inexperience rather than innovation. Learned methods get added to a mature theoretical foundation, not substituted for it.

🔐 The clearance question, answered properly: screening depends on verifiable residence history as much as nationality, takes months, and is role-specific rather than company-wide. Export control is a separate restriction again. Ask in the first conversation — recruiters here answer this routinely and it saves everyone weeks.

🏭 The underrated part: the regional machine-building layer needs no clearance at all. Machine vision for quality inspection with real-time constraints and near-zero tolerance for false negatives on safety-critical parts. Many of these firms have no data specialist — you'd define what the capability means there.

🎓 Prepare differently. Expect probability, estimation and uncertainty propagation, not coding puzzles and recent architectures.
