For a genuinely narrow AI specialization, no single sourcing channel reaches the whole realistic candidate pool — because that pool is itself scattered: some browse job boards, some rely on academic networks, some aren't actively looking at all.

A specialist job board reaches active seekers cheaply. Direct outreach reaches strong passive candidates the other channels can't touch. Academic/professional network visibility matters especially for research-adjacent roles. None replaces the other two.

For a narrow role, budget effort across more than one channel rather than optimizing a single one further.
