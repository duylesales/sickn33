📦 Logistics data work has a reputation problem among engineers — it sounds like dashboards. The substantive version is neither. Vehicle routing with time windows, capacity limits, driver-hours regulations and dynamic order arrival remains genuinely hard; real instances are too large for exact methods. It's closer to operations research than to standard ML.

🍅 What makes Venlo distinctive is what it moves. Fresh produce degrades, and that single fact propagates through everything. Quality stops being binary and becomes a continuous, temperature-dependent state. Cold chain sensor data becomes an input to routing and allocation decisions rather than a compliance record. Shelf-life prediction with expensive, late-arriving ground truth becomes a core problem.

📈 A structural opportunity worth knowing: data science training has concentrated on statistical learning while operations research is taught far less than a generation ago — even though demand never fell. The optimisation side is a much less crowded specialisation. And an integer programming formulation learned today stays valid indefinitely, which isn't true of every skill in this field.

🇩🇪 The underrated feature: Düsseldorf is an hour away, the Ruhr region within ninety minutes. For a German-speaking candidate that roughly doubles the accessible market.

⚠️ Don't try to replace the human planners. Their overrides encode real constraints the software doesn't model, and that knowledge is undocumented.
