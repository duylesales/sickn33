🌱 Search "machine learning jobs Netherlands" and you will almost never surface Wageningen. Not because the technical work isn't there — because it's titled "researcher plant phenotyping" or "data specialist horticulture" instead.

🔬 The work is harder than the framing suggests. Hyperspectral and multispectral imagery where pretrained models transfer badly. Satellite, drone and ground sensors reconciled across different resolutions. And datasets so small and expensive that a field trial gives you one harvest per season — you can't collect more when a model underperforms, you wait a year.

📊 That inverts a core assumption of commercial ML. With 300 labelled examples and a result a domain scientist must defend in peer review, you're forced into careful experimental design and real uncertainty quantification. Harder skills than scaling a training run — and they transfer to healthcare and industrial work.

⚠️ One organisation dominates employment here. That makes internal mobility, grant funding cycles and fixed-term contract norms far more important to understand than in a market of many employers.
