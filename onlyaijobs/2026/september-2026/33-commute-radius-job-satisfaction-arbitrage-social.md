Two offers with the same salary and similar teams aren't actually the same offer if one adds 90 minutes to your day that the other doesn't. That difference rarely shows up in an offer-comparison spreadsheet.

Salary is concrete at the moment of decision. Commute cost is diffuse — it shows up as fatigue six months in, not as a number you see on the day you accept. An extra hour of daily commute is roughly 230 hours a year: nearly six working weeks spent traveling instead of doing anything else.

A city name on a job listing hides this. An exact address doesn't.
