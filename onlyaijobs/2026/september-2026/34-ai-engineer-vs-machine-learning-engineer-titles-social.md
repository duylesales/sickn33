The rise of large language models gave companies a trendier title to reach for — "AI Engineer" — for roles that a few years earlier would simply have been called "Machine Learning Engineer." The two titles overlap far more than they diverge.

Where a real distinction sometimes shows up: AI Engineer leaning toward integrating existing models (API calls, prompt engineering, agent workflows), ML Engineer leaning toward training and fine-tuning custom ones. Far from universal though.

Don't filter out a posting by title. Ask directly: does this build custom models, integrate existing ones, or both?
