A take-home, a live coding round, a system-design interview, a case study, a culture-fit conversation — each stage looks reasonable in isolation. The problem is cumulative: a strong candidate evaluating three offers in parallel will often withdraw from the longest process, not because they weren't interested, but because someone else moved faster.

What actually drives drop-off: elapsed calendar time more than number of stages, redundant assessment of the same skill, and silence between rounds — read as low priority regardless of your actual internal urgency.

Audit each stage for what it tests. Consolidate overlap. Communicate even with no news.
