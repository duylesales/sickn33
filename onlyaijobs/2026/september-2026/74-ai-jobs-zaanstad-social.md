🏭 Zaanstad is one of the oldest industrialised areas in Europe, and food processing has been its defining industry for centuries. It's also twelve to seventeen minutes from central Amsterdam — shorter than many journeys within Amsterdam itself.

🌾 Food processing data is genuinely its own domain. A chemical plant receives feedstock with a specification; a cocoa processor receives beans whose composition varies by origin, harvest and storage. Settings optimal last week may not be optimal now, and the variation isn't fully observable in advance. Quality isn't one number — it's composition, texture, colour, flavour and stability, some measured in a lab with delay, some assessed by trained human panels.

⚠️ The thing that catches experienced data scientists out: continuous processes break the discrete-record assumption. Material flows. Product leaving a line at 10am is a mixture of material that entered at various earlier times, through vessels with residence time distributions rather than fixed delays. Time alignment becomes a modelling problem in itself — and autocorrelation means random train-test splits leak information and produce accuracy estimates that collapse in production.
