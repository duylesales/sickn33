Every team with a model in production eventually needs someone whose job is keeping it running. Almost every team calls that job something different.

MLOps Engineer, ML Platform Engineer, DevOps Engineer (AI focus), sometimes just Data Engineer with an added remit. The function is consistent — deployment, retraining pipelines, monitoring for silent model drift. The title reflects which department originated the hiring need, not a standard job architecture.
