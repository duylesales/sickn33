🛒 If you're trying to enter machine learning work in the Netherlands and every sector feels closed, this is where to look. Retail and e-commerce has more junior positions than any other sector — and four structural reasons why.

Unrestricted data: no ethical review, no clearance, no patient confidentiality, no export control. You're productive in week one because you can see the data. Well-understood problems with extensive public literature, so you're not required to invent a method. Teams large enough to include and actually develop juniors.

And the one that matters most: you can build a relevant portfolio before being hired. Public retail and recommendation datasets exist. Unlike healthcare or defence, you can demonstrate ability on realistic problems without institutional access.

📊 The work is more substantial than its reputation. Forecasting hundreds of thousands of sparse, intermittent product-location series — with promotions, seasonality, weather and cannibalisation between products — is genuinely hard, and the loss function matters more than the model. Marketing measurement is statistically the most fraught area in commercial analytics, because naive attribution systematically misleads and experimentation is the only reliable answer.

🎯 What actually distinguishes candidates: correct evaluation. Most get three things wrong — random splits on time series data, offline metrics that don't reflect online behaviour, and measures that ignore cost asymmetry. Avoid those and you stand out immediately. Understanding experimentation properly is worth more here than knowing another model family.

⚖️ The honest cost of entering here: differentiation. Almost every data scientist has done forecasting and recommendation. Three years produces a competent but common profile, where three years of semiconductor metrology or clinical validation produces something scarcer.

So use it deliberately rather than by default — enter, build real production experience, then decide whether to deepen within retail or move that experience somewhere it becomes rarer.

💡 Practical tip: target mid-sized retailers and wholesalers, not the largest marketplaces. Real problems, smaller applicant pools, often better mentoring.
