A job posting is written before you ever speak to anyone at the company — one of the few honest, unfiltered artifacts in a hiring process. Read carefully, it reveals a lot about how prepared a team actually is.

Does it describe a specific model in production with a known limitation, or only ambitions ("leverage AI to transform our business") with no concrete current state? Is the requirements list oddly specific — sometimes a sign of a team overcorrecting after a past mismatch? Is there any technical detail at all, or only business language?

Read it twice: once for requirements, once for what it reveals.
