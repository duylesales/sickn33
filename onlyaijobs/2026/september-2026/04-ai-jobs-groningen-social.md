Every "best cities for AI talent in the Netherlands" list runs the same four names in the same order. Groningen is never one of them.

That's not a verdict on the work — it's a side effect of how those lists get built, usually from employer-brand spend that concentrates in the Randstad by default. Groningen has a research university that's produced AI and data science graduates for decades, plus a region managing one of the country's more consequential energy-transition data problems. Neither shows up in a marketing budget.

Fewer listings doesn't mean fewer opportunities relative to you — it can mean less competition per role.
