## Regulation That Shapes Insurance Data Work

Risk and fraud work at insurers falls under a specific supervisory framework. The European **Solvency II** regulation requires insurers to substantiate risks quantitatively, with an actuarial function and well-controlled models; De Nederlandsche Bank provides prudential supervision and the AFM supervises conduct towards customers. In addition, the **EU Artificial Intelligence Act** classifies AI systems for risk assessment and pricing in life and health insurance as high-risk. For a data scientist, that means documentation, validation, explainability and attention to possible discrimination are part of the job, not an afterthought.

Employers in Amersfoort therefore recruit from the same graduates as Utrecht companies — with the advantage that some of those graduates prefer to live and work in a quieter city. For candidates, this means you'll find colleagues in Amersfoort with the same educational background as in Utrecht, without the bustle of the bigger city.

## Living and Working in the Same City

The housing markets in Utrecht and Amsterdam are among the tightest in the Netherlands. Amersfoort is popular too, but for anyone who finds a job in the city itself, the daily commute disappears entirely. That makes the difference between "living in Amersfoort and working in Utrecht" and "living and working in Amersfoort" bigger than it seems: not just travel time, but also travel costs, flexibility around childcare and the option of cycling to the office. Factor those in explicitly when you set a local vacancy against a role in a bigger city.
