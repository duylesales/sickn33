Ede sits immediately next to Wageningen, home to one of the world's most respected agricultural and food-science research clusters. That shapes the region's AI-adjacent job market specifically: yield prediction, food-safety modeling, agricultural supply-chain optimization.

Job titles here are shaped by agricultural and food-science departments, not tech recruiters — expect "Data Analist Voedselveiligheid" or "Onderzoeker Precisielandbouw," not anything containing the word AI. A candidate searching standard tech titles will miss this segment entirely, despite genuine statistical and ML work underneath.

If applied data science with real physical-process constraints interests you, this corridor is worth a direct look.
