Brainport Eindhoven gets cited constantly as a model for regional tech development — and just as often, regions elsewhere try to replicate it by copying the visible parts (branding, cluster marketing) while the parts that took decades to build go unaddressed.

What doesn't transfer: a multi-decade industrial history and anchor institution. What does: the visibility principle at a smaller scale — local opportunity existing but not being visible to local talent — and coordinated relationships between education, employers and local government.

A more useful question than "how do we become the next Brainport": what employers already exist here doing AI-adjacent work, and how visible is that to local talent?
