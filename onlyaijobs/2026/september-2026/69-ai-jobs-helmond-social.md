🚗 Helmond holds the Netherlands' automotive campus plus a stretch of instrumented public motorway for connected and automated driving trials. For perception and sensor fusion work, it's the most concentrated automotive AI location in the country.

⚠️ But automotive changes the practice more than candidates expect. A perception system contributing to vehicle control must be argued safe, not merely measured accurate — and that argument is a formal document that constrains design from the start. A model that scores better on a benchmark but whose failure modes aren't describable may be unusable regardless of its numbers.

📉 Aggregate accuracy is close to irrelevant. The engineering effort concentrates on the long tail: unusual lighting, partial occlusion, atypical road users, degraded sensors. Many roles here spend more effort on validation infrastructure than on modelling.

🔓 The overlooked entry route: simulation and data infrastructure. Terabytes per vehicle per day to ingest, index so specific scenarios are retrievable, and replay deterministically against new software. Needs software and data engineering strength more than domain knowledge — and it's frequently the bottleneck, so it leads naturally into perception.

🚆 What de-risks this market: Eindhoven is 12 minutes away. You're effectively inside Brainport, so your fallback is a deep regional cluster rather than a handful of local employers.
