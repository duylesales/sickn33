🌊 This one starts with the constraint, because it determines everything else: Zeeland is the most geographically isolated part of the Netherlands. Rotterdam is ~90 minutes from Middelburg, Amsterdam over two hours, and no large city sits within a normal commute.

That makes it different in kind from other regional markets. Leeuwarden has Groningen at 40 minutes. Assen at 25. Hoorn has Amsterdam at 35. Middelburg has nothing comparable — if a role ends, your options are another provincial employer, remote work, or leaving Zeeland.

🏗️ What's genuinely here: the most concentrated flood defence system in the world. Storm surge barriers where the decision problem is driven by asymmetry — closing unnecessarily disrupts shipping and ecology, failing to close when needed is catastrophic. Structural monitoring on assets designed to last generations, with sparse failure data and enormous consequence. Morphological modelling of a delta whose channels physically move.

💨 And offshore wind, which is the growing domain. The maintenance problem is the interesting one: offshore turbines need a vessel, suitable weather and a crew to reach. Weather windows are limited. Predicting which turbine needs attention and scheduling visits to use those windows efficiently has large cost consequences.

🇧🇪 Underrated: Ghent is 60–75 minutes and Antwerp 75–90. Antwerp's port and chemical cluster is a substantial market — closer than Rotterdam in practical terms.

📍 Honest recommendation: this province suits engineers committed to water or offshore wind, and people with personal reasons to be here. For everyone else, equivalent work exists in Delft, Dordrecht or Rotterdam with far better fallback markets.
