"Remote-friendly" on a Dutch job posting can mean fully distributed, or two mandatory office days dressed up in flexible language. The label alone tells you very little.

This matters more for AI/ML work specifically: a lot of useful work happens in unplanned, adjacent conversations about data quality and edge cases. Teams that handle this well have built deliberate structures to replace that. Teams that haven't often quietly default to "whoever's in the office gets heard first" — without anyone intending it.

Ask: how are technical decisions documented? What's the real in-office-to-remote ratio today?
