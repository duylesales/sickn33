Haarlem sits close enough to Amsterdam that most residents default to commuting in for work, including for AI roles — a habit reinforced by how job search itself works, since a national search naturally surfaces the larger Amsterdam market first.

What actually sits locally: a Schiphol-adjacent logistics position plus regional service and consumer-goods companies, producing modest but real demand with correspondingly less competition per listing.

A candidate who genuinely compares a local role against an equivalent Amsterdam commute sometimes finds the local option is the better trade.
