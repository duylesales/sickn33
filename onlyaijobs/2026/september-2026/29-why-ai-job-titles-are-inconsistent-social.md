Ask five Dutch companies to define "AI Engineer" and you'll get five different answers. Not sloppiness — a predictable side effect of a field that professionalized faster than its job titles could standardize.

AI Engineer vs. Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist vs. Applied Scientist, ML Engineer vs. MLOps Engineer — the overlaps cause real confusion, and plenty of postings use the terms interchangeably in the same job description.

Don't filter out a posting because of its title. Read the first three bullet points of responsibilities before deciding whether it matches.
