A job posting titled "Data Scientist" that spends three of five bullets on pipeline maintenance isn't unusual in the Dutch market. It's a data engineering role wearing a data science title — usually because a smaller company hasn't split the two functions apart yet.

Data engineering builds the systems that make data usable. Data science uses that data to answer questions and build models. At a smaller company, one person often does both, and the title reflects whichever half the hiring manager thought of first.

Ask directly: what percentage of this role is pipeline work vs. modeling work today, and how is that expected to shift?
