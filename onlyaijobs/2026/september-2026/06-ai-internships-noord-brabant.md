If you study AI, data science or machine learning in Brabant, you have probably thought at some point: "for a good internship, I'll have to go to Amsterdam or Utrecht." The thought is understandable — and it is usually wrong. It also costs you something concrete: travel time, rent, or a student room you did not really want.

This is not a call to "be proud of your region." It is a simple observation about how vacancies become visible, and why that visibility is systematically skewed towards the Randstad — even when the best opportunity for you is simply closer by.

## Why It Looks as if Brabant Has Nothing to Offer

Job sites mainly show you what other people are also searching for, and most search volume for "AI internship" comes from the cities with the largest student populations. Companies that do recruit in Brabant often have no separate budget for Randstad-style employer branding — they post one vacancy in one place, and it disappears in the results beneath companies that do advertise.

The result: you mostly see vacancies from the companies that shout loudest, not from the companies with the best work. That is a difference you can easily miss as a starter, because you do not yet have a frame of reference to weigh what is on offer.

## What Kind of AI Work Actually Happens in Brabant

**High-tech and industrial AI.** The region around Eindhoven has a strong high-tech and manufacturing industry, where machine learning is increasingly used for quality control, predictive maintenance and process optimisation. This is less visible than a consumer app, but often just as technically demanding.

**Logistics and e-commerce data.** Brabant has a large logistics and e-commerce cluster, with companies dealing daily with inventory forecasting, route planning and customer data at scale.

**Companies you don't know yet.** OnlyAIJobs currently lists, among others, Boltrics, 4Dotnet, Holland Innovative and Eminent Groep — names you won't find on a list of "best AI employers in the Netherlands," but which genuinely fill AI and data roles.

## Searching for an Internship: Two Strategies Compared

| | Searching by name ("well-known companies") | Searching by distance from home |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Companies you already know from the media or LinkedIn | Every company within a radius that works for you |
| Competition per vacancy | High — everyone knows the same ten names | Lower — fewer people search locally |
| Travel time | Often ignored until the first working day | Visible upfront, because that is exactly what you filter on |
| Chance of an unknown but strong company | Low | Higher |
| Compromise needed? | Often — moving, long commute | Not necessarily |

Neither strategy is wrong. But if you only use the left column, you never consciously choose the right one — you simply never saw it.

## Why "Closer" Doesn't Have to Be a Compromise

The idea that the best internship is by definition far away does not come from data — it comes from the visibility of vacancies. An internship ten minutes from home is not automatically less valuable than one in Amsterdam; it has simply appeared on your feed less often, because the company had no marketing budget to get there.

That is exactly why OnlyAIJobs shows every vacancy with an exact address and the distance from your home, instead of just a city name: so that a strong internship in Tilburg, Breda or Den Bosch is as visible as one in the Randstad, instead of disappearing beneath companies with larger advertising budgets.

## Brabant's Knowledge Institutions as the Starting Point of Your Search

Brabant has one of the densest networks of educational institutions in the Netherlands that train people for AI and data work. That matters, because many internships run through these institutions and their existing company contacts.

**Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e).** Programmes in computer science, data science and electrical engineering, and the Eindhoven Artificial Intelligence Systems Institute (EAISI), which combines AI research with high-tech systems, mobility, energy and health. Graduation projects are often carried out at companies in the Brainport region.

**Tilburg University.** Strong in data science, econometrics and cognitive science & artificial intelligence. Tilburg graduates often end up in data and analytics roles at business service providers, logistics companies and government organisations.

**Jheronimus Academy of Data Science (JADS).** A collaboration between TU/e and Tilburg University, based in 's-Hertogenbosch. JADS combines data science with business administration and entrepreneurship, and works closely with Brabant companies on practical projects.

**Fontys University of Applied Sciences.** With locations in Eindhoven, Tilburg and Venlo, among others, Fontys trains large numbers of ICT students with specialisations in AI and data. Fontys has an extensive network of internship companies, especially among SMEs.

**Avans University of Applied Sciences.** With locations in Breda, 's-Hertogenbosch and Tilburg, Avans offers computer science and data programmes with a strong professional focus.

**Breda University of Applied Sciences (BUas).** Known for games, media and logistics, with a data science and AI programme that connects to creative technology and logistics challenges.

**Practical tip:** start your search at the internship office or internship coordinator of your own programme. Don't just ask "which internships are available?", but also "at which companies within 30 minutes of where I live have students done AI or data projects in recent years?" That often produces names that are advertised nowhere.

## A Map of AI Work in Brabant, by Region

Noord-Brabant is not one labour market, but a network of urban regions, each with its own economic profile.

| Region | Economic profile | Typical AI and data internships |
|---|---|---|
| Eindhoven, Veldhoven, Helmond (Brainport) | High-tech, semiconductors, automotive, manufacturing | Machine vision, predictive maintenance, sensor data, embedded AI |
| 's-Hertogenbosch and surroundings | Business services, construction and infrastructure, data science education | Data analysis, project data, JADS practical projects |
| Tilburg and Waalwijk (Central Brabant) | Logistics, e-commerce, business services | Inventory forecasting, route planning, customer data |
| Breda and West Brabant | Logistics, agrifood, creative technology, games | Supply chain analytics, simulation, data in games and media |
| Veghel and Oss (Northeast Brabant) | Logistics, food production, life sciences, automation | Warehouse automation, process data, quality control |

Economic development organisations such as **Brainport Development** (Eindhoven region) and the **Brabant Development Agency (BOM)** work at provincial level on innovation and keeping talent in the region. For you as a student, that mainly means there are more companies with serious technological ambitions than the well-known names suggest.

## Practical Matters to Arrange in Advance

**Internship allowance.** In the Netherlands, an internship allowance is not legally required, but many companies do pay one. The amount varies widely by sector and organisation. Ask about it in the first conversation, and compare not only the allowance but also the travel costs you will have to bear yourself.

**Internship agreement.** An internship usually runs through a three-party internship agreement between you, the company and your educational institution. It sets out learning objectives, supervision, duration and any allowance. A company with no experience of this is not necessarily unsuitable — but ask extra carefully who will supervise you on content.

**Travelling with your student travel product.** With the student travel product from DUO, you travel free on public transport on weekdays or at weekends, depending on your choice. That often makes an internship in another Brabant city cheaper than it seems — but travel time is still travel time. A Tilburg–Eindhoven or Breda–'s-Hertogenbosch journey is easy by train; a business park without a good bus connection may require a bike ride or a car.

**Technical supervision.** For an AI internship, the most important question is not how big the company is, but whether someone can review your code and models on content. Ask in the interview: "who reviews my work, and how often?" A small team with one experienced data scientist who looks at your work weekly often teaches you more than a large company where you sit in a corner as an intern.

**From internship to job.** Many Brabant SMEs actively use internships as a recruitment channel. A company closer to home also has an interest in you staying — and you run less risk of having to move anyway after graduating.

## A Note on Your Internship Allowance

In the Netherlands, an internship allowance generally counts as wages, from which the employer withholds payroll tax. With the general tax credit there is often little or nothing to pay, but check whether your employer applies the payroll tax credit. If you receive student finance, also check with DUO whether your income affects any supplementary grant.

## Well-Known Names or SMEs: What You Really Learn in an Internship

Brabant has both large, internationally known high-tech employers and thousands of SMEs. For an AI internship, the experience differs considerably.

| Aspect | Large high-tech company | SME |
|---|---|---|
| Scope of your assignment | Narrow and well defined | Broader, often from data collection to presentation |
| Supervision | Formal, with fixed internship procedures | More informal, dependent on one or two people |
| Access to data | Lots of data, but strict access rules and confidentiality | Less data, but often faster access |
| Visibility of your result | Part of a larger whole | Directly visible to management and clients |
| Chance of a job afterwards | Depends on vacancy space in a large organisation | Often personal and quickly decided |

Neither is better. If you want to learn how a large, mature data team works, choose the first. If you want to learn broadly and take on a lot of responsibility, you will often feel at home in the second.

## Five Questions for Your First Internship Interview

1. What concrete problem will I solve, and who will use the result?
2. What data will I get, and when will I have access to it?
3. Who supervises me on content, and how often will we meet?
4. May I publish my graduation report, or does confidentiality apply?
5. Have previous interns moved on to a job here?

**International students in Brabant.** If you study at a Brabant institution and come from outside the EU, your internship usually runs through your programme's internship agreement. After graduating, you can apply to the IND, under conditions, for the orientation year for highly educated persons (*zoekjaar hoogopgeleiden*), which makes the step from internship to a job with a regional employer considerably easier.

## Key Takeaways

- The assumption that a good AI internship is in the Randstad comes from the visibility of vacancies, not the quality of the work.
- TU/e, Tilburg University, JADS, Fontys, Avans and BUas together have a large network of internship companies across the province.
- Each Brabant region has its own profile: high-tech in Brainport, logistics in Central and West Brabant, food and automation in Northeast Brabant.
- Arrange your internship allowance, internship agreement, travel time and technical supervision in advance.
- Search by distance and category, not by well-known company names.

### (Scenario: starter who only searches for well-known company names) Why do I only find the same few well-known companies when I search for "AI internship"?
Because job sites show vacancies based on what many people already search for and what companies advertise, not on which work suits you best. Companies with large recruitment budgets rank at the top, regardless of whether their internship suits you better than one at a less well-known company.

Often, yes. Being unknown says more about marketing budget than about the quality of the work. Companies such as Boltrics, 4Dotnet, Holland Innovative and Eminent Groep currently have open AI and data vacancies without appearing on any "well-known employers" list.
