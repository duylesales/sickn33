Most student AI portfolios contain the same project: a Kaggle dataset, a notebook trained to 90-something percent accuracy. A hiring manager who's seen it a hundred times learns almost nothing about you from it.

What actually signals something: a problem you defined yourself, not one a dataset came pre-packaged with. Evidence you understood why the model failed sometimes, not just a clean final number. Something deployed, even badly — the gap between a notebook and something that runs is where a lot of real junior work happens.

Three imperfect, original projects beat one polished, familiar one.
