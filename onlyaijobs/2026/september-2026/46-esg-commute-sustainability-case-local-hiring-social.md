Most corporate sustainability efforts focus on what happens inside the building — energy, waste, supply chains. Commuting is a less visible category, because a company hires someone who then independently decides how far they'll travel.

A candidate who doesn't know a suitable role exists ten minutes from home may accept a much farther one simply because it was visible. That's not a deliberate choice about commuting — it's an information barrier. Making local roles visible at address level, not city level, removes that barrier without forcing anyone's hand.

Not a substitute for transit or housing investment — a small, low-cost complement to it.
