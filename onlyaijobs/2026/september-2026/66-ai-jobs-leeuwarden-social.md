💧 Leeuwarden's AI market is genuinely small — not under-publicised, small. If a role ends here, your options are a handful of local employers, a commute to Groningen, or relocation. Worth stating before the opportunity.

🔬 What it does have: a water technology research cluster with real European standing. Soft sensors that infer laboratory-measured quantities from continuous sensor data. Anomaly detection on buried drinking water networks instrumented only at points. Optimisation where the constraints are regulatory and non-negotiable.

🐄 And a second domain most people never consider: modern dairy is heavily instrumented. Per-animal time series across large herds — yield, composition, activity, rumination — with irregular sampling, individual variation and weak labels derived from later veterinary outcomes. Genuinely interesting problems, rarely discussed in ML conversations.

🎯 The opening: water research positions are advertised in domain language, so the applicant pool comes from process engineering and chemistry. Computational skills are scarce there. You face far less competition than for an equivalently interesting role framed in AI vocabulary — provided your domain interest is real.

🚆 Groningen at under 40 minutes is what makes a Leeuwarden base viable. Amsterdam at 2h15 is not a fallback.
