A bootcamp certificate and a university degree aren't competing on the same axis. A degree signals depth over years; a bootcamp signals a compressed sprint toward employability. Treating them as ranked on one scale is where career switchers get discouraged unnecessarily.

What actually gets scrutinized regardless of path: can you explain why a technique works, not just apply it. What's your track record since the credential — a bootcamp finished 8 months ago with no projects since reads very differently than the same credential followed by continuous practice.

Target roles by what they actually test for, not by title prestige.
