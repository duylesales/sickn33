Few job categories in the Dutch AI market are as new, or as unsettled, as AI ethics and governance. The roles exist because of EU AI Act compliance requirements — but the job titles are still being figured out company by company.

"AI Ethics Officer," "Responsible AI Lead," "AI Compliance Manager," or folded into an existing Data Protection Officer role — there's no established convention yet. Search by concept (governance, compliance, responsible AI, risk), not by title.

Two backgrounds show up disproportionately here: engineers who developed a strong interest in policy, and legal/policy professionals who built technical literacy.
