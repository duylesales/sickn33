Research roles are the smallest category on almost any Dutch AI job board. Easy to misread that as absence — it isn't.

Product-company hiring follows roadmap and headcount planning. Research hiring follows grant cycles and specific project funding — a group may not hire for a year, then post two highly specific roles in the same month. Many research hires also happen through academic networks and conference contacts before a role is ever posted publicly.

If you're specifically looking for research work: check institutional pages directly, and treat a job board's research category as a supplement to network visibility, not a replacement.
