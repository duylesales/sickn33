🗺️ Apeldoorn is one of the least discussed and most data-intensive job markets in the Netherlands. It hosts the national land registry, major tax administration operations, and a large insurance and pensions cluster — national-scale registers with no commercial equivalent in the country.

⚖️ National register data works differently from commercial data. A commercial dataset is a sample of behaviour and errors are noise. A land register is the legal truth about who owns what — an error is a defect with legal consequences requiring formal correction. Data quality stops being preprocessing and becomes a core function.

🛰️ The geospatial work is the standout. Change detection comparing aerial imagery against the building register has an unusual property: the ground truth is a legal register, so a disagreement is either a model error or a real unregistered change. Distinguishing the two is the actual job — the segmentation model is the easy part.

💰 One thing candidates consistently misjudge: compare disposable income, not gross salary. Housing costs differ enough from the Randstad that a moderate offer here can leave you materially better off.
