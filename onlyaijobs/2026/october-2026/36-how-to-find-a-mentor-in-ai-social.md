💡 "Will you be my mentor?" is a hard question to say yes to. Mentorship that works rarely starts that way.

🎓 Start with one small, specific question someone can answer in two minutes. Report back on what you did with the advice. Repeat. Study associations, alumni networks, graduation internships, ICAI labs, PyData meetups, Women in AI Netherlands and ADPList are good places to meet people.

✅ In the Dutch context, a direct, specific question is usually appreciated.
