💡 The EU Pay Transparency Directive is changing what AI job postings must disclose.

💰 Candidates get pay range information before the interview, and employers may no longer ask about pay history. Employees can request pay information, larger employers report gender pay gaps on a phased schedule, and an unexplained gap of 5% or more triggers a joint pay assessment.

✅ AI roles will likely show wide ranges at first. Use them to compare offers — and check the status of Dutch implementing law.
