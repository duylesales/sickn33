💡 Most AI hiring processes lose time in the second interview round — usually because it repeats the first.

💰 Give each round a separate purpose and scorecard, appoint a process owner, and use a five-minute briefing between rounds. Share salary ranges early, run reference checks in parallel and reserve interview slots in advance.

✅ In a tight ICT labour market, a process that takes six weeks mostly contains waiting time — and loses candidates.
