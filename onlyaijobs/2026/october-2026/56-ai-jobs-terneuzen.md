Terneuzen hosts one of the country's larger chemical industry clusters, connected to a cross-border port operation shared administratively with Belgium. That combination produces genuine process-optimization and logistics data work, in a region that rarely comes up in conversations about Dutch AI hubs despite the scale of industry present.

## What This Work Actually Involves

Chemical process optimization requires modeling with direct safety and efficiency stakes — a wrong prediction in a chemical process has consequences well beyond a bad recommendation. Port logistics data work here shares characteristics with Rotterdam's, but at a different scale and with the added complexity of cross-border administration.

## Why This Stays Under the Radar

Chemical and industrial employers rarely compete for tech-scene visibility, and job titles reflect chemical-engineering or process vocabulary rather than AI terms — a candidate searching nationally is unlikely to encounter this cluster by keyword alone.
