💡 How much math do you really need for an AI career? It depends on the role.

🎓 Applied roles need a working understanding of linear algebra, probability and statistics — enough to explain why a model does what it does. Research roles need active mathematical reasoning at master's level.

🤖 Fill gaps from concrete problems, not full theory courses. 3Blue1Brown, "An Introduction to Statistical Learning" and "Mathematics for Machine Learning" match different levels.
