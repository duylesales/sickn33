Sittard-Geleen hosts one of the country's larger integrated chemical and materials science industrial campuses, where multiple companies share infrastructure for chemical production and materials research. That concentration produces genuine process-optimization and materials-data work, entirely distinct in character from consumer AI.

## What This Work Actually Involves

Materials science data work involves modeling chemical processes and material properties — predicting how a formulation or process change affects output quality, with real production and safety stakes. This is applied statistics and machine learning grounded in chemistry and materials engineering, not software product work.

## Why This Segment Is Hard to Find

Job titles here reflect chemical and process-engineering vocabulary, and the companies on this campus don't compete for tech-scene visibility the way consumer AI companies do — a national AI job search will consistently miss this cluster.
