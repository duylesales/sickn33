💡 Switching to AI or data work after 40? Age is rarely the real obstacle.

💰 What counts: recent, demonstrable projects and a clear link between your previous experience and the new role. Quantitative backgrounds and deep sector knowledge are strong starting points. Dutch law prohibits age discrimination, and routes like the Open Universiteit, Make IT Work and CAO training budgets help.

✅ Expect a temporary step back — most successful switchers catch up within two to four years.
