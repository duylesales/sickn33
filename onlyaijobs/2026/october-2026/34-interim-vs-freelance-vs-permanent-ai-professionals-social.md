💡 Freelance or permanent isn't the only choice. Interim work through a secondment agency is a third route with its own risk profile.

💰 The agency carries part of the acquisition risk in exchange for part of your rate. The Waadi, sector agreements, the SNA quality mark and payrolling rules define the framework.

✅ Before signing, ask: what happens between assignments, how is the margin built, can I decline assignments, and is there a non-solicitation clause?
