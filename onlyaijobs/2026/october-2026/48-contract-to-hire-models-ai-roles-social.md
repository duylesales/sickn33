💡 Contract-to-hire can reduce the risk of a wrong AI hire — but not in every situation.

💰 It fits roles with uncertain scope and first AI hires. In the Netherlands it takes three forms: a fixed-term contract, agency placement or a freelance route. Each has different rules: short probation periods, the chain rule, transition payments, a higher WW premium, the Waadi and DBA risk.
