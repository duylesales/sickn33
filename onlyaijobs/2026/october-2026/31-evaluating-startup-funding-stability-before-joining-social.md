💡 An exciting AI product doesn't prove a startup can fund your role for more than a few months.

🎤 Ask about runway, revenue and the next funding round — professionally, it rarely harms your candidacy. Check KVK filings, the Central Insolvency Register, Dealroom and Techleap.nl. WBSO, the Innovation Credit or Invest-NL signal validation, often tied to milestones.

✅ The UWV wage guarantee scheme offers limited protection in bankruptcy. Stock options usually don't survive it.
