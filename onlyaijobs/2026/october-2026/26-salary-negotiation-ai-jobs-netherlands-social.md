💡 Negotiating an AI job offer in the Netherlands isn't a US bidding war. Aggressive tactics often backfire; preparation and reasoning work better.

💰 Check every offer for holiday allowance, CAO scale, pension contributions and allowances. International candidates: check current IND thresholds and the updated 30% ruling. Non-compete clauses and equity terms are often more negotiable than base salary.

✅ And the EU Pay Transparency Directive makes asking for the salary range early completely normal.
