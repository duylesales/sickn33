Hungary has a remarkable tradition in mathematics and science, and Budapest has long produced world-class mathematicians, physicists and computer scientists. That tradition, combined with a large pool of engineering graduates and competitive costs, has attracted R&D and technology centres from global companies in automotive, telecom, finance and software. Hungarian-founded companies such as Prezi and LogMeIn showed that Budapest could also build global software products. Machine learning jobs in Budapest span automated driving, telecom networks, financial technology, product analytics and research. This guide explains where the work is and what experienced engineers should consider before moving.

## Hungary's Mathematical and Scientific Tradition

Budapest's reputation in mathematics goes back more than a century and includes a strong culture of mathematical competitions and specialised secondary schools. The Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics is an internationally recognised research institute, and Hungarian mathematicians have made fundamental contributions to combinatorics, probability and computer science. This tradition shapes the local talent pool: many Hungarian engineers have strong theoretical foundations, which is valuable for machine learning.

## Universities and Research

**Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME)** is Hungary's leading technical university, with strong faculties in electrical engineering, informatics and mathematics, and research groups in machine learning, computer vision, speech technology and autonomous systems.

**Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE)** has a strong Faculty of Informatics and Faculty of Science, with research in machine learning, data science and AI, and collaborates with industry on applied projects.

**HUN-REN Hungarian Research Network** brings together Hungary's research institutes. HUN-REN SZTAKI, the Institute for Computer Science and Control, conducts research in AI, machine learning, robotics and data science, and the Rényi Institute contributes mathematical research relevant to AI theory.

Hungarian researchers have also worked on language models for Hungarian, a language with rich morphology and relatively limited data, creating opportunities in NLP for Hungarian and other smaller languages.

## Language and Culture

Hungarian is a unique language, unrelated to most European languages, and learning it is challenging. Fortunately, English is the working language in most international R&D centres, finance hubs and software companies. Daily life in Budapest is manageable in English in many situations, especially in central districts, but learning basic Hungarian helps with bureaucracy and social integration. Hungarian workplace culture tends to be professional and fairly hierarchical in traditional organisations, while tech companies and startups are more informal.

## Pay, Taxes and Currency

Salaries in Hungary are paid in Hungarian forints and are generally lower than in Western Europe, although international R&D and finance centres pay competitively by local standards. Hungary has a flat personal income tax rate, currently 15%, plus employee social contributions, which makes the tax calculation relatively simple. Currency fluctuations can affect the value of salaries relative to euro-based costs, which is worth considering if you have financial obligations in euros. Living costs in Budapest, including rent, are lower than in most Western European capitals, though they have risen.

## Permits

EU citizens can work freely in Hungary and register their residence if staying longer than three months. Non-EU professionals typically need a work and residence permit, and highly qualified workers can apply for the EU Blue Card under Hungary's implementation of the recast Blue Card Directive. Hungary has revised its immigration framework in recent years, so check current rules with official Hungarian sources and your employer.

## Living in Budapest

Budapest is one of Europe's most beautiful capitals, with the Danube, historic architecture, thermal baths and a lively cultural scene. Public transport — metro, trams, buses and suburban trains — is extensive and affordable. Many R&D centres and finance hubs are located in central Budapest or in business districts along the river, while some automotive engineering campuses are further out. Housing is more affordable than in Vienna, Munich or Amsterdam, and the city offers a high quality of life for its cost.

## Evaluating an R&D Centre Role

Many machine learning jobs in Budapest are in R&D or technology centres of international companies. These can offer excellent work, but their quality varies. Key questions:

- **Ownership:** does the Budapest centre own products, components or research areas, or does it execute tasks defined elsewhere?
- **Decision-making:** are architects, product owners and senior managers located in Budapest?
- **Career paths:** can you grow into principal engineer or leadership roles locally?
- **Stability:** how long has the centre existed, and how has it grown?
- **Technical environment:** what tools, data and compute are available?
- **Collaboration:** how often do you work with colleagues in other countries, and in which time zones?

Centres that own significant parts of their products tend to offer more interesting work and better growth. Talking to current engineers — for example at meetups — is the best way to find out.

## Automotive AI in Budapest

Budapest has become an important location for automotive software and automated driving development. Engineering centres work on perception, sensor fusion, planning, simulation and validation, often as part of global development teams. Hungary also hosts ZalaZONE, an automotive proving ground in the west of the country used for testing automated and connected vehicles. For ML engineers, automotive work in Budapest offers experience with large sensor datasets, simulation, safety standards and embedded deployment — skills in demand across Europe.

## Finance Technology Hubs

Several international banks and asset managers operate large technology centres in Budapest, supporting global operations in areas such as risk management, trading systems, compliance, data platforms and analytics. Data science roles in these hubs include building risk and pricing models, detecting fraud and market abuse, automating document processing and developing data platforms. These organisations offer stable employment, structured career paths and international collaboration, though work may be part of global programmes led from other locations. For engineers with quantitative skills, finance hubs are a natural fit for Hungary's mathematical tradition.

## Hungarian Software Companies

Prezi, the presentation software company, and LogMeIn, the remote access and collaboration software company, were founded in Budapest and grew into international businesses, helping to build the city's software engineering culture. Other Hungarian-founded companies work on navigation software, cybersecurity, data analytics and B2B software. Product companies offer engineers ownership and product impact, and many use ML for analytics, personalisation, search and AI features, increasingly built on language models.

## Networking in Budapest

Budapest has an active tech community with meetups on machine learning, data science, Python and software engineering, as well as conferences that attract international speakers. University events at BME and ELTE are often open to professionals. Many international engineers find the community welcoming, and meetups are a good way to learn about the quality of different employers, understand local salary levels and build friendships in a new city.

## Questions to Ask Budapest Employers

- Is the salary set in forints or euros, and how are adjustments handled?
- What is the working language, including for internal communication and documentation?
- What relocation support is offered, including help with residence registration and housing?
- Is the role hybrid, and how many office days are expected?
- How does the company handle training budgets and conference attendance?

## Telecom Research and Network AI

Global telecom vendors have run R&D in Budapest for decades, and network AI is an important part of their work. Engineers develop models that predict and prevent network faults, optimise radio resources, reduce energy consumption, detect anomalies and automate operations. Research on future network generations explores networks in which AI is built into the architecture from the start. For ML engineers with interests in systems, signal processing or reinforcement learning, telecom R&D in Budapest offers technically deep work with global impact, often in close collaboration with universities such as BME.

## Language Technology for Hungarian

Hungarian's agglutinative structure, rich morphology and relatively small speaker population make it an interesting challenge for natural language processing. Hungarian researchers have developed corpora, benchmarks and language models for Hungarian, and companies need models that handle the language well for customer service, document processing and search. For NLP engineers, working on Hungarian offers experience with low-resource and morphologically rich languages — expertise that transfers to many other European languages.

## Career Development in Budapest

Budapest's mix of R&D centres, finance hubs, product companies and research institutes allows varied careers without relocating. Engineers often move between automotive, telecom, finance and product roles, and some return to research or found startups. Building a reputation in the local community — by speaking at meetups, contributing to open source or collaborating with universities — helps in making these moves. For international engineers, Budapest can be both a destination and a stepping stone: the experience gained in large R&D centres is recognised across Europe.

## Settling In

After arriving, register your address, obtain a tax identification number and a social security number, and open a bank account. Many employers help international hires with these steps, and relocation agencies in Budapest offer support with housing and paperwork. Budapest's international community is large, and expat groups share practical advice on neighbourhoods, schools and healthcare, which makes the first months easier.

## Who Budapest Suits Best

Budapest suits engineers who want substantial R&D or finance technology work at a lower cost of living, appreciate a beautiful, culturally rich capital and are comfortable in English-speaking workplaces within a Hungarian-speaking society.

## Real example

### A perception engineer moving from Munich to Budapest

A perception engineer working on automated driving in Munich receives an offer from an automotive supplier's Budapest engineering centre to lead a validation team. The salary is lower in absolute terms, but the role is a step up in responsibility.

She calculates her net income under Hungary's flat tax, compares Budapest rents with Munich rents and considers currency risk, since her savings goals are in euros. She also checks that the team works in English and that the centre owns significant parts of the perception stack rather than only supporting other sites. Satisfied, she accepts. After a year, she says her disposable income is comparable to what she had in Munich, her responsibilities are broader and Budapest's quality of life surprised her positively.
