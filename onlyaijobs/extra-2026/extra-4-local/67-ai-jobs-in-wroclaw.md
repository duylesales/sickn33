Wrocław, the capital of Lower Silesia in south-western Poland, is one of the country's most dynamic cities. Its colourful market square, islands on the Oder river and large student population make it a popular place to live, and its economy has grown rapidly on the back of technology, business services and manufacturing. A major telecom R&D centre, international finance and technology centres, battery and automotive factories and a strong software services scene all employ engineers, and the city's universities have a notable tradition in computer science and algorithmic programming. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Wrocław offer telecom and network AI, financial analytics, industrial AI and software projects for international clients, at a cost of living below Western Europe. This guide explains the landscape and what to consider.

## Telecom R&D

Nokia has one of its largest research and development centres in Wrocław, employing thousands of engineers working on telecommunications networks, including radio access, core networks and software. AI in telecom covers network optimisation, anomaly and fault detection, automation of network operations, energy efficiency and research on future network generations. For ML engineers with interests in systems, signal processing or reinforcement learning, telecom R&D in Wrocław offers technically deep work within global product teams.

## Finance and Technology Centres

Wrocław hosts numerous international business service, finance and technology centres of banks, financial institutions and corporations. These centres employ data engineers, analysts and increasingly data scientists and ML engineers working on risk analytics, fraud detection, process automation, reporting and data platforms for global operations. They offer stable employment and international collaboration; it is worth checking how much ownership the Wrocław team has over the models and products it builds.

## Battery, Automotive and Manufacturing

The Wrocław region has become an important manufacturing location. LG Energy Solution operates one of Europe's largest battery factories for electric vehicles near Wrocław, and automotive component manufacturers and other industrial companies run plants in the special economic zones around the city. AI applications include quality inspection, process optimisation, predictive maintenance and battery production analytics, where data from complex electrochemical processes must be monitored and improved.

## Software Houses and Startups

Wrocław has a strong software services sector, including companies specialising in AI and machine learning projects for international clients, as well as product startups. Software houses offer variety — projects in healthcare, e-commerce, fintech or industry — and often English-speaking teams. The Wrocław Technology Park and other innovation hubs support startups and technology companies.

## Research and Education

The Wrocław University of Science and Technology (Politechnika Wrocławska) is one of Poland's leading technical universities, with strong departments in computer science, electronics and telecommunications and research groups in AI, NLP, computer vision and data science. The University of Wrocław has a respected Institute of Computer Science with a strong tradition in algorithms and theoretical computer science; its students have performed well in international programming competitions. The Wrocław Centre for Networking and Supercomputing provides computing resources for research.

## Contracts and Pay

As elsewhere in Poland, many tech professionals choose between an employment contract (umowa o pracę) and a B2B contract as a self-employed contractor. B2B can mean higher net income but fewer protections, such as paid leave and sick pay, unless negotiated. Many job postings in Wrocław include salary ranges for both forms. Salaries for experienced ML engineers are high by Polish standards, though below Western European hubs. Get tax advice before choosing B2B, particularly if you are moving from abroad.

## Language and Permits

Polish is the language of daily life and many local organisations, but English is the working language in telecom R&D, finance centres, software houses and many startups. EU citizens can work freely and register their stay. Non-EU professionals typically need a work and residence permit or can use the EU Blue Card if they meet the requirements. Processing times can be significant; start early.

## Living in Wrocław

Wrocław is a lively, green city with a historic centre, many bridges, a large student population and an active cultural life. Trams and buses cover the city, and cycling is increasingly popular. Housing costs have risen but remain below those of Warsaw and Western European cities. Prague, Berlin and Kraków are reachable by train or car, and Wrocław's airport offers European connections.

## Network AI in Practice

Modern telecom networks are enormously complex, with millions of configuration parameters, constantly changing traffic and strict reliability requirements. Machine learning helps in several ways: predicting and detecting faults before customers notice them, optimising radio parameters for coverage and capacity, reducing energy consumption by switching off resources when traffic is low, classifying traffic and automating operational workflows. Research on future network generations explores networks in which AI is built into the architecture. Engineers in telecom R&D work with large volumes of network data, simulations and test environments, and their models are deployed in products used by operators worldwide. The work combines ML with deep systems knowledge, which makes it intellectually demanding and highly transferable.

## Battery Manufacturing and Industrial AI

Battery production for electric vehicles involves many precise steps — electrode coating, cell assembly, formation and ageing — each generating large amounts of process and quality data. Machine learning helps detect defects early, predict cell performance from process parameters, optimise yields and reduce scrap, and analyse equipment data for predictive maintenance. As Europe expands battery production, engineers with experience in this field are increasingly valuable. Work in manufacturing usually requires on-site presence and close collaboration with process engineers, and it offers tangible impact on product quality and costs.

## Software Houses and AI Projects

Wrocław's software houses deliver AI projects for clients in Western Europe, the United States and elsewhere: computer vision systems for healthcare or retail, recommendation engines, forecasting tools, language model applications and data platforms. For ML engineers, software houses offer variety, exposure to different industries and technologies and the chance to work directly with clients. The trade-offs include less long-term ownership of products and project-driven workloads. Many engineers alternate between software houses and product companies over their careers.

## Evaluating Finance Centre Roles

Before joining a finance or technology centre, ask which models and systems the Wrocław team owns, where decisions are made, how career progression works locally and whether ML work is part of the centre's core mandate. Centres that own meaningful work offer more interesting roles and better growth.

## Networking in Wrocław

Wrocław has an active tech community with meetups on machine learning, data science, Python and software engineering, as well as conferences and hackathons. The universities host seminars and competitions, and the city's large student population keeps the community young and energetic. Attending events regularly helps newcomers learn about employers, understand local salary levels and build friendships.

- Which contract forms do you offer, and what does each include?
- What does the Wrocław team own, and where are decisions made?
- What is the working language, including for documentation?
- Where exactly is the office or site, and how is it connected?
- What relocation and permit support is offered?

## Settling In

Newcomers need a PESEL number, a Polish bank account and, for B2B contractors, registration of a business activity. Employers experienced with international hires can guide you through these steps.

## Skills That Stand Out

Wrocław employers value strong algorithmic and programming skills — reflecting the local tradition — as well as production ML experience, data engineering and MLOps. Telecom roles reward systems knowledge and signal processing; manufacturing roles reward time series, vision and process understanding; software houses reward versatility and client communication. English is expected in most tech roles, and Polish helps with career progression in local organisations.

## Who Wrocław Suits Best

Wrocław suits engineers who want substantial telecom, industrial or software AI work in a lively, affordable city, and who value a strong engineering culture and a young, international community.

## Research and Polish Language AI

Researchers at the Wrocław University of Science and Technology have been active in natural language processing for Polish, including language resources and tools used in Poland's digital research infrastructure. National initiatives to build Polish language models have involved research institutions across the country. For NLP engineers, this creates opportunities in research and in companies that need models handling Polish well for customer service, document processing and search. Research positions at the universities offer PhD and postdoc opportunities, often in collaboration with industry.

## Evaluating an Offer

Compare net income under the offered contract form, benefits such as private healthcare, sports cards and training budgets, and the stability of the employer. For relocating engineers, include realistic housing costs and the value of relocation support. A slightly lower salary with strong ownership and mentoring can be the better career choice.

## Automotive and Industrial Suppliers

Beyond batteries, the special economic zones around Wrocław host automotive component makers, household appliance manufacturers and electronics companies. Their data teams work on computer vision for inspection, predictive maintenance of production lines, energy optimisation and supply chain forecasting. Many of these plants are part of international groups that are building shared data platforms and AI competence centres, sometimes with teams located in Wrocław serving plants across Europe. For ML engineers who enjoy industrial problems, these roles combine hands-on work on the factory floor with the resources of large organisations. They typically require close collaboration with engineers who may prefer Polish, so language skills help, but many technical teams also work in English. The experience gained — deploying reliable models in production environments with strict quality requirements — is valued across European manufacturing, and it becomes even more relevant as the EU AI Act introduces requirements for AI systems used as safety components in regulated products.

## Remote and Hybrid Arrangements

Many Wrocław tech employers offer hybrid work, and some engineers work remotely for foreign companies. Clarify employment status, tax and social security implications before accepting a remote arrangement with a non-Polish employer.

## A Final Tip

Ask to speak with an engineer who joined the team in the past year; their experience of onboarding, ownership and workload is the most honest preview of your own first year in Wrocław.
