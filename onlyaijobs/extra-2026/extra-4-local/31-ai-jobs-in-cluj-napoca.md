Cluj-Napoca, the unofficial capital of Transylvania, has grown into one of Romania's most important technology centres. It is a university city with tens of thousands of students, a large software industry and a reputation for a lively cultural scene, from film festivals to large music events. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Cluj-Napoca offer a realistic starting point for a machine learning career: universities with strong mathematics and computer science, a broad base of software companies, a growing number of product and AI-focused teams and a cost of living that remains below Western European levels, though it has risen quickly. This guide explains how the Cluj ecosystem works and how to land a first AI role.

## The Academic Foundation

**Babeș-Bolyai University (UBB).** Romania's largest university has a Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science that teaches in Romanian, Hungarian, German and English and has research groups in machine learning, data mining and intelligent systems. UBB's multilingual tradition reflects the region's diverse history and gives students strong language skills.

**Technical University of Cluj-Napoca (UTCN).** The technical university trains computer, electrical and automation engineers, with research in computer vision, robotics, embedded systems and AI. Its graduates are a major source of engineers for the region's companies.

**Research.** The Romanian Institute of Science and Technology (RIST), based in Cluj-Napoca, conducts research in machine learning and related fields and participates in international collaborations. University research groups work with companies on applied projects, and there are opportunities for research assistants and doctoral candidates.

**Other universities.** The University of Medicine and Pharmacy and the University of Agricultural Sciences and Veterinary Medicine add health and agriculture domains, where data science is increasingly applied.

## Where AI Jobs in Cluj-Napoca Come From

Cluj's technology sector grew initially around software services: companies building software for clients in Western Europe and North America. Over time, the ecosystem has matured to include product companies, R&D centres, startups and engineering centres of international firms. Romania also produced UiPath, founded in Bucharest, which became one of the country's best-known technology companies — an inspiration for many Romanian engineers and founders. The Cluj IT Cluster brings together companies, universities and public authorities to support the regional technology sector.

| Product companies and startups | Product ML, analytics, recommendation, NLP | Ownership, faster learning, broader scope |

Bosch has a significant presence in the Cluj region, including an engineering centre and a manufacturing plant nearby, and other automotive and industrial companies run engineering teams in the city. These are good places to learn how ML works in safety-critical and production environments.

## Entry-Level Paths

1. **Internships.** Many Cluj companies run summer internships and year-round programmes for students, often with structured training. They are the most common way into a first job.
2. **Junior roles in software services.** Junior data engineer, data scientist or ML engineer positions in services companies offer exposure to many projects and technologies.
3. **Graduate programmes and academies.** Some companies run training academies that teach specific skills, followed by hiring.
4. **Research assistant positions** at universities or RIST, sometimes combined with a master's or PhD.
5. **Startups.** Smaller companies offer broad responsibility but less structure; they suit graduates who learn independently.

## From Outsourcing to Product Work

A common question among Cluj graduates is whether to start in a software services company or a product company. Services companies offer structured onboarding, variety and exposure to international clients, which is excellent for learning engineering practices and communication. The trade-off is that you may work on parts of systems designed elsewhere and change projects frequently. Product companies offer ownership and depth in one domain but can be less structured for juniors. Many Cluj engineers start in services, build solid engineering skills and then move to product work — or grow into technical leadership within services companies, where AI practices are expanding.

## Pay and Cost of Living

Salaries in Romania's IT sector are high relative to national averages, although generally below Western European levels for comparable roles. Romania previously offered an income tax exemption for software developers that made net pay particularly attractive; this incentive has been reduced and changed in recent years, so check the current rules. Cluj's cost of living, especially housing, has risen significantly with the growth of the tech sector and the student population, making it one of Romania's more expensive cities. Compare offers on net pay, benefits (such as private health insurance and meal vouchers) and learning opportunities.

## Language

Romanian is the language of daily life and administration, and Hungarian is also widely spoken in the region. In the tech sector, English is the working language in most companies, especially those serving international clients. German and French are assets for companies working with clients in those countries. International graduates can find English-speaking roles, but basic Romanian helps greatly with daily life.

## Building a Portfolio for Cluj Employers

Cluj employers see many applications from graduates with similar degrees. What sets candidates apart is practical evidence:

- **An end-to-end project:** data collection, cleaning, modelling, evaluation and a simple deployment, documented in a public repository.
- **Clean code:** readable, tested code with version control history shows that you can work in a team.
- **A project with real data:** Romanian open data, public datasets or data from a student project with a company.
- **A language model application:** for example a retrieval-based assistant over documents, with a clear evaluation of its answers — increasingly relevant given how many companies are building such tools.
- **Your thesis:** especially if done with a company or research group; be ready to explain it in depth.

Competitions and hackathons, which are frequent in Cluj, are excellent for building portfolio projects and meeting employers.

## What Interviews Look Like

Interviews for junior AI roles in Cluj typically include a technical test (programming, algorithms or SQL), questions on ML fundamentals (overfitting, evaluation metrics, bias-variance, common algorithms), a discussion of your projects and sometimes a small take-home assignment. Services companies may also assess English communication, since you will work with international clients. Prepare to explain not just what you did but why, and practise describing a project clearly in five minutes.

## Student Communities and Events

Cluj has a vibrant student and tech community. Student associations at UBB and UTCN organise hackathons, coding contests, workshops and career fairs, often with company sponsors. Local meetups on AI, data science, Python and cloud technologies are held regularly, and technology conferences bring international speakers to the city. Companies frequently present their projects at university events and recruit interns directly. Active participation in these communities is one of the best ways to discover opportunities that are never widely advertised, to meet engineers who can recommend you and to learn which teams offer the best mentoring.

## Automotive and Embedded AI

Cluj's automotive engineering centres offer a distinctive path for graduates interested in computer vision and embedded systems. Work on driver assistance, sensor processing and vehicle software involves strict safety standards, rigorous testing and close collaboration with hardware engineers. ML engineers in these teams learn how to validate models for safety-critical applications, handle large volumes of sensor data and optimise models for embedded hardware. These skills are valued across Europe, and graduates who start in automotive often build strong, transferable engineering profiles.

## The First Two Years

In your first role, prioritise learning over title or salary differences. Seek teams with experienced engineers who review your code and explain their decisions. Take ownership of small deliverables, ask for feedback regularly and learn how models reach production and are monitored. Develop communication skills — explaining your work to non-technical colleagues or clients is as important as technical ability. Keep a record of what you build and learn; it will make your next career step much easier.

## Staying or Moving Abroad

Many Romanian engineers eventually consider working abroad, and some return after gaining experience. Remote work has also made it possible to work for foreign companies while living in Cluj. For your first role, however, in-person mentoring in a local team is usually more valuable than a remote position abroad. Later, if you consider moving, compare concrete offers on net pay, cost of living, career growth and quality of life rather than assuming that abroad is automatically better.

## Reading Job Postings in Cluj

Graduate postings in Cluj vary in quality. Strong postings describe the project, the team, the technologies and the learning path, and increasingly include salary ranges — something the EU Pay Transparency Directive will make standard across the EU. Weaker postings list many unrelated technologies or use vague titles. Signals of a good junior role include mentoring, code review, a clear onboarding plan and realistic requirements. If a posting for a junior role demands years of experience with many advanced tools, it may reflect unclear expectations. In the first conversation, ask what the last junior hire worked on, who reviews their work and how performance is evaluated.

## Responsible AI Skills

As the EU AI Act takes effect, companies that build AI systems for European clients need engineers who understand documentation, data governance, evaluation and human oversight. Services companies in Cluj that deliver AI projects for Western European clients increasingly face these requirements. Graduates who learn the basics of responsible AI — measuring bias, documenting datasets and models and understanding GDPR — bring a practical advantage to their first employer and position themselves well for future roles.

## Living in Cluj

Cluj is compact and walkable, with a historic centre, parks and nearby hills and forests. Many young professionals live in neighbourhoods such as Mărăști, Gheorgheni, Zorilor or Grigorescu, and public transport has improved, though traffic can be heavy at peak times. Check the commute to your employer's exact office before choosing where to live.

## Other Romanian Tech Cities

Bucharest has the largest concentration of tech companies in Romania, while Iași, Timișoara and Brașov also have strong universities and growing technology sectors. Comparing offers across these cities can reveal opportunities with lower living costs.

## A Weekly Plan for Your Search

Apply to two or three well-chosen roles per week, attend one community event per month, improve one portfolio project continuously and follow up personally with people you meet. Consistency matters more than volume.

## Real example

### A UBB graduate who moved from services to product ML

A computer science graduate from Babeș-Bolyai University joined a software services company in Cluj through its summer internship programme. In her first two years, she worked on data pipelines and ML projects for clients in retail and logistics in Western Europe, learning cloud platforms, testing practices and client communication.

With this foundation, she applied to a Cluj product company building a recommendation engine for an international market. Her experience with production data pipelines stood out, and she was hired as an ML engineer. She says starting in services gave her engineering discipline and confidence, while the product role gave her depth and ownership. She stayed in Cluj throughout, benefiting from the city's growing ecosystem without having to move abroad.
