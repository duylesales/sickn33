Malmö and Lund, in Sweden's southernmost region of Skåne, form a compact technology region just across the Øresund Bridge from Copenhagen. Lund is a historic university city with one of Scandinavia's leading universities, a science park that incubated famous technology companies and two major research facilities for big science. Malmö, Sweden's third-largest city, is a young, multicultural city with game studios, startups and a growing tech scene. The region is home to a global leader in network cameras, telecom and chip design teams, a packaging giant and a life science cluster that spans the border with Denmark. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Malmö and Lund span video analytics, edge AI, telecom, games, industry, life sciences and scientific computing. This guide explains the landscape.

## Cameras, Chips and Telecom

Axis Communications, headquartered in Lund, is a global leader in network video cameras and related solutions. Video analytics — detecting objects, people and events on cameras, often directly on the device — is central to its products, making Lund a notable location for edge AI and computer vision. Ericsson has a major site in Lund working on mobile technology, and chip design companies including Arm have teams in the city. Lund's Ideon Science Park, one of Sweden's first science parks, has hosted many technology companies and startups over the decades.

## Games and Software in Malmö

Malmö has an active game industry, including Massive Entertainment, a Ubisoft studio, and other studios developing games for global audiences. Game data scientists work on player analytics, matchmaking and live operations, and studios explore ML for content tools and testing. Software companies and startups in Malmö work on fintech, SaaS and consumer products.

## Industry and Packaging

Tetra Pak, the food packaging and processing company, has major operations in Lund. Industrial companies in Skåne apply AI to manufacturing, quality control, predictive maintenance and supply chains, and food processing and packaging offer data applications in efficiency and sustainability.

## Life Sciences: Medicon Valley

Skåne and Greater Copenhagen form Medicon Valley, one of Europe's strongest life science clusters. In Lund, Medicon Village hosts life science companies and research groups, and Lund University's medical faculty and Skåne University Hospital conduct research using imaging and clinical data. Data science and AI support drug discovery, diagnostics, medical imaging and clinical research.

## Big Science: MAX IV and ESS

Lund hosts MAX IV Laboratory, a synchrotron radiation facility, and the European Spallation Source (ESS), a multinational neutron science facility. These facilities generate vast amounts of scientific data and need expertise in data processing, control systems, simulation and machine learning for experiment automation and analysis. They offer rare opportunities for engineers interested in scientific computing.

## Research and Education

Lund University is one of Scandinavia's leading universities, with strong research in computer science, mathematics, automatic control, signal processing and medicine, and it participates in the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP). Malmö University focuses on technology, society and interaction design, including research on the Internet of Things and human-centred computing.

## Cross-Border Work With Copenhagen

The Øresund Bridge connects Malmö with Copenhagen in around 20 to 40 minutes by train or car. Many people live on one side and work on the other, making the region a combined labour market of millions. Cross-border work involves specific rules for taxation and social security, which have been subject to agreements between Sweden and Denmark; check current rules with official Øresund information services before accepting a role across the border.

## Swedish Working Culture, Permits and Housing

Swedish workplaces are flat, consensus-driven and balanced, and English is widely used in tech. EU citizens can work freely and register for a personal identity number; non-EU professionals need a work permit or EU Blue Card. Housing in Malmö and Lund is in demand, and first-hand rental contracts use queues, though costs are generally lower than in Stockholm or Copenhagen.

## Living in Malmö and Lund

Lund is a green, student-filled university town; Malmö is a lively, diverse city with beaches, parks and a strong food scene. The two are 10 to 15 minutes apart by train. Copenhagen's airport is a short trip across the bridge, providing extensive international connections.

## Edge AI and Video Analytics in Depth

Running AI directly on cameras and other devices has advantages: lower bandwidth, faster response and better privacy, because video does not need to leave the device. It also brings constraints: limited compute, memory and power, and the need for models that work reliably in varied lighting, weather and scenes. Engineers in this field work on efficient architectures, quantisation and pruning, hardware accelerators, dataset curation for diverse conditions and evaluation that reflects real deployments. Video analytics also raises privacy and ethical questions, especially regarding people detection and biometric identification. The EU AI Act prohibits certain uses of real-time remote biometric identification and classifies others as high-risk, so responsible design and compliance are part of the work. Engineers who master edge AI and responsible video analytics build a rare and valuable profile.

## Scientific Computing at MAX IV and ESS

Big science facilities combine physics, engineering and computing on a large scale. At a synchrotron like MAX IV, beamlines produce high-rate data from experiments in materials science, biology and chemistry; ML helps with automated alignment, real-time data reduction, image reconstruction and experiment steering. ESS, as it becomes operational, will generate neutron scattering data for thousands of scientists from across Europe. Engineers work on data pipelines, control systems, simulation and analysis software, often in international teams and open-source projects. These roles suit engineers with physics or scientific computing backgrounds who enjoy supporting research at the frontier.

## Game Development and Player Analytics

Malmö's game studios develop large titles for global audiences. Data scientists analyse player behaviour, support game balancing, run experiments and forecast engagement, while ML engineers explore matchmaking, anti-cheat systems, automated testing and content tools. Studios value engineers who understand both data and the creative process, and the work combines statistics with product thinking.

## Life Science Data in Medicon Valley

Medicon Valley's companies and hospitals on both sides of the Øresund work on pharmaceuticals, medtech and diagnostics. In Lund, data science supports drug discovery, imaging and clinical research, within strict data protection and regulatory frameworks. The cross-border cluster gives engineers access to a wide range of life science employers.

## Questions to Ask Employers in Skåne

- At which site will I work, and how is it connected by train?
- What is the working language of the team?
- If the role is in Denmark, how will taxation and social security work if I live in Sweden?
- Which collective agreement applies, and what benefits are included?
- What relocation support is offered, including temporary housing?

## Networking and Settling In

Malmö and Lund have active tech communities, with meetups, game industry events, university seminars and startup activities, and the Copenhagen tech scene is close by. Newcomers need a Swedish personal identity number and should register with the Swedish Tax Agency early; Øresund information services help with cross-border questions.

## Skills That Stand Out

Employers value computer vision and edge AI, hardware-aware ML, telecom and signal processing, game analytics, scientific computing and life science data skills. English is common; Swedish or Danish helps with integration.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews usually include technical deep dives, coding tasks and discussions of collaboration. Camera and chip companies may ask about model optimisation and embedded constraints; game studios about experimentation and player behaviour; research facilities about scientific data and software engineering practices.

## Evaluating an Offer

Compare net pay after Swedish taxes, pension contributions and benefits, and housing costs. If comparing with Copenhagen offers, account for different tax systems and cross-border rules.

## Who Malmö and Lund Suit Best

The region suits engineers interested in edge AI, telecom, games, life sciences or scientific computing, who value a compact, international region with Copenhagen on the doorstep.

## Research Careers at Lund University

Lund University offers PhD and postdoc positions in machine learning, computer vision, automatic control, signal processing, mathematics and medicine, many through WASP and its industrial PhD track, where doctoral students are employed by companies while conducting research at the university. Lund's strength in automatic control and signal processing connects naturally with robotics, autonomous systems and telecom, and its medical faculty works on imaging and clinical data. Swedish PhD positions are paid employment with benefits. The close relationships between the university, Ideon Science Park and companies such as camera, telecom and chip firms create smooth paths between research and industry, and many engineers in the region move back and forth over their careers.

## Startups in Malmö and Lund

The region has a lively startup ecosystem, supported by incubators at Ideon, Medicon Village and Malmö, as well as investors from both Sweden and Denmark. Startups work on AI applications, health tech, fintech, gaming, food tech and sustainability. For engineers, startups offer broad responsibility and equity, and the region's large employers provide both talent and potential customers.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Many employers in Skåne offer hybrid arrangements, consistent with Swedish work culture. Cross-border remote work — for example living in Malmö and working remotely for a Danish employer — has specific tax and social security implications, so clarify arrangements carefully.

## Responsible AI

Video analytics, healthcare AI and telecom all involve sensitive data. Swedish organisations take GDPR and the EU AI Act seriously, and engineers who design privacy-preserving, well-documented systems are valued across the region.

## Food, Packaging and Sustainability Data

Skåne is also an important food and packaging region. Packaging and processing companies analyse machine data from equipment installed at customer sites around the world, predicting maintenance needs and optimising performance, while food producers use data for planning, quality and waste reduction. Sustainability is a strong driver: companies measure and reduce the environmental footprint of packaging and production, and data science supports life-cycle analysis, material optimisation and supply chain transparency. These roles combine industrial data with global operations and clear environmental relevance.
