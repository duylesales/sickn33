Twente, in the east of the Netherlands near the German border, is often overlooked by graduates who assume that serious tech careers happen in the Randstad. That assumption misses a region with a strong technical university, a tradition of entrepreneurship, a cluster of high-tech and defence electronics companies and a lively startup ecosystem. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Twente span robotics, high-tech systems, radar and sensing, health technology, manufacturing and software — often in companies that struggle to find talent precisely because graduates leave for Amsterdam or Utrecht. This guide maps the region and explains why staying can be a smart career move.

## The University of Twente and Saxion

The University of Twente (UT) in Enschede is known as an entrepreneurial university with a green campus. Its programmes in computer science, electrical engineering, applied mathematics, mechanical engineering, biomedical technology and technical medicine include substantial AI and data science content. The university's Digital Society Institute brings together research on AI, data science, cybersecurity and the societal impact of digital technology, and UT has well-known robotics research, including in medical robotics and inspection robots.

Saxion University of Applied Sciences, with campuses in Enschede, Deventer and Apeldoorn, trains practically oriented ICT, engineering and data professionals and has applied research groups working on smart industry, robotics and data science with regional companies.

## Kennispark and the Startup Ecosystem

Kennispark Twente, the innovation district between the university campus and the city, hosts startups, scale-ups and technology companies. Novel-T, the regional innovation organisation, supports entrepreneurs with programmes, funding connections and incubation. The University of Twente has produced a remarkable number of spin-offs over the decades, in areas such as sensors, microfluidics, medical devices, software and robotics. For graduates, startups in this ecosystem offer roles with broad responsibility and fast learning.

## High-Tech, Defence and Manufacturing

Twente has a strong base in high-tech systems and manufacturing. Thales Nederland, based in Hengelo, develops radar and naval combat systems, where signal processing and machine learning play a growing role; some roles require security screening and nationality conditions. Demcon, headquartered in Enschede, develops high-tech systems and medical devices for clients across industries. Many other companies in the region build machines, sensors, packaging systems and industrial equipment, and increasingly use AI for inspection, predictive maintenance and smarter products.

## Health Technology

Twente has a notable health technology cluster, combining UT's biomedical and technical medicine research with hospitals such as Medisch Spectrum Twente and companies developing medical devices, imaging systems and digital health solutions. Technical physicians — a profession pioneered at UT — work at the intersection of medicine and technology. AI applications include medical imaging, surgical robotics, patient monitoring and clinical decision support.

## Robotics in Twente

Robotics is one of the University of Twente's recognised strengths, with research on medical robotics, inspection and maintenance robots, drones, soft robotics and human–robot interaction. Regional companies and startups develop robots for industry, agriculture, healthcare and infrastructure inspection. Machine learning supports perception, grasping, navigation and learning from demonstration. For graduates, robotics roles combine software, control, mechanics and AI, often in small teams where you see your code move real hardware. UT's field lab facilities and collaboration with companies make it possible to test systems in realistic environments.

## Signal Processing, Radar and Sensing

Twente's tradition in radar, sensors and electronics creates demand for engineers who combine signal processing with machine learning. Applications include classifying objects from radar signals, detecting anomalies in sensor streams, fusing data from multiple sensors and optimising embedded processing. These problems require understanding of physics and signal theory as well as ML, and they often involve real-time constraints and specialised hardware. Graduates with electrical engineering or applied physics backgrounds are well suited to this work, which is scarce in many other regions.

## Smart Industry and Manufacturing

Twente and the wider eastern Netherlands have many manufacturing companies — in machinery, packaging, textiles heritage turned into technical textiles, food and components — that are adopting data and AI. Typical graduate projects include vision-based quality inspection, predictive maintenance of production lines, optimisation of planning and energy use, and analysis of process data. Saxion's applied research groups and regional smart industry programmes connect students with these companies. The work is practical and visible: improvements show up directly in production figures.

## Building a Profile for Twente Employers

- **Engineering foundations**: Twente employers value graduates who understand systems, not only models.
- **Hands-on projects**: robotics, sensor or embedded projects, student teams and hackathons.
- **Programming discipline**: clean code, testing, version control; C++ or embedded experience helps for high-tech roles.
- **Domain curiosity**: willingness to learn radar, manufacturing, medicine or infrastructure.
- **Communication in English and ideally Dutch**.

UT's student teams — for example in robotics, solar vehicles or drones — offer excellent experience and visibility with regional employers.

## The German Border

Enschede is only a few kilometres from the German border, and Münster, with its university and companies, is within about an hour. Some Twente residents work across the border and vice versa. Cross-border work involves questions about tax, social security and health insurance, which should be checked with cross-border information points. For job seekers, searching by distance rather than by country can reveal further opportunities, and German language skills are an asset for some regional employers with German customers.

## Living in Twente

Enschede is a lively university city with a green campus, a compact centre and a strong cultural scene. Housing costs are considerably lower than in the Randstad, and many graduates can afford their own apartment or even consider buying earlier than peers in Amsterdam or Utrecht. Hengelo, Almelo and smaller towns are close, and the countryside offers cycling and nature. Trains connect Enschede to Utrecht and Amsterdam in around two hours, and to Zwolle and Deventer more quickly, so the Randstad remains reachable for occasional visits.

## Questions to Ask Twente Employers

- What will I work on in my first six months, and who will mentor me?
- What is the working language, and is Dutch or German expected?
- For defence roles: are there security screening or nationality requirements?
- Which collective agreement applies, and what is the salary range?
- Is hybrid work possible, and how often is on-site presence needed for hardware work?

## Your First Two Years

Focus on engineering foundations, depth in one domain — robotics, sensing, manufacturing or health technology — and the ability to take systems from prototype to reliable operation. Stay connected with the university and the startup community; Twente's close-knit ecosystem rewards people who share knowledge and build relationships.

## PhDs and Research Careers

PhD positions at the University of Twente are paid employment positions in the Netherlands, with salaries, pension contributions and holiday allowance. Many projects involve industry partners, particularly in robotics, sensing, health technology and data science. For graduates who enjoy research, a PhD in Twente can lead to careers in regional companies, startups or research organisations across Europe. UT's entrepreneurial culture also encourages researchers to consider founding spin-offs, with support from Novel-T and the university.

## Dutch Graduate Conditions

Dutch employment for graduates typically includes a holiday allowance of around eight percent, pension contributions, travel reimbursement and a training budget. First contracts are often fixed-term with a path to permanent employment. International graduates of Dutch universities can use the orientation year and then the highly skilled migrant scheme, which has a reduced salary threshold for recent graduates; check current IND rules.

## Health Technology in Practice

Twente's health technology cluster offers graduates concrete AI projects: segmentation of medical images for surgical planning, analysis of wearable sensor data for rehabilitation, prediction of complications from patient monitoring data and support for robot-assisted procedures. Technical physicians and engineers collaborate closely with clinicians at regional hospitals, and UT's research groups test new technologies in clinical settings. AI tools intended for clinical use may fall under the EU Medical Device Regulation and the high-risk requirements of the EU AI Act, which require careful validation, documentation and post-market monitoring. Graduates who combine ML skills with an understanding of these frameworks and a genuine interest in clinical practice are highly valued, both by hospitals and by the medical device companies in the region.

## Responsible AI and the Digital Society

The University of Twente places strong emphasis on the societal impact of technology, from ethics and philosophy of technology to cybersecurity and public administration. Graduates who have taken courses in these areas bring an understanding of responsible AI that employers increasingly need, particularly for systems used in healthcare, public services or security. This perspective is a genuine differentiator for Twente graduates in the job market.

## Networking in Twente

UT and Saxion host career events and company days, Novel-T organises startup meetups and Kennispark companies regularly open their doors to students. Twente's tech community is small enough that people remember each other: attend a few events, show genuine interest and follow up personally. Many regional roles are filled through such connections before they are ever advertised.

## A Final Tip

Visit two or three regional employers before graduating; seeing the labs and machines in person often changes how you see a career in Twente.
