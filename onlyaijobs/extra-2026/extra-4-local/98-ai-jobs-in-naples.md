Naples is one of Europe's oldest and most vibrant cities, and its technology scene has grown considerably over the past decade. The University of Naples Federico II, founded in 1224, is one of Italy's largest universities, and its campus in San Giovanni a Teduccio has become a hub where developer academies, research groups and companies work side by side. International technology and consulting companies have opened delivery and innovation centres in the city, aerospace and automotive manufacturing operate in the metropolitan area, and national research institutes add scientific depth. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Naples offer accessible entry points into software, data engineering and applied AI, in a city with low living costs and an extraordinary quality of life. This guide explains where to look and what to expect.

## Federico II and the San Giovanni a Teduccio Campus

Federico II's engineering and computer science programmes are among the largest in southern Italy, producing many graduates each year. Its campus in San Giovanni a Teduccio, in the east of Naples, hosts academies and innovation centres created with international technology companies, where students learn software development, design and emerging technologies, including AI. These academies have become well-known routes into the technology industry for young people in the region, and the campus also houses research laboratories and startup spaces.

The University of Campania Luigi Vanvitelli, the Parthenope University and other institutions add engineering, economics and data science graduates.

## Technology Delivery and Innovation Centres

International consulting and technology companies have opened delivery centres, innovation hubs and engineering offices in Naples, attracted by the supply of graduates and lower costs than northern Italy. These centres work on software development, cloud migration, data platforms and increasingly AI projects for clients across Europe. For graduates, they offer structured onboarding, training programmes and exposure to enterprise technologies — a common first step into a data or AI career.

## Aerospace and Automotive

Campania has a significant aerospace cluster. Aerostructures and aerospace engine operations are located in the metropolitan area, including at Pomigliano d'Arco, where a major vehicle plant also operates. These industries use data for quality control, predictive maintenance, production planning and testing analysis. Research collaborations with universities support advanced manufacturing and materials.

## Research Institutes

CNR, Italy's national research council, has institutes in Naples working on high-performance computing, applied mathematics, biostructures, food science and environmental research. Naples also has strong biomedical and health research, including at university hospitals. Città della Scienza, a science centre and innovation hub in the city, promotes science communication and startups.

## Entry Paths

1. **Academies and training programmes** at the university campus, which combine learning with company contacts.
2. **Internships (tirocini)** at delivery centres and companies.
3. **Master's thesis** with a company or research group.
4. **Research fellowships and PhD positions**, including through Italy's national AI PhD programme.
5. **Graduate hiring programmes** at consulting and technology companies.

## Pay, Language and Cost of Living

Salaries in southern Italy are generally lower than in Milan or northern Europe, though technology delivery centres pay competitively for the region. Italian employment contracts follow national collective agreements, with salaries usually paid in 13 or 14 instalments and a severance fund (TFR). Italian is essential for daily life and most employers; international centres often use English for client work. The cost of living in Naples, including housing, is among the lowest of major Italian cities, which partly compensates for lower salaries.

## What Delivery Centre Work Involves

Technology delivery centres build and maintain software and data systems for clients, often large European companies. Junior engineers typically start on a project team, learning the client's domain, the technology stack and professional practices such as code review, testing and agile delivery. Data-oriented roles involve building pipelines, migrating systems to the cloud, creating dashboards and, increasingly, developing machine learning components and language model applications. The advantages for graduates are structured training, mentorship, exposure to many industries and a clear path from junior to senior roles. The trade-offs are less long-term product ownership and workloads driven by client deadlines. Many engineers spend a few years in delivery centres and then move to product companies, research or specialised AI teams — and the experience makes them attractive candidates because they know how to work professionally with real systems and clients.

## Aerospace and Manufacturing Data

Campania's aerospace cluster produces aerostructures, engine components and systems, and the region's vehicle plant manufactures cars for international markets. Data science supports quality inspection with computer vision, predictive maintenance of production equipment, analysis of test and flight data, supply chain planning and process optimisation. Aerospace work follows strict certification and documentation requirements, teaching graduates rigorous engineering practices. Collaborations between companies, Federico II and research centres support innovation in materials, manufacturing and digital technologies.

## Research and High-Performance Computing

CNR institutes and university groups in Naples work on high-performance computing, applied mathematics, image analysis, bioinformatics and environmental modelling. Southern Italy has received investment in research infrastructure and doctoral programmes in recent years, including through national programmes for AI and digital technologies. Research fellowships and PhD positions offer graduates a path into advanced technical work and international collaborations.

## Health and Public Data

University hospitals and health organisations in Campania generate clinical and administrative data used for research and service improvement. Data analysts and scientists support epidemiology, capacity planning and quality analysis, under strict data protection rules. Public administrations in the region also employ analysts for policy and services.

## Building a Profile for Naples Employers

- **Solid programming** in Python, Java or C++, depending on the sector.
- **Data engineering basics**: SQL, pipelines and cloud fundamentals, valued by delivery centres.
- **A thesis or project with real data**, ideally with a company or research group.
- **English skills** for client work and research.
- **Certifications** in cloud or data platforms, which many employers value for junior roles.

## Questions to Ask Employers

- What projects will I work on, and who will mentor me?
- What training and certifications are provided?
- Is the contract permanent, fixed-term or an apprenticeship?
- What is the working language for the team and clients?
- What are the prospects for moving into data science or ML roles?

## Networking

University events, academy demo days, startup meetups at Città della Scienza and local tech communities bring together students, engineers and employers. In Naples, personal connections matter, and attending events regularly helps graduates find opportunities.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews for junior roles usually include technical questions on programming and databases, an assessment of your thesis or projects and conversations about motivation and teamwork. Delivery centres may include aptitude tests and English assessments. Prepare clear explanations of what you built and why, and show willingness to learn.

## Staying in Naples or Moving

Many graduates from southern Italy move north or abroad. The growth of delivery centres, academies and research funding has made staying more realistic, especially for those who value family, community and low living costs. Experience gained in Naples — particularly in enterprise technology and aerospace — is recognised elsewhere in Italy and Europe, so starting locally does not close doors.

## A Simple Action Plan

Apply to an academy or internship, build one strong portfolio project, attend local tech events and follow up with engineers you meet.

## Startups and the Neapolitan Ecosystem

Naples has a growing startup ecosystem, supported by university incubators, Città della Scienza's innovation programmes, regional funding and private investors. Startups in the region work on digital health, tourism technology, agri-food, logistics, cultural heritage and AI applications. Some are founded by researchers from Federico II or by graduates of the campus academies, and several have attracted national and international investment. For graduates, joining a startup offers broad responsibility, close contact with founders and the chance to shape products from an early stage — although salaries are usually lower than at large delivery centres and job security is less certain. Before joining, ask about funding, customers and technical leadership, and make sure there is someone in the team from whom you can learn. Many graduates find that a year or two in a startup, combined with a strong portfolio of work, accelerates their career considerably, especially when they later apply to larger companies or move to other regions.

## Southern Italy's Investment in Digital Skills

National and European programmes have channelled investment into digital skills, research infrastructure and innovation in southern Italy. This has funded doctoral programmes, research centres, training academies and support for companies adopting digital technologies. For graduates, this means more opportunities than a decade ago, especially in AI-related fields — though the market remains smaller than in northern Italy, and patience and persistence are still needed.

## Responsible AI

Health data, public services and manufacturing safety all require careful governance. Graduates who understand GDPR and the EU AI Act, and who can document and evaluate their systems, bring practical value to employers in Campania.

## Living in Naples

Naples offers a dense historic centre, the bay with Vesuvius on the horizon, a famous food culture and easy access to the Amalfi Coast, the islands and archaeological sites. Housing is among the most affordable of major Italian cities, which makes a graduate salary go further than in Milan or Rome. Public transport includes metro lines, funiculars and buses, though traffic can be heavy; many employers are located in the eastern part of the city or in industrial areas outside the centre, so check your commute before choosing where to live. The city has a strong sense of community, and many graduates who stay say that quality of life and family proximity are decisive factors in their choice.

## Remote Work From Naples

Some engineers in Campania work remotely for companies in northern Italy or abroad, combining higher salaries with low living costs. If you consider this path, clarify the employment arrangement, tax residence and social security implications, and remember that for a first job, in-person mentoring usually matters more than a higher salary. Combining remote work with participation in local tech communities helps you keep building a network.

## Staying Connected

Keep in touch with academy classmates and former colleagues; in Naples's growing tech scene, recommendations open many doors.

A computer engineering graduate from Federico II completed a training programme at the university's San Giovanni a Teduccio campus, building a mobile application with a machine learning component. Through the programme's company network, he joined a technology delivery centre in Naples as a junior data engineer.

He stayed in Naples, where his rent is low and his family close, and says the combination of the academy and a large employer gave him a start that would have been hard to find otherwise in the region.
