Maastricht sits at the southern tip of the Netherlands, closer to Brussels, Aachen and Liège than to Amsterdam. Many students come to Maastricht University from abroad, attracted by its international programmes and problem-based learning, and many leave after graduation, assuming that the data jobs are in the Randstad. That assumption overlooks a region with specialised campuses for health, chemicals and smart services, public organisations with large data operations, manufacturing companies and a cross-border labour market spanning three countries. Data science jobs in Maastricht and South Limburg are more varied than most graduates realise. This guide maps the region and explains how to find them.

## Maastricht University and Zuyd

Maastricht University is one of the most international universities in Europe. Its Department of Advanced Computing Sciences offers programmes in data science, AI and computer science, with research in machine learning, robotics, game AI, optimisation and knowledge engineering. The university's Institute of Data Science connects data science with health, law and society, and its School of Business and Economics trains analysts and econometricians. Maastricht UMC+, the academic hospital, conducts research in cardiology, oncology, imaging and nutrition, with strong groups in clinical data science and medical imaging.

Zuyd University of Applied Sciences, with campuses in Maastricht, Heerlen and Sittard, trains practically oriented ICT, data and engineering professionals and has applied research groups working with regional companies.

## The Brightlands Campuses

South Limburg has organised much of its innovation around four Brightlands campuses, three of which are highly relevant for data science:

**Brightlands Maastricht Health Campus**, next to the university and hospital, focuses on health, regenerative medicine and medical technology, with companies and research groups using data for imaging, diagnostics and precision medicine.

**Brightlands Chemelot Campus** in Sittard-Geleen is built around one of Europe's major chemical sites, where companies work on chemicals, materials and circular plastics. Data science supports process optimisation, materials research, energy efficiency and predictive maintenance.

**Brightlands Smart Services Campus** in Heerlen focuses on data science, digital services and cybersecurity, and hosts organisations with large data operations, including a major location of Statistics Netherlands (CBS) and service providers in pensions and public services.

## Heerlen: A Hub for Public and Financial Data

Heerlen, 25 minutes from Maastricht, hosts organisations that process enormous volumes of data for the Dutch public. Statistics Netherlands has a large office there, and pension administration and public service organisations operate in the city. Data scientists in these organisations work on official statistics, fraud and error detection, forecasting, process automation and data quality — work with direct societal relevance, often in Dutch but increasingly with English-speaking data teams.

## Manufacturing and Industry

South and Central Limburg have a strong manufacturing base, including automotive production, machinery, materials and logistics near the German and Belgian borders. Venlo and the northern part of the province are important logistics hubs for European distribution. AI applications include quality inspection, predictive maintenance, supply chain optimisation and warehouse automation.

## Health Data Science on the Maastricht Campus

Maastricht UMC+ and the Brightlands Maastricht Health Campus offer a range of health data science opportunities. Researchers apply machine learning to cardiac imaging, radiotherapy planning, oncology outcomes and nutrition studies. Maastricht has been active in radiomics — extracting quantitative features from medical images — and in privacy-preserving approaches that allow hospitals to learn from each other's data without sharing it directly. Companies on the campus work on diagnostics, medical devices and digital health. Health data is protected by GDPR and medical ethics rules, and AI tools for clinical use may fall under the EU Medical Device Regulation and the high-risk requirements of the EU AI Act. Graduates who combine ML with statistical rigour and an understanding of these frameworks are in demand.

## Chemicals, Materials and Industrial Data

The Chemelot site in Sittard-Geleen is one of Western Europe's major chemical and materials locations. Companies there produce chemicals, polymers and materials and are working intensively on sustainability, circular plastics and energy transition. Data science supports process control and optimisation, prediction of product properties, energy efficiency, safety and maintenance. Research institutes and startups on the Brightlands Chemelot Campus work on new materials and processes, where machine learning accelerates experimentation. For graduates with chemistry, physics or engineering backgrounds combined with data skills, these roles offer technically demanding work with visible impact on emissions and resource use.

## Official Statistics and Public Data

Working with official statistics is a distinctive opportunity in Heerlen. Statistical agencies combine administrative registers, surveys and new data sources to produce statistics on the economy, population and society. Data scientists work on record linkage, imputation of missing data, classification of text (for example business activities or occupations), nowcasting, privacy protection and quality assurance. The work requires methodological care, because statistics inform public debate and policy. Similar skills are used in pension administration and public services, where data quality, fraud and error detection and forecasting matter at national scale.

## Building a Profile for Limburg Employers

- **Statistical and methodological rigour** for health and public data roles.
- **Domain curiosity** about chemistry, medicine, manufacturing or public administration.
- **Engineering skills**: SQL, Python, version control, data pipelines.
- **Communication** with specialists who are not data scientists.
- **Dutch language skills**, which broaden options considerably in public and regional organisations.

## The Euroregion: One Market, Three Countries

Maastricht is part of the Meuse-Rhine Euroregion with Aachen in Germany and Liège and Hasselt in Belgium. RWTH Aachen, Forschungszentrum Jülich and German industry are within 30 to 60 minutes; Liège has a university, a science park and industry; and Hasselt and Genk in Belgian Limburg host companies and research. For job seekers, this creates a cross-border labour market much larger than South Limburg alone. Cross-border work, however, raises questions about tax, social security, health insurance and pensions. Cross-border information points in the region provide free advice, and it is wise to consult them before accepting a role in another country.

## Living in Maastricht

Maastricht is a historic, walkable city on the Meuse, with a strong café culture, a large international student community and hills that are unusual for the Netherlands. Housing costs are lower than in the Randstad, though demand from students is high near the city centre. Heerlen, Sittard and smaller towns offer further options, connected by regional trains and buses. Brussels, Liège, Aachen and Cologne are close for weekends, and Maastricht Aachen Airport and Eindhoven Airport offer flights.

## Questions to Ask Limburg Employers

- Which campus or site will I work at, and how is it connected by public transport?
- What is the working language of the team?
- Are you an IND-recognised sponsor if I need a permit?
- Which collective agreement applies, and what is the salary range?
- For health and public roles: how is access to sensitive data organised?

## Dutch Graduate Conditions and Permits

Dutch packages usually include a holiday allowance of around eight percent, pension contributions, travel reimbursement and a training budget; public organisations and hospitals follow collective agreements with transparent pay scales. PhD candidates are paid employees. International graduates of Dutch universities can use the orientation year and then the highly skilled migrant scheme, which has a reduced salary threshold for recent graduates. Check current IND rules.

## Your First Two Years

Use your first years to build depth in one domain — health, chemicals, public data or manufacturing — along with strong engineering and statistical skills. If you plan to stay, invest in Dutch. Keep in touch with Maastricht University and its alumni, many of whom work across the Euroregion.

## A Weekly Search Routine

Set your home address, choose a commuting radius that includes Heerlen, Sittard-Geleen and possibly Aachen, review new vacancies weekly and attend Brightlands and university events. Follow up personally with people you meet — regional employers appreciate proactive interest.

## Logistics in North and Central Limburg

Venlo and the surrounding area form one of Europe's leading logistics hubs, with distribution centres for retailers and manufacturers serving Germany, the Benelux and beyond. Data science in logistics covers demand forecasting, warehouse optimisation, transport planning and automation. For graduates who enjoy operational problems with measurable results, logistics employers in Limburg offer accessible first roles, often with rapid responsibility, and the skills transfer to employers anywhere in Europe.

## Why Staying Can Be a Smart Choice

Graduates who stay in South Limburg often find that they gain responsibility faster, pay less for housing and enjoy shorter commutes than peers in the Randstad. The region's employers compete hard for data talent, which creates opportunities for early ownership of projects. And because the Euroregion connects three countries, a Maastricht-based career can be more international than one in many larger Dutch cities.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews for junior data roles in Limburg usually include a conversation about motivation and background, a technical interview covering statistics, programming and machine learning fundamentals, and often a case based on the employer's domain — for example estimating missing values in a register, forecasting demand for a warehouse or evaluating a model for detecting anomalies in a chemical process. Public organisations may add an assessment or ask for a certificate of good conduct. Prepare by explaining your thesis and projects clearly, including limitations and what you would do differently. Maastricht University's problem-based learning prepares students well for group discussions and case interviews; draw on that experience. Dutch interviewers appreciate directness and honest reflection more than polished sales pitches, and they often discuss salary early, so research typical pay scales beforehand.

## Networking in the Region

Brightlands campuses host regular events, Maastricht University organises career days and data science meetups take place in Maastricht, Heerlen and Aachen. Attending a few events each semester is the fastest way to discover employers that rarely appear in national job searches.

## Responsible AI in Public and Health Organisations

Many South Limburg employers work with sensitive data: patient records, pension and benefit data, official statistics. The Dutch public sector has placed strong emphasis on responsible algorithm use, including algorithm registers and impact assessments, and the EU AI Act classifies several public and health uses as high-risk. Graduates who understand fairness, transparency, data protection and documentation are therefore especially valuable in the region.
