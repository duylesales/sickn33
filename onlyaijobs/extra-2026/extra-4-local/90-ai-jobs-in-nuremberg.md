The Nuremberg metropolitan region in northern Bavaria — Franconia — combines a historic city with a dense concentration of technology companies and research. Erlangen, next door, is the global headquarters of a leading medical technology company and home to a major Siemens campus. A Fraunhofer institute in Erlangen gave the world the MP3 format. Herzogenaurach hosts the headquarters of two global sportswear brands and a large automotive supplier. Nuremberg adds enterprise software, market research and federal agencies. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Nuremberg and Erlangen span medical imaging, audio and sensor AI, e-commerce and sports, automotive, enterprise software and public-sector data. This guide explains the landscape.

## Medical Technology in Erlangen

Siemens Healthineers, headquartered in Erlangen, is one of the world's largest medical technology companies, producing imaging systems such as MRI, CT and X-ray, laboratory diagnostics and digital health solutions. AI is central to its products: image reconstruction, automatic detection and segmentation, workflow automation and decision support. Erlangen has become one of Europe's leading locations for medical imaging AI, supported by the university hospital and FAU's medical imaging research. AI in medical devices is regulated under the EU Medical Device Regulation and, for AI components, the high-risk requirements of the EU AI Act, so validation and documentation are core parts of the work.

## Audio, Sensors and Fraunhofer IIS

Fraunhofer IIS in Erlangen is one of the largest Fraunhofer institutes, known for audio coding technologies including MP3 and AAC. Today it works on audio and media technologies, positioning and localisation, sensor systems, communication and AI, including machine learning for signal processing and embedded AI. For engineers interested in audio, sensors and edge AI, it offers applied research roles with strong industry links.

## Siemens and Industrial Technology

Siemens has a large campus in Erlangen, with activities in industrial automation, energy and digital industries. AI applications include industrial automation, digital twins, energy systems and smart infrastructure.

## Sportswear and E-commerce in Herzogenaurach

adidas and PUMA both have their global headquarters in Herzogenaurach, a small town near Erlangen. Their data teams work on demand forecasting, supply chain optimisation, e-commerce personalisation, pricing, marketing analytics and product design support. Schaeffler, a global automotive and industrial supplier, is also headquartered in Herzogenaurach, applying AI to manufacturing, condition monitoring and vehicle components.

## Enterprise Software and Public Agencies in Nuremberg

DATEV, headquartered in Nuremberg, provides software and IT services for tax advisers, accountants and businesses in Germany, and increasingly uses AI for document processing and automation. Market research companies in Nuremberg analyse consumer data. The Federal Employment Agency and the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees have their headquarters in Nuremberg and employ data analysts and scientists for labour market analysis and public administration, under strict data protection and fairness requirements.

## FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg

Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU) is one of Germany's largest universities, with strong research in pattern recognition, medical imaging, machine learning, signal processing and engineering, and close collaboration with Siemens Healthineers, Fraunhofer and the university hospital.

## German Employment Conditions

German employment includes permanent contracts after probation, at least 20 statutory holiday days (commonly 28 to 30), social insurance and notice periods. Many large employers have collective agreements and works councils. English is widely used in medical imaging research, sportswear companies and research institutes; German matters in public agencies, enterprise software and daily life. EU citizens can work freely; non-EU professionals typically use the EU Blue Card.

## Living in Franconia

Nuremberg has a historic old town, a famous Christmas market and a strong local food and beer culture; Erlangen is a compact university city with a relaxed, cycling-friendly atmosphere. Housing is more affordable than in Munich, and the S-Bahn and regional trains connect the cities. Munich is about an hour away by high-speed train.

## Medical Imaging AI in Depth

Medical imaging AI covers the whole imaging chain. Reconstruction algorithms turn raw scanner data into images, and deep learning can reduce scan times or radiation dose while preserving quality. Detection and segmentation models find and outline organs, lesions or anomalies, supporting radiologists. Workflow tools automate repetitive steps such as positioning, protocol selection and report drafting. Decision support integrates imaging with other clinical data. Each application must be validated on diverse patient populations, scanners and clinical sites, and documented for regulators. Engineers work closely with physicists, clinicians and regulatory specialists. The combination of deep learning, physics and clinical impact makes Erlangen one of the most rewarding places in Europe for this work.

## Sportswear Data Science

Global sportswear companies face complex forecasting and supply chain challenges: thousands of products in many sizes and colours, seasonal collections, long production lead times and sales through stores, wholesale partners and online channels worldwide. Data scientists forecast demand, optimise inventory allocation, personalise e-commerce experiences, analyse marketing effectiveness and support sustainability efforts such as reducing overproduction. Language models help with product content and customer service. These companies work in English with international teams, making Herzogenaurach an unusually international small town.

## Public-Sector Data in Nuremberg

Federal agencies headquartered in Nuremberg analyse labour market data, support job matching and administer public services. Data scientists work on statistics, forecasting, matching algorithms and process automation, under strict data protection rules and with particular attention to fairness, because decisions affect people's livelihoods. Under the EU AI Act, AI systems used in employment and access to public services can be classified as high-risk, requiring careful governance. These roles typically require German.

## Audio and Sensor AI

Fraunhofer IIS applies ML to audio coding and enhancement, speech processing, localisation with radio signals, sensor systems for industry and health and embedded AI on low-power chips. Research engineers work on projects with companies worldwide, often combining signal processing expertise with deep learning.

Interviews usually include technical deep dives on past projects, ML fundamentals and domain-specific questions. ## Research Careers

FAU and Fraunhofer IIS offer PhD, postdoc and research engineer positions, often in collaboration with industry. The pattern recognition and medical imaging groups at FAU have strong international reputations.

## Enterprise Software and Document AI

Enterprise software for accounting, tax and payroll handles enormous volumes of documents and transactions. AI helps extract information from invoices and receipts, classify bookings, detect anomalies, answer questions over regulations and automate routine processes for accountants and businesses. Language models open new possibilities, but they must be accurate, explainable and compliant with strict data protection and professional standards, because errors can have legal and financial consequences. Engineers in this field combine NLP, document understanding and software engineering with careful evaluation. Nuremberg's enterprise software sector offers stable employers with large customer bases and long-term product roadmaps, making it attractive for engineers who value depth over hype. Some of these roles require German because customers, documents and regulations are German-language, but engineering teams increasingly include international members.

## Automotive and Industrial Supply

The automotive supplier headquartered in Herzogenaurach and other industrial companies in Franconia apply AI to bearings and components, electric drives, manufacturing quality and condition monitoring. Smart components report their condition, enabling predictive maintenance for customers. As the automotive industry electrifies, suppliers invest in new technologies and digital services, creating demand for data scientists and ML engineers who understand mechanical systems.

## Market Research and Consumer Data

Nuremberg has a long tradition in market research, with companies analysing consumer behaviour, retail sales and media consumption. Data scientists in this sector work on panel data, statistical modelling, forecasting and increasingly ML-based analytics, with strong attention to methodology and privacy.

## Responsible AI

Medical technology, public agencies and enterprise software all involve high-stakes decisions and sensitive data. Engineers who understand GDPR, the EU AI Act and rigorous validation are especially valued across Franconia's employers.

## Getting Around the Region

Nuremberg, Fürth and Erlangen form a closely connected urban area, linked by S-Bahn, regional trains and a U-Bahn line between Nuremberg and Fürth. Herzogenaurach, home to the sportswear and automotive headquarters, is reached mainly by bus and car, so employees there often live in Erlangen or Herzogenaurach itself. The Siemens and medical technology sites in Erlangen are well served by buses and cycling paths. Before choosing a home, check the commute to your employer's exact site; a flat in central Nuremberg suits jobs in the city, while Erlangen is more convenient for medical technology and Fraunhofer roles.
