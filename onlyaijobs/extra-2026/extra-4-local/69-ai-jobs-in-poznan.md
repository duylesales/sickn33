Poznań, halfway between Berlin and Warsaw, is one of Poland's most economically dynamic cities. It is the home of Allegro, Poland's largest e-commerce marketplace, and of one of Europe's significant supercomputing and networking centres. Automotive and pharmaceutical plants, logistics hubs along the Berlin–Warsaw corridor and international business service centres add depth to the local economy, while two major universities supply engineers and researchers. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Poznań offer large-scale e-commerce ML, high-performance computing, industrial AI and language research, in a city with a strong engineering culture and lower living costs than Warsaw. This guide explains the landscape and what to consider.

## E-commerce at National Scale

Allegro, headquartered in Poznań, is Poland's largest online marketplace, with millions of buyers and sellers. Marketplaces at this scale rely heavily on machine learning: search ranking, recommendations, advertising, pricing suggestions, fraud and abuse detection, product categorisation, image understanding and logistics. Allegro has invested in ML research and engineering and has published work on topics such as search, recommendation and language models for Polish. For ML engineers, this offers experience with very large datasets, a strong experimentation culture and products used by a large share of the country's population. Other e-commerce, retail and logistics companies in the region add further roles.

## Supercomputing and Networking

The Poznań Supercomputing and Networking Center (PSNC), affiliated with the Polish Academy of Sciences, operates high-performance computing systems and national research networks and participates in many European research infrastructure projects. It works on HPC, cloud, data management, digital libraries, quantum computing and AI infrastructure. For engineers interested in the infrastructure behind AI — distributed training, data platforms, research computing — PSNC offers rare opportunities in Poland.

## Automotive, Pharma and Manufacturing

Volkswagen has a large plant in Poznań producing commercial vehicles, and automotive suppliers operate across the region. GSK runs a pharmaceutical manufacturing site in Poznań, and food, furniture and consumer goods manufacturers are also present. AI applications include quality inspection, predictive maintenance, process optimisation, supply chain planning and, in pharma, validated analytics for manufacturing and quality control.

## Business Services and Logistics

International business service and technology centres in Poznań employ data engineers, analysts and data scientists working on finance, analytics and process automation for global operations. The city's position on the A2 motorway and rail links between Berlin and Warsaw makes it a logistics hub, with distribution centres and transport companies using forecasting and optimisation.

## Research and Education

Poznań University of Technology (PUT) is one of Poland's leading technical universities, with strong computing and AI research, including machine learning, computer vision, robotics and operations research. Adam Mickiewicz University (AMU) is one of Poland's largest universities, known for mathematics, computer science and linguistics, with research in natural language processing and machine translation for Polish. Together they supply a steady stream of graduates and researchers.

## Contracts, Pay and Language

As elsewhere in Poland, tech professionals often choose between an employment contract and a B2B contract; compare net income and protections and get tax advice before choosing B2B. Salaries for experienced ML engineers are high by Polish standards but below Western Europe; living costs in Poznań are lower than in Warsaw. English is common in tech teams; Polish helps in daily life and local organisations. EU citizens can work freely; non-EU professionals typically need a work and residence permit or the EU Blue Card.

## Living in Poznań

Poznań has a lively old town with a colourful market square, a large student population, lakes and parks within the city and an efficient tram network. Housing is more affordable than in Warsaw or Kraków, though prices have risen. Berlin is less than three hours by train, and Warsaw a little over two hours, which makes Poznań a convenient base in Central Europe.

## Marketplace ML: Search in a Morphologically Rich Language

Search and recommendation for a Polish marketplace involve challenges that English-centric systems rarely face. Polish is highly inflected, so a single product name can appear in many grammatical forms, and users type short, ambiguous queries with typos. Listings are written by millions of sellers with varying quality. Ranking models must balance relevance, price, delivery speed, seller reputation and personalisation, and they are evaluated through offline metrics and online experiments. Language models help with query understanding, product categorisation and attribute extraction, and increasingly with conversational shopping assistants. Fraud and abuse detection protects buyers and sellers. For ML engineers, this combination of scale, language complexity and business impact makes e-commerce in Poznań unusually rich.

## HPC and AI Infrastructure

Training and serving modern AI models depends on infrastructure: GPU clusters, fast networks, storage, schedulers and software stacks. Research computing centres such as PSNC build and operate this infrastructure for scientists and increasingly support AI projects, including participation in European initiatives to provide computing for AI research and industry. Engineers in this field work on performance optimisation, distributed training support, data management and cloud services for research. The skills — distributed systems, GPU computing, MLOps at scale — are in high demand across industry, including at companies training large models.

## Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Analytics

Pharmaceutical plants operate under good manufacturing practice, which requires validated computerised systems and rigorous documentation. Data science supports process monitoring, quality prediction, deviation analysis, maintenance planning and supply chain management. Models must be explainable and validated before they influence production decisions. Engineers who enjoy regulated environments and careful work find pharmaceutical analytics rewarding, and the experience transfers to medical devices and other regulated industries.

## Automotive Manufacturing Data

Commercial vehicle production involves complex assembly, painting and quality processes. Computer vision assists inspection, predictive maintenance reduces downtime and data analytics supports logistics and planning across suppliers. Many automotive groups are building shared data platforms, and Poznań plants take part in these efforts.

## Language Research at Adam Mickiewicz University

Adam Mickiewicz University has a long tradition in linguistics and computational linguistics, with research on machine translation, language resources and NLP for Polish. National initiatives to develop Polish language models have involved universities and research institutes across the country. For NLP engineers, Poznań offers research positions and collaborations with industry — including e-commerce companies that need strong Polish language understanding.

## Networking and Settling In

Poznań has an active tech community with meetups on machine learning, data science and Python, conferences and university events at PUT and AMU. Newcomers need a PESEL number, a Polish bank account and, for B2B contractors, registration of a business activity.

## Skills That Stand Out

Poznań employers value large-scale ML engineering, experimentation and search expertise for e-commerce; distributed systems and GPU computing for HPC; and time series, vision and validated analytics for manufacturing and pharma. English is expected in most tech roles, and Polish helps with career progression in local organisations. Engineers who understand Polish-language NLP have a particular advantage in e-commerce and public-sector projects.

## Who Poznań Suits Best

Poznań suits engineers who want large-scale product ML or infrastructure work in a lively, affordable city between Berlin and Warsaw, and who value a strong engineering culture.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews at Poznań tech companies usually include coding tasks, ML fundamentals, a system design discussion — for example designing a ranking or fraud-detection system — and a deep dive into a past project. Given Poland's algorithmic tradition, coding rounds can be demanding. E-commerce employers probe experimentation: how you choose metrics, detect novelty effects and interpret null results. HPC roles focus on systems knowledge, performance profiling and distributed computing. Manufacturing and pharma employers look for careful reasoning about data quality, validation and documentation. Prepare to explain trade-offs honestly and to ask about team ownership, deployment practices and mentoring. Many postings include salary ranges for both employment and B2B contracts, which makes salary discussions straightforward, but negotiation is expected for experienced candidates.

## Business Service Centres

International business service and technology centres in Poznań offer data roles in finance analytics, process automation, reporting and risk. They provide stable employment and international collaboration, often in English. Before joining, ask which systems the Poznań team owns and how career progression works locally; centres that own complete analytics products or services usually offer more interesting work.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Many Poznań employers offer hybrid work, and some engineers work remotely for companies abroad, including in Germany. Clarify employment status, tax and social security implications before accepting a remote role with a non-Polish employer.

## Evaluating an Offer

Compare net income under the offered contract form, benefits such as private healthcare and sports cards, training budgets and team ownership. A slightly lower salary in a team with strong mentoring and product impact is often the better long-term choice.

## Logistics Along the Berlin–Warsaw Corridor

Poznań's location on Europe's main east–west transport corridor has made the region a major logistics hub. Distribution centres for e-commerce and retail, parcel companies, freight forwarders and rail terminals operate around the city. Data science supports demand forecasting for warehouses, staff planning during seasonal peaks, picking and slotting optimisation, route planning for delivery vehicles and coordination of rail and road transport. E-commerce growth has increased the complexity of these operations, with next-day delivery expectations and large return volumes. For ML engineers, logistics roles offer concrete optimisation problems, immediate feedback on results and the chance to combine forecasting with operations research. The experience is highly transferable across Europe, where similar logistics challenges exist in every major distribution region, and it pairs well with e-commerce experience gained at marketplace companies in the city.

## A Final Tip

Visit the office and meet the team before accepting; Poznań's tech companies are close-knit, and team culture matters as much as the technology.

## Responsible AI in E-commerce and Industry

Marketplaces must comply with the EU Digital Services Act, including transparency about recommender systems, and all AI deployments must respect GDPR. In manufacturing and pharma, AI used as a safety component may fall under the EU AI Act's high-risk requirements. Engineers who understand these frameworks and can document and evaluate systems carefully bring clear value to Poznań employers.

Poznań's tech community is compact; join a meetup or two in your first month and your network will grow quickly.
