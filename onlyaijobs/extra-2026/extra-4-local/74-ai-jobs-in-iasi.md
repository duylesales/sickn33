Iași, in north-eastern Romania, is one of the country's oldest university cities and its largest centre in the historical region of Moldavia. With tens of thousands of students, two major universities and a growing number of technology companies, it has become Romania's third major tech hub after Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca. International companies run development centres in the city — including teams working on voice AI and automotive software — alongside cybersecurity, software services and product companies. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Iași offer a realistic entry into machine learning, often in English-speaking teams, with a cost of living below Bucharest and Cluj. This guide explains where to look and how to get started.

## Universities

**Alexandru Ioan Cuza University (UAIC).** Romania's oldest modern university has a Faculty of Computer Science with programmes in computer science, data science and AI, and research in machine learning, NLP, computer vision and security. Its graduates are a key source of talent for local companies.

**Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași (TUIASI).** The technical university trains computer, electronics and automation engineers, with research in embedded systems, robotics, control and AI applications.

Together, the universities produce thousands of graduates each year in technical fields, which is a major reason why companies choose Iași.

## International Development Centres

**Voice and language AI.** Amazon operates a development centre in Iași whose teams have worked on speech and language technologies, among other areas. For graduates, this offers exposure to large-scale ML systems, speech recognition, natural language understanding and rigorous engineering practices.

**Automotive software.** Continental has a large R&D presence in Iași working on automotive software and electronics, including driver assistance and vehicle systems. AI roles involve computer vision, sensor data, embedded ML and validation under automotive safety standards.

**Cybersecurity.** Bitdefender, the Romanian cybersecurity company, has an office in Iași, where engineers work on security products and threat analysis.

**Software services and product companies.** Many software services companies and product firms have offices in Iași, working on projects for clients in Western Europe and North America, including data engineering and AI projects.

## Entry Paths

1. **Internships.** Large companies in Iași run structured internship programmes, often in summer, that frequently lead to full-time offers.
2. **Junior roles in development centres** after graduation.
3. **Graduate programmes** in services companies, with training in specific technologies.
4. **Research assistant positions** at the universities.
5. **Data annotation and quality roles** in AI teams, which can be a first step into ML for some graduates, though it is important to choose roles with a clear path to engineering work.

## Pay and Cost of Living

Salaries in Iași's IT sector are high relative to local averages and have grown quickly, though generally lower than in Bucharest and below Western Europe. The cost of living, including rent, is lower than in Bucharest or Cluj-Napoca. Romania previously offered an income tax exemption for many IT professionals; this has been reduced and changed, so check current rules when comparing offers.

## Language

Romanian is the language of daily life; English is the working language in international development centres and most tech companies. International graduates can find English-speaking roles, and learning basic Romanian helps with daily life.

## Voice and Language AI: What Juniors Do

Working in a voice and language AI team as a junior engineer can involve many tasks: building tools that measure recognition accuracy across accents and noise conditions, processing large speech and text datasets, maintaining training pipelines, analysing errors, supporting multilingual expansion and helping deploy models into production. Over time, juniors take on model development and experimentation. The experience teaches rigorous evaluation, scalable data processing and collaboration in large global teams — skills that are valued throughout the AI industry. Privacy and responsible data handling are central in voice AI, because speech data can be sensitive.

## Automotive Software: Safety and Discipline

Automotive R&D teams in Iași develop software for vehicle systems, including functions that rely on cameras, radar and other sensors. Graduates in AI-related roles may work on perception algorithms, data tools for test drive recordings, simulation, validation or embedded optimisation. Automotive development follows strict processes and safety standards, so documentation, testing and traceability are part of daily work. This discipline is excellent preparation for any career in safety-critical AI and is increasingly relevant under the EU AI Act, which classifies AI used as safety components in certain regulated products as high-risk.

## Building a Profile for Iași Employers

- **Programming fundamentals**: Python, and C or C++ for automotive and systems roles.
- **Algorithms and data structures**: many interviews test these rigorously.
- **A focused ML project** with honest evaluation and clean code.
- **Linux, Git and basic cloud skills**.
- **English communication**, since most teams are international.

Student associations, hackathons and competitive programming contests are good ways to build skills and meet employers.

## Software Services as a Starting Point

Software services companies offer graduates exposure to many technologies and client projects, with structured training. They can be a good first step before moving into specialised AI roles in product companies or development centres.

## Questions to Ask Iași Employers

- What will I work on in my first six months, and who will mentor me?
- What is the working language of the team?
- How do interns and juniors progress into ML roles?
- What training and certification opportunities are offered?
- Is hybrid work possible?

## Living in Iași

Iași is a green, hilly city with a historic centre, many churches and cultural institutions, a large student population and a relaxed pace of life. Housing is affordable by Romanian and EU standards, though demand from students is high. The city is connected by train and air to Bucharest and other cities, and the Moldovan border is close.

## Networking

Iași has an active tech community with meetups, hackathons and conferences, often supported by the local tech cluster and companies. Universities host job fairs and company presentations. Attending events regularly is one of the best ways to find internships and junior roles.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews for internships and junior roles in Iași usually include online coding tests, technical interviews on algorithms, data structures and object-oriented programming, and questions on ML fundamentals for AI-related roles. Development centres of large companies may also include behavioural interviews based on specific situations from your studies or projects. Prepare by practising coding problems, revisiting your projects in depth and preparing concise stories about teamwork, challenges and learning. Clear communication in English and honest answers about what you don't know leave a better impression than overconfidence.

## Your First Two Years

Use your first years to build strong engineering foundations, experience with production systems and depth in one area such as speech, NLP, computer vision or data engineering. Ask for feedback regularly and document what you learn. These foundations prepare you for senior roles in Iași, Bucharest, Cluj or abroad.

## Staying in Iași or Moving

Many graduates from Iași have moved to Bucharest, Cluj or abroad in the past, but the growth of local development centres has made staying more attractive. A first job in Iași with strong mentoring and global products can be as valuable as a role in a larger city, while allowing you to benefit from lower living costs. Later, you can compare concrete offers based on net pay, cost of living and career growth.

## A Simple Action Plan

Apply to two or three internship programmes each year of your studies, build one strong project, attend local tech events and follow up with engineers you meet. Consistency and genuine curiosity are noticed in Iași's growing tech community.

## Cybersecurity as a Career Path

Iași's cybersecurity presence gives graduates a distinctive option. Security companies analyse malware, phishing, network attacks and fraud at global scale, and machine learning plays a central role in detecting threats quickly. Junior roles may involve analysing samples, building data pipelines for threat intelligence, evaluating detection models or developing tools for researchers. Over time, engineers can specialise in security ML, reverse engineering or threat research. The work is adversarial — attackers adapt constantly — which makes it intellectually demanding and keeps skills sharp. Security experience is valued across industries, from banking to cloud providers, and it pairs well with AI skills as the EU AI Act requires robustness and cybersecurity for high-risk AI systems. Graduates who enjoy puzzles, low-level systems and data analysis should consider this path seriously.

## Research at the Universities

UAIC and the Technical University offer research assistant and PhD positions in machine learning, NLP for Romanian, computer vision, robotics and security, often funded by national and European projects. Research experience helps graduates who want to work on advanced ML later, and collaborations between university groups and local companies create bridges between research and industry. Before committing to a PhD, talk to current doctoral students about supervision, funding and typical careers of graduates.

## Reading Graduate Offers

Compare contract type, gross monthly salary, meal vouchers, private health insurance, training budgets and hybrid policy. Ask how juniors are mentored and how quickly they can move into ML roles. For a first job, learning opportunities matter more than small salary differences.

## Responsible AI Skills for Graduates

Companies in Iași build AI systems used by customers across Europe and beyond, so they must comply with GDPR and the EU AI Act. Graduates who understand how to evaluate bias, document datasets and models, protect personal data and design systems with human oversight bring practical value from their first day. Voice data, in particular, can reveal sensitive information about speakers, and automotive systems affect safety, so responsible engineering practices are part of everyday work rather than an abstract topic. Taking a course or seminar on AI ethics and regulation during your studies, and mentioning relevant experience in interviews, can set you apart from other applicants.

## A Final Tip

Treat your internship as a long interview: deliver carefully, ask good questions and build relationships — many full-time offers in Iași start this way.

## Startups in Iași

Iași's startup scene is smaller than Bucharest's or Cluj's but growing, with founders from the city's universities and development centres building products in software, health tech and AI applications. Startup roles offer graduates broad responsibility and fast learning, with more uncertainty than large companies.

## Staying Connected

Keep in touch with classmates and former internship colleagues; Iași's tech community is close-knit and recommendations matter.

## Real example

### A UAIC graduate who started in voice AI

A computer science graduate from UAIC completed a summer internship at a voice AI development centre in Iași, working on evaluation tools for speech recognition. The internship led to a junior software engineer offer.

In her first two years, she moved from tooling to model evaluation and then to training pipelines, learning large-scale data processing and ML engineering practices. She stayed in Iași, where her rent is low and her family nearby. She says the internship was the turning point: it gave her access to world-class engineering practices without leaving her home city.
