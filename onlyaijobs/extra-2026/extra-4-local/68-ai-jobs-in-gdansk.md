Gdańsk, Gdynia and Sopot form the Tricity, a metropolitan area of around a million people on Poland's Baltic coast. Gdańsk is known for its Hanseatic old town and its role in modern European history; Gdynia for its port and modernist architecture; Sopot for its beaches and pier. Beyond tourism, the region has built a substantial technology sector: R&D centres of global technology companies, speech and language technology teams, finance and software companies, and industries linked to the sea — ports, shipbuilding and a fast-growing offshore wind sector. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Gdańsk and the Tricity offer hardware and software R&D, speech AI, logistics and energy analytics, with a quality of life shaped by the sea and forests. This guide explains the landscape.

## Technology R&D Centres

Intel has run a large R&D centre in Gdańsk for many years, working on software, drivers, platforms and technologies related to its products, including AI software stacks and performance optimisation. Amazon operates a development centre in Gdańsk known for work on speech and language technologies, including text-to-speech. Other international technology companies have engineering teams in the Tricity. These centres offer ML engineers work on global products, from optimising AI workloads on hardware to building speech and language models.

## Speech and Language Technology

The presence of speech technology teams has made the Tricity one of Europe's notable locations for text-to-speech, speech recognition and language modelling. Gdańsk University of Technology has long-standing research in multimedia and speech processing. For NLP and speech engineers, this is an unusual concentration of relevant roles outside the largest capitals.

## Ports, Logistics and Maritime

The ports of Gdańsk and Gdynia are among the largest on the Baltic Sea, handling containers, bulk goods and energy. Logistics companies, terminal operators and shipping services in the region use data for vessel traffic prediction, terminal planning, hinterland logistics and demand forecasting. Shipbuilding and ship repair yards and maritime technology companies add industrial data applications.

## Offshore Wind and Energy

Poland is developing large offshore wind farms in the Baltic Sea, and the Tricity is a hub for this sector, with installation ports, service companies, engineering firms and developers. Data science supports wind resource assessment, energy production forecasting, predictive maintenance of turbines and cables and optimisation of offshore operations. Energy companies, including refining and fuels operations in the region, also employ data scientists.

## Finance, Software and Startups

Business centres such as the Olivia Business Centre in Gdańsk host finance and technology centres of international companies, software houses and startups. Data roles include risk analytics, fraud detection, process automation and product ML. Gaming and e-commerce companies add further opportunities.

## Research and Education

Gdańsk University of Technology is one of Poland's leading technical universities, with strong electronics, telecommunications and computer science and research in AI, speech processing, computer vision and HPC. Its CI TASK centre provides supercomputing resources. The University of Gdańsk contributes mathematics, physics, economics and biotechnology, and the Medical University of Gdańsk supports health data research.

## Contracts, Pay and Language

Polish tech professionals often choose between employment contracts and B2B contracts; compare net income and protections carefully and get tax advice before choosing B2B. Salaries for experienced ML engineers are high by Polish standards but below Western Europe. English is the working language in R&D centres and most tech companies; Polish helps in daily life and in local organisations. EU citizens can work freely; non-EU professionals usually need a work and residence permit or the EU Blue Card.

## Living in the Tricity

The Tricity stretches along the coast, connected by the SKM commuter railway, which makes it easy to live in one city and work in another. Sopot and parts of Gdynia are close to beaches; Gdańsk has a historic centre and modern districts. Housing costs have risen but remain below Warsaw levels in many areas. Forests, beaches and the Kashubian lakes are close, and Gdańsk airport connects to many European cities.

## Speech Technology in Depth

Speech technology combines signal processing, deep learning and linguistics. Text-to-speech systems must generate natural, expressive voices in many languages and styles; speech recognition must handle accents, noise and domain vocabulary; and language models connect them in conversational systems. Engineers work on data collection and curation with voice talent, model architectures, evaluation with human listeners, efficiency for on-device deployment and responsible use, including safeguards against voice misuse. The EU AI Act includes transparency obligations for certain AI-generated content, which affects synthetic speech. For engineers who enjoy combining research and product quality, the Tricity's speech teams offer rare opportunities.

## AI Software and Hardware Optimisation

Working at a hardware company's software R&D centre means making AI run efficiently on specific processors and accelerators. Engineers optimise deep learning frameworks, develop compilers and libraries, profile workloads and tune models for performance and energy efficiency. This work sits at the intersection of ML, systems programming and computer architecture, and it is increasingly valuable as organisations seek to reduce the cost and energy use of AI. Experience in this area transfers to cloud providers, chip companies and teams training or serving large models.

## Offshore Wind Data Science

Offshore wind farms are complex, remote assets. Data scientists forecast power production using weather models and turbine data, detect early signs of component failure, plan maintenance windows around weather and vessel availability and analyse cable and foundation monitoring data. As Poland's first large Baltic offshore wind farms come online, operations and maintenance teams in the Tricity will need data expertise for decades. Engineers who combine time series modelling with an interest in energy can build long-term careers in this sector.

## Port and Logistics Analytics

Baltic ports handle growing volumes of containers and energy cargoes. Data science helps predict vessel arrivals, optimise berth and yard planning, coordinate rail and truck connections and forecast demand. Maritime data from ship tracking systems, weather and port operations provides rich material for forecasting and optimisation.

## Skills That Stand Out

Tricity employers value speech and audio expertise, systems programming and performance optimisation, time series and forecasting for energy and logistics, and strong software engineering practices. English is expected in R&D centres, and Polish helps in local companies. Engineers who can combine research depth with production quality are particularly sought after in speech and platform teams.

## Evaluating an Offer

Compare net income under the offered contract form, benefits such as private healthcare, sports cards and training budgets, and housing costs in your chosen part of the Tricity. A role close to an SKM station can save significant commuting time.

## Who the Tricity Suits Best

The Tricity suits engineers interested in speech technology, AI software optimisation, logistics or offshore energy who value living by the sea in a lively, historic region with lower costs than Western Europe.

## Research Careers

Gdańsk University of Technology offers PhD and research positions in speech and multimedia processing, computer vision, AI and high-performance computing, often in collaboration with industry. CI TASK's supercomputing resources support research that requires large-scale computing. The Medical University of Gdańsk and the University of Gdańsk offer opportunities in health data and bioinformatics. Research positions pay less than industry but provide scientific depth and connections with the region's R&D centres.

## Finance and Business Technology Centres

Gdańsk's business districts host finance and technology centres of international companies in banking, insurance, logistics and consumer goods. Data teams in these centres build reporting platforms, automate processes, develop risk and fraud models and increasingly apply language models to documents and customer communication. They offer stable employment, structured career paths and international collaboration, often in English. Before joining, ask which models and systems the Gdańsk team owns, where architects and managers sit and how career progression works locally. Centres that own complete services or analytics products tend to offer more interesting work and better growth than those that execute tasks defined elsewhere.

## Gaming and E-commerce

The Tricity also has game studios and e-commerce companies that use data science for player analytics, recommendations, pricing and fraud detection. These product companies often work in English and follow modern experimentation practices, offering ML engineers visible impact on user-facing products.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

Many Tricity employers offer hybrid arrangements, and some engineers work remotely for companies abroad. Clarify employment status, tax and social security implications before accepting a remote role with a non-Polish employer, and remember that local networks remain valuable for career growth.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews at Tricity R&D centres typically include coding rounds, ML fundamentals, a system design discussion and a deep dive into a past project. Speech teams may ask about signal processing, sequence models and evaluation with human listeners; platform teams about performance profiling, parallel computing and low-level optimisation; energy and logistics employers about forecasting, uncertainty and operational constraints. Given Poland's strong algorithmic tradition, coding interviews can be rigorous, so practise problem solving under time pressure. Prepare to discuss trade-offs honestly — for example why you chose a simpler model or how you handled noisy labels — and to ask thoughtful questions about team ownership, deployment and mentoring. Many companies publish salary ranges, so salary discussions are usually straightforward, but negotiation is expected for experienced candidates, especially for specialised skills such as speech or performance engineering.
