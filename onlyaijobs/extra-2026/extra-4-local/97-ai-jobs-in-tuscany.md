Tuscany is famous for art, landscapes and food, but it also has one of Italy's strongest concentrations of research in computer science, robotics and life sciences. Pisa is home to a leading university in computer science, two elite schools and a large national research area; Pontedera hosts a renowned robotics institute; Florence combines engineering, fashion and a European university for social sciences; and Siena has a life science cluster. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Tuscany offer research-oriented paths, robotics and industrial AI, fashion and energy analytics and health data work, in a region with a high quality of life. This guide explains where to look and how to get started.

## Pisa: A Computer Science Tradition

The University of Pisa has one of Italy's oldest traditions in computer science and offers programmes in computer science, AI, data science and computer engineering. Pisa also hosts the Scuola Normale Superiore and the Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies, two highly selective institutions with research in mathematics, physics, engineering and robotics. The CNR research area in Pisa is one of Italy's largest, including the Institute of Information Science and Technologies (ISTI-CNR), which conducts research in AI, data science, digital libraries and visual computing. Pisa coordinates Italy's national PhD programme in artificial intelligence, which brings together universities and research institutes across the country.

## Robotics in Pontedera

The BioRobotics Institute of the Sant'Anna School, located in Pontedera near Pisa, is internationally known for research in bio-inspired robotics, surgical and rehabilitation robotics, soft robotics and assistive technologies. Startups and companies in the area work on robotics for health, agriculture and industry. For graduates interested in robotics and AI, Pontedera offers research positions and spin-off opportunities rarely found elsewhere in Italy.

## Florence: Engineering, Fashion and Policy

Florence has a significant industrial base. Energy technology and turbomachinery companies have major operations in the city, with data science applied to equipment monitoring, predictive maintenance and digital services. Aerospace and defence electronics firms operate in the metropolitan area. Florence is also a centre of luxury fashion and leather goods, where data science supports demand forecasting, supply chains and customer analytics. The University of Florence has engineering and computer science research, and the European University Institute in nearby Fiesole conducts research on law, economics and policy, including the governance of technology.

## Siena: Life Sciences

Siena has a strong life science cluster, including vaccine research and development and Toscana Life Sciences, a foundation supporting biotech startups and research. The University of Siena has research in medicine, biotechnology and computer engineering. Data science roles involve bioinformatics, clinical data and lab automation.

## Entry Paths

1. **Master's thesis** with a research group or company.
2. **Research fellowships (assegni di ricerca)** and **PhD positions**, including through the national AI PhD programme.
3. **Internships (tirocini)** in companies and startups.
4. **Junior roles** in engineering, fashion, life sciences and consulting.

## Language and Practicalities

Italian is essential for daily life and most companies; research groups often work in English. Italian employment contracts are governed by national collective agreements, with salaries typically paid in 13 or 14 instalments and a severance fund (TFR). Research fellowships and PhD grants have their own conditions. Living costs in Pisa and Siena are lower than in Milan; Florence is more expensive, especially for housing.

## Research Careers in Pisa

Pisa offers a dense research environment for graduates who enjoy research. The University of Pisa, Scuola Normale, Sant'Anna School and CNR institutes host groups in machine learning, data mining, NLP, computer vision, robotics, high-performance computing and theoretical computer science. The national AI PhD programme offers doctoral positions across themes such as AI for health, industry, society and security. Research fellowships allow graduates to work on funded projects before or after a PhD. Early research careers in Italy often involve fixed-term positions, so it is worth discussing funding and prospects with supervisors. Research experience from Pisa is well regarded internationally and opens doors in industry and academia across Europe.

## Robotics and AI for Health

Robotics research in Pontedera focuses on applications that help people: surgical robots, prosthetics, exoskeletons for rehabilitation, robots for elderly care and bio-inspired machines. Machine learning supports perception, control and adaptation to users. Medical robotics must meet the EU Medical Device Regulation and, for AI components, high-risk requirements under the AI Act, so safety, validation and documentation are central. For graduates, this field combines engineering, AI and human impact.

## Energy and Industrial Analytics in Florence

Energy technology companies in Florence design and service equipment for power generation and industrial processes. Data scientists monitor equipment remotely, predict maintenance needs, optimise performance and develop digital services for customers worldwide. These roles combine time series analysis with engineering knowledge and offer stable employment.

## Fashion and Luxury Data

Florence's fashion and leather goods industry uses data for demand forecasting, inventory allocation, supply chain transparency and customer insights. Data roles in fashion combine analytics with an understanding of brand, craftsmanship and creative processes.

## Building a Profile

- **Strong fundamentals** in programming, statistics and ML.
- **A thesis with real-world relevance**.
- **Italian language skills** for industry roles.
- **Engineering habits**: version control, documentation.
- **Domain curiosity**: robotics, health, energy, fashion.

## Questions to Ask Tuscan Employers

- What will I work on in my first months, and who will mentor me?
- Is the position an employment contract, a research fellowship or an internship?
- What is the working language?
- Which collective agreement applies, and how many salary payments are there?
- What training opportunities are offered?

## Living in Tuscany

Pisa is a lively university town with a compact centre; Florence is a world-famous art city with higher living costs; Siena is a medieval city with a relaxed pace. Trains connect the cities, and the Tuscan coast and countryside are close. Quality of life is a major attraction.

## Networking

Universities, CNR and research institutes host seminars and events, and startup programmes in Pisa, Florence and Siena connect founders and graduates. Attending regularly helps find opportunities.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews for junior roles in Tuscany usually include a discussion of your thesis, technical questions on ML and programming and conversations about motivation. Research positions may involve presentations of your work. Prepare to explain your projects clearly, in Italian and English.

## Staying in Tuscany

Many graduates leave for Milan or abroad, but research institutions, robotics spin-offs and industry in Tuscany offer meaningful roles with a high quality of life. Research experience from Pisa is internationally recognised.

## A Simple Action Plan

Choose a thesis with a research group or company, apply for fellowships or the national AI PhD, attend local events and follow up with people you meet.

## Life Sciences and Vaccine Research in Siena

Siena's life science cluster has a long history in vaccine research and development, and today it includes pharmaceutical research sites, biotech startups supported by Toscana Life Sciences and university research in medicine and biotechnology. Data science supports analysis of experimental and clinical data, bioinformatics, immunology research, lab automation and manufacturing quality. For graduates with backgrounds in bioinformatics, statistics or computer science, Siena offers a focused environment where AI contributes to health research, and the small size of the city makes it easy to connect with researchers and companies. Work in this field requires rigour, careful documentation and respect for data protection rules governing health data. AI tools that influence clinical decisions may fall under the EU Medical Device Regulation and the high-risk requirements of the EU AI Act, so validation and transparency matter from the start. Graduates who combine biology knowledge with ML skills find their profile in demand not only in Tuscany but across European life science clusters.

## Data Science for Cultural Heritage and Tourism

Tuscany's cultural heritage and tourism industry offer unusual data science applications: digitising and analysing artworks and archives, 3D reconstruction of monuments, visitor flow management in crowded cities and demand forecasting for tourism. Research groups in Pisa and Florence, including visual computing teams at CNR, work on cultural heritage technologies. These roles are niche but distinctive.

## Software and Consulting

Software houses and consultancies in Tuscany deliver IT and data projects for companies in manufacturing, fashion, banking and public administration. They offer graduates exposure to varied projects and technologies and can be a good first step before specialising.

## Responsible AI

The European University Institute and Tuscan universities contribute research on the governance of technology, and graduates who understand GDPR and the EU AI Act bring practical value to employers in health, public services and industry.

## Reading Graduate Offers in Italy

When comparing offers, check the contract type (permanent, fixed-term, apprenticeship or internship), the applicable collective agreement, the gross annual salary (RAL) and how many instalments it is divided into, meal vouchers, welfare benefits and training opportunities. Research fellowships and PhD grants are paid as stipends with different social security and tax treatment, so understand their conditions before accepting. For a first role, mentoring, learning and the chance to work on meaningful problems matter more than small differences in salary. Italy's tax regime for young workers and for those relocating from abroad has changed over time; check current rules if relevant to your situation.

## Startups and Innovation

Tuscany's startup ecosystem includes robotics, biotech, digital health, fintech and software companies, often spun out of Pisa's universities and research centres or supported by incubators in Florence and Siena. Startups offer graduates broad responsibility and the chance to turn research into products, with more uncertainty than established companies.

## A Final Tip

Visit a research lab open day in Pisa or Pontedera; meeting researchers in person often leads to thesis projects and first positions.

## Real example

### A Pisa graduate who joined a robotics spin-off

A computer engineering graduate from the University of Pisa wrote her master's thesis on reinforcement learning for robotic grasping in collaboration with the BioRobotics Institute. She received offers from a Milan consultancy and from a Pontedera robotics spin-off developing assistive devices.

She chose the spin-off, where her thesis work continued directly and she could contribute to products helping patients. The salary was lower than in Milan, but her living costs were much lower too. After two years, she leads the perception software for a new device and says Tuscany gave her research depth and meaningful work without leaving the region.
