Gothenburg, Sweden's second-largest city, is the industrial heart of the Nordic region. It is home to the headquarters of both Volvo Cars and the Volvo Group, an electric car brand, an autonomous driving software company, a global bearings manufacturer and a major pharmaceutical R&D site, as well as Scandinavia's largest port. Chalmers University of Technology is one of Sweden's leading engineering schools, and AI Sweden, the national centre for applied AI, has its headquarters at Lindholmen Science Park. For experienced machine learning engineers, AI jobs in Gothenburg span automated driving, vehicle software, industrial AI, life sciences, logistics and applied AI projects. This guide explains the landscape and what to consider.

## Automotive and Autonomous Driving

Gothenburg is Sweden's automotive capital. Volvo Cars has its headquarters and main development centre in the city, and Polestar, the electric performance car brand, is also based there. The Volvo Group, which makes trucks, buses and construction equipment, is headquartered in Gothenburg. Zenseact, a software company owned by Volvo Cars, develops software for driver assistance and autonomous driving.

AI work in this cluster includes perception from cameras, radar and lidar, sensor fusion, prediction of other road users, planning, simulation-based testing, validation for safety, and data platforms that handle enormous volumes of driving data. Vehicle software also includes connected services, energy management for electric vehicles and predictive maintenance for trucks and fleets. Much of this work takes place under automotive safety standards, and the EU AI Act adds requirements for AI used as safety components in regulated products.

## Industry and Life Sciences

SKF, the global bearings and seals manufacturer, is headquartered in Gothenburg and uses data for condition monitoring, predictive maintenance and smart products. Other industrial companies in the region work on manufacturing, energy and marine technology. AstraZeneca has one of its major R&D centres in Mölndal, south of Gothenburg, where data science and AI support drug discovery, clinical development and imaging. Hospitals, including Sahlgrenska University Hospital, and the University of Gothenburg add health data research.

## Port and Logistics

The Port of Gothenburg is the largest port in Scandinavia, and logistics companies in the region handle a large share of Sweden's foreign trade. Data science supports terminal operations, forecasting, route planning and emissions reduction.

## AI Sweden and Research

AI Sweden, the national centre for applied AI, has its headquarters at Lindholmen Science Park in Gothenburg and brings together public and private partners on AI projects, including language models for Swedish and Nordic languages, data sharing initiatives and AI for public services. Chalmers University of Technology has strong research in machine learning, autonomous systems, computer vision and data science, and participates in the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP). The University of Gothenburg contributes research in computational linguistics, medicine and data science. Lindholmen Science Park hosts companies, research and education focused on mobility, digitalisation and media.

## Swedish Working Culture and Conditions

Swedish workplaces are flat and consensus-driven, with an emphasis on work-life balance, fika breaks and long summer holidays. Most employees are covered by collective agreements that regulate pay revisions, pensions and insurance. English is widely used in automotive and research teams; Swedish helps with integration and in some organisations.

## Permits and Housing

EU citizens can work freely and register to obtain a personal identity number. Non-EU professionals need a work permit from the Swedish Migration Agency or an EU Blue Card; rules and salary requirements have changed in recent years. Housing is a challenge, as in Stockholm: first-hand rental contracts are allocated through queues, and newcomers often start with second-hand rentals. Ask employers about relocation support.

## Living in Gothenburg

Gothenburg is known for its friendly atmosphere, canals, archipelago islands reachable by public transport, a strong food scene and an extensive tram network. It is more relaxed and generally more affordable than Stockholm. The west coast offers beaches, sailing and nature, and trains connect to Oslo, Copenhagen and Stockholm.

## Autonomous Driving Data at Scale

Developing driver assistance and autonomous driving systems requires collecting and processing huge amounts of sensor data from test fleets, labelling objects and scenarios, training perception and prediction models, and validating them against millions of kilometres of real and simulated driving. ML engineers work on data pipelines that select relevant scenarios, active learning to prioritise labelling, simulation to test rare situations and metrics that relate model performance to safety. Validation is central: showing that a system is safe enough requires systematic testing, traceability and close collaboration with safety engineers. Gothenburg's automotive cluster has deep experience in vehicle safety, which gives AI work there a distinctive emphasis on rigorous validation.

## Trucks, Fleets and Connected Vehicles

Commercial vehicles are increasingly connected, sending data on engine performance, fuel or energy use, location and driving behaviour. Data scientists at truck and bus manufacturers build models for predictive maintenance, fuel and energy optimisation, route planning, fleet management services and battery management for electric trucks. These applications combine time series, physics and logistics, and they directly affect customers' costs and emissions.

## Condition Monitoring and Smart Products

Industrial companies in Gothenburg use AI to monitor the condition of rotating machinery, detecting early signs of wear from vibration and other signals. These capabilities are increasingly sold as services, helping customers avoid unplanned downtime. Engineers in this field combine signal processing with ML and domain knowledge of mechanics.

## Drug Discovery and Life Sciences

Pharmaceutical R&D in Mölndal uses AI for target identification, molecular design, prediction of compound properties, analysis of imaging and omics data and optimisation of clinical trials. These projects combine chemistry, biology and ML, and they take place in regulated environments with high standards for validation and documentation.

## Applied AI for Society

AI Sweden's projects include building and evaluating language models for Swedish and other Nordic languages, enabling secure data sharing between organisations and supporting public-sector AI adoption. Engineers who join such initiatives work on AI infrastructure with broad societal impact.

## Questions to Ask Gothenburg Employers

- At which site will I work, and how is it connected by tram or bus?
- What is the working language of the team?
- Which collective agreement applies, and what pension and insurance benefits are included?
- How are safety and validation handled for ML components?
- What relocation support is offered, including temporary housing?

## Networking and Settling In

Gothenburg has an active tech community with meetups, events at Lindholmen Science Park and AI Sweden, and Chalmers seminars. Newcomers need a Swedish personal identity number for most services and should register with the Swedish Tax Agency early. Free Swedish language courses (SFI) are available.

## Skills That Stand Out

Gothenburg employers value perception and sensor fusion, simulation and validation, time series and condition monitoring, data engineering at scale, and experience with safety standards. English is common; Swedish helps.

## Preparing for Interviews

Swedish interviews are usually friendly and structured, with technical interviews, cases and conversations about collaboration. Automotive employers may ask about safety processes, testing strategies and handling large datasets. Expect questions about how you work in teams and make decisions collaboratively.

## Evaluating an Offer

Compare net pay after Swedish taxes, pension contributions under collective agreements, holidays and parental leave, and housing costs. Gothenburg is generally more affordable than Stockholm.

## Who Gothenburg Suits Best

Gothenburg suits engineers who want AI work in automotive, industry or life sciences, value a collaborative, balanced culture and appreciate a friendly coastal city with easy access to nature.

## Research Careers

Chalmers and the University of Gothenburg offer PhD and postdoc positions, often through WASP, with industrial PhD tracks in collaboration with companies. Swedish PhD positions are paid employment.

## Electric Vehicles and Battery Analytics

The shift to electric vehicles has transformed automotive engineering in Gothenburg. Battery systems require sophisticated monitoring: estimating state of charge and state of health, predicting degradation, optimising charging and managing thermal behaviour. Data scientists work with data from test benches, validation vehicles and customer fleets, combining physics-based models with machine learning. Energy management also extends to charging infrastructure and grid interaction, as electric vehicles become part of the energy system. For ML engineers, battery and energy analytics is one of the fastest-growing areas in automotive, and Gothenburg's car and truck manufacturers are at the centre of it. The experience transfers well to energy storage, grid services and other electrification industries across Europe.

## The Port and Sustainable Logistics

The Port of Gothenburg handles a large share of Scandinavian container traffic and works on digitalisation and emissions reduction. Logistics companies optimise rail shuttles to inland terminals, truck routing and warehouse operations. Data scientists support forecasting, planning and sustainability reporting — practical problems with measurable effects on costs and emissions.

## Startups and Lindholmen

Lindholmen Science Park and other hubs host startups in mobility, AI, media and software, often founded by engineers from the automotive industry or Chalmers. Startups offer broad responsibility and equity, and the region's strong industrial customers provide opportunities for pilots and partnerships.

## Responsible AI

Automotive safety, healthcare and public-sector AI all require careful governance. Swedish organisations generally take GDPR and the EU AI Act seriously, and engineers who can document, evaluate and explain their systems are valued.

## The Changing Automotive Landscape

The automotive industry is going through profound change — electrification, software-defined vehicles, new competitors and cost pressure — and Gothenburg feels these shifts directly. Demand for software, data and AI skills remains significant as manufacturers build in-house capabilities, but restructuring can affect individual units and projects. When evaluating an automotive role, ask how central the team is to the company's strategy, how it is funded and how its roadmap has evolved. Building experience across automotive, industry, energy or life sciences makes your career more resilient in a region so closely associated with vehicles, and Gothenburg's diverse employer base makes such moves possible without relocating.

## Settling In

Newcomers should register with the Swedish Tax Agency soon after arrival to obtain a personal identity number, which is needed for banking, BankID and healthcare. Joining the housing queue early, even if you start with a second-hand rental, improves your chances of a first-hand contract later.
