Lille, the capital of the Hauts-de-France region, sits at a crossroads: one hour from Paris, 35 minutes from Brussels and under an hour and a half from London by high-speed train. Once a textile and industrial city, it has reinvented itself as a centre for retail, e-commerce, cloud infrastructure and startups, with a large student population and one of Europe's biggest startup campuses. Several of France's major retailers have their headquarters in the Lille metropolitan area. For students and recent graduates, AI jobs in Lille offer a realistic entry into data science and machine learning, often through internships and work-study contracts, at a lower cost of living than Paris. This guide explains where to look and how to get started.

## Retail and E-commerce Headquarters

The Lille metropolitan area is home to the headquarters of several large retail groups, including Decathlon in Villeneuve-d'Ascq and Leroy Merlin in Lezennes, as well as other retail, fashion and e-commerce companies in Roubaix, Croix and the surrounding towns. These companies run large data and digital teams working on demand forecasting, supply chain optimisation, pricing, recommendations, personalisation, customer analytics and store operations. For graduates, retail data science is an accessible and varied field: problems are concrete, data is abundant and results are measurable.

## Cloud Infrastructure

OVHcloud, one of Europe's largest cloud providers, was founded in Roubaix and has its headquarters there. Cloud infrastructure companies need engineers in data engineering, infrastructure analytics, capacity forecasting, energy optimisation and increasingly AI platforms and services. The growing importance of European cloud and AI infrastructure makes this an interesting sector for graduates with systems interests.

## EuraTechnologies and the Startup Scene

EuraTechnologies, located in a converted textile mill in Lille, is one of Europe's largest startup campuses, hosting incubators, accelerators, startups and larger companies' innovation teams. Startups in Lille work on e-commerce, fintech, health, education, logistics and AI applications. Startup roles offer graduates broad responsibility and fast learning.

## Research and Education

**University of Lille and Inria.** The University of Lille is one of France's largest universities. The CRIStAL laboratory (research centre in computer science, signal and automatic control) and Inria's Lille centre conduct research in machine learning, reinforcement learning, data science, robotics and human–computer interaction. Inria Lille is known for research on sequential decision-making and reinforcement learning.

**Engineering and business schools.** Centrale Lille, IMT Nord Europe, JUNIA and other engineering schools train engineers, and business schools such as EDHEC, IÉSEG and SKEMA (with a Lille campus) train managers with growing emphasis on data and analytics.

## Entry Paths: Internships and Work-Study

In France, most engineering and business school students complete internships (stages) as part of their degree, typically ending with a five- to six-month final internship. Work-study contracts (alternance), in which students alternate between school and a company, are widespread and often lead to permanent jobs. In Lille, retail groups, cloud companies, consultancies and startups offer many internships and work-study positions in data. Choosing these carefully — with real projects and good mentoring — is one of the most important decisions for a graduate's first job.

France's CIFRE scheme also allows companies to employ PhD students supervised by academic labs, which is used in Lille for research projects in retail, logistics and AI.

## French Graduate Conditions

Graduates from engineering schools are usually hired on permanent contracts (CDI) with cadre status, often with a days-based working-time arrangement and additional rest days (RTT). Salaries are expressed as annual gross amounts; net pay is lower after social contributions. Benefits commonly include meal vouchers or a canteen, supplementary health insurance, profit-sharing and reimbursement of half the cost of public transport passes.

## Language

French is essential for daily life and for most employers in Lille. International companies, research labs and some startups work in English. Proximity to Belgium means Dutch can be an asset for some roles.

## Retail Data Science in Practice

Retail offers graduates a broad range of data problems. Demand forecasting must handle thousands of products across hundreds of stores and online channels, with seasonality, promotions, weather and local events. Supply chain teams optimise inventory, replenishment and warehouse operations. Pricing teams analyse price elasticity and competition. Marketing teams personalise offers and measure campaign impact. E-commerce teams work on search, recommendations and conversion. Increasingly, retailers use language models for product descriptions, customer service and internal knowledge. For graduates, the advantage of retail is that results are visible quickly — a better forecast reduces stock-outs and waste — and that retail data teams in Lille are large enough to offer mentoring and career paths.

## Cloud, Infrastructure and AI Platforms

Working for a cloud provider exposes graduates to data problems at infrastructure scale: forecasting capacity needs in data centres, predicting hardware failures, optimising energy consumption and cooling, detecting abuse and fraud, and building managed AI services for customers. As European organisations look for cloud and AI infrastructure that meets EU data protection requirements, cloud providers based in Europe play a growing role. Graduates with interests in systems, distributed computing and MLOps can build valuable careers in this sector, starting from Lille.

## Reinforcement Learning and Research in Lille

Inria Lille and the CRIStAL laboratory have a notable research tradition in sequential decision-making, bandits and reinforcement learning — methods used in recommendation, online advertising, robotics and resource allocation. For graduates interested in research, Lille offers PhD positions in these areas, often in collaboration with companies. Research experience in reinforcement learning is valued by companies working on personalisation, pricing and optimisation.

## Building a Profile for Lille Employers

- **Solid foundations** in statistics, Python, SQL and machine learning.
- **A retail or business-oriented project**, for example forecasting or customer segmentation with public data.
- **Engineering habits**: version control, clean code and basic cloud skills.
- **Communication**: explaining results to business teams in French.
- **Internship or work-study experience** with real projects.

## Living in Lille

Lille is a lively, friendly city with Flemish-influenced architecture, a strong food and beer culture and a large student population. Housing is considerably more affordable than in Paris, and the metropolitan area includes Roubaix, Tourcoing and Villeneuve-d'Ascq, connected by automated metro lines, trams and buses. The city's rail connections are exceptional: Paris in about an hour, Brussels in 35 minutes and London in under an hour and a half. For graduates, this makes Lille an attractive base with access to several major job markets.

## Questions to Ask Lille Employers

- What projects will I work on during the internship or work-study contract?
- Who will mentor me, and how often will I receive feedback?
- What happened to previous interns or work-study students — were they hired?
- Which collective agreement applies, and what is the cadre working-time arrangement?
- What is the working language, and is English used in the team?

## Your First Two Years

Use your first years to build strong foundations: clean code, reliable data pipelines, careful evaluation and clear communication with business teams. Choose a domain to deepen — retail, supply chain, cloud or finance — and learn how models reach production and are monitored. Stay connected with your school and with EuraTechnologies' community, which links startups, corporates and investors across the region.

## Cross-Border Opportunities

Lille's proximity to Belgium creates cross-border opportunities: Kortrijk, Ghent and Brussels are within reach, and some people live on one side of the border and work on the other. Cross-border work involves tax and social security questions that should be checked with official information services before accepting an offer.

## Preparing for Interviews

Interviews for junior data roles in Lille usually include a conversation about motivation and your school projects, a technical test on Python, SQL and machine learning basics, and often a business case — for example forecasting sales for a new product or analysing why a promotion underperformed. Retail companies look for curiosity about their business and the ability to translate data into recommendations. Prepare by explaining your projects clearly, including limitations, and by reading about the company's products and strategy. Asking thoughtful questions about the data team's organisation and how models are used in decisions leaves a good impression and helps you assess whether the role offers the learning you need.

## Networking in Lille

EuraTechnologies hosts regular events, meetups and demo days, and data science and Python meetups bring together students and professionals. Engineering and business schools organise company forums where retail groups and startups recruit interns. Attending regularly helps you learn which teams are growing and meet people who can recommend you.

## Who Lille Suits Best

Lille suits graduates who want accessible entry into data science through internships and work-study, enjoy concrete business problems and value an affordable, lively city with fast connections to Paris, Brussels and London.

## Responsible AI in Retail

Retailers use customer data extensively, so GDPR compliance, transparency about personalisation and fairness in pricing and marketing matter. Graduates who understand these issues bring practical value, and awareness of the EU AI Act helps when retailers use AI in areas such as recruitment of store staff, which can be classified as high-risk.

## Consulting and IT Services

Lille has a large consulting and IT services sector, with firms that serve retail, banking, insurance, logistics and public-sector clients across the north of France and Belgium. For graduates, consulting roles offer rapid exposure to different industries, technologies and data problems, as well as structured training programmes. A junior data consultant might build a forecasting model for a retailer in one project, a customer churn analysis for an insurer in the next and a data platform migration after that. The pace can be demanding, and long-term ownership of products is limited, but the breadth of experience is valuable. Many graduates spend two or three years in consulting before joining an in-house team in the sector they enjoyed most — a path that works well in Lille, where consulting firms and retail headquarters are close together.

## Logistics in Hauts-de-France

The region's position between Paris, Belgium and the Channel ports makes it a major logistics hub, with distribution centres and transport companies using data for forecasting, warehouse optimisation and route planning — another accessible field for data graduates.

## A Final Tip

Ask each internship host what the previous intern built and whether they were hired; the answer tells you more than any brochure.

## Real example

### A work-study student who joined a retail data team

A master's student in data science at a Lille engineering school chose a work-study contract with a retail group's data team, alternating between school and the company for two years. She worked on demand forecasting for regional stores, learning to clean messy sales data, account for promotions and weather and present results to supply chain managers.

At the end of her studies, the company offered her a permanent position as a data scientist. She accepted, attracted by the team, the concrete impact of her work and Lille's affordable rents. She says her work-study years gave her an advantage over classmates who only had short internships: she already knew the company, its data and its people.
