## 1. `/pages/about-us`

### EN

**About OnlyAIJobs**

OnlyAIJobs started with a simple observation: too many job seekers from Noord-Brabant were leaving for the Randstad — not because they wanted to, but because they didn't know which good companies were operating right in their own region.

We built OnlyAIJobs to fix that specifically for AI, ML, and Data roles. Every vacancy on the platform shows you the exact address and the distance from your home — not just a city name. If there's a company hiring for the role you want ten minutes from where you live, you should be able to see that in one search.

We're a specialist board, not a general one. Every listing here is an AI, ML, or Data role — nothing else. That means less scrolling through unrelated jobs for you, and a level playing field for the companies posting: a small scale-up shows up next to a large enterprise with no advertising budget deciding who gets seen first.

**Who's behind this**

**Questions?** Reach us at [info@onlyaijobs.eu](mailto:info@onlyaijobs.eu).

## 2. `/pages/info-for-employers`

### EN

**Post your AI vacancy — free**

Your first vacancy on OnlyAIJobs is free. No account, no contract, no self-serve dashboard to figure out.

**How it works**
1. Email a link to the job listing on your own website to [info@onlyaijobs.eu](mailto:info@onlyaijobs.eu).
2. We publish it on the platform, categorized under the right AI/ML/Data specialization and shown with an exact location — not just a city.
3. Candidates browsing by city, category, or distance from home see your listing alongside companies of any size, on equal footing.

**Why AI/ML/Data teams post here**
- **A specialist audience.** Everyone browsing OnlyAIJobs is looking specifically for AI, Machine Learning, or Data roles — not general tech candidates skimming past your posting.
- **A level playing field.** There's no bidding for placement. A five-person AI startup and an enterprise team appear the same way — visibility isn't for sale.
- **Regional reach that general boards miss.** Candidates search by distance from home, which surfaces roles outside Amsterdam that often get buried on national boards.

**Companies already hiring here** include Accenture, Cegeka, Sendcloud, Mollie, Heijmans, VINCI Energies, and other teams ranging from enterprise to scale-up. *(Companies listed reflect vacancies live on the platform at time of writing.)*

**Posting a second vacancy, or hiring at volume?**
*[🟥 Cần bổ sung: mô hình giá cho tin thứ 2 trở đi — chưa có trang giá, chưa chốt. Tạm thời: "Email us at info@onlyaijobs.eu to talk about posting more than one role."]*

[**Email us your vacancy →**](mailto:info@onlyaijobs.eu)

## 3. `/pages/info-for-job-seekers`

### EN

**Find AI jobs near you — not just "in the Randstad"**

OnlyAIJobs shows you AI, Machine Learning, and Data vacancies at the exact address, with the distance from your home — so you can see what's actually close by before assuming you have to move to Amsterdam or Utrecht.

**How to use it**
- **Filter by city, category, or job type** (full-time, part-time, internship, remote) to narrow down fast.
- **Sort by distance from home** to find roles you didn't know existed nearby.
- **Save vacancies** you're interested in and come back to them later.
- When you're ready, you're taken straight to the company's own application page — no extra account needed on our end.

**Why a specialist board**
General job boards mix AI roles in with everything else, and companies with bigger ad budgets get pushed to the top regardless of fit. Here, every listing is AI/ML/Data, and every company — large or small — is shown the same way.

**No account required.** Browse, filter, and apply directly — nothing to sign up for.

[**Browse AI jobs →**](/jobs)

### NL

**Vind AI-vacatures bij jou in de buurt — niet alleen "in de Randstad"**

OnlyAIJobs toont AI-, Machine Learning- en Data-vacatures op het exacte adres, met de afstand tot je huis — zodat je ziet wat er écht dichtbij is, voordat je aanneemt dat je naar Amsterdam of Utrecht moet verhuizen.

**Zo gebruik je het**
- **Filter op stad, categorie of dienstverband** (fulltime, parttime, stage, remote) om snel te selecteren.
- **Sorteer op afstand tot huis** om rollen te vinden waarvan je niet wist dat ze in de buurt bestonden.
- **Bewaar vacatures** die je interesseren en kom er later op terug.

**Waarom een specialistisch platform**
Algemene vacaturesites mixen AI-functies met alles daaromheen, en bedrijven met een groter advertentiebudget komen bovenaan te staan, los van of de vacature bij je past. Hier is elke vacature AI/ML/Data, en wordt elk bedrijf — groot of klein — op dezelfde manier getoond.

**Geen account nodig.** Bekijk, filter en solliciteer direct — nergens voor aanmelden.
