🚨 A full rewrite promises a clean slate. It usually delivers an 18-month project quietly becoming 24 months — while the business runs on an increasingly unmaintained old system the entire time. 🏭⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **All-or-Nothing Value:** Nothing usable until the entire multi-month rewrite finishes.
❌ **Dual-System Burden:** Supporting the legacy system AND building the new one, simultaneously.
❌ **Concentrated Cutover Risk:** One big-bang replacement instead of risk spread across smaller stages.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Strangler-Pattern Modernization:** Highest-value components modernized incrementally, behind a compatibility layer.
✅ **Value Delivered at Every Stage:** Real wins within weeks, not a promise 18 months out.
✅ **Distributed, Manageable Risk:** Smaller cutovers — a problem in one piece doesn't jeopardize the rest.

Modernization that reduces risk instead of concentrating it. 🛡️
