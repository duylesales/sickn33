❌ **Hidden Workaround Tax:** A tool that fits 80% of your process costs you the other 20% every single day, forever.
❌ **Optimistic Build Estimates:** Custom-build costs quoted to win the deal, not to reflect reality.
❌ **One-Year Thinking:** Build-vs-buy spreadsheets that miss the compounding cost — or value — over time.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Honest Operational-Cost Modeling:** Subscription fee plus real workaround time cost, priced explicitly.
✅ **Realistic Custom-Build Pricing:** Governance-inclusive estimates, not optimistic sales numbers.
✅ **Multi-Year Comparison:** The true cost of fit — or friction — evaluated over years, not one budget cycle.
