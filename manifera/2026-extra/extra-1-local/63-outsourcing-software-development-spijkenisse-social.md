🚨 You outsource software development expecting a faster version of what your team would build. Six weeks later: technically functional code nobody in-house would have architected that way. ⚙️💥

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Shared Definition of Done:** Test coverage, review process, architecture conventions — never documented, never agreed before sprint one.
❌ **Separate Review Pipeline:** Outsourced code ships through a different, lower-bar process than internal work.
❌ **Standards Erosion Under Deadline:** Without empowered technical leadership, quality quietly slips sprint by sprint.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Aligned Definition of Done, Before Sprint One:** Amsterdam-based leads set the shared standard and verify it's held.
✅ **Same Review Pipeline as Internal Code:** No separate, lower-bar process — ever.
✅ **Named, Empowered Tech Lead:** Ho Chi Minh City pods led by someone who can push back on requests that would break the standard.

Outsourced doesn't have to mean inconsistent. 🛡️
