Four acquisitions in eighteen months. Four ERPs, three support platforms, eleven hosting contracts — and an operating partner ready to standardize all of it by the next board meeting. 📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Forced Migration on Embedded Systems:** Ripping out a system the acquired team has built five years of workflow around, just to tidy the portfolio vendor list, costs more than it saves.
❌ **Hidden Exit Penalties:** Multi-year contracts with auto-renewal and minimum commitments can make a standardization timeline financially impossible until someone actually reads the termination clause.
❌ **Ambiguous Authority:** Without a defined council deciding what standardizes and when, mandates stall through quiet inaction at the operating-company level.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Sequenced Standardization:** Commodity categories consolidate fast; embedded platforms align to natural renewal cycles instead of forced early exits.
✅ **Contract-Exit Awareness Built In:** Migration business cases weigh real termination penalties against projected savings, not just list price.
✅ **One Delivery Relationship, Multiple Portfolio Companies:** A single development partner can absorb custom work across the portfolio without forcing premature platform migration.

Standardization is a real lever — but only when it's sequenced against switching economics, not slide-deck tidiness. 🎯
