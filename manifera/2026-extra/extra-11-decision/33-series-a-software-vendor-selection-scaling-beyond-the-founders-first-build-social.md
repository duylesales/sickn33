The first engineer hired after a €10M Series A usually spends two weeks just reading code — trying to understand an MVP built for speed to a demo, not for a team of fifteen. 🧩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Tribal-Knowledge Codebase:** Thin test coverage and loose architecture were the right call at seed stage — and a direct liability the moment the team scales past four or five engineers.
❌ **Ramp Time That Won't Budge:** New hires taking over a month to ship an independent change is a clear signal the codebase needs remediation, not just more headcount.
❌ **The Hard-Cutover Gap:** Switching vendors with no overlap period routinely costs 6-10 weeks of velocity while the new team rebuilds lost context.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Structured Debt Audits:** Test coverage, CI/CD maturity, infrastructure-as-code, and documentation, scored against your actual growth plan.
✅ **Process Rigor That Scales:** Defined sprint cadences, code review participation, and SLA-grade reliability — not seed-stage speed alone.
✅ **Overlap-Based Transitions:** 4-8 week handoff periods with documentation tied to final payment, so velocity doesn't collapse mid-switch.

Keep, expand, or replace — it's an engineering leadership decision, not a default to inertia. ⚙️
