Fourteen internal apps. One low-code platform. A seat bill that quietly passed what a mid-level engineer costs. Nobody noticed the threshold until it hurt. 🧩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The silent ceiling:** Low-code is the right call for tool one and quietly the wrong call by tool nine — and nobody re-evaluates once a tool ships.
❌ **Fake portability:** Some platforms store your logic in a proprietary format that only runs inside them — "leaving" means rebuilding from scratch, not exporting code.
❌ **Seat cost creep:** Per-seat pricing that looked cheap for five reviewers gets expensive fast rolled out to forty warehouse staff across a dozen tools.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A real graduation framework:** We help teams define the specific signals — complexity, criticality, workaround cost — that mean a tool should move from low-code to custom.
✅ **Portability-first evaluation:** Before you adopt a low-code vendor, we help you ask the one question that matters: what do you actually own if you leave?
✅ **Clean migration when it's time:** When a tool outgrows its platform, we extract the accumulated business logic into a proper custom build without losing years of institutional knowledge.

Low-code isn't a strategy — it's a starting point that needs a plan for when it stops working.
