**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"E-signature compliant" as a blanket claim:** eIDAS has three legally distinct tiers with different evidentiary weight — vendors rarely specify which one they actually support.
❌ **Manual signature-tier selection:** Without a rules engine tied to contract type and jurisdiction, high-stakes contracts can slip through with a signature level too weak to hold up.
❌ **Audit trails that aren't cryptographically sealed:** Some "tamper-evident" logs are just separate files that could be edited independently of the signed document.

✅ **Tier-by-tier verification:** We confirm exactly which eIDAS tier (SES, AES, QES) and US ESIGN/UETA evidentiary elements the platform captures — not the marketing summary.
✅ **Rules-engine review:** We check whether signature standards apply automatically by contract type and counterparty jurisdiction, not manual per-document guesswork.
✅ **Long-term validation checks:** For multi-year contracts, we confirm signatures stay verifiable after the original signing certificate expires.
