Your new proptech vendor demoed leasing beautifully. Nobody asked what "real-time" access control sync actually meant until residents got locked out on move-in day. 🔑

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Batch-interval IoT sync:** Smart lock credentials update every 15 minutes instead of instantly, so move-ins and move-outs create real access gaps.
❌ **One-way work order integration:** Maintenance tickets show closed in the facilities system and open in the PM platform, with nobody sure which is true.
❌ **Migration that skips trust accounting detail:** Vendors promise "seamless migration" but only move current balances, not the multi-year ledger history auditors require.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Sandbox-first validation:** Test accounting sync, credential activation, and work order flow in a non-production environment before a single tenant record moves.
✅ **API rate limits and latency in writing:** No integration commitment gets accepted on a verbal claim — documented SLAs only.
✅ **Reference checks at matching scale:** We push vendors for customers who migrated a similar unit count in the last 12 months, then ask what broke.

A polished leasing demo tells you nothing about whether your smart locks and your GL will actually agree with each other. 🏢
