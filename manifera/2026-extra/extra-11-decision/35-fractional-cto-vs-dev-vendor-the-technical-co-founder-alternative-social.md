"Give up 20% equity for a technical co-founder, or hire a dev shop blind" isn't actually the choice most non-technical founders face. There's a third option most skip. 🤔

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Negotiating Blind:** Without any technical oversight, evaluating a vendor's quote, architecture, or timeline is guesswork — and it's exactly how scope creep and quality issues go unnoticed.
❌ **The Equity Math:** 20% co-founder equity on a company that later raises at a €6M valuation is roughly €1.2M in deferred value for one year of work.
❌ **The False Binary:** Treating "co-founder or vendor" as the only two options skips the option that actually solves the judgment gap.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Fractional CTO + Vendor:** Technical strategy and vendor oversight from a fractional CTO, paired with a dedicated team handling execution.
✅ **Zero-Dilution Judgment:** €4K-€10K/month buys architecture review, vendor vetting, and hiring support — no equity given up.
✅ **Structured From Day One:** Fractional CTOs involved in vendor selection from the start set up the accountability that keeps engagements on track.

Most founders don't need a technical co-founder. They need someone technical in the room before they sign a vendor contract. 🎯
