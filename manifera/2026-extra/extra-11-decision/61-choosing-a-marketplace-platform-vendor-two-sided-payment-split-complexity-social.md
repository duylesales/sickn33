Your marketplace demo showed a clean 90/10 split. Nobody showed you what happens when a buyer disputes the charge six weeks after the seller AND the delivery partner already got paid. 💸

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Chargeback black hole:** Reversed transactions across a three-way split (platform, seller, delivery partner) often require manual clawback because the vendor's tooling was only built for two parties.
❌ **Tax liability mismatch:** Marketplace facilitator laws shift sales tax remittance to the platform, but many vendors only calculate tax at the order level, not per split — producing errors you find in Q1 filing season.
❌ **Payout policy is invisible:** Rolling reserves of 5-15% held for 30-90 days catch sellers off guard when the vendor doesn't expose the policy anywhere before onboarding.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Sandbox-tested split logic:** We push vendors to simulate a real three-party split with a mid-cycle chargeback before you sign — not a happy-path demo.
✅ **Reconciliation layer audit:** When a vendor's native tooling can't handle your commission tiers or multi-party payouts, we build the reconciliation layer on top instead of forcing a full replatform.
✅ **Cross-border payout mapping:** We verify local payout rails (SEPA, ACH, PIX) and FX lock timing before you commit sellers in multiple countries to a vendor that only does wire transfers.
