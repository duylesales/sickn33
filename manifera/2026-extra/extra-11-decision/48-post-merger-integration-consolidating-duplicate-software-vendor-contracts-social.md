Two CRMs, three project management tools, two dev vendors — combined software spend 40% higher than either finance team modeled during deal planning. Nobody had inventoried the overlap. 📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Consolidation Timed to the Fiscal Year, Not Readiness:** Forcing a migration around a synergy-reporting deadline instead of contract-exit windows disrupts teams and erodes the projected savings.
❌ **The License True-Up Trap:** Assuming combined license cost is just the sum of both legacy counts ignores redundancy overlap and vendor repricing at higher volume tiers.
❌ **Default-to-Incumbent Bias:** Assuming the acquirer's legacy vendor automatically wins skips a real evaluation the combined entity's needs actually deserve.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Full Contract Inventory First:** Every vendor, including department-level and shadow IT contracts, mapped before any consolidation decision is made.
✅ **Exit-Cost-Sequenced Migration:** Contracts consolidate in order of exit penalty, renewal timing, and disruption risk — not reporting-cycle convenience.
✅ **A Real Fresh Evaluation:** Both legacy vendor relationships assessed against the combined entity's actual needs, with flexible delivery capacity ready either way.

Consolidation savings are real — but only if the sequencing respects the contracts, not the board deck. 📄
