A VC-backed founder spends someone else's money against a runway clock. A bootstrapped founder spends this month's revenue — and that difference should change almost every term in a vendor contract. 💰

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Upfront Payment Mismatch:** Standard 30-50% upfront terms assume a lump sum of runway sitting in the bank — not a cash position tied to this month's actual sales.
❌ **The Fixed-Price Concentration Risk:** One large contract concentrates payment into one or two big outlays that have to land regardless of what revenue does that quarter.
❌ **Scoping Against Hope:** Sizing a build against projected future revenue instead of trailing actuals is how a healthy business gets blindsided by its own vendor invoice.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Milestone-Tied Payment:** Pay as working increments deliver, smoothing cost against your own revenue timing.
✅ **Phased, Sequential Builds:** Smaller phases with natural pause points instead of one large fixed-price commitment.
✅ **Flexible Monthly Capacity:** Scale hours up or down month to month without breaching a contract when revenue dips.

Cash-flow-safe doesn't mean cheap — it means payment timing that matches how your business actually makes money. 📊
