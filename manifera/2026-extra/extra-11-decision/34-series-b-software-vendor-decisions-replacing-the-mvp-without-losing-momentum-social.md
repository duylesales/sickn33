**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Second System Effect:** Rewrite scope creeps as teams try to fix every known limitation at once instead of the one actually blocking growth.
❌ **The Parity Trap:** Years of undocumented edge-case handling in the old system routinely double the estimated rewrite timeline.
❌ **The Feature Freeze:** Every month heads-down on a full rewrite is a month competitors keep shipping and you don't.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Strangler-Fig Migration:** Replace the system piece by piece behind a stable interface, validated against real production traffic before each cutover.
✅ **Parallel-Run Safety:** Start at 1-5% traffic with automated output comparison and a tested rollback path at every stage.
✅ **Dedicated Migration Track:** A separate team runs the replatforming so your core roadmap keeps shipping the whole way through.
