"Digital twin" means two completely different products, and vendors rarely say which one they're selling you. One predicts. One just looks good in a demo. 🏭

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Dashboard dressed as prediction:** Some "twins" are just live telemetry in 3D — zero underlying simulation model, zero forecasting ability beyond what's already been observed.
❌ **Unvalidated confidence:** Vendors claim predictive accuracy with no quantified error rate against real ground truth, and no defined recalibration trigger as the asset ages.
❌ **Silent connectivity gaps:** Factory-floor network interruptions are common, not exceptional — but many platforms interpolate missing data silently instead of flagging the gap.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Fidelity-first evaluation:** We push vendors to demonstrate validated output for scenarios outside their training data, not replay historical data dressed as prediction.
✅ **Calibration you can audit:** We require quantified error bounds against ground truth and a defined drift-detection trigger before trusting any model's forecasts.
✅ **Resilient sync architecture:** We evaluate OPC-UA, MQTT, and legacy PLC gateway options specifically for how they handle — and flag — connectivity gaps.

A well-rendered dashboard isn't a digital twin. Ask for the validation data before you believe the pitch.
