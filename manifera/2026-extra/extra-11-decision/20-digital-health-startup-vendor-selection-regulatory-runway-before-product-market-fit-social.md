❌ **The Silent Retrofit Bomb:** Skip classification now, and it resurfaces as an investor's due diligence finding you can't undo.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Classification Before Architecture:** We ask the intended-use question before a single sprint gets planned — not after.
✅ **CDS-Exemption-Aware Design:** When it's a legitimate fit, we architect the product so recommendations stay clinician-reviewable, keeping you off the SaMD pathway.
✅ **Deferred, Not Ignored:** We build the classification discipline in from day one and defer the expensive submission work until product-market fit justifies it.
