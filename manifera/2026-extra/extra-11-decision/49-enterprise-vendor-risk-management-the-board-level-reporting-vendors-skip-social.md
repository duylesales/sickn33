"Vendor risk within acceptable parameters." One line in the board deck. Underneath: a vendor at 30% of critical infrastructure spend with no vetted alternative, and a SOC 2 report nobody's checked in eighteen months. 🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Concentration Risk Invisible:** Vendor-by-vendor risk registers miss the portfolio-level question of how much depends on one relationship with no fallback.
❌ **Stale Certifications Treated as Permanent:** A SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report from initial vetting doesn't mean the posture holds eighteen months later.
❌ **Only Realized Incidents Get Reported:** Near-misses that reveal real fragility never make it into board materials at all.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit Concentration Metrics:** Top vendor dependencies mapped against critical processes, with alternative-vendor status documented, not assumed.
✅ **Certification Currency Tracking:** A defined field for when a vendor's security certification was last reviewed, flagged when it's gone stale.
✅ **A Real Translation Layer:** Operational vendor risk data converted into consistent, trend-visible board reporting — not a summary written under deadline pressure.

DORA didn't invent this reporting gap — it just made ignoring it a compliance risk too. 📑
