Your telehealth platform can be flawlessly HIPAA compliant and still get you a cease-and-desist from a state medical board. Here's why. ⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Licensing left as "your problem":** Most vendors nail video encryption and BAAs but leave state-by-state clinician licensing to a spreadsheet.
❌ **No patient-location logic:** Licensing follows where the patient physically is during the visit, not their home address — few platforms check this in real time.
❌ **DEA prescribing confusion:** Post-Public Health Emergency rules on controlled substance prescribing have shifted repeatedly, and "we support e-prescribing" isn't a real answer.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **License-matching built into the data model:** Clinician license status, compact participation, and patient location get checked before a visit starts, not after a complaint.
✅ **Full-stack HIPAA verification:** BAAs confirmed across the video SDK layer, not just the surrounding app.
✅ **Regulatory-aware architecture:** We design for the current DEA and state consent rules at build time, not what was true during the pandemic flexibilities.

Compliant video and legal medical practice are two different problems. Your vendor needs to solve both. 🩺
