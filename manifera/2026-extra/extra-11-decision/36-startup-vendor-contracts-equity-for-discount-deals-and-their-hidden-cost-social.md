A dev shop offers to build your MVP for half price in exchange for 5% equity. It sounds like a smart way to stretch a thin budget. It's actually a permanent tax on every future round. 📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Compounding Cost:** 5% equity traded for a €30K discount can be worth €750K at a €15M Series A valuation — and it dilutes alongside you at every round after that.
❌ **The Cap Table Red Flag:** Unusually large or unvested vendor equity stakes routinely trigger investor due diligence friction that slows down a round.
❌ **The 409A Paperwork Gap:** Handshake equity deals without proper documentation surface as a costly gap during Series A due diligence — right when you have the least time to fix it.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Cash-Based Engagements:** Straightforward cash structures avoid the complications equity introduces on both sides.
✅ **Deferred Payment Terms:** Paying a portion 60-90 days after invoice solves genuine cash constraints without touching the cap table.
✅ **Milestone-Discount Structures:** A modest 10-20% cash discount for a longer commitment aligns incentives with zero dilution.

If cash is the real constraint, there's a structure that solves it without a permanent stake in your company. 🔑
