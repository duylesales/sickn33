Twenty-six weeks from RFP to signature. Only eleven were real evaluation work. The other fifteen were sequencing debt. ⏱️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Legal and Security Run in Sequence:** Two functionally independent reviews wait on each other purely out of habit, adding six to eight weeks for no diligence gain.
❌ **Ambiguous RFPs Trigger Clarification Rounds:** Vague requirements produce vendor responses that don't map to each other, costing two to four extra weeks.
❌ **Approval Waits on One Calendar:** A fifteen-minute sign-off stalls for a week or more because no delegate or SLA was ever defined.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Parallel Review Tracks:** Legal and security evaluation run concurrently from finalist selection, not sequentially.
✅ **Tight, Well-Scoped RFPs:** Structured requirements workshops upfront eliminate the clarification-round tax on vendor responses.
✅ **Defined Approval SLAs:** Hard deadlines and designated delegates keep final sign-off from depending on a single calendar.

Twelve to sixteen weeks is achievable without cutting a single hour of real diligence. 🎯
