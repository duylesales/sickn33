❌ **Marketing Over Mechanism:** Vendors lean on "FERPA compliant" claims instead of the actual contract clause — the school official exception — that makes them compliant at all.

❌ **Ghost Subprocessors:** Vendors that can't name every subprocessor touching student data — including AI model providers — can't actually demonstrate data minimization.
