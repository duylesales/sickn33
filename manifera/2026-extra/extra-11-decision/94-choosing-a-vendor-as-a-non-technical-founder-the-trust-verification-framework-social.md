Three vendors pitch you the same project. All three sound confident. Two of them will still miss deadline by months. Confidence is not a signal. 🎯

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Pitch Quality ≠ Delivery Quality:** The vendor best at making you comfortable in a sales call is not reliably the vendor best at shipping working software.
❌ **Worthless References:** Most founders never call references with real questions — so testimonials filter out nothing.
❌ **Paying for Progress You Can't See:** Screenshots and recorded demos instead of a live, clickable staging environment let problems hide until the budget's already spent.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Structured Reference Scripts:** Four specific questions, including "can I see the actual product," that separate real delivery from a polished pitch.
✅ **Staging Access Before Payment:** A working environment you can click through yourself, before any milestone beyond the initial deposit.
✅ **Milestone Payments That Cap Downside:** Stop after a bounded loss if the fit is wrong — instead of discovering it after the full budget is gone.

You don't need to read code to make a good vendor decision — you need evidence, not confidence. 🔍
