"Up to 20% conversion lift." Every personalization vendor's slide says some version of this. Almost none disclose the baseline, the methodology, or whether it survived a real A/B test. 🎯

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Unverifiable lift claims:** Vendor-reported numbers often compare personalized-session revenue to non-personalized sessions — a comparison biased toward already-engaged shoppers, not proof the algorithm works.
❌ **Cold-start blind spot:** Demos show steady-state accuracy for returning users, but never how the engine performs for first-time visitors or newly launched products — where most retail traffic actually lives.
❌ **Vendor-graded A/B tests:** Results reported only through the vendor's own dashboard, with no independent analytics validation, is the vendor grading its own homework.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Offline evaluation first:** We push for Precision@K, Recall@K, and catalog coverage testing against your own historical data before any live pilot begins.
✅ **Independently validated A/B tests:** We insist on your own analytics tracking as the source of truth, with a pre-registered primary metric.
✅ **Cold-start scrutiny:** We test new-visitor and new-product performance specifically, not just the easier established-user case.

A recommendation engine's real accuracy lives in your catalog and your traffic — not in someone else's case study. 📊
