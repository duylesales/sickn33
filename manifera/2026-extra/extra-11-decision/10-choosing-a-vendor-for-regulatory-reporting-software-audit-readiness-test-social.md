A bank refiled to the Fed 11 months late. Why? A reporting platform quietly changed a mapping rule mid-year — with no flag, no diff, no way to trace which filings it touched. 📑

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Lineage Tracing:** Most platforms can't show, for a single filed number, every source input and transformation that produced it — until an examiner asks.
❌ **Silent Overwrites:** Restatements get handled by simply overwriting the prior value, with no versioned record of what changed, when, or why.
❌ **Stale Taxonomies:** Regulatory technical standards update annually — a vendor even one cycle behind can produce a filing that gets rejected outright.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Live Lineage Demos:** We push vendors to trace a real filed number back to source, live, before signature — not just describe the capability.
✅ **Restatement-First Evaluation:** We verify prior-period corrections are versioned with a full audit record, not silently overwritten.
✅ **Staging Before Production:** We confirm taxonomy updates get validated in staging before they touch a live filing.

A polished dashboard isn't audit-ready. A reconstructable trail is. 🔍
