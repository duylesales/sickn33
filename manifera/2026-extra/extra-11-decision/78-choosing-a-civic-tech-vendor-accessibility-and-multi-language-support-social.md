An English toggle to machine-translated Spanish isn't multi-language support — not if your residents also speak Arabic, Tigrinya, or Polish. 🌍🏛️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Translation Bolt-On, Not Architecture:** Platforms without real i18n foundations can't add right-to-left languages or handle text expansion without a rebuild.
❌ **Machine Translation on Legal Content:** A mistranslated eligibility rule or deadline isn't just a UX gap — it's a due-process risk for the public body.
❌ **Generic Language Defaults:** Vendors ship the same standard language list everywhere, ignoring the actual demographic makeup of your jurisdiction.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **i18n From Day One:** Externalized text, locale-aware formatting, and RTL support built into the architecture, not patched on later.
✅ **Human Review Where It Matters:** Professional translation and a staleness-tracking workflow for legal and procedural content.
✅ **Demographic-Matched Coverage:** Language support scoped against your actual resident data, not a generic global-languages list.

Accessibility and localization are architecture decisions, not late-stage features. ♿🗣️
