A vendor selection reaches month nine, scored and ready to sign — then Internal Audit, never formally looped in, flags an undisclosed subcontracting model and sends it back to security review. 🔁

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Undefined Stakeholder:** A team not scoped into the framework at RFP stage surfaces objections at the finish line, restarting weeks of work.
❌ **Sequential Bottlenecks:** Legal, then security, then finance, each waiting on the last, turns a ten-week evaluation into a nine-month one.
❌ **Opinions Instead of Weights:** Without a published scoring weight, disagreement between stakeholders gets resolved by seniority, not criteria.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Complete Stakeholder Mapping:** Every approver, their criteria, and their deadline defined before the RFP goes out — not discovered during it.
✅ **Parallel Evaluation Tracks:** Security, legal, and commercial review run concurrently with shared convergence gates, not one long queue.
✅ **A Documented Decision Log:** Scores, rationale, and exceptions captured at the time, so the decision survives an audit or a leadership change years later.

A framework built after the fact is a list of people who got annoyed. A framework built before the fact is governance. 📋
