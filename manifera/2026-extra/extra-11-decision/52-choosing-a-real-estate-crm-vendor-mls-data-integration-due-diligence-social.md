A brokerage's new CRM kept showing agents listings that had gone under contract two days earlier. The vendor's MLS feed was still running on a protocol the MLS had already scheduled for retirement. 📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Vague "we integrate with MLS" claims:** Sales teams rarely specify RESO Web API vs. legacy RETS — and RETS is being sunset region by region.
❌ **Aggregator connections disguised as direct feeds:** Some CRMs pull through a third-party data hub, adding latency and a second point of failure nobody disclosed.
❌ **Display rule violations that hit the brokerage, not the vendor:** Broker reciprocity and sold-price display rules are enforced against your MLS membership.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Protocol verification, not vendor claims:** We confirm RESO Web API certification and request sample field-mapping documentation before contract signing.
✅ **Feed-break incident review:** We ask for actual sync-failure history and the vendor's rollback process — not just an uptime percentage.
✅ **Multi-MLS licensing checks:** For brokerages spanning regions, we verify active licensed connections to every MLS you operate in, not just the demo market.

A CRM demo runs on clean data. Your live MLS feed won't. 🏠
