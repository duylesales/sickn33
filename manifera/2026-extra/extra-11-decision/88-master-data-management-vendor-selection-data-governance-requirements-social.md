**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Platform before governance model:** Teams shop for MDM software before deciding whether registry, consolidation, coexistence, or centralized governance actually fits how their org creates data.
❌ **Untested matching claims:** Vendors cite generic accuracy benchmarks from other industries instead of testing match/merge against a real sample of your own messy data.
❌ **One-size survivorship rules:** "Most recent update wins" applied to every field produces golden records that are right on some fields and silently wrong on others.

✅ **Stewardship workflow that works:** We evaluate the human review layer as carefully as the automated engine, because that's where most MDM rollouts actually fail.
