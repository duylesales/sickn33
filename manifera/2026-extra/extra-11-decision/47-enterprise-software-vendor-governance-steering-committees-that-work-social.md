Every status slide was green for two straight quarters. Then a milestone slipped six weeks — visible in the vendor's own tracking a month before anyone in the room heard about it. 🚦

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Cadence Too Slow to Catch Anything:** Quarterly-only reviews on high-risk engagements let problems become unrecoverable before the committee ever sees them.
❌ **Nobody in the Room Can Decide Anything:** A committee full of informed-only attendees produces discussion, not action, when a real issue surfaces.
❌ **Self-Reported Status You Can't Verify:** Vendor account teams have real incentive to present optimistically, especially ahead of a renewal.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Risk-Tiered Cadence:** Monthly operational reviews for high-risk engagements, quarterly strategic sessions layered on top — not one calendar for every vendor.
✅ **Real RACI, Real Authority:** People with actual budget and decision power sit in the Accountable and Responsible seats.
✅ **Independently Verifiable KPIs:** Client-side monitoring and ticketing data supplement vendor-reported metrics, closing the self-disclosure gap.

Governance that only meets on schedule isn't governance — it's a calendar invite. 📅
