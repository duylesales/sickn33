A mid-size EU tender runs a minimum 35 days from publication to submission — and that's before evaluation, standstill, or a challenge window. None of that calendar compresses. ⏳🏛️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Wrong Procedure, Wrong Fit:** Running an open procedure for a requirement complex enough to need competitive dialogue produces mismatched proposals and forces a restart.
❌ **Vague Weighting:** Publishing "technical approach: 40%" as one line item invites a challenge — evaluators without granular sub-criteria can't score consistently.
❌ **Ignoring Abnormally Low Bids:** Awarding a suspiciously cheap tender without formally querying the bidder first is a common, successful ground for a legal challenge.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Procedure-Aware Proposals:** We respond to tenders in the format and rigor the specific procedure — open, restricted, competitive dialogue — actually requires.
✅ **Documentation-Ready Delivery:** A governance model built to withstand scrutiny during and after the standstill period.
✅ **Realistic, Defensible Pricing:** Bids grounded in real delivery cost, not numbers that trigger an abnormally-low-tender query.

The procedure isn't paperwork overhead — it's what makes the award stick. 📋
