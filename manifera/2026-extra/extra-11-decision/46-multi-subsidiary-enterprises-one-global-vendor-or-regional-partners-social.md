One global MSA, four subsidiaries. Eighteen months later: a blocked German rollout, a mispriced Brazilian contract, and a Polish team quietly running its own shadow vendor. 🌍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Works Council Blind Spots:** A global rollout that skips statutory employee-consultation requirements in specific jurisdictions stalls regardless of how good the commercial terms are.
❌ **One Time Zone Fits None:** Support hours optimized for headquarters routinely fail subsidiaries six or eight hours away, pushing teams toward unauthorized workarounds.
❌ **Rate Mismatch Across Markets:** A headquarters-negotiated global rate can be disproportionately expensive in one market and suspiciously cheap in another.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Global Framework, Regional Execution:** Centralized standards for core platforms, with delivery flexibility for subsidiary-specific work.
✅ **Time-Zone-Aligned Delivery Teams:** Regional development capacity structured around local working hours, not headquarters convenience.
✅ **Explicit Governance Scope:** Clear rules for what's centrally mandated versus regionally delegated, so shadow vendors never become necessary.

Global consistency and local fit aren't opposites — they just need a structure that actually separates them. 🏗️
