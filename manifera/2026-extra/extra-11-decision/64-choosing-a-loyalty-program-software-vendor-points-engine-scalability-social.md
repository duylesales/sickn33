A points engine that works great with 5,000 pilot members can collapse entirely at 2 million accounts during a Black Friday spike. The demo never shows you that moment. 🎯

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Balance-only ledgers:** Platforms that store just a current points number, not a full transaction history, make disputes and audits nearly impossible to resolve cleanly.
❌ **No throughput ceiling disclosed:** Points-earning events cluster exactly when transaction volume spikes — a vendor with no documented capacity number is asking you to find the limit in production.
❌ **Finance left out:** Unredeemed points are a real balance-sheet liability, but most vendors treat expiration and liability reporting as a marketing afterthought, not a finance deliverable.

✅ **Ledger architecture scrutiny:** We check whether a vendor runs event-sourced ledgers (full audit trail) or balance mutation (opaque and hard to reconcile) before you commit.
✅ **Real load testing:** We calculate your actual peak-season transaction rate and push vendors to prove their engine handles it, not average daily volume.
✅ **Liability reporting checks:** We verify the platform can hand your finance team auditable, expiration-cohort points liability reports from day one.
