If the founder disappeared tomorrow, could anyone else log into your order system? For most family businesses, the honest answer is no. 🔑

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The One-Person Login:** Admin credentials for critical systems often exist only in the founder's head or a personal password manager — not documented anywhere a second person could find them.
❌ **Relational, Not Contractual, Vendors:** A developer the founder knows personally maintains core systems on a handshake, with no written contract, no assignment clause, and no backup contact.
❌ **Undocumented Handover:** When the retiring generation leaves, the incoming leader inherits the business and an undocumented technical mess in the same week.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Business-Owned Accounts:** Every credential and infrastructure account registered to the business, not an individual — from day one of onboarding.
✅ **Documented Handover as a Deliverable:** Runbooks, architecture diagrams, and access logs delivered as contract line items, not informal courtesies.
✅ **Assignable Contracts:** Agreements that transfer to a successor owner or leadership team without renegotiation from scratch.

Succession-proofing your stack is a governance decision, not an IT ticket. 🏢
