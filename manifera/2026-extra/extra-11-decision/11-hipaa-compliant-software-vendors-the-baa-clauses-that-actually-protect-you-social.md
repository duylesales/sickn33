**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Liability caps that don't cover you:** Many vendor BAAs cap total liability at annual fees paid — a $60K contract against a seven-figure OCR settlement is no protection at all.
❌ **A 60-day "notification standard" that's really a ceiling:** HITECH's 60-day outer limit gets copy-pasted into BAAs as if it were the goal, not the maximum.
❌ **Invisible subcontractors:** Your vendor's cloud host or AI tool may be touching PHI with no flow-down BAA in place, and you'd never know from the contract alone.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Carve-outs that matter:** We negotiate liability cap exceptions for PHI breaches and gross negligence, not just accept vendor boilerplate.
✅ **Tighter notification clocks:** 5-10 business day contractual notification, not the regulatory maximum dressed up as a commitment.
✅ **Full subprocessor visibility:** Every subcontractor touching PHI gets named, with evidence of their own flow-down BAA, before any data moves.

A BAA that only meets the regulatory minimum protects the vendor, not you. 🔍
