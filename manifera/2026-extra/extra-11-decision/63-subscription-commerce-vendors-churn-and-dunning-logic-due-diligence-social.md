20-40% of your subscription churn isn't customers leaving. It's cards that failed to charge. That number lives entirely in a layer most product teams never inspect: dunning logic. 💳

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fixed retry schedules:** Retrying every failed charge on the same 3-5-7 day schedule regardless of decline reason wastes retry attempts and can flag your processor account for excessive retries.
❌ **No account updater integration:** Without Visa Account Updater or Mastercard ABU, expired cards force customers into a manual update flow you could have avoided entirely.
❌ **Churn metrics lumped together:** When involuntary and voluntary churn report as one number, you can't tell if you have a product problem or a billing recovery problem.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Decline-code-aware evaluation:** We push vendors to show real recovery rate benchmarks broken out by decline reason, not marketing averages.
✅ **Segment-level dunning checks:** We verify cadence, channel mix, and grace periods can be configured per plan tier, not forced into one global sequence.
✅ **Custom reporting where needed:** When a vendor can't natively split involuntary from voluntary churn, we build the cohort reporting layer that shows you where revenue is actually leaking.

Your dunning engine is a revenue lever hiding as a support feature. Treat it that way in vendor due diligence. 📉
