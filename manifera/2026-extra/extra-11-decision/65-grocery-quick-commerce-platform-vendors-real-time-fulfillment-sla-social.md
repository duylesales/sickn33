10 minutes. That's the delivery promise. It has almost nothing to do with your storefront and everything to do with a dispatch engine most vendor demos never actually show you. ⏱️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Stale pick-time inventory:** If pickers aren't doing real-time stock checks, substitutions and cancellations get discovered after dispatch already started — blowing the SLA on the spot.
❌ **Opportunistic batching:** Combining orders onto one route without verifying every order stays SLA-safe means the third stop in a batch is quietly doomed before the rider leaves the store.
❌ **One blended on-time number:** Vendors that report a single aggregate SLA percentage hide whether misses come from picking, dispatch, or transit — so you can't fix the actual bottleneck.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Dispatch algorithm scrutiny:** We push vendors to explain exactly how batching decisions verify SLA-safety before assignment, not after the fact.
✅ **Cold-chain and substitution checks:** We verify perishability rules and category-level substitution logic are actually configurable, not bolted-on defaults.
✅ **Granular SLA reporting demands:** We insist on median/90th-percentile breakdowns by failure cause before any contract is signed.

A 10-minute promise is only as real as the dispatch logic underneath it. Everything else is a nice storefront. 🛵
