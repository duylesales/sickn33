🚨 A customer unsubscribed from marketing emails and kept getting them anyway — because the unsubscribe only applied to one of six independent systems that all send email, none checking a shared preference. 📧⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Six Systems, No Shared Record:** Every team bolted on its own sending logic, disconnected from the rest.
❌ **Unsubscribe That Doesn't Actually Unsubscribe:** A real compliance risk under GDPR and anti-spam regulations, not just an annoyance.
❌ **Sender Reputation at Risk:** Rising spam complaints can push even password-reset emails into spam folders.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Single Shared Preference Record:** Every send, from every team, checks the same source of truth.
✅ **Explicit Transactional vs. Promotional Categorization:** Clear rules, consistently applied.
✅ **Cross-Channel Volume Monitoring:** See the full notification load a customer actually experiences.

An unsubscribe should mean unsubscribed. Everywhere. Immediately. 🛡️
