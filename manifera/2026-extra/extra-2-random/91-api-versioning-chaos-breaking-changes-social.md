🚨 A field got renamed to fix an inconsistency. Deployed Tuesday afternoon. By Wednesday morning, 14 integration partners had opened tickets — their production systems all broke at once, with no warning and nothing to roll back to. 🔌⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Versioning, No Isolation:** One unversioned endpoint means every change risks breaking every partner simultaneously.
❌ **Zero Advance Notice:** Partners discover the change when their own production system starts throwing errors.
❌ **Trust Erosion, Repeat Offense:** Partners start building defensive workarounds — or evaluating your competitors.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit Versioning Strategy:** New versions ship alongside old ones — never a forced simultaneous break.
✅ **Formal Deprecation Communication:** 60-90 days' notice, sent directly, not buried in a changelog.
✅ **Cross-Version Contract Testing:** Changes to the new version can't accidentally break the old one.

Turn a breaking change into a routine migration — not a trust event. 🛡️
