Two weeks is short enough that a vendor can't fake it with polish, and long enough to watch a real sprint rhythm play out once, blocker included. That's what makes it the sharpest diligence tool a CTO has. ⏱️🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Proposals Only Describe Process:** A pitch deck can claim strong code review and clear communication. Only watching a real sprint proves it.
❌ **Synthetic Tests Prove Nothing:** A task invented just for the evaluation has no real ambiguity — nothing like the backlog you'll actually hand them.
❌ **The Trial That Never Ends:** Without a written pass/fail date, a trial drifts into becoming the engagement by default, losing its clean checkpoint.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Day-by-Day Scoring Framework:** Onboarding questions (days 1-2), communication specificity (3-5), the blocker test (6-8), code review depth (9-11), honest retrospective (12-14).
✅ **Real, Paid Statement of Work:** Clean IP terms upfront, named engineers who'd join the full engagement — not a lightweight team assembled to win a free trial.
✅ **The Blocker Test as Highest Signal:** Time from a blocker appearing to it being clearly flagged with a proposed path forward — the single data point that previews month eight.

A trial that surfaces problems isn't wasted spend — it's a fraction of the cost of discovering the same gaps six sprints into a signed contract. 🎯
