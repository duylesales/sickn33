❌ **Migration Underestimated as "Just Technical":** Legacy modernization projects routinely run 40-60% over estimate because undocumented business rules get rediscovered mid-project, not planned for.

✅ **Portability by Default:** Predictable folder structures, pinned dependencies, and documentation a new team could onboard from within an afternoon.
✅ **Written Ownership Terms:** Source code, infrastructure config, and custom tooling ownership confirmed in the contract, not assumed.
✅ **Staged Engagement Option:** A smaller, scoped first phase with an explicit handover checkpoint — proof of portability before switching costs get high.
