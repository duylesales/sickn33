How much of a €400K build should leave your account before a single feature actually works? If your vendor's answer is "half, upfront" — that's the whole risk problem in one number. 💶

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Flat 50/50 Deposits:** Pays for the riskiest phase of the project — architecture and setup — before any working software exists to validate it.
❌ **Vague Milestone Criteria:** "Backend complete" invites dispute; without specific, pre-agreed acceptance tests, review windows can stretch for weeks.
❌ **No Post-Launch Holdback:** Paying 100% at go-live removes any financial incentive for the vendor to prioritize your bug reports over their next client.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Risk-Weighted Milestones:** Four to six payment tranches sized to deliverable complexity, not divided evenly by calendar time.
✅ **Pre-Signed Acceptance Criteria:** Specific, testable conditions agreed before each phase starts — not negotiated after delivery.
✅ **10% Post-Launch Retention:** Held for a defined stabilization window so the vendor stays engaged exactly when real-world edge cases surface.

A milestone schedule is a governance tool, not just a payment plan. 📊
