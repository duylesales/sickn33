Three vendors, all polished portfolios, all quoting within 10% of each other, all claiming "senior teams" and "agile delivery." How do you actually tell them apart? 🏆📲

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Compliance Page vs. Compliance Practice:** A vendor surprised or defensive about GDPR and SOC 2 questions is showing you exactly how they'll handle an actual audit request 18 months in.
❌ **No Real Tenure Number:** "Our team is very stable" without a figure usually means the real average tenure isn't flattering — and turnover means your app's institutional knowledge walks out the door.
❌ **The Mission-Statement SLA:** "High availability" and "responsive support" with no severity-based response times aren't enforceable commitments.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Seven-Point Scorecard, Weighted to What Keeps You Up at Night:** Compliance, vertical depth, team stability, communication, code ownership, SLA specificity, and post-launch support.
✅ **Ask for the Real Number:** Average engineer tenure, deployments per week, response times by severity — not reassurance.
✅ **A Real Sprint Report, Not a Description of One:** Thirty seconds of reading tells you if their reporting is substantive or decorative.

The absence of a straight answer on any one criterion is itself the scorecard result. 🔍
