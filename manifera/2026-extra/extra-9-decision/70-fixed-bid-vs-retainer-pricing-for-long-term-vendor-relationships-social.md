Your vendor relationship started as one fixed-bid project 18 months ago. Now the renewal has landed with two pricing proposals — and defaulting to whichever one feels familiar is exactly the trap. 💶

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Renewal Fatigue:** Sequential fixed-bid phases can burn 2-4 weeks per cycle just on re-scoping and paperwork instead of delivery.
❌ **Uncertainty Buffers, Every Time:** Each new fixed-bid negotiation gets priced with a fresh buffer against scope ambiguity — a cost that compounds across renewals.
❌ **Unscrutinized Retainers:** Without utilization reporting, a retainer becomes a recurring line item nobody questions closely enough.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Retainer After Proven Fixed-Bid Build:** Continuity and institutional knowledge that compounds instead of resetting every "Phase."
✅ **Quarterly Capacity Reporting:** Real utilization detail reviewed with the same rigor as any recurring vendor spend.
✅ **~15-20% Less Administrative Overhead:** Time reclaimed from scoping and negotiation, redirected to actual delivery.

Neither model is universally right — but "renew as fixed bid" shouldn't be your default without asking the question. 📊
