Three months into a vendor relationship that isn't working, a founder opens the contract looking for the exit clause — and finds three sentences that say almost nothing. 🚪⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **For-Cause Only:** Without a termination-for-convenience option, you're stuck arguing whether mediocre work legally counts as "material breach."
❌ **Handover Tied to Payment:** Vague code-handover language lets a vendor hold your own codebase hostage during a billing dispute.
❌ **No Transition Assistance:** A clean code dump with zero knowledge transfer leaves a new team reverse-engineering decisions for months.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Symmetric Notice Periods:** ~30-day termination for convenience, balanced both ways — not 90 days for you and 14 for the vendor.
✅ **Fixed-Timeline Handover:** Source code, commit history, and credentials delivered within 5-10 business days, independent of any payment dispute.
✅ **Standard Transition Support:** 2-4 weeks of documented knowledge transfer built into every contract by default.

The cheapest insurance in your contract is the clause you never intend to use. 🛡️
