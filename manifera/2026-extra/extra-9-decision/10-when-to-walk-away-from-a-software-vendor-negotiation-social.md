Six weeks into vendor evaluation, runway clock louder than ever, and the vendor across the table just pushed back on a term that matters. The instinct is to sign anyway. That instinct is exactly backwards. 🚩✋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Let's Figure It Out Together":** Resistance to writing scope down in specific terms sounds like a partnership — it's actually room to interpret ambiguity in their favor later.
❌ **Vague IP Language:** Hedged intellectual property clauses aren't a detail — they're leverage a vendor can use once your product already depends on the code.
❌ **Blocked From the Real Team:** "You'll meet them at kickoff" means committing capital before verifying the single factor that determines your outcome most.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Full IP Transfer, Written Before Signature:** No hedged licensing-back language, no ambiguity to exploit later.
✅ **Named Engineers During Evaluation:** Direct conversations with the actual team before you sign — not a rotating cast introduced after.
✅ **Verifiable Long-Tenured References:** Real clients across 120+ relationships, willing to speak candidly about how the engagement aged.

A negotiation's final stretch is the cheapest, most honest preview you'll get of how a vendor behaves once the leverage shifts. Trust the signal, not the sunk cost. ⚖️
