Every first-time founder is told to pick the vendor with the lowest app development cost because runway is tight. That advice is backwards — and it's the most expensive advice a non-technical founder can follow. 📱⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The "Efficient" Lowball:** A quote 40-50% below the median almost never reflects efficiency — it reflects fewer design rounds, thinner device testing, and a scope that's been quietly narrowed.
❌ **"Fixed-Price" That Isn't:** A contract is only as fixed as its change-order terms — undefined "out of scope" language is where a locked-in number quietly becomes open-ended.
❌ **The Aggressive Timeline Tell:** A vendor promising half the realistic 10-16 week build time is usually compressing QA, not working smarter.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Force the Line-Item Breakdown:** Design, development, QA, and support as separate figures from every finalist — variance becomes a signal, not a coincidence.
✅ **Ask the Change-Order Rate Upfront:** A pre-agreed hourly or daily rate protects the number you actually signed.
✅ **Two Minutes, No Jargon:** Any finalist should explain native vs. cross-platform for your specific use case — a generic answer means you're hearing their default pitch.

The most expensive mistake isn't overpaying — it's underpaying for something you'll pay for twice. 💡
