A CTO split her dev work across three agencies so no single failure could take down the roadmap. 18 months later, she consolidated back to one — the coordination tax cost more than the risk it was solving. ⚖️🧩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Diffused Accountability:** When a release slips across three interdependent vendors, figuring out who actually caused it becomes its own investigation.
❌ **Drifting Standards:** Three teams optimizing locally for their own slice of the codebase quietly produce a system that's harder to maintain than one built under a single architecture.
❌ **Solving the Wrong Risk:** Diversification protects against a vendor's business failure — it does little against the far more common risk of plain underperformance.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **One Accountable Team:** No ambiguity about who owns a slipped release.
✅ **Internal Redundancy:** Documented architecture and cross-trained engineers, not one irreplaceable lead — risk mitigated from within the relationship.
✅ **Escrow and Transition Clauses:** The protection diversification promises, without fragmenting a tightly coupled codebase across teams never designed to coordinate.

Before splitting your next engagement across vendors, make sure the boundary is genuinely clean — not just reassuring on a slide. 🔍
