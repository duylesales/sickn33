68% of IT outsourcing engagements that fail do so because the buyer never structured a real comparison — they compared price sheets, not delivery capability. 📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Day Rate ≠ Total Cost:** A team costing 15% more per hour but shipping working features without rework beats a cheaper team on total cost within the first release cycle.
❌ **Promises Without Proof:** Every vendor claims predictable sprint velocity and clean retrospectives — almost none will show you a redacted sprint report or burndown chart unprompted.
❌ **Silent Contract Gaps:** Notice periods for scaling down, IP access on termination, and swapping an underperforming engineer rarely surface in the sales conversation — until something changes.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Flexible Team Sizing:** Scale up or down within two to four weeks, with the exact notice period and cost written into the contract, not left as "we're flexible."
✅ **Escalation With Names Attached:** A documented protocol — technical lead flags blockers in daily standup, escalates to the account lead within four hours if unresolved.
✅ **A Decade of Proof:** 160+ delivered projects and 120+ clients across more than a decade, with real long-tenured client relationships to reference, not just a highlight reel.
