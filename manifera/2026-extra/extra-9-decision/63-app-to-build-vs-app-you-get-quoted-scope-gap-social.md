The gap between the app in your head and the app in your quote isn't a communication problem. It's a set of quietly held myths on both sides of the table — and they get expensive right before signature. 📝⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Feature List ≠ Scope Document:** "User accounts with login" can mean five different things — two honest vendors can quote tens of thousands apart reading the same page.
❌ **Budget-Matched Quotes:** A number landing exactly at your ceiling often means scope was narrowed quietly, not that efficiencies were found.
❌ **False Confidence on "Simple" Apps:** Simple-sounding apps get the least scoping scrutiny, yet cancellations, timezones, and edge cases still need defined rules.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Written Scope Before a Euro Committed:** Data entities, user flows, and acceptance criteria agreed upfront — detailed enough for a second vendor to estimate against.
✅ **Change-Order Process in Writing:** No reliance on "we'll figure it out together" — a defined proposal-price-approval mechanism from day one.
✅ **Flexible Team Scaling:** MVP squad to full build team within two to four weeks once scope is confirmed, not locked to a rigid headcount early.

A vendor who welcomes a detailed scoping conversation is telling you they intend to be held to what they build. 🎯
