Vendor A: credentials trickled in over two weeks, first commit landed at week five. Vendor B: access ready before kickoff, a reviewed pull request by day nine. Same skill level. Different plan. ⏱️🚀

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Signature ≠ Finish Line:** Treating contract sign-off as the end of the decision, not the start of the highest-risk period, is what turns a good vendor choice into a slow one.
❌ **Verbal Handoffs:** Architecture "explained" on a kickoff call instead of documented and shared in advance wastes the first week on administrative setup.
❌ **Waiting Until Month Three:** Code review delays or communication gaps visible by day 30 often don't get addressed until they've compounded for months.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Access Ready Before Kickoff:** Credentials, docs, and standards provisioned in the days between signature and day one.
✅ **A Written Working Agreement, Week One:** Named review turnaround times, standup overlap, and escalation contacts — specific enough to hold both sides to.
✅ **Formal Day-30 Check-In:** Velocity, working agreement, and every selection-stage concern reviewed directly with the CTO, not left to assumption.

The fix for a messy onboarding is rarely a better vendor. It's a more deliberate first thirty days. 📋
