"Would you recommend them?" Almost every reference says yes — regardless of how the engagement actually went. It's the least useful question you can ask. 📞❓

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Empty Ratings:** A 1-10 communication score is an easy, low-effort answer that never surfaces what actually happened.
❌ **The Bait-and-Switch Gap:** The team pitched in sales is often not the team that shows up to deliver — and it rarely surfaces unless asked directly.
❌ **Cherry-Picked References:** Vendors choose who you call, biasing the pool toward their strongest, often most recent relationships.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Behavioral Questions, Not Ratings:** "Tell me about a time a deadline slipped — how were you told, and what was the resolution?" is nearly impossible to answer with empty reassurance.
✅ **Long-Tenured References on Request:** Connections to clients 18-24 months into active or completed relationships, from a base of 120+ served clients.
✅ **What They'd Change:** The highest-yield question — surfacing specific contract terms to negotiate into your own agreement before you sign.

A reference call done right stops being a formality and becomes one of the most useful steps in vendor selection. 🔍
