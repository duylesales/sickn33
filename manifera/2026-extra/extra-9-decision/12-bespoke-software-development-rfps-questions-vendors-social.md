Four vendors gave nearly identical RFP answers about "agile delivery" and "senior engineers." Only one could name the actual people who'd work on the project when asked directly. 🕵️‍♀️📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"We'll Assign Our Best Available Team":** That phrase is a structural admission that the vendor doesn't yet control the engineering capacity they just sold you.
❌ **Screenshots Instead of Code:** Portfolios show finished demos, almost never unedited code — a vendor who stalls on a code walkthrough is often not the team writing it.
❌ **"Deliverables Provided Upon Completion":** Vague IP language means terminating in month four could leave you with nothing to walk away with.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Name the Team, With LinkedIn URLs:** Refreshed at signature and again at kickoff — vendors who directly employ their engineers answer this instantly.
✅ **Ask "What Would We Walk Away With in Month 4?":** A strong answer is full repo access, working docs, and a functioning build — not a vague milestone promise.
✅ **Treat Some Questions as Gates, Not Scores:** A failed team-transparency or IP question disqualifies a finalist regardless of price.

The question that predicts relationship longevity best isn't technical — it's how a vendor communicates bad news. 📣
