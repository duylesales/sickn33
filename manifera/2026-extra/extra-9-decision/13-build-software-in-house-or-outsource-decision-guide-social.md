"You have to build in-house or investors won't take your engineering seriously." Repeated in every accelerator Slack, and for most founders at pre-seed through Series A, it's flatly wrong. 🚀💭

❌ **The 5-6 Month Runway Drain:** Sourcing, interviewing, and waiting out a notice period for one senior EU hire can eat an entire fundraising milestone before a line of code ships.
❌ **Concentration Risk:** A 1-2 person in-house team means your entire technical continuity walks out the door if either person leaves — and early hires leave startups at high rates.
❌ **The Hidden Management Tax:** Someone has to run sprint priorities and code review for that hire — and if that's you, it's hours quietly disappearing from sales and fundraising.

✅ **2-4 Weeks to First Sprint:** No recruiting cycle, no notice period — a team that's already assembled and calibrated.
