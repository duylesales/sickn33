A €25K website redesign quote finishes at €68K. No single change request looks unreasonable — that's exactly how the trap works. 💸🕸️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Death By a Thousand "Quick" Additions:** An extra page here, a third design round there — each too small to negotiate over, all compounding.
❌ **"Includes Revisions" With No Number:** The most quietly expensive phrase in a redesign contract, meaning whatever the vendor decides once a dispute arises.
❌ **Invisible Change Orders:** Costs absorbed silently into "the project" until the final invoice reveals the cumulative total.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Paid Discovery Phase First:** A real, detailed scope document — sitemap, functional requirements, revision counts — before any fixed price is signed.
✅ **Explicit Revision Rounds:** A defined number per deliverable, with any extra round priced in advance, not negotiated when your leverage is weakest.
✅ **Visible, Sign-Off Change Orders:** A running total you approve in real time, not a surprise at final invoicing.
