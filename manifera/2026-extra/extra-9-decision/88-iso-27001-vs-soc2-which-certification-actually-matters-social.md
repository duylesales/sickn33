An IT manager rejected a strong dev vendor over one line on a checklist: "ISO 27001 or equivalent." The vendor only had SOC2. She still isn't sure what the difference actually meant. 🔒📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Badge, Not Substance:** Checklists demand a specific certification name without anyone reading the underlying report, scope statement, or exceptions.
❌ **Scope Gaps Nobody Checks:** A vendor's certification can cover HQ while excluding the actual delivery team or data center that would handle your project.
❌ **False Equivalence:** ISO 27001 certifies a management system exists; SOC2 Type II documents whether controls actually worked over 6-12 months of testing — treating them as interchangeable misses what each one really proves.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Report-Level Transparency:** Statement of Applicability and audit documentation shared under NDA, not just a logo.
✅ **Scope Clarity:** Confirmation of exactly which team and systems the certification covers before you sign.
✅ **Technical Backup:** A compliance lead who can walk your security team through control exceptions and remediation history directly.

A badge is a floor, not the whole evaluation. Read the report. 📄
