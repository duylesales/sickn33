68% of IT managers who call an outsourcing engagement "failed" later admit, when pressed, that they picked the wrong contract model — not the wrong vendor. 📄⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Wrong-Model Trap:** Bringing in individual augmented contractors for what's actually a sustained, multi-quarter build kills team cohesion and creates constant context-loss every time someone rolls off.
❌ **The Quiet Drift:** A 3-month augmentation contract for one specialist gets renewed quarter after quarter until, 18 months later, they're core to the team with none of the continuity or backfill guarantees a dedicated contract would have built in.
❌ **Compliance That Scales Linearly:** Rotating augmented contractors mean extending your access-review process to each individual — a burden that grows with headcount instead of staying fixed.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Match the Model to the Work:** Under 4 months points to staff augmentation; beyond 6 months points to a dedicated team — a single question that resolves most of the ambiguity.
✅ **One Compliance Framework, Not Five:** A ring-fenced dedicated team means one security review, one DPA, one SOC 2-ready roster.
✅ **2-4 Week Flex Window:** Adjust team size within an existing contract as roadmap priorities shift, instead of renegotiating individual contractor terms every time.

The contract model isn't paperwork — it's the operating structure your team lives inside for the next several quarters. 🧭
