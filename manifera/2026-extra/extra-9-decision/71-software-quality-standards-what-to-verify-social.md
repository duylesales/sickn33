**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Rigorous Testing" as a Bullet Point:** Sales decks talk quality-first culture but rarely show the actual test pyramid or defect-escape numbers.
❌ **Review Standards That Don't Match:** Ask a sales lead and a technical lead the same question about code review — mismatched answers reveal a process on paper, not in practice.
❌ **Testing Tucked at the Tail End:** A Gantt chart that positions QA as a late phase usually reflects how the vendor actually thinks about it, not just how they scheduled the document.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Visible Test Pyramid:** Unit, integration, and end-to-end tests enforced as automated pull-request gates — not described as happening "before release."
✅ **Four Hard Benchmarks:** Defect escape rate, test coverage on critical logic, mean time to detect/resolve, and rollback-free deployment frequency, shared on request.
✅ **Documented Review Checklist:** Mandatory second reviewer, automated static analysis, and a security checklist — available as an artifact, not a claim.
