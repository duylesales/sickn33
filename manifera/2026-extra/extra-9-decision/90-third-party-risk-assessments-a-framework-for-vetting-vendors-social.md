Nothing was breached. But the incident review found a contractor on a low-priority tool had access to a shared credentials vault far broader than the project needed. 🔑⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **One Questionnaire Fits All:** Applying the same exhaustive review to every vendor regardless of actual data access wastes effort on low-risk vendors and under-scrutinizes high-risk ones.
❌ **Assertions, Not Evidence:** Yes/no self-attestation questions ("do you encrypt data at rest?") without required supporting documentation are weak evidence on their own.
❌ **A One-Time Gate:** Assessing a vendor once at kickoff and never again misses scope creep, personnel turnover, and incidents that change the risk picture entirely.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Tiered by Actual Exposure:** Assessment depth scales to data sensitivity and access level, not a blanket questionnaire.
✅ **Live Technical Interviews:** Direct access to the engineers and security lead who'd actually work on your project, not a sales deck.
✅ **Contractual Teeth:** Breach notification timelines, right-to-audit clauses, and a documented reassessment cadence built into the agreement itself.

A static spreadsheet from onboarding isn't a risk program. Build one that scales. 🛡️
