A founder built her AI onboarding flow in a weekend on a no-code tool, hit €40K MRR — then hit a wall the vendor's roadmap had no plans to fix. Build vs. buy just became real. 🧱🛒

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Roadmap Hostage:** Your product evolves at the pace of someone else's backlog, not your customers' needs.
❌ **Cost That Outpaces Revenue:** AI tooling spend can grow to 34% of infrastructure cost by 10K users — up from under 8% at launch.
❌ **Zero Moat:** If a competitor uses the same wrapper tool, your "AI feature" isn't a differentiator.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Phased Custom Builds:** Replace the highest-value workflow first — 8 to 14 weeks to production, not a full rebuild.
✅ **Flatter Cost Curve:** Purpose-built infrastructure scales on compute, not per-token vendor margin.
✅ **Proprietary Data Leverage:** Build around the dataset only you have — the actual source of differentiation.

Off-the-shelf gets you to launch. Custom gets you to defensible. 🚀
