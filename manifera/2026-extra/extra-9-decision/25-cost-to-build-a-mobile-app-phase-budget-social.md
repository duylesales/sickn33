A single lump-sum number is the weakest thing you can walk into a board meeting with. 💰

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Discovery Gets Cut First:** Skipping the 8-10% discovery phase doesn't remove the cost — it defers it into development as expensive change orders.
❌ **QA Is the Line Item Boards Love to Trim:** Underfunding the 12-15% QA phase just shifts the cost onto your support team and your first-week App Store reviews.
❌ **No Post-Launch Reserve:** Most COOs forget to request the 15-20% reserve for the first 60-90 days, then scramble for emergency budget when real users behave differently than test users.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Phase-by-Phase Budgeting:** Discovery, design, development, QA, and launch each get a defined allocation, mapped to the risk it retires — not treated as an isolated expense.
✅ **Cross-Platform Default:** React Native or Flutter typically cuts combined iOS/Android cost by 30-40% through shared code.
✅ **A Budget Your Board Approves Faster:** Risk-based justification per line item beats a bare number every time.
