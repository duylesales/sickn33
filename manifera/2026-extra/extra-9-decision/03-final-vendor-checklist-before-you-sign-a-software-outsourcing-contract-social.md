A Rotterdam CEO once called us three weeks after signing with another vendor, asking if it was normal that the contract said nothing about who owned the source code once the engagement ended. It wasn't normal. 📝😬

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **IP Assigned by Silence:** Some jurisdictions default code ownership to the creator absent an explicit assignment clause — meaning a generic "work product" line can quietly work against you.
❌ **The Mission-Statement SLA:** "We aim for high availability" isn't a service level agreement — it's a sentence with no measurable response times or consequences attached.
❌ **The Bait-and-Switch Bench:** Senior engineers get presented during sales, then a junior bench gets staffed once the contract is signed — unless named-resource commitments are written in.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **9 Non-Negotiable Gates:** Treat IP assignment, escrow, and termination terms as pass/fail checks, not a wish list — before you sign, not after.
✅ **Repository Access From Day One:** Continuous, admin-level access rather than a promised export at project close.
✅ **Verifiable References, Not Testimonials:** From clients with a comparable stack, called directly, asked specifically what went wrong.
