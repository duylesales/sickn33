Contract drafted. Kickoff date set. Then Security raised a blocking objection three days before signature — about something that should've been asked in week two, not week fourteen. 🚧📅

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Reactive Involvement:** Finance, security, and legal looped in only near the end guarantees the most expensive kind of surprise — an objection after months of investment.
❌ **Ambiguous "General" Buy-In:** A hallway nod isn't alignment. Without an explicit commitment in the room, the late veto is still coming.
❌ **Every Preference Treated as a Veto:** Failing to separate real dealbreakers from soft preferences turns stakeholder alignment into permanent paralysis.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **RACI Mapped on Day One:** Who's Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed defined before evaluation even starts.
✅ **Requirements Gathered Upfront:** A 30-minute conversation with security or finance early costs nothing — and prevents a six-week delay later.
✅ **One Structured Workshop:** 90 minutes, every stakeholder present, ending in an explicit decision — not a string of ambiguous hallway chats.
