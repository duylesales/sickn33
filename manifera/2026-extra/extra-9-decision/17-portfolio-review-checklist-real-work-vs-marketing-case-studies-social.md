❌ **The Clean-Success Narrative:** A portfolio with zero false starts or pivots isn't proof of skill — it's a sign you're seeing a sanitized retelling.
❌ **Implausible Timelines:** A complex, multi-integration build claimed in four weeks by a small team rarely survives basic arithmetic.
