**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Demos Are Cheap to Make Impressive:** Marketing case studies rarely disclose whether a project ever reached sustained production use.
❌ **"Access to Experts" ≠ Your Actual Team:** Boutique studios and enterprise SIs both sell their most credentialed people, then staff the engagement differently.
❌ **Build-Only Pricing Hides the Real Cost:** Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, and retraining commonly add 20-40% of build cost annually — and vendors who skip this in the quote often negotiate it later.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **12+ Month Production References, Verified:** Ask for a reference who can speak to what happened after launch — retraining cadence, incidents, total cost of ownership.
✅ **Named Team, Disclosed Seniority, in Writing:** No substituting a thinner bench after the contract is signed.
✅ **Full Lifecycle TCO:** A 12-month cost estimate up front, not a build quote that wins the deal and negotiates the expensive part later.
