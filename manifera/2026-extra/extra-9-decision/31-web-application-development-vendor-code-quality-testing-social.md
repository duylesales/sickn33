What if the demo that impressed your leadership team was built by a completely different team than the one who'll actually staff your project? 🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Invisible Until It Breaks:** A finished web app looks the same in a browser whether it has 70% test coverage or none — the difference only shows up eighteen months later.
❌ **The Cherry-Picked Portfolio:** Sales engineers show you the flagship case study; almost nobody asks to see a real pull request from the actual engineers assigned to your account.
❌ **Deflection Under Pressure:** "That project is under NDA" or "our best engineers are staffed right now" are common excuses that, stacked together, predict your first year with a vendor.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Request a Representative Code Sample:** Check test coverage above 70% on business logic, static analysis cleanliness, and commit discipline — not the highlight reel.
✅ **Live Pairing Session:** Thirty minutes with an assigned engineer walking a real pull request filters out vendors staffing sales calls with architects and delivery with juniors.
✅ **Benchmark Real Load:** Ask for response times at 100, 500, and 1,000 concurrent users — not a synthetic test with cached requests.

The vendors who welcome this scrutiny are the ones with nothing to hide. 🛡️
