Two sprints of architecture decisions, made by someone who never appeared in the original SOW. That's what happens when nobody verifies who your vendor actually staffs. 🧑‍💻🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Titles Lie:** One vendor's "senior engineer" has led three architectures; another's got promoted for reasons unrelated to technical depth — job titles aren't standardized across companies.
❌ **Diluted Attention:** A named architect "overseeing" your project might really be splitting a few hours a week across two other concurrent client accounts.
❌ **Silent Turnover Risk:** Forrester has flagged unplanned staff rotation, not initial skill mismatch, as one of the most damaging and underestimated risks in multi-month engagements.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Dedicated, Not Shared:** Engineers assigned full-time to your project, with allocation percentages written into the SOW itself, not verbal assurance.
✅ **Documented Continuity Plans:** A guaranteed knowledge-transfer period and replacement-vetting process if a named senior rotates off the account.
✅ **The Right to Verify:** A 30-minute technical conversation with the actual named engineers before you countersign — not a generic "meet the team" call.
