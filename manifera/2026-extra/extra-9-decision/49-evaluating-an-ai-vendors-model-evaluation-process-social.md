**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Demo Theater:** Three curated prompts that work perfectly say nothing about production reliability.
❌ **Unquantified Hallucination:** "We review outputs and they look good" isn't a metric — it's a shrug.
❌ **No Adversarial Testing:** Skipping red-teaming means your users find the jailbreaks before you do.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Live Eval Harness:** A real dashboard against a held-out test set, shown on request — not "we'll follow up."
✅ **Quantified Hallucination Regression:** A defined threshold checked before every release, not a vibe check.
✅ **Staffed Human Review Tiers:** Named reviewers, defined ratios, for every high-stakes output category.
