A proposal is a promise. A pilot project is evidence. For a six-figure engagement, that gap is exactly where the riskiest vendor decisions get made. 🔍💻

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Reference Calls Are Selection-Biased:** The vendor picks who you call, and even an honest reference describes a different team, project, and moment than the one you're signing.
❌ **Synthetic Test Projects Lie:** A task built only for evaluation has no real ambiguity and no real stakeholders — nothing like your actual roadmap.
❌ **Unpaid "Trials" Get the B-Team:** Vendors unwilling to structure a paid pilot are often signaling they don't expect the free work to represent their real quality.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **2-4 Week Real, Paid Pilot:** A bounded deliverable from your actual backlog, delivered by the named engineers who'd join the full engagement — 45% fewer major disputes in the first six months versus skipping straight to signature.
✅ **Four Tracked Metrics:** Escalation speed, communication clarity, estimate honesty, and code review discipline — the process signals that predict fit better than code quality alone.
✅ **Written Pass Criteria Upfront:** Defined acceptance criteria and a named go/no-go date before work starts, so the pilot never drifts into an indefinite trial.

A pilot that surfaces problems isn't wasted spend — it's cheaper than discovering the same gaps six months into a full contract. 🎯
