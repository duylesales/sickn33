**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Price Parity Hides Risk:** When finalist quotes land within 15% of each other, procurement instinct says "negotiate harder" — but a tight price band tells you nothing about how a vendor handles the requirements you didn't write down.
❌ **No Artifacts, Just Claims:** A vendor who can't produce a reference architecture, a scalability narrative, or their own technical debt honestly is showing you a sales deck, not engineering.
❌ **Reseller Signals:** A team lead named "by title only," a templated architecture diagram, and per-hour pricing with no milestones all point to subcontracted work you can't actually evaluate.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Five Artifacts, Unprompted:** Reference architecture, scalability narrative, tech-debt transparency, security approach, and an integration plan for your actual stack.
✅ **A Weighted Scorecard:** Architecture depth 30%, team continuity 20%, communication 20%, compliance 15%, price 15% — deliberately last.
✅ **Two Independent Scorers:** Have two senior engineers fill out the scorecard separately; where they diverge is exactly where the real signal is hiding.
