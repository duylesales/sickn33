Three colleagues, three finalists, three confident opinions — one liked the sales rep, one liked the low day rate, one liked the flashy demo. Two hours later, still no decision. 🗳️😤

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Loudest Voice Wins:** Without a shared framework, vendor decisions default to whoever argues most persistently in the room.
❌ **Fake Neutrality:** Equal-weighting every category isn't neutral — it silently says cost matters exactly as much as security, which is rarely true for any specific project.
❌ **Anchored Group Scoring:** Scoring vendors together in real time lets an early comment from a senior voice quietly set everyone else's number.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit, Agreed Weights:** Stakeholders set category weights before scoring begins — based on this project's actual priorities, not vendor selection in general.
✅ **Independent Scoring First:** Every evaluator scores alone, submitted before group discussion, surfacing real disagreement instead of quiet consensus.
✅ **A Defensible Record:** A documented scorecard that answers "why this vendor" months later — better than "the team felt good about them."

Structure doesn't remove judgment. It makes judgment explicit. ⚖️
