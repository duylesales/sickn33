A vendor proposal lands with two pricing options attached. Your engineer wants the flexible one. Your gut wants the number you can forecast next quarter without a footnote. 📊⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Fixed" Isn't Fixed:** About 40% of fixed-price contracts see at least one change order — not vendor dishonesty, just specs written before a line of code exists.
❌ **Silent Margin Pressure:** A vendor squeezed by a too-tight fixed number has a direct incentive to cut testing and documentation — a cost that shows up 18 months later as a maintenance budget running 30-40% over.
❌ **T&M Without a Dashboard:** Open time-and-materials billing with no weekly reporting is a corporate card with no statement review — invisible until finance close.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Discovery Before the Number:** 2-4 weeks of paid discovery locks a specification tight enough to make "fixed" actually mean fixed.
✅ **Capped, Reported T&M:** Not-to-exceed ceilings plus weekly itemized reporting track 15-20% closer to budget than monthly-invoice-only engagements.
✅ **Hybrid Sequencing:** Fixed-price for the well-understood build, converting to governed T&M once real usage data informs what's next — with an explicit transition trigger, not a vague handoff.

The right model isn't a preference — it's a risk-allocation decision matched to how much of your scope is actually known today. 🎯
