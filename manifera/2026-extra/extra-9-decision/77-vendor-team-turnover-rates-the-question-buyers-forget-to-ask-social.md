Rates, stack, time zones, references — most IT Managers ask all of it. Almost none ask the question most predictive of a relationship surviving year two: what's your annual turnover rate? 🔄🤔

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Silent Ramp-Up Cost:** A replacement engineer needs 4-8 weeks to reach full productivity — billed at full rate the whole time, and it's rarely budgeted for.
❌ **Diluted Numbers:** Company-wide retention stats can mask a high-turnover delivery team behind a stable admin or sales headcount figure.
❌ **No Advance Warning:** Without a named-resource clause, your vendor can swap engineers with zero notice.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Real Numbers, Not Reassurance:** Actual team tenure and attrition figures disclosed during evaluation, not after signing.
✅ **Replacement SLA:** Equivalent-seniority replacement with a mandatory 2-4 week knowledge-transfer overlap, written into the contract.
✅ **Continuous Documentation:** Architecture and decision records maintained as they happen, not reconstructed after someone leaves.

Well-run teams report 8-15% annual voluntary attrition. If a vendor can't give you their number, that's the answer. 📊
