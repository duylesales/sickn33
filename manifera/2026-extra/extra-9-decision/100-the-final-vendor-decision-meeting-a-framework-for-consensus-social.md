Four months of vendor research. Scorecard done, references checked, technical interviews finished. Then a co-founder raised a concern he'd never voiced before — and 90 minutes later, still no decision. 🤝⏳

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Blindsided in the Room:** Key information encountered for the first time live in the meeting derails a decision months of diligence already supported.
❌ **Unclear Decision Rights:** A room that thinks it's reaching consensus behaves completely differently than one advising a single accountable decision-maker — and nobody said which one this was.
❌ **Debate as the Fix:** Trying to resolve a substantive concern purely through argument in the room instead of naming what evidence would actually settle it.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Complete Pre-Read, 48 Hours Out:** Scorecard, reference summaries, and unresolved concerns sent in advance — no blindsides.
✅ **One Explicit Opening Question:** "Does anyone have a concern not already reflected here?" — asked on purpose, not stumbled into.
✅ **Real vs. Anxiety, Named:** A structured way to tell a substantive gap from ordinary last-minute nerves before deciding.

Their second attempt at this meeting took 40 minutes. Same vendor, same evidence — just a meeting built to actually close it. 📋
