Two vendors pitched a clean-slate rewrite, cut over in one weekend. The third pitched something slower — and considerably less likely to end the CTO's career. 🔧🌙

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Single-Night Gamble:** A big bang cutover compresses months of latent risk into one go-live event — when something breaks, there's no fallback that doesn't mean a multi-day outage.
❌ **Relabeled Risk:** Some vendors pitch a "phased" plan whose actual rollback answer is "we revert to the big bang plan" — that's not a phased strategy, just the same risk with a friendlier slide.
❌ **Misaligned Incentives:** Fixed-price contracts for one monolithic scope reward the vendor for compressing the timeline and skipping validation windows.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Strangler Fig Sequencing:** Module-by-module migration with a routing layer and a defined production observation window before the next cutover.
✅ **Milestone-Tied Payment:** Vendor payment tied to each validated module, not one lump sum — incentives aligned with your operational safety.
✅ **Rehearsed Rollback, Every Phase:** A tested, contractually required rollback procedure before each cutover, not a "we'll figure it out."

The differentiator isn't confidence — it's whether the vendor can describe what happens if module three doesn't go as planned. 🧩
