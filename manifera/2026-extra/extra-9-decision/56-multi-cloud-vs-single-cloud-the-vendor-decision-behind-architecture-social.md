Two DevOps vendors, two proposals. One pushed multi-cloud "for resilience." Only one could actually name a client where it prevented a real outage. ☁️⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Folklore, Not Evidence:** "Avoid lock-in" gets pitched as best practice without proof it fits your risk profile.
❌ **The Hidden Staffing Tax:** Multi-cloud has required 2.3x the infrastructure headcount of single-cloud setups to reach the same maturity.
❌ **Arbitrage That Doesn't Arbitrage:** Below ~€80K/month cloud spend, cross-provider savings rarely beat duplicated tooling costs.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Risk-Profile-First Architecture:** We recommend multi-cloud only when scale, regulation, or existential risk actually justifies it.
✅ **Multi-Region Resilience:** Single-cloud, well-architected setups routinely hit 99.95%+ availability.
✅ **Transparent Cost Modeling:** The real engineering headcount cost, stated upfront — not buried in the proposal.
