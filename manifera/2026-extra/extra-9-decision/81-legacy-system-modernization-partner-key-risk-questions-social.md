Gartner has repeatedly flagged that most large-scale legacy modernization initiatives fail on the first attempt — not because the old code was unsalvageable, but because the migration approach was chosen for the vendor's convenience. 🏚️➡️🏗️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Emergency Mindset:** Signing with whoever starts soonest skips discovery, and six months later you're left with modern infrastructure that still hides every undocumented quirk of the original system.
❌ **Fixed-Price Guesswork:** A quote delivered before any codebase audit means nobody has actually priced what they haven't inspected.
❌ **One Big-Bang Cutover:** A migration plan with a single cutover date and no intermediate checkpoints concentrates all your risk into one moment.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Structured Discovery First:** Every legacy engagement starts with a paid audit of the codebase, dependencies, and undocumented business rules before any roadmap is proposed.
✅ **Risk-Matched Architecture:** Full rewrite, strangler fig, or lift-and-shift chosen based on your system's actual coupling and downtime tolerance — not a one-size-fits-all pitch.
✅ **90-Day Performance Commitments:** Deployment frequency, recovery time, and cost-per-transaction reported in writing after go-live, not just promised at handover.

The architecture of the migration matters more than the tech stack on the slide. 🧭
