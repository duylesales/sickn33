Is your contract type protecting you — or is it just the template your procurement team had on file? For most IT Managers, that question never gets asked before signature. It should be the first one. 📄🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Fixed" Price, Undefined Scope:** Without a documented change-control clause, "fixed" becomes a starting point for renegotiation where the vendor holds all the leverage.
❌ **T&M Without Guardrails:** Time and materials without a not-to-exceed cap and burn-rate reporting turns flexibility into an open-ended liability.
❌ **Retainer Ambiguity:** No clear line between "maintenance" and "new billable feature" turns a support retainer into a monthly argument.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Matched to Risk Profile:** Fixed-price and milestone-based for stable scope, T&M or dedicated team with strong governance for genuine uncertainty.
✅ **Hybrid Transition Triggers:** Pre-agreed, specific conditions for shifting from fixed-price build to dedicated-team enhancement — no vague handoff.
✅ **Consistent Reporting Regardless of Structure:** Sprint reporting and named points of contact that don't reset when the pricing model changes.

The contract type shapes your legal recourse and budget exposure more than most vendor comparisons ever do. ⚖️
