**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fixed Price, Except When It Isn't:** The moment a change request is raised, that work often reverts to an open-ended rate the vendor sets unilaterally.
❌ **Vague Scope, Guaranteed Overrun:** "A reporting dashboard" instead of defined specs guarantees a stream of change tickets at premium pricing.
❌ **No Cumulative Tracking:** Change requests approved one at a time, never totaled against the original budget until it's too late.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Rates Locked Upfront:** Change request pricing negotiated before the project starts — not set under mid-project leverage.
✅ **Real-Time Cumulative Visibility:** Running change spend visible to your finance team throughout, not reconciled at close.
✅ **Governance Caps:** Approval thresholds and a spend cap that trigger renegotiation instead of silent creep.
