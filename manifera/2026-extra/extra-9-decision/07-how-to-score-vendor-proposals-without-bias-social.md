One of your three vendor proposals just looks better — cleaner layout, slicker cover page. That tells you nothing about delivery, yet evaluators rank polished proposals 15-20% higher on technical merit even when the content is equivalent. 📄⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Criteria Drift:** Building scoring categories after reading proposals unconsciously shapes the rubric around whoever already looks strongest.
❌ **Cost Dominance:** An unweighted cost line silently carries 40-50% of the decision just because it's the most concrete number on the page.
❌ **Deciding in the Room:** Live discussion is swayed by whoever presented last or most confidently — a documented recency effect, not a fair read.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Rubric Locked First:** 6-8 weighted categories — technical approach, team composition, communication, security posture — defined before a single proposal is opened.
✅ **Blind + Independent Scoring:** Anonymized technical excerpts, individual scores collected before group discussion, to strip out brand and rapport bias.
✅ **Live Technical Q&A, Scored:** Named engineers, not sales reps, answering on the record — the session that reveals what a proposal document can't.

Tabulate the weighted totals before the final meeting. Discuss the numbers — don't generate them live. 🧮
