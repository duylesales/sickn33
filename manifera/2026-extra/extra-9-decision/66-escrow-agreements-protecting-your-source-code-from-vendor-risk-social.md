A CTO paid her escrow fee faithfully for three years. When the vendor collapsed and she invoked it, the deposited code was 11 months stale and didn't even compile. 🗝️💔

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Paperwork, Not Protection:** A signed escrow agreement means nothing if the deposit was never verified.
❌ **Vague Triggers:** "Vendor ceases operations" doesn't cover slow decline — missed SLAs, ambiguous acquisitions.
❌ **False Sense of Security:** Escrow that isn't checked removes the incentive to seek protection elsewhere.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Verified Deposits:** Regular build-and-run checks in a clean environment — not a one-time setup and forget.
✅ **Specific Release Triggers:** Insolvency, cessation, and measurable support failure thresholds, all defined upfront.
✅ **Continuity by Design:** Stable Amsterdam leadership and long-tenured engineering teams that reduce the risk escrow hedges against.

An untested escrow deposit is a promise, not a plan. 📄
