Uncomfortable truth: the vendor who gives you the most confidence in a sales call is not statistically more likely to deliver good work. 🚩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Beautiful Portfolio" Is a Trap:** It shows the best 5% of everything they've ever shipped, curated by marketing — not who'll actually work on your project.
❌ **Silence Feels Safe, Isn't:** Teams heading toward a bad outcome often go quiet hoping to fix it internally, so you learn about a six-week delay in week five instead of week one.
❌ **The Real Cost of Ignoring Signals:** Founders who miss these red flags lose an average of 2-4 extra months hiring a second team just to audit and partially rebuild what exists.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Five-Point Pre-Signing Checklist:** Named engineers, a 20%-scope-shift cost scenario, a sample status report, an escalation process, and a reference call with a client over a year old.
✅ **Predictable Cadence, Not Milestone Silence:** A working demo every 1-2 weeks and a named point of contact who responds within a business day.
✅ **Push-Back as a Feature:** A vendor who challenges a requirement before signing is showing you how they'll handle truth-telling mid-project.

You can't code-review a vendor — but you can spot these five patterns before you sign. 🧭
