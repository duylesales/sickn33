Your site went down for 40 minutes on Black Friday. You pulled the contract for your SLA credit and found "uptime" mentioned once, with no definition attached. 📉😤

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Self-Graded Uptime Number:** "We measure uptime internally" has an obvious incentive problem — no external verification, no enforceability.
❌ **Averages That Hide Reality:** Average load time can look great while the slowest 5% of real users suffer, invisible in the mean.
❌ **No Consequence, No Contract:** An SLA with no service credit schedule is a statement of intent, not an enforceable term.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **P95 Metrics From Real User Monitoring:** LCP under 2.5s, TTFB under 600ms — measured on real traffic, not ideal-lab synthetic tests.
✅ **Severity-Tiered Incident Response:** 15-30 minute acknowledgment on a full outage, defined resolution targets, not vague "we'll get to it."
✅ **Automatic Service Credits:** Tied to documented breaches, no renegotiation required each time.

Read the SLA like you'll need to enforce it — because eventually, you will. 📋
