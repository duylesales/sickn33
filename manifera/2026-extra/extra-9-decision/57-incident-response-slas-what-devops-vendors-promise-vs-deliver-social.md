2:14am. Checkout outage. "15-minute response SLA." At minute 14 she got an autoresponder — not an engineer. That's the gap nobody reads for in the fine print. ⏱️🚨

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Response ≠ Resolution:** "Response" often just means acknowledgment, not an engineer actively working the fix.
❌ **Self-Graded Severity:** Vendors classifying their own incidents can quietly downgrade a Sev-1 into a Sev-2 in the moment it matters most.
❌ **Toothless Penalties:** Small service credits cost the vendor far less than the outage costs you.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Separated SLA Definitions:** Response and resolution time, by severity tier, spelled out in the contract — not the pitch deck.
✅ **Objective Severity Criteria:** Classification tied to measurable impact, agreed upfront, not on-call discretion.
✅ **Amsterdam Escalation Point:** A real account team, not just an offshore support queue, when things go wrong.

Read the fine print before the outage teaches it to you. 📄
