The relationship was fine. Not great, not troubled — just steadily adequate. That's exactly the kind of contract that renews on autopilot while price quietly creeps upward. 📈🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Fine" Isn't a Standard:** Not bad enough to replace is a very different bar than actually the best available option at today's market rate.
❌ **Complacency Compounds:** Adequate vendors sail through unexamined renewals — the exact condition where price creep and lowered SLA performance accumulate unnoticed.
❌ **No Benchmark, No Leverage:** A vague sense you "could probably find this cheaper" is worth nothing at the negotiating table.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Market-Rate Benchmarking:** A lightweight competitive check before every meaningful renewal, whether or not you intend to switch.
✅ **A Real Performance Scorecard:** SLA adherence, delivery accuracy, and incident history as standard renewal inputs — not a general feeling.
✅ **Actively Earned, Not Assumed:** A relationship that welcomes scrutiny performs like one that knows it has to keep earning the contract.

Ask the harder question: would we choose this vendor again today, at today's rate? 💭
