"Cast a wide net, get 10 proposals, the best one will rise to the top." It won't. More vendors doesn't sharpen a decision — it degrades it. 📉🎯

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Choice Overload Is Real:** Past a fairly low threshold, decision quality drops and confidence in the final pick falls too — even when the eventual choice is objectively fine.
❌ **Shallow x8 Isn't Deep x1:** Juggling eight vendor conversations isn't eight thorough evaluations — it's eight rushed ones, plus the coordination overhead of tracking which detail came from which proposal.
❌ **One Vendor, No Baseline:** Evaluating a single strong-seeming vendor in isolation means judging them against your expectations, not a real market comparison.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Three-Stage Funnel:** 8-12 for initial sourcing, 3-5 for proposals and first calls, 2-3 finalists for deep diligence — narrowing deliberately, not staying wide throughout.
✅ **Deep Diligence Where It Counts:** Reference calls, technical Q&A with named engineers, and pilot discussions reserved for a field small enough to actually finish properly.
✅ **Built for the Narrowed Field:** Proposals sized to slot cleanly into a disciplined 2-3 finalist comparison, not add noise to an overloaded search.

If you're already juggling seven vendor conversations, the fix isn't finishing them all — it's cutting the field now. ✂️
