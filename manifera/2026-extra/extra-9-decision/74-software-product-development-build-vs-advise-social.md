"Just execute the spec" sounds efficient — until your partner builds a flawed idea perfectly, on time and on budget, and you don't find out until real users show up. 🚧

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Momentum ≠ Direction:** Sprints close, features ship, velocity charts look healthy — none of it tells you whether the product is one your market actually wants.
❌ **Silent Scope Gaps:** A pure execution partner has limited incentive to challenge a flawed assumption baked into the spec they were handed.
❌ **The "Cheaper" Illusion:** An €80K quote can balloon past €150K once an unchallenged data-model assumption forces a rebuild — Deloitte flags unvalidated-assumption rework as one of the biggest hidden cost drivers in software.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Structured Discovery First:** 1-3 weeks of business analysis before a single sprint, catching expensive mistakes before they're built.
✅ **Advisors Who Push Back:** Full-stack teams that flag oversized scope and recommend leaner alternatives before committing engineering hours.
✅ **A Model That Shifts With You:** Amsterdam-based leads own strategy discovery while the Ho Chi Minh City team executes — scaling from advisory-heavy to execution-focused as your product direction stabilizes.

Pick the partner role that matches how much product uncertainty you're really carrying. 🎯
