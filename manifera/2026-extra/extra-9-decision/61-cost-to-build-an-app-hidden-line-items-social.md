Fewer than half of fixed-price app quotes hold their original number by the time the app hits the App Store. The rest grow — quietly, through "out of scope" emails. 📱💰

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Discovery Skipped to Look Cheap:** Quotes built from a discovery call, not a real spec, turn every scope gap into a marked-up change order.
❌ **Bundled Integrations:** "Payment integration" as one line item hides 20-40 hours of webhooks, refunds, and failed-payment logic per integration.
❌ **Silent Post-Launch Costs:** Hosting, monitoring, and OS-compatibility maintenance continue indefinitely — most quotes stop counting at launch.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Structured Discovery Sprint:** Wireframes, data models, and a written spec that a second vendor could estimate against and land near the same number.
✅ **Line-Item Transparency:** Every integration, revision round, and QA device matrix priced individually — not bundled into a vague total.
✅ **Realistic Full-Year Budgeting:** A €46K-€66K realistic total for a mid-complexity MVP, not a €25K headline number designed to win the bid.
