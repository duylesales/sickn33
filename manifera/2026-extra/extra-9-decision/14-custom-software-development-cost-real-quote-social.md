❌ **Divergent Assumptions, Not Divergent Efficiency:** One vendor prices in full discovery and structured QA; another skips both and treats testing as something developers do informally between features.
❌ **Costs That Never Made the Quote:** Third-party service fees, unbudgeted post-launch support, and change-order pricing routinely surprise CEOs only after signature.
❌ **The Cheapest Quote Loses Over 18 Months:** Rework on under-scoped builds commonly runs 15-30% of the original cost within the first year — enough to flip which quote was actually cheaper.

✅ **Six Itemized Categories:** Discovery, design, development, QA, DevOps, and project management — every euro accounted for, not collapsed into one number.
✅ **Total Cost of Ownership, Projected 18 Months Out:** Build cost plus realistic infrastructure, rework, and maintenance — not just the headline figure.
✅ **A Named Team Structure per Line Item:** Who owns backend architecture, who owns the design system, who runs CI/CD — verifiable, not templated.
