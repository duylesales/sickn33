Everyone assumes that once a vendor says "we use AI coding assistants," the hard part of due diligence is done. That assumption is exactly backwards. 🤖

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Confident-Looking Code Isn't Reviewed Code:** AI-generated output can reference a library method that doesn't exist or duplicate logic elsewhere — and it looks more polished than a typical human mistake, making it harder to catch by eye.
❌ **"Cheaper Because AI Does the Work" Is a Red Flag:** A dramatically lower price often means the review layer got skipped entirely, not that the project got genuinely cheaper to deliver safely.
❌ **AI Writing Its Own Tests:** A flawed implementation paired with a test written to match that same flaw passes every time without validating real behavior.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Independent Review, Every Time:** AI-assisted pull requests reviewed by someone other than the person who prompted the draft — a fixed standard, not one that loosens as trust builds.
✅ **Specific Checks Built In:** Hallucinated dependencies, license conflicts, and duplicated business rules flagged explicitly, not caught by a generic pre-AI checklist.
✅ **Written Into the Statement of Work:** Ask for it in writing — vendors with a real standard answer immediately; vendors without one improvise on the spot.

You don't need to read code to use this checklist — you need to hear how confidently they answer it. 📋
