Three finalist proposals, all claiming strong Agile process, all with polished portfolios, all within 12% on day rate. An IT manager with a Friday deadline still couldn't tell which vendor deserved the recommendation. 🗂️⏰

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Unweighted Scoring:** Adding price, process, and portfolio on the same 1-5 scale treats them as interchangeable — a weak process with a beautiful portfolio is a liability waiting to surface in sprint three.
❌ **Claims Without Evidence:** Every finalist describes the same sprint cadence and code review discipline in nearly identical language — table-stakes claims, not real differentiators.
❌ **Deciding in the Room:** Making the call right after the most recent, most persuasive sales pitch is a well-documented recency bias trap.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Name the Binding Constraint First:** Fixed budget makes price a filter, not a weight; a compliance-heavy legacy system means process maturity should dominate the scoring.
✅ **Evidence Over Description:** Request a real sprint board or redacted code review thread — a vendor producing it within a day has an actual functioning process.
✅ **Confirmed Overlap and Flexibility:** Amsterdam governance layer with a Ho Chi Minh City engineering hub, giving a documented daily overlap window instead of a vague "good communication" promise.

Build the weighted scorecard before the final call, not after. 🎯
