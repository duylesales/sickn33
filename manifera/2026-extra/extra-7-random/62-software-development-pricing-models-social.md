🚨 The right software development pricing model depends on one variable: how genuinely well-defined the requirements are. Not which model sounds most reassuring. 💶⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fixed Price on Unstable Requirements:** A "certain" number padded to cover unstated risk.
❌ **Certainty That's Actually Illusory:** Change orders relocating the same budget uncertainty elsewhere.
❌ **One Model Forced on a Mixed Project:** Stable and uncertain components priced identically.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Model Matched to Genuine Clarity:** Fixed-price where requirements are stable, time-and-materials where they're not.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Honest assessment of remaining ambiguity before pricing is agreed.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Transparent delivery under whichever model genuinely fits each component.

We wanted budget certainty so badly we paid for a fixed number on something nobody could have specified yet. The hybrid approach cost us less and gave us more real visibility. 🛡️
