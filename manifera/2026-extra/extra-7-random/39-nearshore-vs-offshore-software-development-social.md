🚨 Nearshore vs. offshore is usually framed as a geography question. The real question underneath is what type of collaboration the work actually requires — geography is just a proxy. 💶⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Paying for Convenience You Don't Use:** A 20-40% nearshore premium for synchronous capacity bounded, well-specified work never needed.
❌ **Cutting Corners on the Wrong Work:** Offshore coordination friction on work that genuinely needed real-time alignment.
❌ **The Wrong Primary Variable:** Deciding on raw rate and time-zone overlap instead of actual collaboration needs.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **One Honest Test:** How much coordination genuinely needs to happen in real time, versus asynchronously?
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Diagnosing collaboration type before defaulting to a geography-based decision.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Structured, asynchronous-first execution that captures offshore efficiency without the tradeoff.

We paid the nearshore premium because it felt safer. We were paying for something we barely needed. 🛡️
