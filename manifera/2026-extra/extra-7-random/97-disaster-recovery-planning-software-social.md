Most disaster recovery plans exist as a document. Most have never once been tested against a real failure. 🔥📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Never Actually Tested:** A runbook discussed in a meeting, never executed as a real failover.
❌ **Backups Nobody Restored:** A "successful" backup job that's never been proven actually restorable.
❌ **One Person's Memory:** Recovery procedures that live in a senior engineer's head, not a documented runbook.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Per-System RTO/RPO:** Recovery targets set by actual business impact, not one blanket number.
✅ **Amsterdam Governance:** Incident protocols defined before the crisis, not during it.
✅ **Vietnam Execution:** Real, scheduled failover exercises that prove the plan actually works.

One untested plan promised a 4-hour recovery. The real, tested number was 11 hours. Now it's 3.5. ⏱️
