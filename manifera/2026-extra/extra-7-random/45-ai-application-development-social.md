🚨 The one-time build cost of an AI application usually gets a careful estimate. The ongoing, usage-based model cost gets a rough placeholder that's frequently wrong by a wide margin. 💶🤖

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **A Careful Build Budget, A Guessed Running Cost:** The variable most likely to blow the total is the one estimated least carefully.
❌ **Costs Scale With Real Usage:** Which doesn't exist yet during the budgeting phase.
❌ **A Surprise Bill:** Discovering the overrun only when a monthly invoice arrives triple the estimate.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **An Honest Usage-Cost Model:** Estimated before launch, not a rough afterthought.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Cost forecasting and monitoring treated as seriously as the build budget.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Cost-aware technical design — model tiering and caching built in from the start.

We budgeted the build carefully and guessed at the running cost. The guess was wrong by a lot. 🛡️
