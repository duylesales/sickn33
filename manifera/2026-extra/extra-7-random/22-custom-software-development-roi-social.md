🚨 Most custom software ROI calculations compare build cost against solving the immediate problem, and stop there. No ongoing maintenance. No compounding value. Just the easy half of the math. 💶⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Maintenance Cost, Ignored:** 15-25% of build cost, every year, indefinitely — often exceeding the original build cost over time.
❌ **Opportunity Cost Treated as Sunk:** Internal engineering capacity that could have delivered something else entirely.
❌ **Compounding Value, Uncounted:** A reusable capability that enables future initiatives, missed entirely from the calculation.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Categories, Counted Fully:** Full lifetime maintenance cost, real opportunity cost, compounding value, and risk-reduction value.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Building the complete ROI model before committing — the number that actually reflects the decision.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Built with reusability and extensibility in mind, so compounding value is realized, not just assumed.
