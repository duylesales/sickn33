A team adopts Kubernetes to fix a real deployment problem. 18 months later: a dozen clusters nobody can fully explain. 🐳📈

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Cluster Sprawl by Default:** Spinning up a new cluster has zero friction, so it just... happens.
❌ **Idle Capacity Everywhere:** Unmanaged environments often run at just 20-35% real utilization.
❌ **Security Debt Accumulates:** More clusters means more attack surface and more configs to keep patched.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Consolidate with Real Ownership:** Fewer clusters, namespace-level RBAC doing the isolation work.
✅ **Amsterdam Governance:** A resource and security policy that prevents sprawl from creeping back.
✅ **Vietnam Execution:** Right-sized requests, tuned autoscaling, continuous maintenance.

One audit took eleven clusters down to four and cut Kubernetes spend 41% with zero lost capability. ⚙️
