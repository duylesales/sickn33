🚨 Every software design decision feels important in the room. Almost none actually are. Most are cheap to reverse later — a small handful are genuinely expensive. Can your team tell them apart? ⚙️🏗️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Equal Deliberation, Unequal Stakes:** Weeks burned on naming conventions, minutes spent on a payment provider that becomes entangled in your data model.
❌ **The Wrong Mistake Twice:** Over-deliberating cheap choices, under-deliberating the ones that actually matter.
❌ **€80,000-€200,000 in Avoidable Migration:** For a data model or architecture pattern that a few extra days upfront would have gotten right.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit Reversibility Triage:** Does reversing this require touching live data or breaking an external contract? That answer decides how much deliberation it gets.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Design decisions triaged by reversibility before development starts.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Fast on genuinely low-stakes choices, careful review flagged for the ones that aren't.

Picking the payment provider felt easy. It turned out to be one of three decisions that actually mattered. 🛡️
