🚨 CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation are the visible, easy-to-buy part of DevOps. The cultural change that actually determines whether tools deliver DevOps' promised benefits is a lot easier to skip. ⚙️🔧

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Modern Tools, Unchanged Culture:** Automated deployments inside the same siloed, blame-oriented dynamics.
❌ **Blame Instead of Root Cause:** Incidents that trigger blame-seeking instead of honest, systemic learning.
❌ **Handoffs, Not Collaboration:** Development decides what ships, operations finds out after.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Three Cultural Elements, Explicit:** Shared reliability ownership, blameless review, and genuine cross-functional collaboration.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Scoping culture and process with equal weight to tooling — not an assumed side effect.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Tooling implemented inside a genuinely collaborative operating model.

We had all the modern tools and none of the actual DevOps culture. Once we changed how incidents got reviewed, things started genuinely improving. 🛡️
