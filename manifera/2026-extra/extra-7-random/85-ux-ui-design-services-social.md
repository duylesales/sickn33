Everyone internally loved the new design. Three months later, conversion hadn't moved at all. "Looks great" and "works" are not the same scorecard. 🎨📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Validated by Applause, Not Users:** Stakeholder approval mistaken for proof the design actually works.
❌ **Invisible Navigation Problems:** Beautiful individual screens hiding an information architecture that confuses real users.
❌ **Design Debt That Compounds:** One-off screens re-solved inconsistently with every new feature.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Real Usability Testing:** Unassisted users attempting actual tasks, not internal review of polished mockups.
✅ **Full Task-Flow Architecture:** Navigation assessed across real user goals, not screen by screen.
✅ **Reusable Design Systems:** Consistency and accessibility built once, applied everywhere, from the start.

A demo that impresses a room isn't evidence a product works for the person using it alone. 🖱️
