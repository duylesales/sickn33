🚨 Most build-vs-buy software decisions aren't decisions — they're justifications for a preference someone already had. Run the numbers honestly afterward, and the analysis is usually reverse-engineered. ⚙️💥

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Engineering Prefers Build, Ops Prefers Buy:** Numbers assembled to make the pre-existing preference look objectively correct.
❌ **Maintenance Cost Left Out of the Build Column:** An "analysis" that only counts the initial acquisition cost.
❌ **The Wrong Call Is Expensive at Scale:** Multiples of an off-the-shelf cost maintaining something that never needed to be custom.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Variables, Applied Consistently:** Genuine differentiation, full multi-year total cost, real speed to value, and lock-in risk weighed for both paths.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Resisting the pull toward whichever answer a team walked in preferring.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Honest, realistic build timelines and maintenance-cost estimates — not an optimistic pitch.

The original analysis only compared the numbers that made building look good. All four variables, run honestly, told a different story. 🛡️
