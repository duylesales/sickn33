🚨 The standard whiteboard coding interview tests a skill that has almost nothing to do with the day-to-day work it's supposed to screen for. ⚙️🧑‍💻

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Testing an Artificial Skill:** Memorized algorithms under pressure, no tools, nothing like real work.
❌ **Rejecting Strong Candidates:** People who don't interview well in that specific narrow format, regardless of real ability.
❌ **Passing the Wrong Candidates:** People who prepared for the format, not the job.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Job-Realistic Assessment:** Tool-permitted exercises, code review, and structured discussion of real past work.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Designing hiring around what the job actually requires, not interview tradition.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Engineers evaluated on skills that genuinely predict their working ability.

We were rejecting people who couldn't invert a binary tree from memory, then wondering why our code quality wasn't improving. 🛡️
