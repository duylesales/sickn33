🚨 Every SDLC diagram shows the same tidy boxes: planning, design, development, testing, deployment, maintenance. Every real project skips at least one under deadline pressure — usually the same one. ⚙️📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Testing Gets Cut First:** The last stage before delivery, the easiest to compress when time runs short.
❌ **The Cost Is Deferred, Not Eliminated:** Surfaces weeks later as a "separate" production incident nobody connects to the original shortcut.
❌ **Planning Skips Force a Mid-Build Pivot:** 2-3x the original estimate once rework is counted.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Every Stage Treated as Real:** Planning, testing, deployment, and maintenance protected as non-negotiable — not optional under pressure.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Protecting the stages most likely to be silently compressed.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Genuine testing and repeatable deployment automation as standard practice, not a rushed click-through.

We saved 4 days by rushing testing. We spent 3x that cleaning up the mess. 🛡️
