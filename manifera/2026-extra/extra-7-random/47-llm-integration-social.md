🚨 A well-crafted prompt behaves well against the cases it was tested on. Treating it as your primary reliability mechanism means relying on something that degrades unpredictably outside those cases. ⚙️🧠

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Prompt Quality, Trusted Implicitly:** No structural check for when the model doesn't do what was asked.
❌ **Confidently Wrong:** Incorrect output presented with the same confidence as correct output.
❌ **No Plan for Failure:** Broken or nonsensical output surfaced straight to the end user.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Structural Safeguards:** Output validation, confidence-aware routing, graceful degradation, and ongoing evaluation.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Designing for genuine reliability, not just a well-tuned prompt.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Building the safety net as core infrastructure, not an afterthought.
