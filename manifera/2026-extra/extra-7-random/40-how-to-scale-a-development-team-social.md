🚨 Coordination overhead grows roughly with the number of communication pathways between people — which grows quadratically as headcount grows linearly. Scaling a team without restructuring is scaling costs faster than output. ⚙️📈

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **More People, Less Output Each:** Declining output per engineer, easily misdiagnosed as a hiring-quality problem.
❌ **Structure Set Once, Never Revisited:** A team structure that worked at eight people, still in place at twenty-two.
❌ **Reactive Restructuring:** Waiting until symptoms are already visible instead of anticipating the threshold.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Proactive Decomposition:** Clear sub-team boundaries at anticipated size thresholds, before coordination costs outpace growth.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Restructuring deliberately, not just hiring more people into the same structure.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Clearly-bounded sub-teams with explicit connective ownership across boundaries.

We kept hiring good people and getting less out of each one. It wasn't the people — we never restructured while we grew. 🛡️
