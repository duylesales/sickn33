Design brought in after requirements are locked can only decorate a decision that's already been made. It can't test whether the decision was right. 🎯🖌️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Design as an Afterthought:** Brought in only to style requirements someone else already finalized.
❌ **Anchoring on the First Idea:** No divergent concepts tested before committing engineering resources.
❌ **Post-Launch Discovery:** Problem-fit issues found only after the full feature is already built.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Research Before Requirements Lock:** Real user friction surfaced before the spec is written.
✅ **Divergent Concept Validation:** Multiple directions prototype-tested before any full build begins.
✅ **Strategic Coherence:** A defined product vision that gives every feature decision a consistent frame.

Built exactly to spec, barely touched by customers — because the spec was never tested. 🧭
