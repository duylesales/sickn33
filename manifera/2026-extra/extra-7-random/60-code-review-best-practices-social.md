🚨 A team that optimizes code review purely for turnaround speed gets exactly that — and loses much of what review actually exists to provide. Speed and depth are frequently in real tension. ⚙️👀

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fast, Shallow Approvals:** Reviewers under pressure catch typos, miss logic errors and architecture concerns.
❌ **A Healthy Metric, Hollowed-Out Practice:** Turnaround time looks great while review value quietly erodes.
❌ **Invisible Until It Isn't:** Deeper bugs reach production, discovered long after reviews got "faster."

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Practices, Balanced:** Smaller PRs, automated surface checks, reasonable response times, risk-weighted depth.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Actively managing the speed-depth tension, not defaulting to the visible metric.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Disciplined small changes and risk-weighted review that deliver both quality and speed.

We fixed the complaint about slow reviews by making them fast, and it turned out fast reviews were barely reviews at all. 🛡️
