🎯 A polished portfolio tells you what a vendor can do on a good day. It tells you nothing about what happens on a bad one — and that's exactly when most vendor relationships actually fail. 🤝

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Portfolios All Look the Same:** Every vendor in a competitive process has case studies that clear the bar.
❌ **The Bait-and-Switch:** The senior team from the pitch gets rotated away right after you sign.
❌ **Untested Escalation Paths:** You find out how a vendor handles a crisis only once you're already in one.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Reference Calls About Problems:** Asking how friction was handled, not just whether clients were satisfied.
✅ **Same Team, Pilot Through Delivery:** No bait-and-switch — the people you evaluate are the people who deliver.
✅ **A Real Paid Pilot:** Scoped, real work that tests fit before any long-term commitment.

Test the relationship before you commit to it, not after. 🔍
