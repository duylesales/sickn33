🚨 A software project has a defined end state. A software product has neither. Treating product development like a project is why so many "successful" launches quietly stall a year later. ⚙️📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Launch Treated as Completion:** Project instincts applied to something that was never meant to be "done."
❌ **The Team Disbands at Launch:** Taking institutional knowledge with it, right when it's needed most.
❌ **12-18 Months to Decay:** A product frozen at launch-day state while the market keeps moving.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Ongoing, Not One-Time:** Success measured continuously through usage and retention, not a single delivery date.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Structuring product engagements for evolution from day one — never treating launch as the finish line.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Persistent teams that retain institutional knowledge well beyond launch.

We shipped it, called it done, and moved the team on. A year later it barely reflected the market anymore. 🛡️
