🚨 Most advice on reducing software development costs is actually advice on reducing what gets delivered. That genuinely cuts the invoice — and relocates the cost somewhere more expensive later. 💶⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Cheaper Team, More Rework:** Less experienced engineers, lower-quality code, costlier fixes later.
❌ **Less Testing, More Bugs:** "Savings" that show up as post-launch bug-fixing instead.
❌ **Scope Cuts That Miss the Point:** A cheaper project that doesn't actually solve the business need.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Genuine Levers:** Requirements rigor, build-vs-buy discipline, fully-loaded cost comparison, and coordination efficiency.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Eliminating real waste before ever touching scope or quality.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Structural discipline that reduces cost without reducing output.

We cut testing and hired cheaper to save money, and ended up spending more fixing what broke after launch than we'd saved. 🛡️
