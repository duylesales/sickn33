📜 Paying for custom software doesn't automatically mean you own it. "Work made for hire" doesn't apply the same way everywhere, and the IP clause is the one part of the contract most CEOs skim past. ⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Assumed Ownership:** "Work made for hire" language alone doesn't reliably cover every category of work product.
❌ **Undistinguished Pre-Existing IP:** Vendor frameworks baked into your deliverable can mean you only got a license, not ownership.
❌ **Boilerplate Liability Caps:** A cap set at fees paid is nowhere near enough if the software handles payments or sensitive data.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit IP Assignment:** Unambiguous, jurisdiction-independent language reviewable by your own counsel.
✅ **Clear Pre-Existing IP Terms:** No confusion about what's yours outright versus licensed.
✅ **Source Access From Day One:** Ownership as a verifiable fact throughout delivery, not a promise at handoff.
