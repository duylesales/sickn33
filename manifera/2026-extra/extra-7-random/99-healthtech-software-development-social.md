A product can pass every item on a HIPAA checklist and still make a breach far more likely. Architecture is the real test. 🏥🔒

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **BAA Signed, Architecture Wrong:** Paperwork looks compliant, PHI access isn't actually scoped down.
❌ **Uniform Access, Not Minimum-Necessary:** One permissions model applied across all patient data.
❌ **Failed Security Reviews:** Hospital and payer procurement stalls for months over PHI architecture gaps.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **PHI Segmentation by Design:** Access scoped to the minimum necessary, at the data-model level.
✅ **Amsterdam Regulatory Architecture:** Built before a single feature, not retrofitted after launch.
✅ **Vietnam Execution:** Real HL7/FHIR interoperability and granular PHI audit logging.
