⏱️ "Best effort response times" and "commercially reasonable uptime" sound protective. They're unenforceable. The gap only shows up during the exact incident the SLA was supposed to cover. 📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Vague Clocks:** "Prompt response" defines nothing — no severity tier, no start time, no obligation.
❌ **Uptime Without Methodology:** A 99.9% promise means nothing if both sides can't calculate it the same way.
❌ **Token Remedies:** Service credits that round to nothing give vendors no real incentive to perform.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Severity-Tiered Response Times:** Unambiguous clocks that start and stop on defined triggers.
✅ **Real Uptime Measurement:** A methodology both parties calculate identically from the same data.
✅ **Meaningful Remedies and Named Escalation:** Financial stakes that matter, and roles accountable by name.

An SLA that only reads well is worse than no SLA — it creates false confidence. 🔐
