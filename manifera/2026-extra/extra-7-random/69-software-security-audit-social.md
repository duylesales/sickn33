📋 A security audit that's just an automated scan with a cover page isn't an audit. The gap between a real one and a superficial one only becomes visible after the incident the superficial one missed. 🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Scan-and-Report Passed as an Audit:** Default tool output reformatted, never testing real authorization logic.
❌ **A "Clean" Report Becomes a Liability:** It proves the gap was looked for and missed, not that it didn't exist.
❌ **Half the Attack Surface Ignored:** Application-only reviews skip cloud infrastructure misconfigurations.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Architecture and Trust Boundary Review:** Mapping where the system trusts what it structurally shouldn't.
✅ **Role-Based Authorization Testing:** Verifying access control across tenancy and roles, not just login.
✅ **Full Transitive Dependency and Infrastructure Review:** Nothing three levels deep goes unchecked.

A report that holds up when someone else checks your work — that's the actual bar. 🎯
