🚨 Ask a team to describe their software development process and they'll recite the same words every team recites. Watch how they actually work for a week — the described process and the real one are often only loosely related. ⚙️📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Process on a Wiki, Not in Practice:** Sprints, standups, retros — all documented, none reliably followed.
❌ **The Illusion of Predictability:** A roadmap commitment planned around sprint estimates that have never actually been reliable.
❌ **The Same Retro Complaints, Every Cycle:** Because action items never get tracked or implemented.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Verifiable Signals, Not Self-Description:** Estimate calibration, real PR review substance, retro follow-through, and a genuinely enforced definition of done.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Auditing process reality against documentation using actual artifacts — not trusting the team's self-report.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** A genuinely enforced definition of done and calibrated estimation — sprint commitments that actually mean something.

Our process looked perfect in the wiki. It just wasn't the process we were actually running. 🛡️
