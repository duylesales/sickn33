🚨 Nearly every company that sets out to build an MVP ends up building something considerably bigger than minimum. "Minimum" is the hardest word in the phrase to actually hold the line on. ⚙️📏

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Death by a Thousand Reasonable Additions:** Each feature seems fine alone; the cumulative scope drifts past minimum.
❌ **Lost Speed:** A bloated MVP takes considerably longer to build than a genuinely minimal one.
❌ **Lost Clarity:** Too many variables at once makes it impossible to isolate what's actually driving user behavior.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Written Boundary, Defended:** The exact hypothesis and success action the MVP exists to test — defined before the build.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Every proposed addition judged against that boundary, not its own individual merit.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Out-of-scope ideas deferred to a backlog, not absorbed mid-build.

Our first MVP took nine months and we still couldn't say what we'd learned. The second took six weeks and gave us an answer we could act on. 🛡️
