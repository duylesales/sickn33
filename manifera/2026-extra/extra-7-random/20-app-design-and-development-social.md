🚨 A beautiful design file and a functioning app are two different deliverables. The gap between them — technical feasibility, real data instead of placeholders — is exactly where a design-then-development handoff breaks down. 🎨⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Design "Final," Feasibility Discovered Later:** Beautiful concepts that assume interactions the platform can't actually support.
❌ **Shared Decisions, Excluded Discipline:** Loading states and edge cases nobody in the room owns.
❌ **Two Vendors, One Dispute:** Each with a reasonable case for why the gap isn't their fault, while the launch date slips.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Integrated Design and Development:** The same team designs and implements — feasibility validated before anything is finalized.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Technical feasibility review built into design decisions from the start.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** One accountable team, continuous collaboration — no static file passed between strangers.

One team designing and building together never had that argument to have in the first place. 🛡️
