A 10-person team and a 200-person team running the same monolith are not solving the same problem — but both keep reaching for the same answer. 🧩⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fashion Over Diagnosis:** Microservices proposed as the default fix without checking if the bottleneck is even architectural.
❌ **Boundaries Drawn Wrong:** Service lines drawn around technical layers instead of team ownership.
❌ **Deployment Gets Worse:** More services, more network calls, more failure modes — and slower releases, not faster.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Diagnose First:** Determine if the real problem is architecture, team structure, or deployment process.
✅ **Amsterdam Architecture Discipline:** Boundaries mapped to team ownership, not technical layers.
✅ **Vietnam Strangler-Fig Execution:** One service extracted and validated in production at a time.

Migrations without operational maturity in place often make deployment frequency worse for a year or more. 📉
