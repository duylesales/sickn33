🚨 Today's best AI models will be meaningfully different two years from now — same as two years ago vs. today. Building on today's model as a permanent foundation means building on ground guaranteed to shift. ⚙️🔄

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Tightly Coupled Logic:** Prompts and output handling implicitly tuned to one specific model's quirks.
❌ **A Swap That's Actually a Rebuild:** Switching models means redoing the tuning work from scratch.
❌ **No Consistent Evaluation Baseline:** Deciding whether to switch models becomes an ad hoc judgment call.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Three Architectural Disciplines:** Separated core logic, model-agnostic safeguards, and a portable evaluation framework.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Architecting for inevitable model change from day one.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Building a product genuinely able to evolve with the underlying technology.

The first time a better model came out, switching cost us almost as much as building the product. The second time, it took two weeks. 🛡️
