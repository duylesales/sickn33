🚨 Most guides to building an application from scratch start with technology choices. They skip the one step that determines whether any of those choices matter. ⚙️🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Conviction Mistaken for Validation:** Jumping straight to technical build based on the founder's own certainty.
❌ **A Formality, Not a Test:** Validation designed to confirm a conclusion already reached protects nothing.
❌ **€80,000-€250,000 at Risk:** Discovered after launch instead of before the build, when it's cheap to fix.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Genuine Validation First:** Landing pages, real conversations, or a manual test — cheap ways to test the core assumption.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Genuinely willing to hear the idea doesn't hold — real validation, not theater.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** A focused minimum viable build scoped to what validation actually confirmed.
