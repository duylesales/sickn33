🚨 Some technical debt genuinely deserves to be paid down. Some is genuinely fine to carry indefinitely. Treating "reduce technical debt" as one undifferentiated goal wastes real engineering effort. ⚙️💳

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Fixing What's Visible, Not What's Costly:** Paying down debt in stable, rarely-touched code that was never actually slowing anyone down.
❌ **The Real Friction, Ignored:** High-touch, high-cost debt persists because it's harder to address.
❌ **A Smaller Number, No Velocity Change:** Aggregate debt reduction that doesn't translate to actual improvement.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Two Factors, Weighted:** Ongoing cost (by touch frequency) versus paydown cost — prioritized explicitly.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** A genuine cost-weighted portfolio, not a backlog to clear to zero.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Paydown effort concentrated where it actually improves development velocity.

We spent a year paying down the biggest, most visible debt and could barely tell the difference in how fast we were moving. 🛡️
