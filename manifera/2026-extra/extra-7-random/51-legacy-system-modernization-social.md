🚨 A full legacy system rewrite feels most satisfying to propose — a clean break from technical debt. It's also the modernization approach with the highest failure rate. ⚙️🏚️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Scope Rediscovered, Not Estimated:** Undocumented edge cases surface mid-rewrite, blowing past every timeline.
❌ **Two Systems to Maintain:** The legacy system still needs upkeep while resources go toward replacing it.
❌ **Abandoned Midway:** Significant budget spent, left with two half-functional systems instead of one working one.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Strangler Fig, Not All-or-Nothing:** New functionality built alongside the legacy system, migrated piece by piece.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Incremental approaches as the default — reserving full rewrites for when logic itself is wrong.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Each piece validated in production before the next is tackled.

We tried to rewrite everything at once and got fourteen months in with nothing to show. The second approach gave us something working in weeks. 🛡️
