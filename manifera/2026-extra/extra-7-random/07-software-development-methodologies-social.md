🚨 A team running 2-week sprints, calling every meeting a "ceremony," using "agile" in every sentence — might just be running waterfall with better vocabulary, one chunk at a time. ⚙️📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Ceremonies Without Substance:** Requirements locked months ago, the roadmap never actually changes sprint to sprint.
❌ **Worst of Both Worlds:** Agile's overhead without its responsiveness, or waterfall's rigidity without its upfront rigor.
❌ **15-20% of Capacity Wasted:** On process overhead that isn't earning its keep in either direction.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Diagnosed Fit, Not a Default Label:** Matched to the project's actual uncertainty profile — cost and likelihood of a late-discovered requirement.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Selecting a genuine methodology fit before the team starts working.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Real methodological discipline — genuine iteration where it's needed, genuine rigor where it isn't.

We were doing sprint planning for a plan that hadn't changed in 4 months. Admitting that freed up real capacity. 🛡️
