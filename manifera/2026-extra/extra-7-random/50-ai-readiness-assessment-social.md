🚨 An AI readiness assessment focused on infrastructure checks the part that's easy to fix fast. It skips the part that actually predicts success: your data. ⚙️📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Infrastructure Boxes Checked, Data Ignored:** Cloud capacity and APIs assessed carefully; data quality assumed.
❌ **Dashboards Lie by Omission:** Invisible cleanup makes underlying raw data look cleaner than it is.
❌ **A One-Time Cleanup Isn't Enough:** Without ongoing discipline, the same inconsistencies keep coming back.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Three Real Readiness Questions:** Data accessibility, data quality, and organizational data discipline.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Assessments weighted toward the constraint that actually predicts success.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Hands-on examination of real data samples — not optimistic assumptions.
