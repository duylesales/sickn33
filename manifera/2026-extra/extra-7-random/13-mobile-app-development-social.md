🚨 The average mobile app loses roughly 3/4 of its users within the first 3 days. Most mobile app development spends nearly all its attention on the milestone that turns out to matter least: the download. 📱⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Optimizing for the Visible Milestone:** App store listing, download numbers, launch-week press — while retention silently craters.
❌ **Tested on a New Phone With Full Signal:** Not the slow network and older device most real users actually have.
❌ **€40,000-€100,000 in Wasted Marketing Spend:** Acquiring users who were never going to stay past day three.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Real Retention Predictors:** Fast time-to-value, real-condition performance, a genuine return trigger, and calibrated notifications.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Designing around retention-predicting factors from the start — not post-launch fixes.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Built and tested against real device and network conditions, not demo conditions.
