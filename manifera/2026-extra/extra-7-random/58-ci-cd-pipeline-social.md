🚨 A CI/CD pipeline being fully automated and being genuinely fast are two different properties. A team can have the first without the second, and still be losing real productivity every day. ⚙️⏱️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Automated" Treated as Done:** A 45-minute pipeline that technically runs without manual steps.
❌ **Context-Switching, Uncounted:** The real cost is switching away and back, not the raw wait time.
❌ **Speed That Degrades Silently:** Pipeline duration creeps up as the test suite grows, unmonitored.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit Speed Targets:** Pipeline duration tracked and actively maintained, not left to degrade.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Automation treated as a starting point, not the finish line.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Parallelized, fast-feedback-ordered pipelines built for rapid, not just automated, feedback.

Our pipeline was automated, which we thought meant it was done. It just meant nobody had to click a button. 🛡️
