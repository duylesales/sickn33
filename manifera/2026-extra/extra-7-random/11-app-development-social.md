🚨 Every app development conversation gets to features, timelines, and cost fast. Almost every one skips the 3 questions that determine whether those numbers turn out realistic at all. ⚙️📱

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Falsifiable Hypothesis:** "Provide value" instead of a specific, testable claim about what version one has to prove.
❌ **Scope Fights Resolved by Committee:** Without a clear target user, every hard fork gets compromised into a worse outcome for everyone.
❌ **Built as If Success Is Guaranteed:** No instrumentation or flexibility for the real possibility the first version doesn't land.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Three Foundational Questions, Answered First:** What the app has to prove, who it's for first, and what happens if it doesn't work.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Working through the foundation before scope gets defined — a validated hypothesis, not an assumption.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Built with the flexibility to pivot cheaply, not locked into a rigid structure that assumes success.

We'd built 4 months of features for a user who barely existed. The fix wasn't more features — it was answering who we were building for. 🛡️
