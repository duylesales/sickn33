✅ Most vendor evaluation checklists cover the easy stuff — portfolio, pricing, tech stack. They skip IP assignment, real bench depth, and whether code review survives contact with a messy production system. 🔎

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Claims, Not Verification:** "We do code review" invites a yes from every vendor — it proves nothing.
❌ **Ambiguous IP Language:** A vague clause can let a vendor claim partial rights to what you paid for.
❌ **Sales-Call Staffing:** The senior team that pitched you isn't always the team that delivers.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Verifiable Practice, Not Claims:** Sample PR history and real incident walkthroughs, not just assurances.
✅ **Explicit IP Terms:** Unambiguous ownership from day one, reviewable by your own counsel.
✅ **Real Bench Depth Answers:** Specifics on seniority, utilization, and continuity — not reassurance.

The questions you skip in evaluation are the ones that cost you later. 📋
