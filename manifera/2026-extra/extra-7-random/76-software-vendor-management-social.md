Most companies manage every outsourced vendor with the exact same weekly call and ticket queue — whether that vendor owns a side tool or 40% of core engineering capacity. 🧩⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **One-Size-Fits-All Governance:** The same cadence applied to low-risk and high-criticality vendors alike.
❌ **Untested Escalation Paths:** "Talk to our account manager" isn't a plan until it's been tested.
❌ **Invisible Key-Person Risk:** No one asks what happens if the two engineers who understand the codebase leave the vendor.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Tiered Criticality Governance:** Escalation depth and oversight scaled to what's actually at stake.
✅ **Documented Knowledge Continuity:** Architecture ownership visible to the client, independent of any one person.
✅ **Independent Output Verification:** Quality checked through review and quality gates, not vendor self-reporting.

A vendor relationship shouldn't depend on any one person staying in their job. 🛠️
