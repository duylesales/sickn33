🚧 Most DevSecOps rollouts fail the same way: block the build on every finding, and within two sprints engineers have quietly figured out how to bypass the gate entirely. ⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Noise Trains Engineers to Ignore It:** Blocking on low-severity findings buries the critical ones.
❌ **Workarounds Become the Norm:** Suppression comments and gate overrides erode security culture.
❌ **Findings With No Owner:** Non-blocking issues pile into a permanent, ignored backlog.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Checks Staged by Cost:** IDE linting, PR-stage SAST/SCA, deploy-stage DAST and IaC scanning.
✅ **Calibrated Blocking:** Only high/critical severity blocks the build — trust in the gate stays intact.
✅ **Remediation SLAs:** Every finding gets an owner and a deadline based on severity.

A gate engineers trust catches more risk than a gate engineers route around. 🔐
