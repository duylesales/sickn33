ETL vs. ELT isn't a modernization question with one right answer — it's a per-source technical decision most teams answer once, dogmatically, for every source they'll ever have. 🔀📦

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **One Pattern Everywhere:** Standardizing on ELT or ETL as policy instead of deciding per source.
❌ **Compliance Gaps:** Sensitive data landing unmasked in the warehouse before a scheduled transform catches it.
❌ **Expensive Retrofits:** Fixing the wrong pattern after production is far costlier than choosing right upfront.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Per-Source Architecture:** ETL or ELT chosen against real transformation, compliance, and source constraints.
✅ **Pre-Load Masking Where Required:** Sensitive fields redacted before data ever lands in the warehouse.
✅ **Compute Matched to Cost:** Elastic warehouse compute used where it's genuinely the cheaper option.

The right pipeline pattern depends on the source, not the trend. ⚙️
