🔓 Most breaches aren't nation-state hackers exploiting zero-days. They're a hardcoded credential in a repo, an unpatched dependency, or an access check that existed on one endpoint but not another. 🛡️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Security Competes With Roadmap:** Without prioritization, security work is perpetually deprioritized until an incident forces the issue.
❌ **Enterprise Deals Stall on Questionnaires:** No SAST coverage or secrets management answer can quietly kill a sales cycle.
❌ **Retrofitting Is Expensive:** Fixing a missing authorization boundary after launch costs far more than catching it at design time.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **CI-Wired Scanning:** SAST/DAST and dependency (SCA) scanning run on every commit, not once a quarter.
✅ **Real Secrets Management:** Centralized, rotated credentials replace hardcoded keys in config files.
✅ **Least-Privilege by Default:** Access is scoped and reviewed structurally, closing the lateral-movement path that turns one bad credential into a breach.

Secure by default beats secure by audit, every time. 🔐
