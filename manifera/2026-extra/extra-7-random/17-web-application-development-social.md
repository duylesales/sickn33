🚨 The engineers who make a web application's foundational decisions are rarely the ones maintaining it 3 years later. The decisions that survive that turnover determine whether the next team inherits a system or an archaeology project. ⚙️🏗️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The "Why" Never Gets Written Down:** Code documents what a system does, rarely why a particular approach was chosen.
❌ **Inconsistent Patterns, Multiplied Onboarding:** Every section of the codebase becomes its own dialect to learn.
❌ **Tests That Verify "Doesn't Crash":** Instead of documenting what the system is actually supposed to do.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Decisions Protected From Deadline Pressure:** Documented reasoning, pattern consistency, framework separation, and behavior-documenting tests.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Longevity-determining decisions protected — not eroded by default under pressure.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Test coverage that functions as executable documentation, surviving team turnover.

The people who built it took all the "why" with them. We spent months reconstructing what an afternoon of writing would have preserved. 🛡️
