❌ **A Recurring Gap, Not a One-Time Surprise:** Underbudgeted maintenance compounds every single year.
❌ **Unaccounted Cost Drivers:** External integrations and active development that the rule of thumb never sees.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Real Factors:** Evolution rate, integration surface, domain complexity, and initial code quality.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Maintenance budgets calibrated to your actual system, not a generic average.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Genuine code quality at launch that keeps real maintenance cost close to budget.
