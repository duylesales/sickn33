🔒 A privacy policy is a promise. An architecture is a constraint. If any engineer can query any field for any reason, your privacy policy is enforced by nothing but goodwill. 🧩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Trust-Based Access:** Broad query access relies on engineers remembering not to misuse it.
❌ **Silent Logging Leaks:** Debug logs and stack traces can capture full personal data in plaintext.
❌ **Manual Retention Policies:** "Delete after 24 months" only matters if a system actually enforces it.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Purpose-Limited Access:** Scoped roles and field-level control make data misuse structurally difficult, not just against policy.
✅ **Automated Lifecycle Jobs:** Retention limits enforced on schedule, not by memory.
✅ **Framework-Level Redaction:** Sensitive fields scrubbed from logs by default, before they ever leave the application.

Privacy that lives in the architecture doesn't need anyone to remember to keep the promise. 🛡️
