🚨 Your board doesn't just want to approve "an outsourcing budget." They want to approve a specific model, a specific accountable party, and specific answers. Do you have all three ready? 📋⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **A Preferred Model Isn't a Justified One:** The board wants to know why a dedicated pod over cheaper staff augmentation.
❌ **An Unbounded Commitment Feels Risky:** Boards want to know the downside is contained before approving the upside.
❌ **A Tabled Proposal Signals Weakness:** 6-10 weeks to return to the agenda, making every future budget request harder.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Five Board Questions, Pre-Answered:** Model rationale, bounded exposure, named accountability, local-hiring comparison, and measurable six-month success criteria.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** A named EU-jurisdiction entity — not "the vendor" — the board can understand as legally accountable.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Delivery against concrete milestones that make the engagement auditable at the next board review.

Reframe from "should we outsource" to "here's the specific, bounded structure we're asking you to approve." 🛡️
