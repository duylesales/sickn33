Every offshore contract negotiation obsesses over how the relationship starts — rates, onboarding, sprint cadence — and almost none spend real time on how it ends. That's exactly backwards. 📄⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Exit Terms as Boilerplate:** Termination gets treated as a remote scenario, but a meaningful share of offshore engagements end within three years for entirely ordinary reasons.
❌ **A Vague "Reasonable Cooperation" Clause:** No minimum notice period, no named knowledge-transfer deliverables, no guaranteed codebase access during transition.
❌ **The Real Cost Shows Up at the Worst Moment:** Companies hitting termination without pre-negotiated handover terms report €60,000-€140,000 in emergency knowledge-recovery and code archaeology.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four Provisions Negotiated at Signing:** A defined transition period, documentation-as-you-go as a standing obligation, permanent client-owned infrastructure access, and a dedicated fast-track dispute path for transition disagreements.
✅ **Amsterdam Commits Contractually, Upfront:** The Dutch entity guarantees a defined transition period and infrastructure access that's never contingent on the relationship staying active.
✅ **Vietnam Documents as Standing Practice:** The Ho Chi Minh City pod maintains documentation discipline throughout the engagement, not as a termination-triggered scramble.
