🚨 Three sourcing options, three completely different cost structures, and a CTO who has to pick before the next board meeting. Why does every comparison online just say "it depends" and stop there? ⚙️💥

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Real Framework:** In-house, local outsourcing, or offshore — no clear picture of which fits a company at your stage and budget.
❌ **Two Ways to Get It Wrong:** Over-committing to local outsourcing before requirements are stable burns €30,000-€60,000 in discovery churn; jumping to offshore without governance risks an even costlier rework cycle.
❌ **A Single Company-Wide Decision, Wrongly Framed:** Treating sourcing as one choice instead of a role-by-role, project-by-project one.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Blended Model, Not a Binary:** Small in-house core for foundational roles, a governed Vietnam pod for bulk capacity, local outsourcing for stakeholder-heavy sprints.
✅ **Amsterdam Strategic Governance:** Architecture and quality accountability absorbed, so the CTO isn't personally carrying that overhead.
✅ **Vietnam Execution Velocity:** Sustained capacity at 40-60% of the fully-loaded Dutch cost, assembled in days.
