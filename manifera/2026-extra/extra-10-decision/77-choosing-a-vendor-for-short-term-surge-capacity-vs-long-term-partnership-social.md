You have a launch date in eleven weeks and a backlog that needs six more engineers than you have. Do you hire a vendor built to spin up fast, or one built to grow with you for three years? ⚡🤝

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Wrong-Horizon Mismatch:** A surge vendor's fast-onboarding model applied to an 18-month engagement plateaus at shallow product knowledge — neither the contract nor the incentives are built for depth.
❌ **The Accidental Long-Term Trap:** A "twelve-week" surge engagement quietly runs six months at surge rates, with nobody revisiting pricing or continuity terms because the extension happened incrementally.
❌ **Pricing That Doesn't Match the Model:** Partnership-style pricing on a surge need, or surge-style pricing on a partnership need, usually means the vendor is generalizing instead of tailoring the engagement.

✅ **Fast-Ramp Surge Capacity:** A pre-vetted bench matched to your spec in 1-2 weeks, with clean, defined exit points built in from day one.

✅ **Both Models, One Vendor:** Scope the right structure before you sign, instead of retrofitting a mismatched contract three months in.
