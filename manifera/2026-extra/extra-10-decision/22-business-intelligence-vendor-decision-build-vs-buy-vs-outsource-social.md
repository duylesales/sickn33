Customer success wants self-service dashboards. Product wants embedded analytics. Finance wants one source of truth. Three requests — and the real decision is build, buy, or outsource. 📊🧩

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Free" Building Isn't Free:** A useful self-service reporting layer is a multi-quarter engineering investment, and the team building it isn't shipping your core roadmap.
❌ **Buying Hides Implementation Cost:** "We'll configure it ourselves" underestimates the semantic modeling work — LookML or a Power BI data model — needed to make a platform actually adopted, not just connected.
❌ **Conflated Requirements:** Internal BI, embedded customer-facing analytics, and self-service reporting have different needs — picking one tool to do all three badly is a common trap.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Clear Framing First:** Define who the actual analytics users are and what not having it costs, before comparing any tool or vendor.
✅ **18-24 Month TCO Modeling:** Real cost comparison across build, buy, and outsource — not just year-one sticker price.
✅ **Implementation Expertise On Demand:** Specialist BI implementation that combines buy-speed with build-level customization, without the internal learning curve.

There's no universally right answer — the right mix depends on whether analytics is core to your product or a supporting capability. 🎯
