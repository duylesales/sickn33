A reseller partner wants their own branded domain, logo, and completely isolated client data live in six weeks. If your vendor's "white-label" is a CSS override and a logo upload, you'll find out the hard way. 🏷️⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Themed, Not White-Label:** Vendors without multi-tenant experience quietly substitute "a config file per client" — meaning a new server provisioned for every reseller instead of same-day onboarding.
❌ **Leaky Data Isolation:** Application-layer filtering alone has a track record of failing on bulk export tools and admin overrides — and a cross-tenant leak of regulated data is a reportable GDPR breach.
❌ **Permanent Vendor Lock-In:** Licensed white-label platforms without source code escrow mean your entire reseller revenue line depends on one vendor never disappearing.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **True Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Custom domains with automated SSL provisioning and per-reseller branding on shared infrastructure — onboard reseller forty as easily as reseller one.
✅ **Database-Enforced Isolation:** Row-level security, not just application trust, keeping every reseller's end-customer data airtight.
✅ **Update Pipeline That Scales:** One codebase, one hotfix, every tenant patched simultaneously — not twenty manual updates and twenty chances for drift.
