Standard vendor onboarding is a checklist: sign, provision, kickoff. Regulated onboarding is a risk process wearing a checklist's clothes — and most templates are missing half of it. ✅🔐

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Uniform Checklists, Uneven Risk:** Treating a dashboard vendor the same as one with direct access to customer financial records either over-burdens the low-risk vendor or under-protects the high-risk one.
❌ **Access Before the DPA:** Provisioning system access to "not lose momentum" before the Data Processing Agreement is signed creates a documented gap an audit is built to catch.
❌ **Skipped Background Screening:** Assuming a reputable vendor has screened personnel, instead of verifying a documented policy exists for the specific individuals assigned.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Risk-Tiered Onboarding by Default:** Diligence depth matched to actual data sensitivity and system criticality.
✅ **DPA-Before-Access, Every Time:** No production data access until the full contractual stack, including the DPA, is executed.
✅ **Pre-Go-Live Security Verification:** Penetration testing and remediation built into the timeline before real data flows, not after.

Every extra diligence step exists because a specific failure mode has actually happened to someone who skipped it. 🎯
