Every failed payment integration lands on a CFO's desk as one of three problems: an unexplainable reconciliation gap, a PCI scope that ballooned, or a conversion rate that tanked. All three are preventable at vendor selection. 💳📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **PCI Scope Inflation:** A vendor who lets card data transit your own backend pulls your whole environment into full Report on Compliance territory — six figures a year versus a lightweight SAQ A.
❌ **Over-Applied SCA:** Blanket two-factor authentication on every transaction, ignoring PSD2's built-in exemptions, has driven double-digit checkout abandonment for merchants who got this wrong.
❌ **Reconciliation Blind Spots:** Partial refunds spanning settlement batches and month-end boundaries are exactly where naive reconciliation logic breaks — usually discovered three months post-launch.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Tokenized Architecture, Minimal PCI Scope:** Card data never touches your servers — SAQ A scope by design.
✅ **SCA Exemption Logic Built In:** Low-value, trusted beneficiary, and transaction risk exemptions architected into checkout, not blanket friction.
✅ **Reconciliation That Handles the Messy Case:** Partial refunds and multi-batch settlements accounted for from day one.

The cheapest-looking payment vendor almost never turns out to be the cheapest once scope, conversion, and reconciliation costs are priced in. 🎯
