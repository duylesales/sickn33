60% of sellers who start onboarding never finish their first listing. Half of buyers who sign up never come back after a search that returned nothing. Same root cause, two different funnels. 🔄🛍️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Onboarding Built Like Single-Sided SaaS:** Two well-crafted flows built in isolation still produce sellers listing into an empty marketplace and buyers searching an empty catalog.
❌ **No Cold-Start Strategy:** Without deliberate sequencing — seeded supply, single-player features, narrow launch segments — liquidity never gets off the ground.
❌ **Zero Funnel Visibility:** Shipping onboarding without step-by-step instrumentation means nobody can see where sellers or buyers actually abandon, or fix it fast.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Deliberate Liquidity Sequencing:** Cold-start strategy — concierge-seeded supply, single-player value, or narrow launch segments — built into product strategy from day one.
✅ **Progressive Seller Onboarding:** Collect only what's needed to publish a first listing; defer payout and verification friction until momentum is visible.
✅ **Instrumented From Sprint One:** Seller and buyer funnels tracked separately, with drop-off moments like payout setup and zero-result search visible immediately.

Seller and buyer onboarding are two different design problems — solving them as one produces a marketplace that serves neither side well. 🎯
