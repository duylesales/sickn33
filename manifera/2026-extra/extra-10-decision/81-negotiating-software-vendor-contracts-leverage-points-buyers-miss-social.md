Most vendor negotiations spend 90% of their energy on the day rate and 10% on everything else in the MSA — which is roughly backwards. 📉📄

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Single-Finalist Trap:** Once you've collapsed to one vendor before commercial terms are discussed, you've given up your strongest leverage before the negotiation even starts.
❌ **Auto-Renewal Amnesia:** A contract that quietly renews at the same rate year after year lets pricing calcify while the operational cost of switching keeps most buyers from ever pushing back.
❌ **Chasing the Wrong Two Points:** Negotiators spend all their capital on the last 2% of rate while termination notice, IP assignment, and liability caps sail through unchallenged.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Terms That Move With Volume:** Real discount triggers tied to verified billed-hour thresholds, not vague promises about future scale.
✅ **Payment Flexibility, Not a Fixed Default:** 30 days isn't gospel — term length is a genuine lever worth negotiating in either direction.
✅ **Clauses That Actually Protect You:** Unconditional IP assignment, buyer-favorable termination notice, and realistic liability caps as standard, not fine print to fight over.

The leverage that shifts total cost over a contract's life isn't the rate — it's the five clauses everyone rushes past. 🎯
