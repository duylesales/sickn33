If your regulator asked to see your software vendor's audit clause tomorrow, would it hold up — or does it just say "reasonable cooperation"? 📋⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The "Reasonable" Loophole:** A right-to-audit clause without defined notice periods, scope, and survival terms is functionally unenforceable during an actual examination.
❌ **The Invisible Subcontractor Chain:** Without disclosure obligations, you have zero visibility into who your vendor subcontracts to — and DORA requires you to register the full chain anyway.
❌ **Vague Breach Windows:** "Prompt notification" isn't 24 hours, and your own GDPR 72-hour clock only starts once your vendor tells you something happened.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Audit Rights Built In, Not Bolted On:** Defined notice periods, scope, and 12-24 month post-termination survival as standard contract terms.
✅ **Full Subcontractor Transparency:** Written pre-approval and flow-down obligations for any subcontractor touching your engagement.
✅ **24-Hour Breach Notification:** A hard commitment, not a vague promise — because your regulatory clock doesn't wait.

A weak audit clause isn't a legal formality gap — it's a finding waiting to happen. 🎯
