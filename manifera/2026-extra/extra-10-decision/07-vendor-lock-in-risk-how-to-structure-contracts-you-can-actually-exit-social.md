If your development vendor disappeared next month, could a different team pick up your codebase and ship a release within six weeks? For a lot of founders, the honest answer is no. 🔒🚪

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Silent IP Ambiguity:** Contracts negotiated quickly under funding pressure often carry vague IP language — you don't discover it until you try to switch vendors and find they claim rights over reusable components.
❌ **Custody Without Control:** "Yours" means little if the repository, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure configs only ever lived in the vendor's own accounts.
❌ **Knowledge Concentration:** Years of undocumented decisions living in two or three heads is a real exit barrier no legal clause fixes on its own — it shows up as a six-week ramp for anyone new.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit IP Assignment:** Code, docs, and configs assigned to you on creation, with clear lines around genuinely general-purpose vendor tooling.
✅ **Continuous Repository Access:** Admin rights in your own accounts from day one, not a promised code drop at contract end.
✅ **Architecture Decision Records as Standard:** Documentation built continuously, not assembled hastily during a crisis handover.

Lock-in isn't one bad clause — it's an accumulation of things nobody checked until the exit became urgent. Check them at signature, when your leverage is highest. 🎯
