Picking the best vendor isn't enough on a public sector tender — you also have to build a process that survives a challenge from the losing bidders. 🏛️📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Retrofitted Criteria:** Evaluating proposals first and formalizing award criteria afterward is one of the most common grounds for a successful procurement challenge under EU law.
❌ **Accessibility as an Afterthought:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance is a legal requirement under the EU Web Accessibility Directive, not a nice-to-have — and remediating it post-launch is far costlier than building it in.
❌ **Incumbency Bias:** Defaulting to "has worked with government before" as a credential often means rewarding a mediocre but familiar vendor over a stronger technical fit.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Documentation-Ready Delivery:** A governance model built to support the audit trail public procurement demands.
✅ **Accessibility Built Into Process:** Automated testing and screen reader verification as standard QA steps, not post-launch remediation.
✅ **Security Baseline Fluency:** Familiar with BIO and equivalent national frameworks, not just generic ISO 27001 claims.

The procedural weight of public procurement isn't overhead — it's what protects the award from being unwound months into delivery. 🎯
