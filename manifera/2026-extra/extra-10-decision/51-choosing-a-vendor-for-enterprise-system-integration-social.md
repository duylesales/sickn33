Your ERP doesn't talk to your CRM, and every month-end your finance team rebuilds the same reconciliation spreadsheet by hand. 🔗📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Quadratic Cost Trap:** Point-to-point connections feel cheap at first, but N systems fully connected can need up to N(N-1)/2 individual connections — each with its own error handling and auth to maintain.
❌ **Shadow IT Blind Spots:** Most enterprises underestimate their real integration surface by 30-40% because a regional office's local CRM or a marketing team's own tool never made the official systems list.
❌ **Compliance Risk Hiding in the Data Flow:** A customer record synced across three systems creates three new processing locations, each needing its own GDPR legal basis — and most vendors treat this as an afterthought, not a deliverable.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Architecture Matched to Your System Count:** Hub-and-spoke pays for itself within 18-24 months once you're past four to five systems — point-to-point stays the right call below that.
✅ **Data Flow Mapping as a Core Deliverable:** GDPR data mapping and DPO involvement built into scope, not bolted on after go-live.
✅ **Documentation That Survives a Vendor Change:** Architecture diagrams and runbooks delivered as contract line items, so year-three maintenance never depends on one team's memory.

The cheapest bid on paper is frequently the most expensive one to maintain by year two. 🎯
