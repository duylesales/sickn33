If your single development vendor disappeared tomorrow, how many weeks of delivery would you lose before a replacement was even ramped? 🧩⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Unexamined Concentration Risk:** One vendor holding all your architecture knowledge and deployment credentials turns their disruption into your outage — and under DORA or NIS2, an unassessed single point of failure is an audit finding waiting to happen.
❌ **The Coordination Tax:** Splitting vendors across a tightly coupled monolith adds 10-20% more time to cross-team alignment and integration debugging — the worst of both worlds.
❌ **Splitting Along the Wrong Line:** Dividing vendors by org chart instead of architecture leaves two teams touching the same codebase, producing friction without real risk reduction.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Architecture-First Vendor Boundaries:** We scope engagements around clean API and system boundaries, not organizational convenience.
✅ **Flexible Engagement Models:** Full-stack ownership as sole delivery partner, or a domain-scoped dedicated team running cleanly alongside your existing setup.
✅ **Governance Built In:** Shared architecture review cadence and a single internal alignment owner, defined before either team writes a line of code.

The question isn't how many vendors feels safe — it's whether your system boundaries can absorb the seam a second vendor introduces. 🎯
