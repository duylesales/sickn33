Your AI vendor's sales deck says "enterprise-grade security." Their sub-processor list has fourteen entities on it — five you've never heard of. 🔒📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Vague Compliance Claim:** "We're GDPR compliant" means almost nothing without specifics — retention numbers, deletion automation, and a Records of Processing Activities entry that names your data directly.
❌ **The Shared Model Training Risk:** If your data trains or fine-tunes a model shared across other customers, deletion requests become technically near-impossible to fully honor once it has shaped model weights.
❌ **The Shortcut Debugging Habit:** Engineers querying raw production data directly for debugging — instead of masked replicas — defeats audit logging and is the most common root cause in third-party breach post-mortems.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named Sub-Processor Chains:** Full disclosure of every sub-processor and its transfer mechanism, no unlisted vendors bundled into the pipeline.
✅ **Environment Isolation by Default:** Synthetic or masked data in staging and development, raw production data touched only where it must be.
✅ **Notification Windows Under 72 Hours:** A contractual breach notification timeline shorter than what you owe your own regulator, with defined content requirements.

Certifications tell you the vendor passed someone else's bar — your own questions tell you if it's high enough for your data. 🎯
