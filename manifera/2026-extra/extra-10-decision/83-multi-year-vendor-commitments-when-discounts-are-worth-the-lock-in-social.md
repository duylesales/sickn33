A vendor offers 15% off for a three-year commitment instead of annual renewal. On a €600K/year engagement, that's €270,000. But is the discount worth what you're giving up? 🔒💶

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Invisible Cost of Lock-In:** The real price isn't in the pricing table — it's the annual walk-away point you're giving up, which is leverage even if you never use it.
❌ **Locking In Before It's Proven:** Committing multi-year before 12-18 months of track record means trading flexibility for a discount on a relationship you haven't finished evaluating.
❌ **A One-Directional Bet:** Without a market rate review clause, you benefit if rates rise past your locked number — but simply overpay with no recourse if they fall.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Discounts Backed by Real Value Exchange:** 10-20% multi-year pricing that reflects genuine vendor investment, not just a sales tactic.
✅ **Usable Exit Ramps:** Annual performance reviews with objective triggers — SLA breaches, quality metrics — that allow real early exit, not just one in name.
✅ **Built-In Market Benchmarking:** Rate review clauses tied to comparable-engagement data at each anniversary, protecting both sides from a stale number.

Price the option value you're giving up as explicitly as you price the discount you're gaining. 🎯
