Six months of monthly invoices, all paid in full, all on schedule. The product still isn't demoable. Nothing about "on schedule" ever said "on track." 💸📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Flat Invoicing Pays for Effort, Not Outcome:** Time-and-materials billing doesn't move whether a sprint produced working software or a pile of half-finished branches.
❌ **Vague Acceptance Criteria Invite Disputes:** "Feature complete" means something different to each side — and that gap becomes a payment fight.
❌ **One-Sided Holdbacks Backfire:** A 30%+ holdback strains vendor cash flow enough to actually increase your delivery risk, not reduce it.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Milestones Tied to Observable Deliverables:** Payment released on named, demoable outputs — not the passage of time.
✅ **A Reasonable 10-15% Holdback:** Real leverage without threatening the vendor's ability to retain your team.
✅ **Acceptance Criteria Defined Jointly:** Specific, testable criteria set with the vendor during negotiation — not imposed after the fact, and not disputable later.

The goal of a milestone structure isn't leverage extraction — it's incentive alignment on both sides of the table. 🎯
