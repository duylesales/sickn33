A data consultancy just quoted €180K for a 12-week strategy engagement. The deliverable is a 60-slide deck. Nobody on that team will write the pipeline it describes. 📊💸

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Shelved Roadmap:** Consultancies often hand off a target architecture and exit, leaving you to source a separate build vendor months later once budget re-approves.
❌ **The Stale Architecture:** By the time a second vendor onboards, the original roadmap can already be outdated relative to new data sources — eating into the value you paid for.
❌ **The Hybrid Bait-and-Switch:** Some "strategy + build" vendors staff the build phase with a different, junior team that has to relearn the architecture from a document instead of lived context.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Architecture Scoped Inside the Build:** Senior data engineers absorb planning in the first 2-3 weeks of the engagement, no separate consultancy fee.
✅ **One Accountable Team, Start to Finish:** No hand-off gap between strategy and execution.
✅ **Cost Scaled to Actual Capacity Needed:** €7K-€12K per engineer per month, not a flat six-figure document fee.
