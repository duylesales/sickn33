Three months in, delivery feels slower and quality feels worse — but you never defined a metric, so all you have is "I feel like things have gotten worse." 📉🤷

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Baseline, No Case:** Without a pre-agreed number, a real performance dip becomes a subjective, easily-deflected conversation instead of an objective one.
❌ **Lagging-Only Metrics:** Tracking only shipped deadlines and production defects means you find out about a problem only after it's already expensive to fix.
❌ **Unwritten Escalation Triggers:** A KPI with no pre-agreed response to a breach is just a chart nobody acts on — the real negotiation happens at the worst possible moment.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Baseline-First KPIs:** Targets set as acceptable variance from an observed baseline over the first 2-3 sprints, not a guessed number from sprint one.
✅ **Leading + Lagging Mix:** Cycle time and code review turnaround tracked alongside defect escape rate, giving 2-3 weeks of early warning.
✅ **Pre-Agreed Escalation Tiers:** Written thresholds for check-in, improvement plan, and contract remedy, agreed before work begins.

KPIs set before a problem emerges are early-warning infrastructure. KPIs set after are just a negotiating tactic. 🎯
