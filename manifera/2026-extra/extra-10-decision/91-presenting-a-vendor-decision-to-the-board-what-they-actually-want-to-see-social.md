You ran a rigorous six-week vendor evaluation, walked into the board meeting, and got sent back for "more information." The decision wasn't wrong — the presentation was. 📊🪑

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Burying the Ask:** A chronological walkthrough of the evaluation process makes the board wait ten minutes to hear the actual recommendation — long enough for them to form an uninformed opinion first.
❌ **Technical Merit Over Risk:** CEOs over-invest in explaining why the vendor is good and under-invest in the risk table boards actually use to decide.
❌ **The Unanswered "What If":** Leaving exit terms and early-warning KPIs unaddressed signals the plan wasn't fully thought through, even when it was.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Decision-First Framing:** State the vendor, the cost, and the ask in the first two minutes, before any supporting narrative.
✅ **A One-Page Risk Table:** Financial, operational, compliance, and concentration risk mapped against mitigations, not buried in prose.
✅ **Reference-Checkable Delivery History:** Concrete, named references and disclosed process detail that resolve board skepticism about offshore delivery on the spot.

Boards don't need convincing the vendor is good — they need to see you know exactly what you're getting into. 🎯
