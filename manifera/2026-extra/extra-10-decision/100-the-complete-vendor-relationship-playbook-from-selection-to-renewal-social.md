"Our third vendor in five years is the one where we finally stopped treating vendor selection like a wedding and started treating it like a lifecycle." That reframe is this entire hundred-article series in one sentence. 🔄📖

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Signing Gets Treated as the Finish Line:** The decision that gets the most attention — which vendor to sign — is genuinely the smallest piece of what determines whether the relationship succeeds.
❌ **Domain Fit Gets Assumed, Not Tested:** A vendor excellent at one kind of build can be a poor fit for another — generic credentials miss this every time.
❌ **Governance Only Shows Up After Something's Wrong:** KPIs, health checks, and exit clauses negotiated in a crisis are worth a fraction of the same terms negotiated calmly, upfront.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Lifecycle Framework, Not a Single Decision:** Selection, domain fit, delivery model, contract mechanics, and post-signing governance — each with its own recurring decision points.
✅ **Fit Tested Deliberately, By Domain:** SaaS architecture, data engineering, QA, design, enterprise integration, and regulated-industry compliance each get their own diligence, not a generic scorecard.
✅ **Governance Built In From Day One:** KPIs set before work begins, a recurring health check cadence, and an exit clause negotiated before you ever need it.

A hundred articles, one arc: the vendor decision that matters most isn't the signature — it's everything you build around it after. 🎯
