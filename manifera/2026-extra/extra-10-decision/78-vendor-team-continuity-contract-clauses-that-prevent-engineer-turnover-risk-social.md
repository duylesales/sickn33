Your lead vendor engineer gives two weeks' notice. Does your contract guarantee a like-for-like replacement with documented context — or does it just let them swap in whoever's free and keep billing full rate? 📄⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Silent MSA:** Most contracts say nothing about backfill timelines, replacement seniority, or billing during a vacancy — turnover risk defaults entirely onto you.
❌ **Tribal Knowledge Walks Out the Door:** Documentation is treated as background hygiene instead of a deliverable, so the real cost of turnover is the context that leaves with the departing engineer.
❌ **"Dedicated Team" Without Teeth:** With no key person clause, a vendor can freely rotate staff off your account based on their own utilization needs — the name on the org chart means nothing.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named Key Person Clauses:** Specific engineers named as material to the engagement, with client input into any replacement's qualifications.
✅ **2-4 Week Backfill SLA:** A bounded window for like-for-like replacement, with a ramp-discount on billing while the new engineer comes up to speed.
✅ **Documentation as a Deliverable:** Architecture decision records and runbooks tied to milestone payments, not left as an unenforced best practice.

Turnover isn't preventable through contract language alone — but its cost to you almost entirely is. 🎯
