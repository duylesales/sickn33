A buyer on your marketplace needs to see contract pricing negotiated six months ago and split one order across three sellers on different payment schedules. If your vendor's first instinct is "we'll fork a Shopify plugin," that tells you everything. 🏗️💼

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Price-as-Attribute Thinking:** Vendors without B2B experience model price as a single number per SKU, missing negotiated contracts and volume tiers — a retrofit that costs six months post-launch.
❌ **RFQ Workflows Built as Contact Forms:** Request-for-Quote flows get underbuilt into simple forms, pushing buyers to finish negotiations manually over email instead of on your platform.
❌ **Payout Complexity Ignored:** Split payments, escrow, and NET 30/60 terms require a real platform payments architecture — and under PSD2, possibly a licensing conversation nobody flagged until launch week.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Contract-Aware Data Models:** Pricing built as a function of product, buyer, contract, and quantity from day one — not retrofitted after launch.
✅ **Real RFQ and Approval Workflows:** Multi-user buyer accounts with requester/approver roles and quote-to-order conversion that doesn't require re-keying.
✅ **Split Payout Architecture:** Stripe Connect / Mangopay-based payments that handle multi-seller splits, escrow, and net terms correctly, with KYB verification built in.

The storefront is the easy 20% — the pricing, payments, and verification logic is where B2B marketplace builds actually succeed or fail. 🎯
