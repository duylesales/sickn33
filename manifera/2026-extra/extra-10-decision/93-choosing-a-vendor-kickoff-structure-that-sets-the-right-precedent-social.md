One-hour kickoff, everyone introduces themselves, engineers start coding Monday. Six weeks later you're renegotiating norms that should've been settled in that first hour. 🚀📋

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Kickoff as Ceremony:** Treating kickoff as an intro meeting instead of a structural decision means every unset norm gets set implicitly — usually badly — by whatever happens first.
❌ **Skipped Sprint Zero:** Cutting the calibration period to "save time" doesn't remove the work, it just moves it into the first real sprint at higher cost.
❌ **No Escalation Path:** Without a defined tier for blockers, teams either sit stuck for days or escalate inappropriately and create friction.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Billed Sprint Zero:** A structured 1-2 week onboarding period with real architecture review, not a rushed courtesy call.
✅ **Documented Escalation Tiers:** Same-day engineer resolution, 24-hour lead-to-lead, 48-hour named executive contact — agreed before it's needed.
✅ **Written Definition of Done:** Agreed and embedded into the workflow before the first ticket is written, not discovered awkwardly at first code review.

Whatever happens in week one becomes the default the team reverts to under pressure for the rest of the engagement. 🎯
