The contract is signed. The vendor's engineers are sitting idle for four days because nobody provisioned repo access and the VPN request is stuck in a ticket queue. 🔑⏳

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Reactive Access Provisioning:** Waiting until day one to start requesting access turns week one into an IT ticket queue instead of shipped work.
❌ **Documentation Fiction:** Teams assume internal docs are current, then discover the gaps live, mid-onboarding-call, in front of the new vendor.
❌ **Overloaded First Sprint:** Loading high-complexity, high-priority work into sprint one under roadmap pressure turns a calibration exercise into a credibility problem.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Access Checklist Before Kickoff:** Repository, VPN, tooling, and environment access requested and provisioned a week ahead of the vendor's start date.
✅ **Structured Documentation Audit:** A standard onboarding template that surfaces documentation gaps as a forcing function, not a surprise.
✅ **Deliberately Modest First Sprint:** A calibration-scoped deliverable that builds trust before complexity ramps up.

The signing is the easy decision — the first 30 days is where dozens of smaller decisions compound into a team that's genuinely productive by week three. 🎯
