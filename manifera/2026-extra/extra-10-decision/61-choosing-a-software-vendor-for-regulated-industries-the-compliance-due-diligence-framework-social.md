"SOC 2 compliant" on a sales deck and "SOC 2 compliant" on an actual audit report are two different claims — and the gap between them is where your regulatory exposure begins. 📋🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Type I Gets Passed Off as Type II:** A Type I report only proves controls were designed properly at one point in time — not that they actually worked over months of operation.
❌ **Certification Scope Doesn't Match the Engagement:** Vendors often hold certification for one product line or data center while proposing to deliver your work through an uncovered team.
❌ **Sub-Processor Risk Never Gets Mapped:** Compliance obligations extend through every fourth party a vendor uses — and most due diligence processes never ask who those are.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Four-Layer Diligence, Not a Badge Check:** Certification scope, contractual audit rights, technical control evidence, and sub-processor mapping — verified in that order, every time.
✅ **Evidence Over Policy Documents:** We ask for pen test results, access logs, and exercised incident runbooks, not just a written security policy.
✅ **Audit Rights With Real Teeth:** Defined-cadence audit access and documentation-on-request written into the contract, not buried in vague "reasonable cooperation" language.

A vendor confident in their compliance posture produces this evidence in days — one who stalls just told you something a deck never would. 🎯
