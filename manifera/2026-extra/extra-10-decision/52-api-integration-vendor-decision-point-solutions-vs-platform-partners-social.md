Three new API integrations this quarter — do you hire a specialist for each, or bring in one platform partner who builds all three on shared infrastructure? 🔌⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The 2am Dashboard Gap:** Three integrations built by three vendors means three logging formats and no single view of integration health — your on-call engineer debugs the vendor's conventions before they even reach the actual bug.
❌ **Distributed Knowledge Lock-In:** When the vendor who built an integration 18 months ago is unreachable, you're just as stuck as with a single platform vendor — the risk was just spread across smaller, less visible dependencies.
❌ **Redundant Infrastructure Tax:** Every point-solution integration re-pays for authentication, logging, and retry logic a platform partner would have built once and reused.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **25-40% Lower Cumulative Cost Past Integration Five:** Shared infrastructure means the sixth integration costs a fraction of building it from scratch.
✅ **One Observability Layer by Construction:** Every integration logs, alerts, and retries the same way — audit-ready by design, not reconstructed after an incident.
✅ **Exit Cost Priced Upfront:** A documented migration path from day one, so lock-in is a known quantity you can plan around, not a surprise during a dispute.

The right model depends on your integration count, not which one sounds cheaper in the moment. 🎯
