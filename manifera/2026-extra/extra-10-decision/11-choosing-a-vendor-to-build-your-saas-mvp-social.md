A stack of vendor proposals all claim they can build your MVP fast. The one promising the lowest price and fastest timeline is usually the wrong pick. ⚡🚀

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Aggressive-Timeline Trap:** A four-week MVP quote against a realistic 8-14 week build isn't a competence signal — it's a scope-cutting signal. Ask what gets deprioritized to hit that date.
❌ **Fixed-Price on Unstable Scope:** MVP requirements are meant to change as you learn — a fixed-price contract forces a vendor to guess against a target that's supposed to move.
❌ **Deferred IP Ambiguity:** Vague code ownership at MVP stage frequently resurfaces as a deal-threatening issue during Series A technical due diligence.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Judgment Over Compliance:** We push back on underspecified requirements and flag what to cut — an MVP's job is to test a hypothesis cheaply, not build every feature on the list.
✅ **Capped T&M With a Pilot:** A 2-3 week paid pilot on real scope before the full engagement, so you see working style and code quality before committing runway.
✅ **Clean IP From Day One:** Full, unambiguous transfer confirmed in writing before a single sprint starts.

Choose based on technical judgment and commercial structure that protects runway — not on the lowest quote or the fastest promise. 🎯
