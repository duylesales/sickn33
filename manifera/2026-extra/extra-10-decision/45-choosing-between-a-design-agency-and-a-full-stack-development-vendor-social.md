You've got a Figma file you're proud of and a launch date you're not sure you'll hit — that's the moment founders realize design and development are about to become two separate vendor problems. 🎨⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Handoff Tax:** Founders who've lived through it report 15-30% of the original design gets silently altered during implementation, as a separate dev team resolves ambiguity in a static Figma file under deadline pressure.
❌ **Two Invoices, Two Timelines:** A 6-12 week design phase followed by development starting from zero, with its own onboarding lag — versus one continuous timeline where design and build happen against real technical constraints from week one.
❌ **Nobody Owns the Failure:** When a shipped feature underperforms, two separate vendors each have a structural incentive to blame the other's layer, leaving the founder to referee instead of ship a fix.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **One Accountable Team:** Design and engineering under a single contract — no "that wasn't our layer" available as an answer.
✅ **Faster First Working Build:** Feasibility gets negotiated in real time instead of idealized on a canvas and re-litigated later.
✅ **Lower Total Cost:** No duplicated project management overhead and no premium agency day-rate stacked on top of a separate build contract.

Integration wins when you need to ship and learn fast — specialization wins when design itself is the product. Know which one you're actually buying. 🎯
