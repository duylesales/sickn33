The vendor relationship ended badly — over budget, behind schedule, a codebase nobody fully trusts. Now you have to explain it to the board and decide what happens next. 💸🩹

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Anchoring on the Invoice:** Direct wasted spend is typically only 20-35% of a failed engagement's real cost — boards who stop there badly underreact to the actual damage.
❌ **Inherited Technical Debt:** Failed engagements show defect density two to three times higher than stable code — debt that surfaces as production incidents over the next 12-18 months, not immediately.
❌ **The Denial Gap:** Weeks lost to renegotiation attempts and internal disagreement before recovery even starts often cost more than the original delay — and market windows don't wait.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Blameless Technical Audit First:** Determine honestly whether the existing codebase is a foundation or a liability before committing a new team to it.
✅ **Full-Cost Board Reporting:** A quantified account across direct spend, technical debt, schedule, and reputation — building more credibility than a minimized version of events.
✅ **Rigorous Recovery Due Diligence:** A replacement vendor selection process explicitly informed by what the original selection process missed.

Recovery starts with an honest audit, not a rushed replacement. The failure is uncomfortable, useful information about what your process missed. 🎯
