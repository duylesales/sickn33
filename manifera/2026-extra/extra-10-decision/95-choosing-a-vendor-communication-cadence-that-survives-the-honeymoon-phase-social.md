Week two: every standup attended, questions answered within the hour. Month four: standups are optional and you find out about blockers only when a deadline slips. Nothing was decided — it just eroded. 📉💬

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Honeymoon Cadence Mistaken for Steady State:** The frequent, enthusiastic communication of week two isn't the real cadence — it's a phase that predictably cools, and treating it as permanent sets you up to miss the drift.
❌ **Standups That Decay Into Theater:** Live video standups build rapport early but often become scripted status recitations by month three, burying real blockers in routine noise.
❌ **No Owner for Cadence Health:** Delivery health has an owner by default; communication health usually doesn't, which is exactly why it erodes quietly.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Overlap-Window Design:** A focused 2-hour sync window between CET and Indochina Time, async-first for everything else with clear response norms.
✅ **Separated Escalation Channel:** Routine updates and same-day blockers flow through distinct channels so urgency never gets buried.
✅ **Monthly Cadence Review:** A calendar-fixed checkpoint asking explicitly whether the communication structure — not just the delivery — is still working.

The cadence that matters isn't the one in week two — it's the one still functioning honestly in month six. 🎯
