A vendor promises 99.9% uptime, a four-hour outage costs six figures in lost revenue, and the SLA credit you're owed is a few hundred euros off next month's invoice. 📉📄

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Measurement Window Trick:** 99.9% measured annually can absorb an 8.76-hour outage with no breach ever triggered — the same headline percentage, measured monthly, catches it in under 43 minutes.
❌ **Downtime Defined Too Narrowly:** A vendor who only counts total outages, not severe degradation, can hit their target while your system is functionally unusable for hours.
❌ **Credits That Don't Change Behavior:** Service credits capped at a fraction of the invoice can make underinvestment in reliability the economically rational choice for the vendor.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Precise Measurement Methodology:** Monthly windows and performance-threshold downtime definitions negotiated upfront, not left vague until an outage happens.
✅ **Tightly Bounded Exclusions:** Capped, specific maintenance allowances and a defined force majeure list — no open-ended catch-alls a legal team can stretch later.
✅ **Mandated Root-Cause Reporting:** Written incident reports within 5-10 business days, so you're never dependent on a vendor's own self-reported dashboard.

The percentage in the contract means nothing without the methodology behind it. 🎯
