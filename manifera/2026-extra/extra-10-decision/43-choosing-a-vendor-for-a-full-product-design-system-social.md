Eighteen months after your design system "shipped," your team is still building one-off buttons because nobody adopted the component library — and three teams have quietly forked their own version of the same dropdown. 🧩📐

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Documentation That Nobody Opens:** A component library without living documentation — usage guidance, accessibility behavior, when to use this versus that — gets used incorrectly or abandoned for a one-off build.
❌ **No Owner After Handoff:** Design systems with no defined governance structure reliably decay within a year, as breaking changes ship with no review process and teams quietly fork their own variants.
❌ **Adoption Treated as Automatic:** Most vendors stop at "shipped" — leaving the hardest part, migrating 3, 5, or 15 product teams onto the new system, entirely in your hands.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Theming-Ready Token Architecture:** Primitive tokens separated from semantic ones, so dark mode or a second brand doesn't mean a rebuild.
✅ **Built-In Governance:** A lightweight working group and semver-versioned component releases set up as part of the engagement, not left to emerge on its own.
✅ **Adoption Support Past Handoff:** Migration guides and embedded support through the first sprints, tracked against a real 2-4 quarter adoption timeline.

The component showcase is the easy 20% of this work. Governance, documentation, and adoption are the other 80% — and where most systems actually die. 🎯
