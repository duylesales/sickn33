Renewal is in six weeks. If your honest answer to "why renew?" is "the team seems fine," you don't have a performance review — you have a vibe check with a signature line. 📋📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Renewal on Autopilot:** Most vendor renewals happen on inertia, not data — renewing pleasant vendors regardless of output and occasionally churning quietly strong ones over communication style.
❌ **Self-Reported Blind Spots:** Vendor-reported defect counts are prone to under-classifying what actually counts as a production bug — pull the numbers yourself.
❌ **The Silent Estimate Problem:** A vendor who underestimates timelines by 30%+ but still "delivers" is quietly wrecking your credibility with stakeholders, even when nothing looks broken sprint to sprint.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Weighted Scorecard:** Delivery, quality, governance, and commercial factors weighted to your actual risk profile — agreed before the review, not after.
✅ **Trend Over Snapshot:** Velocity, defect escape rate, and SLA adherence tracked across the full contract period, not the one good sprint that gets cited in the renewal meeting.
✅ **Governance Built for Review:** A reporting cadence structured from day one so a renewal review never starts from a blank slate.

A renewal decision built on trend data is defensible. A gut call with documentation attached is not. 🎯
