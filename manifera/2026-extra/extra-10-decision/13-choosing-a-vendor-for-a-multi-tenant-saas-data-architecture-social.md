**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Multi-Tenant" Isn't One Architecture:** Vendors default to whichever isolation model is cheapest to build — pool, bridge, or silo — without asking which one your compliance and growth trajectory actually needs.
❌ **Noisy Neighbors Aren't Designed Against:** Without per-tenant resource quotas and query governors, one heavy tenant degrades performance for everyone sharing that infrastructure.
❌ **Data Residency Gets Bolted On Too Late:** A single-region shared-database design can't satisfy an EU enterprise buyer's data residency clause without a rebuild — and that clause routinely stalls procurement.
