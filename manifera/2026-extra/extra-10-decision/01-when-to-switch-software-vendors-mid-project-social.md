**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Mistaking a Bad Sprint for a Broken Vendor:** A single missed estimate is normal project risk — switching over it burns 15-25% of a quarter's budget solving a problem a firmer conversation would have fixed for free.
❌ **Custody Gaps You Discover Too Late:** Roughly a third of mid-market transitions hit incomplete repository or infrastructure access — because it was never confirmed before the switch was announced.
❌ **The Undocumented Knowledge Cliff:** Without a negotiated overlap window, incoming teams lose 30-40% productivity in month one reverse-engineering decisions nobody wrote down.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **The Three-Number Audit:** Velocity trend, defect escape rate, and planned-vs-delivered ratio separate a structural failure from a rough patch before you act on instinct.
✅ **Custody-First Transitions:** Confirm repository, infrastructure, and DPA access before any intent is signaled — not after.
✅ **Parallel Knowledge-Transfer Sprints:** A negotiated two-week overlap window cuts the incoming team's ramp time and protects the roadmap instead of resetting it.
