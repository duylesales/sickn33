**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Standup as Status Report:** Vendor engineers recite yesterday's tasks without engaging with blockers or adjusting their plan — coordination theater, not actual coordination.
❌ **The Pre-Groomed Backlog Handoff:** A vendor that just executes a spec instead of voting in sprint planning strips out the most valuable thing a good engineer contributes: catching problems before they're built.
❌ **Retrospectives Quietly Dropped:** No visible deliverable means retros are the first ceremony to disappear — and the friction points just keep repeating sprint after sprint.

✅ **Real Ceremony Participation:** Engineers who vote on estimates, flag technical risk, and push back on scope — not just receive tickets.

✅ **Your Tools, Not a Parallel Tracker:** Engineers work directly in your existing board and Definition of Done from day one — no weekly reconciliation meeting.
