Engineering wants deep Kubernetes expertise. Finance wants the lowest rate. Product wants a two-week start. All three are looking at the same shortlist. 🤝⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Facts vs. Weighting Confusion:** Stakeholder conflict looks like disagreement about which vendor is "better" but is almost always disagreement about which criteria matter most — and that distinction rarely gets named out loud.
❌ **Scoring Before Weighting:** Show vendor scores before weights are locked, and every stakeholder unconsciously reverse-engineers weights that justify the vendor they already wanted.
❌ **Loudest Voice Wins:** Letting the most senior or vocal stakeholder override the group teaches every other function their input is theater in the next decision.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Weighted Scorecard First:** Lock evaluation weights collectively before anyone sees how vendors score.
✅ **Gates vs. Preferences:** Separate true non-negotiables (compliance, data residency) from preferences that belong in the weighted score, not a veto.
✅ **Paid Pilot for Near-Ties:** A 2-4 week pilot sprint replaces subjective debate with observed evidence when the scorecard lands close.

The best vendor decision isn't the one the loudest stakeholder wanted — it's the one the weighting process actually earned. 🎯
