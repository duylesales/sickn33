A systems integrator shows up with a methodology deck and a staffing pyramid where the senior people who sold you the deal rarely write the code. A development-first vendor shows up smaller and closer to it. 🏢⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Staffing Pyramid Bait-and-Switch:** The seniority pitched during sales often isn't the seniority actually building your system — a small senior layer sells, a much larger junior layer delivers.
❌ **Day Rate Isn't Total Cost:** A cheaper-looking blended rate erodes fast if a lean vendor has to informally build program-management capability mid-project because the project actually needed it.
❌ **Formal Change Control as a Mismatch:** The discipline that prevents scope creep on a 15-workstream program is the same discipline that slows down a project that actually needed fast technical iteration.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named Staff Before Signature:** Specific individuals, seniority, and billing-rate breakdown committed to upfront — not a staffing plan you have to trust blind.
✅ **Senior-Weighted Teams Throughout:** No pyramid diluting the technical depth you were sold once delivery starts.
✅ **Direct Access, Faster Iteration:** Decisions sit close to the engineers doing the work, built for projects where the right approach gets discovered through iteration.

The right model depends on where your project's real risk sits — organizational coordination, or technical depth. 🎯
