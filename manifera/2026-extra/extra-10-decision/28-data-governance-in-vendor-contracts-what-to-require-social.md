The vendor's DPA is nine pages of standard language legal has seen a hundred times. It's also the document that decides your exposure the day they have a breach. 📄⚖️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Rubber-Stamped DPA:** Pulled in at the eleventh hour to bless a contract that's already emotionally closed, so real negotiating leverage never gets used.
❌ **"General Authorization" Sub-Processors:** Most vendor DPAs let them add new sub-processors with only 30 days' notice — silence becomes approval unless you push for specific consent.
❌ **The Placeholder Breach Clause:** "Vendor shall notify customer promptly" with no number attached isn't a commitment, it's a placeholder that fails you exactly when you need it most.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Article 28-Compliant DPAs as Standard:** Naming the specific processing activity, not a generic trust-page reference.
✅ **24-48 Hour Notification Windows:** A real number, leaving room to meet your own 72-hour regulatory clock.
✅ **Liability Carve-Outs for Data Breaches:** Data protection breaches sit outside the standard 1x-2x contract value cap, not buried inside it.
