Try exporting the actual business logic behind "active customer" out of your BI tool today. In most platforms, there's no clean export path — you'd be reverse-engineering your own rules from a UI. 🔓📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Invisible Lock-In:** It doesn't look like lock-in at signing — it looks like a fast, well-designed platform. The cost shows up at renewal or replatforming time.
❌ **Vendor-Silo Storage:** If your data lives inside the vendor's proprietary storage instead of your own warehouse, leaving means a full extraction and re-import, not pointing a new tool at the same source.
❌ **The Ransom Migration Fee:** Some vendors charge a "migration assistance" professional services fee to export your own data — a cost that should be priced in at selection, not discovered at exit.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Warehouse-Native by Default:** Your data stays in infrastructure you own and control, regardless of which BI tool sits on top.
✅ **Open Metrics Layer Architecture:** Business logic defined in a portable format, not trapped inside one vendor's proprietary UI.
✅ **Portability Tested Before Signing:** A real export attempt during the pilot, not a promise you find out is empty three years in.

The goal isn't zero lock-in everywhere — it's knowing which parts of your stack are portable by design. 🎯
