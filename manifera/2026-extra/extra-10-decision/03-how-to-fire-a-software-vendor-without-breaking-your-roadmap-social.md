You've made the decision — the vendor relationship is ending. The question that decides your next quarter is how you sequence the exit, not whether to do it. 🚪⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Cooperation Cliff:** Once a vendor knows termination is coming, cooperation on access and documentation requests reliably drops — so custody has to be secured before the notice goes out, not after.
❌ **Silent Roadmap Absorption:** A vendor exit with a real knowledge-transfer period costs four to six weeks of reduced delivery — CTOs who don't name that cost end up quietly absorbing it through unsustainable pace.
❌ **Access Revoked Too Early:** Cutting a departing vendor's production credentials before the new team confirms independent deploy-and-rollback capability has stranded more than one CTO mid-incident.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Custody-First Sequencing:** Repository history, infrastructure-as-code, and documentation confirmed before intent is ever signaled.
✅ **The Three-Phase Notice Period:** Knowledge transfer, parallel access, and an explicit cutover date — treated as a scoped project, not a passive wait-out.
✅ **Handover-Ready Onboarding:** Structured technical due diligence run in parallel with an outgoing vendor's notice period, so the new team isn't reverse-engineering decisions from scratch.

Firing a vendor well isn't a courage problem — it's a sequencing problem. 🎯
