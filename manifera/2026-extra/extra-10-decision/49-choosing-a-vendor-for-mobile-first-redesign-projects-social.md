Two vendors quoted your mobile redesign: €80K/14 weeks and €280K/8 months — for what looks like the same Figma deck. Neither number tells you if the app will actually be fast. 📱⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **A Desktop Layout Squeezed Onto a Phone:** "Responsive" without adaptive breakpoints just compresses the same layout — exactly the failure mode driving the redesign in the first place.
❌ **No Performance Budget, No Accountability:** Without a contracted target for bundle size and time-to-interactive, a redesign can look better and load slower, tested only on the vendor's own iPhone on office wifi.
❌ **Edge Cases Discovered Mid-Build:** Empty states, error states, offline behavior, and legacy API mismatches are the single most common source of late-stage schedule slip when they're not scoped upfront.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Real Performance Budget, Contracted:** Bundle size under 4-6MB, time-to-interactive under 2-3 seconds on a mid-tier device over throttled 4G, 60fps animation — measured, not assumed.
✅ **Stack Choice Matched to Your Team:** React Native, Flutter, or native, decided by your existing engineering skills and interaction complexity — not the vendor's default.
✅ **Feature-Flagged Rollout, Staged by Segment:** New users first, power users last, with A/B testing on real conversion metrics before the old experience is retired.

The vendor question that matters isn't "how good does the mockup look" — it's "what's the time-to-interactive on a mid-tier Android phone." 🎯
