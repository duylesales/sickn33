Nobody wakes up one morning to a failed vendor relationship. It's a demo that slips from Thursday to "early next week," a response time that creeps from same-day to next-day, repeated until two quarters later it's a formal escalation. 🩹📉

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Drift Is Individually Excusable, Cumulatively Fatal:** Every single small slip is easy to wave off — until three quarters of them compound into a crisis nobody structurally saw coming.
❌ **Only Lagging Metrics Get Watched:** By the time delivery output visibly degrades, the underlying relationship issue has usually existed for months already.
❌ **Health Checks That Arrive Late Read as Suspicion:** Introducing structured review only after trust is shaken puts a vendor on the defensive instead of inviting honest disclosure.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Built-In Two-Tier Cadence:** A short monthly pulse plus a deeper quarterly review, baked into the engagement from kickoff, not bolted on after a concern.
✅ **Leading Indicators, Not Just Output:** Response time drift, commitment reliability, recurring escalation patterns, and proactive risk disclosure — tracked before they become delivery problems.
✅ **A Genuinely Two-Directional Conversation:** We ask what would help the engagement run better from the vendor's side too — collaborative, not a grading exercise.

Most drift caught early is a fifteen-minute conversation. Caught late, it's a formal escalation with a paper trail. 🎯
