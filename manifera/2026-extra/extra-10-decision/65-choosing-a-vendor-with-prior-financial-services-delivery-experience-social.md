**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Mutable Balance Trap:** A vendor without real domain depth models account balance as an editable field instead of a derived, append-only ledger — fine in a demo, wrong the moment an audit needs to reconstruct history.
❌ **Missing Idempotency:** Without idempotency keys, a client retry after a network timeout executes the same payment twice — one of the most common real fintech incidents.
❌ **Bolted-On Compliance:** PSD2 SCA and regulator reporting requirements shape architecture from day one — retrofitting them after launch often means an incomplete data backfill.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Ledger-First Architecture:** Append-only, reversal-not-edit design that survives an audit, not just a demo.
✅ **Exactly-Once Processing Built In:** Idempotency and explicit transaction state machines architected from the start, not patched after an incident.
✅ **Regulatory Fluency From Day One:** SCA exemption logic and DNB-aligned reporting requirements shape the build from the first sprint, not a retrofit.
