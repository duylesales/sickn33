**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The One-Size Framework Pitch:** A vendor proposing the same framework regardless of your architecture is optimizing for their team's familiarity, not your outcome.
❌ **Low-Code Lock-In:** No-code test platforms lower the authoring bar but often trap test assets in a proprietary format that resists version control and code review.
❌ **The Undefined Maintenance Handoff:** A vendor who builds 1,000 tests and exits without a maintenance plan is setting you up for silent, compounding test debt.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Framework Matched to Your Architecture:** Playwright, Selenium, or Cypress chosen on merit, not vendor default.
✅ **Sub-2% Flaky Test Rate Target:** Root-cause tagging on retries, not blind re-runs that mask instability.
✅ **Test Code in Your Own Repository:** Version-controlled from the first commit, reviewed like any other code.

Test automation rarely fails loudly — it fails quietly, as debt that erodes trust until engineers start ignoring red results entirely. 🎯
