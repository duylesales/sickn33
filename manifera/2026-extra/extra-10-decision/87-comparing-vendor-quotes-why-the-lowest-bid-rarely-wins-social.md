€180K, €240K, €310K — same RFP. The €180K bid looked like the obvious winner, until due diligence found it excluded QA entirely. Normalized to real scope, it landed at €260K — the highest of the three. 📉🧾

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Vendors Scope the Same RFP Differently:** One includes QA by default; another prices it as an optional add-on buried in the fine print.
❌ **Change Orders Are the Real Margin:** A narrowly scoped base price with an implicit expectation of paid change orders looks cheap on paper and expensive by month three.
❌ **Post-Launch Support Gets Negotiated After You've Already Signed:** Excluding it from the base quote shifts leverage away from you at exactly the point you have the least of it.

✅ **Itemized Scope From the Start:** Development, QA, PM, infrastructure, and post-launch support broken out line by line — nothing hidden inside an attractive total.

✅ **Direct Answers on Exclusions:** We tell you upfront what's out of scope and how it's priced if it comes up — not after the contract's signed.
