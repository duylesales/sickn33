Three vendor bids land on your desk: €340K, €410K, and €480K for the same build. Do you actually know which one is cheapest? 🧮💰

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Headline Rate Trap:** A day rate is the easiest number for a vendor to make look attractive — but blended team cost, not the printed rate, is what actually determines spend, and vendors know most buyers never recalculate it.
❌ **The Invisible Ramp-Up Tax:** Onboarding and codebase familiarization can run 4-8 weeks at reduced output, quietly costing €15,000-20,000 that never appears as its own line item.
❌ **Vague Change-Order Pricing:** Some vendors price change orders 20-40% above baseline rates, betting you won't push back mid-project once you're already committed.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Transparent Blended Rates:** All-inclusive pricing with role and seniority disclosed upfront — no hidden headcount padding.
✅ **Disclosed Attrition Data:** Trailing 12-month client-facing engineer churn shared openly, not buried in company-wide averages.
✅ **Pre-Agreed Change-Order Terms:** Defined rates and SLA turnaround set before you sign, not renegotiated under pressure mid-project.

The cheapest headline rate and the cheapest actual project are frequently two different bids. 🎯
