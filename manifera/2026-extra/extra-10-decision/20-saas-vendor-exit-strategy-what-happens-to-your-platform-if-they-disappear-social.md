If your development vendor closed its doors tomorrow, could you deploy a fix to your own platform by Friday? Most CEOs pause a long time before answering. 🔑⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Licensing Mistaken for Ownership:** "We built it for you" doesn't always mean you own it outright — licensing arrangements can leave you in a legal gray area the moment the vendor disappears.
❌ **Unverified Escrow Deposits:** A source code escrow nobody has checked for completeness or buildability is close to worthless the one time you actually need it.
❌ **Vendor-Held Infrastructure:** If the cloud account, domain, and DNS live inside the vendor's own accounts, your platform can go dark even with a perfect copy of the code.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Clear Code Ownership From Day One:** Work-for-hire clarity in writing, not assumed based on how the relationship feels.
✅ **Company-Owned Infrastructure:** Production accounts owned and administered by you, with the vendor as a collaborator — never the reverse.
✅ **Real Documentation, Not a Crisis Deliverable:** Architectural decision records and runbooks built into the engagement from the start, so continuity doesn't depend on any one person staying.

Vendor exit planning isn't distrust — it's the same category of thinking as a backup policy. 🎯
