Every UX agency deck opens the same way: gorgeous screens, a case study titled "Reimagining Fintech Onboarding," and a client logo you're supposed to recognize. None of it tells you if they can solve your product's problem. 🎨🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Same Three Flows, Re-Skinned:** Six "different" case studies across six industries that all resolve to the same onboarding, dashboard, and checkout template — one flow re-skinned six times with a new logo pasted on top.
❌ **No Process Trail:** A polished final screen is the easiest artifact for any agency to fake — the wireframes, iterations, and messy in-between work are what separate real UX practice from a visual design service.
❌ **No Before/After Numbers:** A case study that ends at "here's the final design" with no conversion lift, task-completion improvement, or drop-off reduction is a portfolio piece, not evidence of impact.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named-Team Staffing:** The individuals who worked on a case study are confirmed and available to be staffed on your engagement — not a bait-and-switch to two juniors.
✅ **Research-Backed Decisions:** Real usability testing stories where a specific wrong assumption got overturned, not "we always validate with users" in the abstract.
✅ **Measured Outcomes:** Credible, undramatic numbers — an honest 8% activation lift beats an unsupported "300% increase" every time.

The best portfolio review isn't about admiring pixels — it's about interrogating process. 🎯
