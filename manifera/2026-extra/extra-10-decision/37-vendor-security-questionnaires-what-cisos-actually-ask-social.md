Your vendor filled out a 300-question security questionnaire in under a day. Every answer was "Yes." That's not proof of maturity — it's proof nobody checked the answers against reality. 🚩🔒

Experienced CISOs know the real evaluation starts after the clean checkbox questionnaire comes back: 🧠

❌ **Self-Attested ≠ Verified:** "Yes, we encrypt data at rest" without specifying the standard, scope, or evidence is a statement of intent, not proof.
❌ **All-"Yes" Answers Are a Red Flag:** Real security programs have gaps. A vendor unwilling to disclose any hasn't assessed their own posture rigorously enough.
❌ **Fourth-Party Risk Goes Unasked:** Standard forms probe the vendor's own controls but rarely their sub-processors — where invisible risk concentrates.

✅ **Evidence-Backed From Day One:** Documented security practices ready as standard onboarding, not a scramble triggered by the first request.

✅ **Built to Survive Real Scrutiny:** Governance embedded into daily delivery practice, not bolted on for a questionnaire.
