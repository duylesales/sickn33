Your engineering team gets a Figma link two weeks before the sprint starts — forty polished screens, no spacing scale, no error states, just a Slack message that says "let us know if you have questions." Six weeks later, three sprints have gone to rework. 🎨⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Snapshots, Not Systems:** A vendor working in hardcoded hex values and pixel measurements hands off a snapshot — every future design change means manually re-measuring and re-implementing, instead of updating a token value that propagates.
❌ **The Missing 30-40%:** Empty, error, and loading states are typically a third or more of the screens engineering actually needs to build — and the first thing cut when a design vendor is running behind schedule.
❌ **"We'll Figure It Out Together":** A vendor who answers handoff questions with reassurance instead of naming a specific tool, format, or documented artifact is where rework budgets go to die.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Design Tokens, Not Guesswork:** Named, versioned values for color, spacing, and typography that map directly onto your codebase variables, in Figma Dev Mode or Style Dictionary format.
✅ **Full Component + Edge-Case Specs:** Every button variant, form state, and breakpoint reflow documented — plus empty, error, and loading states scoped into the contract explicitly.
✅ **One Accountable Team:** Design and engineering under one roof, so the team handing off specs is on the same delivery timeline as the team building against them.
