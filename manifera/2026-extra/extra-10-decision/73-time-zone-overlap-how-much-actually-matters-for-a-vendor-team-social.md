"We need at least six hours of overlap." Most RFPs write this in without ever asking what those six hours would actually be used for. 🕐🌍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Overlap Treated as One Blanket Number:** Spreading four hours evenly across a whole team wastes the scarce hours that actually matter to the roles resolving real ambiguity.
❌ **More Hours, More Meetings — Not More Speed:** Past a fairly small threshold, extra overlap mostly expands meeting time instead of improving delivery.
❌ **No Async Discipline Behind the Window:** Teams with wide overlap but no written spec culture or decision log still stall the moment someone's offline.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Overlap Allocated by Role, Not Blanket Policy:** Product owners and tech leads get protected real-time hours; engineers executing well-scoped tickets run mostly async.
✅ **A Reliable Two-to-Three-Hour Window:** Our Ho Chi Minh City mornings land in the Central European afternoon — structured, not accidental.
✅ **Async Mechanics That Actually Work:** Written spec discipline, short video walkthroughs, a running decision log, and end-of-shift async updates.

The right question isn't how much overlap you can get — it's which specific moments in the work genuinely need it. 🎯
