Last year's pentest report had 12 findings, all "low" or "informational," and cost €8,000. Six months later a real attacker found a critical auth bypass that report never mentioned. 🔓🕵️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The 48-Hour "Manual" Test:** A genuinely manual assessment takes a team of two testers 5-10 working days minimum — a report that fast is an automated scan wrapped in a template.
❌ **The Aggregate Certification Trick:** Firms list OSCP-certified staff on their website, but the tester actually assigned to your engagement may hold none of them.
❌ **Retesting as an Upsell:** Without retesting scoped into the original engagement, you have no independent confirmation your fixes actually closed the vulnerabilities.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named Testers, Named Certifications:** Know exactly who's testing your application and what they're qualified to find.
✅ **Methodology Against Named Standards:** OWASP, PTES, or NIST 800-115 — with manual business-logic testing no scanner catches.
✅ **Retesting Built Into Scope:** Verification within 30-60 days of remediation, not a separately priced add-on.
