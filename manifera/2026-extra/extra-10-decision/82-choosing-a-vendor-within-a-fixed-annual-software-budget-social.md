The board approved €400,000 for engineering this year. Not "roughly 400" — a fixed number that has to survive vendor proposals and at least one unplanned priority shift. 💰📐

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Rate Comparison Trap:** A vendor at €55/hour needing 1,800 hours costs more than one at €65/hour needing 1,400 — rate alone tells you almost nothing about total cost.
❌ **The Underbid-Then-Amend Play:** A vendor who wins on the lowest number recovers margin through change requests once the engagement is underway, and switching mid-project costs more than absorbing the overage.
❌ **No Contingency Line:** Budgets built without a reserve assume zero scope discovery risk — an assumption that almost never survives month six.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Total Cost to a Specified Scope:** Proposals normalized against actual delivery cost, not a headline rate card.
✅ **Change-Request Transparency:** Historical amendment rates shared up front, so the number you sign is the number you can trust.
✅ **Phased Roadmap Structuring:** Highest-value work sequenced into this fiscal year's number, with future phases scoped as clean, separate decisions.

A fixed budget doesn't eliminate vendor choice — it changes which criteria actually matter. 🎯
