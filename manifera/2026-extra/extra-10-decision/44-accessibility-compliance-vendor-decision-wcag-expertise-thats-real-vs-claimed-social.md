Ask a vendor how they test for accessibility. "We run it through our scanner and fix what comes up red" means they've never watched a screen reader user fail a checkout flow. A named WCAG success criterion they struggled to meet last quarter means something closer to the real thing. ⚖️🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Empty Bullet Point:** "Accessibility-friendly" has sat between "SEO-optimized" and "mobile-responsive" in every pitch deck since 2023 — a claim, not evidence, and the cost of it being hollow arrives 18 months later as a regulatory complaint.
❌ **Scanners Catch 30-40%, Not 100%:** Automated tools like axe and WAVE catch mechanical checks — missing alt text, low contrast — but miss anything requiring judgment: logical focus order, correct custom widget behavior, understandable error messages.
❌ **The EAA Changed the Stakes:** Since 28 June 2025, the European Accessibility Act makes compliance a binding legal requirement for e-commerce, banking, and more — with national-level fines and, in several jurisdictions, private rights of action.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Verifiable Evidence, Not Assurances:** A completed VPAT or Accessibility Conformance Report from a real shipped engagement, client-verifiable on request.
✅ **Manual + Real-User Testing:** Trained specialists running keyboard and screen reader passes, supplemented by actual assistive-technology users — not a simulation dressed up as coverage.
✅ **Built In From Requirements, Not Bolted On:** Accessibility structured into the build process from day one, not audited in as an afterthought before launch.

The right signal isn't a zero-defect claim — it's a credible, documented process for finding and fixing defects. 🎯
