Your board's risk committee wants to know what happens to delivery if your primary vendor disappears tomorrow. "We'd figure it out" doesn't cut it anymore. 🛟💼

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **The Overbuilt Backup:** Hiring a second vendor at full capacity as insurance solves resilience and creates a budget problem large enough to get killed before implementation.
❌ **The Underbuilt Backup:** A "backup vendor" that's just a name in a spreadsheet, with no codebase familiarity, means a 6-8 week cold-start ramp if you ever actually need it.
❌ **Ambiguous Triggers:** Without specific activation conditions written down in advance, activation becomes a judgment call made under pressure — exactly when clear judgment is hardest.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Standby, Not Shadow:** A retained 20-40 hours/month relationship keeps architectural familiarity current at 10-15% of a full parallel team's cost.
✅ **Pre-Negotiated Activation:** A committed scale-up timeframe and locked-in rates agreed before any crisis, so activation isn't a renegotiation.
✅ **Verified Readiness:** Independent security and compliance verification, tested staging access, and periodic joint sessions with the primary team to preserve real institutional knowledge.

The activation clause, priced and agreed in advance, is the actual insurance product — everything else just keeps it credible. 🎯
