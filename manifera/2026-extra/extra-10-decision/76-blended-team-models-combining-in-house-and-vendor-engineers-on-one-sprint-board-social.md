Two engineers pick up tickets from the same sprint board. One sits three desks away, one you've never met in person. Six months from now, could anyone tell which is which from the commit history? 🧩💻

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Standup Theater:** Standups quietly split into two parallel meetings in the same room — vendor engineers report status while in-house engineers absorb all the ambiguous, context-heavy work by default.
❌ **The Vendor Swimlane Trap:** A separate column or label for "vendor work" on a shared board creates a visible two-tier team, even when nobody intended a hierarchy.
❌ **The Career Ceiling Nobody Decided On:** Vendor engineers get quietly excluded from design reviews and technical decisions, capping the engagement at ticket execution instead of real technical contribution.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **One Backlog, One Definition of Done:** No swimlanes by employer — vendor engineers work the same board, same standards, same visibility.
✅ **Role-Based Access, Not Company-Based:** Named accounts and scoped IAM roles matched to actual work, with offboarding on the same cadence as employee departures.
✅ **A Real Seat in Design Review:** Engineers who join your existing ceremonies and get pulled into architecture conversations, not just handed specs to implement.

The blended model doesn't fail on the org chart — it fails on the small mechanics of the sprint board. 🎯
