❌ **The Impossible Triangle:** Specifying a fixed scope, fixed budget, AND fixed timeline simultaneously is a request no vendor can honestly satisfy — someone's proposal is papering over the conflict.

❌ **Vendors Who Promise Both Are the Warning Sign:** A vendor confidently hitting both numbers without naming what's being compressed is either inexperienced or telling you what you want to hear.
