You paid €40K for your design system. Eighteen months later you find out you own the exported PNGs — not the Figma source files, not the design tokens, not the component library. 📄⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **"Deliverables" Left Undefined:** A contract that grants ownership of "the deliverables" without naming source files, design tokens, and component code often means you're left with static exports, not a usable design system.
❌ **"Work for Hire" That Doesn't Apply:** The US work-for-hire doctrine has no direct equivalent under Dutch or EU copyright law — IP only transfers through an explicit written assignment clause, never by default.
❌ **Vague Iteration Rounds:** Without a defined "round," disputes erupt over whether ten scattered comments on one screen count as one round of feedback or ten billable ones.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Explicit IP Assignment, By Name:** Source Figma files, design tokens, and component library code assigned to you on final payment — not licensed, not left ambiguous.
✅ **Defined Iteration Rounds in Writing:** 2-3 consolidated feedback rounds per phase, scoped before work begins, so extra billing is never a surprise.
✅ **A Real Exit Path:** 30-day termination for convenience, IP assignment that survives regardless of cause, and a documented handoff — not "as agreed."

Read the contract for one question before you sign: what happens if I need to leave this vendor in a year? ⚖️
