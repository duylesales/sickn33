Headcount approval just came through for a bigger build. Your current vendor has done solid work — so handing them more scope feels obvious. Obvious isn't always correct. 📈🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Unverified Capability Transfer:** A vendor great at a CRUD-heavy web app doesn't automatically have the bench depth for a real-time pipeline or a payments integration — scaling on reputation skips the actual check.
❌ **Comfortable, Not Sharp:** When governance conversations soften and performance reviews become a formality, "we trust them" quietly substitutes for "we've verified they can do this."
❌ **The Fresh-Start Tax:** A brand-new vendor typically adds 15-25% to first-quarter budget in ramp-up and due diligence overhead that's easy to underprice against their headline rate.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Verification-Gated Scaling:** A scoped 4-6 week pilot on the riskiest part of new scope, evaluated with the same rigor as a new-vendor technical due diligence.
✅ **Institutional Knowledge, Proven:** Existing relationships that pass the gate ramp to full velocity in 2-3 weeks instead of 6-10.
✅ **Governance Matched to Risk:** SLAs and escalation paths rebuilt for the new scope's actual risk level, not inherited from a smaller engagement.

The verification gate — proving the new capability before committing full scope — protects the decision more than which path you pick. 🎯
