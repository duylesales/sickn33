Fixed price feels safer. Time and materials feels riskier. Neither instinct is wrong — they're just answering "who absorbs the cost when requirements change?" differently. ⚖️

Fixed price without real discovery just relocates uncertainty into change-order fights later.

The fix: discovery as T&M, then milestone-based fixed pricing once scope is actually concrete.
