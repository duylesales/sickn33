An Uppsala founder handed Manifera a brief: full feature list, 8 weeks, fixed budget. All three "non-negotiable." Manifera's team asked one question instead of accepting it: which one is actually flexible? 📐

Martin Barnes formalized this in 1969 — the "iron triangle." Scope, time, and budget are mechanically linked. Fix any two and the third is determined. Fix all three and something still gives — usually quality, silently, without anyone telling you.

Turned out the investor deadline was truly fixed. The budget had room nobody had mentioned yet. Scoped down, deferred the rest to phase two — demo shipped, and it actually worked.
