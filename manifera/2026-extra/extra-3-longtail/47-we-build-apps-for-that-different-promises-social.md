🚨 Scaling custom software without rigorous engineering standards creates technical debt that permanently paralyzes business agility. ⚙️📊

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Lack of Clear Architectural Roadmaps:** Building features ad-hoc without formal architecture leads to spaghetti code and brittle integrations.
❌ **Skipping Automated Testing:** Relying on manual QA allows critical edge-case bugs to leak directly into production environments.
❌ **Communication & Governance Gaps:** Disjointed engineering handoffs lead to missed deadlines and misaligned business expectations.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **"How many projects like this have you actually built?":** — a specific number, not "several" or "quite a few," reveals genuine repeated experience versus a first-time attempt framed confidently.
✅ **"What's the hardest technical problem you hit on a similar past project, and how did you solve it?":** — a team with real experience has a specific, textured answer.
✅ **"Which of your current team members have actually worked on something like this?":** — distinguishing whether the relevant experience sits with people who would work on your project, versus experience the company has in the abstract, possibly from people no longer there.
