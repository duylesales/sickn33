**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Lack of Clear Architectural Roadmaps:** Building features ad-hoc without formal architecture leads to spaghetti code and brittle integrations.
❌ **Skipping Automated Testing:** Relying on manual QA allows critical edge-case bugs to leak directly into production environments.
❌ **Communication & Governance Gaps:** Disjointed engineering handoffs lead to missed deadlines and misaligned business expectations.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Specificity.:** A review mentioning a specific project type, timeline, and concrete outcome is more informative than "great to work with, highly recommend" — vague praise is easy to write about a project you barely remember the details of.
✅ **Reviews that mention problems and how they were handled.:** A review acknowledging a mid-project challenge and how the company responded to it is more credible and more useful than a uniformly glowing account, since real projects rarely go perfectly.
✅ **Recency and consistency over time.:** A cluster of five-star reviews from three years ago followed by silence, or a recent shift toward more mixed reviews, can indicate the company's quality or team has changed since the reviews that built its reputation.
