A Coimbra insurer's previous vendor proposed a 14-month full rewrite of their claims system. Leadership rejected it — too much risk, zero value delivered until the very end. 🌳

Martin Fowler named this pattern in 2004 after a fig tree that grows around a host tree, gradually replacing it. Applied to legacy systems: build new modules around the edges of the old one, route traffic incrementally, retire legacy pieces one at a time. Never bet everything on one distant finish line.

Manifera started with the highest-friction module — claims intake. Shipped in 10 weeks. Real, banked value, with actual evidence to decide the next step instead of a plan made blind.
