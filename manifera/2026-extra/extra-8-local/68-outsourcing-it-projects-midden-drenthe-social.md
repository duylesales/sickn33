Most outsourcing advice starts with "how to pick a vendor." Wrong. Most failed projects failed at the scoping table, months earlier. 📋❌

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Feature Lists Instead of Outcomes:** Brief a vendor on "build an inventory module" and that's exactly what you get — whether or not it solves the real problem.
❌ **Integration Risk Discovered Too Late:** Monolithic delivery hides legacy ERP integration problems until UAT, after most of the budget is spent.
❌ **No Measurable Acceptance Criteria:** "Does it work in the demo" isn't a business outcome — and disputes over "done" happen after delivery, not before.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Outcome-First Scoping:** We define the measurable business result before writing a single technical requirement.
✅ **Highest-Risk Integration Tested First:** Within the first 2-4 week increment, not month eight.
✅ **Re-Scope Every Increment:** Acceptance criteria agreed upfront, adjusted with real data as you go.

Monolithic projects run 35-50% over budget on average when a major integration assumption is wrong. Our incremental Pod model runs ~€29,000/month, with the first acceptance test done in ~3 weeks — before more than ~15% of budget is committed. 📊
