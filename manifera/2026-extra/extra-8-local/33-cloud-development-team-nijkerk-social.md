A Nijkerk CIO described her cloud project in one sentence: three vendors, three opinions on how services should talk, and a production API that broke every time one team shipped without telling the others. ☁️🔗

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Conway's Law, Working Against You:** Three vendors with three sprint cadences and three definitions of "done" inevitably produce three loosely-integrated subsystems, no matter how good each vendor's engineers are.
❌ **The Three-Way Blame Cycle:** A production incident becomes a finger-pointing exercise between infra, app, and QA vendors before anyone actually starts debugging.
❌ **Physical Consequences in Food and Manufacturing:** A dropped integration between an inventory service and an ordering system means a truck dispatched with the wrong load, not just a support ticket.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **One Cross-Functional Pod, One Accountable Team:** Infrastructure, backend, frontend, and QA report through a single technical lead, enforcing one API contract standard and one CI/CD pipeline.

✅ **53% Lower Than Three Fragmented Vendors:** A unified pod covering five roles runs €45,000/month versus roughly €96,000/month for equivalent fragmented local hiring.
