Roughly 2 out of 3 outsourcing engagements fail not because of bad code — because "partner" was never actually defined. 🤝⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Staffing Dressed Up as Partnership:** Freelancers fill seats, but nobody owns the architecture — six months in, there's no shared standard and no accountability chain.
❌ **Conway's Law in Action:** No governance structure means the software mirrors that chaos: duplicated logic, unclear service boundaries, six different auth patterns.
❌ **The 2 A.M. Incident Nobody Owns:** When production breaks across services built by different contributors, there's no named escalation path — just finger-pointing.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Named Architecture Ownership:** A documented decision log and bi-weekly architecture review cadence, not ad hoc Slack threads.
✅ **Amsterdam-Governed Escalation Path:** One accountable chain for incidents, not "whoever's online."
✅ **Committed Pod Continuity:** No rotating freelancers optimizing for their next billable hour.
