**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Headcount ≠ Velocity:** Adding contractors without module ownership just multiplies coordination overhead ("Adding manpower to a late project makes it later" — Fred Brooks).
❌ **Informal API Contracts:** Undocumented interfaces between modules become integration incidents waiting to happen.
❌ **No Onboarding Ramp:** New engineers get cross-cutting tickets on day one and never build real depth anywhere.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Bounded-Context Ownership:** Each sub-team owns a defined module, mapped before the pod scales.
✅ **Versioned API Contracts:** Documented interfaces, not tribal knowledge.
✅ **12-Week Ramp to Full Velocity:** vs. 20+ weeks for an ad hoc contractor team — at ~€52K/month, roughly 38% lower than a comparable Dublin-rate team.
