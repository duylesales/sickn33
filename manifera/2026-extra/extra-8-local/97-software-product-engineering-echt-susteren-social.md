"The feature is done, technically" — three words that close a ticket and say nothing about whether the product still holds together underneath it. 🧱⚙️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **A Feature Factory Wearing Product-Engineering Language:** A backlog of independently specified tickets, implemented by whoever picks one up next, judged done when its own isolated tests pass and nothing more.
❌ **Three Different "Customers" by Month Six:** Without a shared domain model, the same core entity quietly forks across modules — reconciled by a fragile nightly script nobody fully trusts.
❌ **Rework Eating 20-30% of Capacity:** Past a product's second year, incoherence routinely consumes a fifth to a third of engineering time with zero new features to show for it.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **A Documented Domain Model as a Living Artifact:** Every new feature reviewed against it before implementation, with a named architecture owner holding veto authority separate from sprint delivery.
✅ **Amsterdam Owns the Model, Sits in Every Review:** Dutch-based leads hold the authority to reject a feature that would fork the domain model — before it ever reaches a sprint board.
✅ **Vietnam Builds and Verifies Continuously:** Contract and integration tests on every merge, at a day rate 40-55% below the €650-€900/day Dutch senior-engineer range.
