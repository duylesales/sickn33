The fastest MVP build and the fastest path to a fundable Series A are frequently two different products. Nobody mentions that in the pitch. 🚀🕳️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Corners Nobody Can Evaluate:** No tests, a schema that won't survive real growth, and copy-pasted authentication code.
❌ **The Deal-Killing Demo:** A competitor's six-week MVP closed a seed round, then a security gap killed its biggest enterprise deal during due diligence.
❌ **Frozen Post-Funding Velocity:** Untested code means every post-raise iteration risks breaking something else, right when speed matters most.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Minimum Core, Built Correctly:** Scope down to what proves the value proposition, not the full pitch-deck vision thinly built.
✅ **Non-Negotiable Security Fundamentals:** Established auth libraries, not tutorial code, as standard practice.
✅ **Documented Technical Debt:** Corners cut for the deadline are tracked and prioritized for the first post-funding sprint, not discovered as a surprise.

A modest premium over a corner-cutting build is recovered the first time due diligence asks a hard security question and gets a clean answer.
