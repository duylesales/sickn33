Manifera's founder has said the most expensive mistake he's watched growth companies make wasn't building the wrong app — it was building app one with zero thought toward app two. 📱🏗️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Re-Solving Auth From Scratch, Every Time:** Building each app as an isolated island means re-building authentication, user management, and notifications on every single build.
❌ **Design Drift Across the Portfolio:** No shared component library means every new app restyles buttons and forms from a blank canvas, quietly eroding trust in the brand as a coherent product.
❌ **The Second App Costs Almost As Much As the First:** Without a shared services layer, app number two and three each carry nearly the full original build cost.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Shared Services Layer, Built Once:** Authentication, user management, and notifications extracted into a common backend with versioned APIs any future app can call.
✅ **Amsterdam Governs the Shared/App-Specific Boundary:** Dutch architects define what belongs in the platform layer and revisit that boundary explicitly as the app portfolio grows.
✅ **Vietnam Builds the Platform for ~55% Cheaper Follow-On Apps:** A €18,000 upfront platform investment on the first build cuts each subsequent app's cost by roughly 55%.

A Portuguese manufacturer's third app came in faster than its second — because the platform, not luck, did the work. ⚡
