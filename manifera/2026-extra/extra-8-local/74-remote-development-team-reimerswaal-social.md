"Remote" and "unaccountable" got treated as the same word by the last vendor. They're not supposed to be. 🦪🔍

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **No Named Ownership:** When a shellfish-safety compliance bug hit, nobody could say who had touched the affected module or when.
❌ **Unenforced Code Review:** Sprint commitments got missed with no explanation, and a three-week forensic audit was needed just to trace what shipped.
❌ **No Rehearsed Incident Process:** The team improvised under pressure, turning a fixable bug into a multi-week compliance crisis.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Tooling-Enforced Review:** Every critical change requires a named, mandatory approver — no exceptions.
✅ **Named Component Ownership:** "Who owns this" is answerable in seconds, not reconstructed during a crisis.
✅ **Rehearsed Incident Drills:** Escalation paths tested before a real incident, not during one.

Incident-diagnosis time typically drops by more than half within two quarters of adopting this structure.
