"Full-stack" has gotten so broad that two candidates can both earn the label and have almost nothing in common in what they can actually deliver. 🧩💻

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Breadth Over Depth:** Three hires interviewed brilliantly across a checklist of frameworks, then stalled on the one hard problem the product actually has.
❌ **The Expensive Rework:** Shallow database schema design required a six-week senior re-architecture, costing an estimated €19,000.
❌ **Confidence Rewarded Over Calibration:** Interviews reward confident answers, not honest self-awareness about the edge of one's expertise.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Depth-First Testing:** Interviews weighted toward the one or two layers of the stack carrying real technical risk for this specific product.
✅ **Representative Working Sessions:** Paid, real-problem exercises replace generic algorithm puzzles.
✅ **Reference Checks That Actually Predict:** Ask about the hardest problem solved, not generic teamwork questions.

Hires under this model reach expected depth within six months at a meaningfully higher rate than under generic breadth-focused hiring.
