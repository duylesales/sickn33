Deploying an HRIS in Europe without works council data requirements in the architecture? That's a deployment blocker, not a compliance checkbox. 🇪🇺👥

Works councils in Germany, the Netherlands, and France have legally mandated consultation and data access rights that affect which employee data fields are collected, who can view them, and what changes require council approval. An HRIS that doesn't model these constraints architecturally will be blocked at deployment by legal requirements no amount of configuration can fix.
