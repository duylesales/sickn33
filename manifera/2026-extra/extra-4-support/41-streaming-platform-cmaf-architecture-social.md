Your streaming platform encodes every video twice — once for HLS, once for DASH. CMAF eliminates that duplication at the architecture level. 🎬⚡

Without it, you're maintaining two parallel encoding workflows, doubling your storage and CDN costs, and doubling your QA surface. The architectural decision to adopt CMAF from day one has compounding cost savings over time.
