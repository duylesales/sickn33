Your connected vehicle platform collects telematics data through the OEM's proprietary API. What happens when the OEM changes the API terms? You lose your data access overnight. 🚗📡

The Extended Vehicle standard (ISO 20078) defines OEM-independent access to vehicle data. A platform built on proprietary OEM APIs is functionally dependent on that OEM's business decisions. The Extended Vehicle model decouples your data architecture from any single manufacturer's roadmap.
