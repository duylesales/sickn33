🚨 "If a Model Performs Well on Standard Accuracy Metrics, It's Ready for Public ..." — 3 myths that create massive legal and operational liabilities faster than they save budget. 🤖⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Myth 1: "If a Model Performs Well on Standard Accuracy Metrics, It's Ready for Public Sector Deployment":** AI models produce convincing isolated outputs but hallucinate, misinterpret edge cases, and lack deep domain context.
❌ **Myth 2: "Explaining That a Model Is 'Highly Accurate' Satisfies the Public's Right to Understand Government Decisions Affecting Them":** Human curation, error checking, and compliance verification eliminate most projected raw volume savings.
❌ **Myth 3: "Full Automation Is the Natural End Goal, With Human Review as a Temporary Transitional Step":** Regulatory and civil liability remains 100% on the operator regardless of whether an algorithm generated the output.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Build disaggregated fairness evaluation across relevant demographic groups into the validation process from the start:** not relying on aggregate accuracy metrics alone to establish deployment readiness.
✅ **Architect the system for genuine case-level explainability:** capable of articulating why a specific individual received a specific decision outcome, not just describing overall system performance.
✅ **Design meaningful, substantive human oversight as a permanent architectural feature:** not a temporary safeguard planned for eventual removal once the system demonstrates sufficient statistical reliability.
