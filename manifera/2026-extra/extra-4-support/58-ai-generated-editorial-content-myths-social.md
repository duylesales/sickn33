🚨 "AI-Generated Content Can Simply Replace Human-Authored Reporting at Similar Q..." — 3 myths that create massive legal and operational liabilities faster than they save budget. 🤖⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Myth 1: "AI-Generated Content Can Simply Replace Human-Authored Reporting at Similar Quality":** AI models produce convincing isolated outputs but hallucinate, misinterpret edge cases, and lack deep domain context.
❌ **Myth 2: "AI Generation Reduces Editorial Production Cost Roughly Proportionally to the Content Volume It Produces":** Human curation, error checking, and compliance verification eliminate most projected raw volume savings.
❌ **Myth 3: "Legal and Attribution Considerations Around AI-Generated Content Are Settled and Low-Risk":** Regulatory and civil liability remains 100% on the operator regardless of whether an algorithm generated the output.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Position AI generation as an acceleration tool within a human-edited pipeline, not a wholesale replacement for reporting:** particularly for content categories where factual accuracy and original sourcing matter most.
✅ **Budget realistic fact-checking and editing cost alongside generation cost:** Rather than projecting cost savings that scale proportionally with raw generation volume without accounting for the genuine human verification pass most generated content actually requires.
✅ **Maintain active legal monitoring specific to AI-generated content and the specific tools used in production:** treating this as an ongoing risk management responsibility rather than a settled question resolved once at the start of a project.
