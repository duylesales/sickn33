Your embedded finance product is built on one BaaS provider. If they change their terms, raise prices, or lose their banking license, your product goes down with them. 🏦⚡

Embedded finance products that hardcode a single Banking-as-a-Service provider into their architecture are operationally dependent on that provider's business continuity. Treating BaaS as a replaceable layer — with abstraction interfaces, failover routing, and provider-agnostic data models — is the architectural decision that determines whether your product survives a provider disruption.
