🚨 "A Model That Detects Safety Violations Accurately in Testing Will Perform Sim..." — 3 myths that create massive legal and operational liabilities faster than they save budget. 🤖⚠️

**The Pain Points:**
❌ **Myth 1: "A Model That Detects Safety Violations Accurately in Testing Will Perform Similarly Reliably on a Real, Active Site":** AI models produce convincing isolated outputs but hallucinate, misinterpret edge cases, and lack deep domain context.
❌ **Myth 2: "False Positive Safety Alerts Are a Minor Inconvenience Compared to the Value of Catching Real Violations":** Human curation, error checking, and compliance verification eliminate most projected raw volume savings.
❌ **Myth 3: "AI-Detected Safety Violations Can Be Used Directly for Disciplinary or Liability Documentation Purposes Without Additional Verification":** Regulatory and civil liability remains 100% on the operator regardless of whether an algorithm generated the output.

**The Manifera Solution:**
✅ **Validate detection accuracy against genuinely representative real-site imagery:** Including poor lighting, dust, and occlusion conditions, not just curated, idealized test datasets.
✅ **Explicitly tune and monitor false positive rate as a genuine safety consideration:** recognizing the alert fatigue risk a high false positive rate creates, rather than defaulting to maximum sensitivity without weighing this tradeoff.
✅ **Require human verification before AI-detected violations are used for disciplinary or liability documentation purposes:** treating AI detection as a triage and alerting tool, not standalone authoritative evidence.
